# Strategy Guides

Most board game strategy advice is written for people who already win. It assumes you know the opening, speaks in shorthand, and treats the table as a math problem. This category is written for the other player: the one who likes Catan, has played twenty games, and keeps finishing third.

Each guide covers one game and answers three questions. What decides the game more than new players think it does? What do good players do in the first few turns that you can copy tonight? And where do most losses actually come from? The answers are specific. In Wingspan it is eggs and round-four planning. In Carcassonne it is farmers. In 7 Wonders it is the science cards that look like a plan and usually are not.

We tried to keep the advice at the level of a real table. Not the tournament meta, not a simulation of ten thousand games, but what works against your brother-in-law and the quiet friend who somehow always wins. Where a bit of counting helps, we show it. Azul floor line math and Splendor tempo fit in a paragraph each, and both change results immediately.

A note on the goal. Winning more is nice, but the better outcome is that the game gets closer. A table where everyone understands why the leader is leading plays a different Catan than one where three people are confused and one is smug. Good strategy spreads. Teach it to the people you play with and the nights get better for everyone.

Start with the game your group plays most. Each guide stands alone, so there is no order to follow. If you host, the Codenames piece is the one to read first, because it fixes the most common frustration at the most common party table: clues that are too clever by half.

Two guides cover cooperative games, Pandemic on Heroic and the Dominion opening, which is a different kind of problem. In those the opponent is the deck, and the advice is about sequencing and discipline rather than reading people. They pair well with our cooperative games category if you want more.

Everything here assumes the base game with no expansions unless the guide says otherwise.
