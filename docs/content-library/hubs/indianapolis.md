# Indianapolis

The Ludarium is being built in Indianapolis, so this is the category we know from the ground. Board games in Indianapolis are easier to find than most people think and harder to find than they should be. There are good stores, free brewery meetups, library afternoons, and the largest tabletop convention in North America every August. What there is not is one place that lists it all. This is our attempt.

The guides split three ways. First, where to play. The stores piece sorts Indianapolis game stores by neighborhood and says what each one is good for, including which ones let you sit and play. The weekly game nights guide goes day by day through public events you can walk into. Separate pieces cover D&D on the north side, Magic: The Gathering across the metro, family-friendly places, and date night.

Second, Hamilton County. Carmel, Fishers, Noblesville and Westfield have their own scene, and it is growing faster than downtown. The Hamilton County piece maps it and names what is still missing, which is part of why we are building what we are building.

Third, Gen Con. Three guides here are for the convention: one for first-timers, one for surviving four days on your feet, and one for locals who want to enjoy the weekend without a badge. There is a separate conventions category for the rest, from packing lists to hotel strategy.

We check hours and events, but they change. Each piece notes when it was last updated, and every venue is worth a quick look at its own calendar before you drive over. If you run a game night we have missed, tell us.

This category will keep growing as the lounge gets closer. For now it is the guide we wish had existed when we started looking for a table.
