# Cube Commonwealth

Cube Commonwealth is a Magic: The Gathering Cube community in Indianapolis, and the first community branch of The Ludarium. For about three years we drafted every week with 12 to 16 players and a Discord of roughly 300, most of them local. We paused when life got in the way. Now we are relaunching, built this time so the night does not depend on any one person.

If you have never drafted a cube: it is a curated set of Magic cards, usually 360 or 540 of the best and most fun cards ever printed, that a group drafts over and over. Nobody buys packs and nobody needs a collection. You draft from the pile, build a 40-card deck, and play three rounds. The same cube plays differently every week because the people around the table change what gets taken. That is why a cube community lasts: the game is deep enough to hold veterans and open enough to teach a first-timer in one night.

A Cube Commonwealth night has a fixed weekday and venue, pods that fire at a fixed time, a teaching pod for anyone new, pairings run by a pod captain, and no entry fee beyond what the venue asks. <!-- EDITOR: add night, venue, fire time, Discord invite -->
