# Inbound link strategy

Goal: more sites linking to theludarium.com, so Google trusts the site sooner, crawls more of it, and ranks the articles. Links also bring readers directly, which is the point of most of these plays.

The rule for every play: earn the link by being the most useful thing on the subject. No bought links, link swaps at scale, paid directories, or private blog networks. Google penalizes those, and they don't fit the brand.

Who does what: Claude builds the pages, assets, lists, and draft emails. Levi sends every email, posts every comment, and appears on every podcast. Nothing goes out under Levi's name without his OK.

## 1. Make the site easy to link to (build once)

These assets earn links on their own once people find them.

- **Teach sheets.** 36 printable one-pagers. Each PDF footer reads "Free to print and share", and each teach sheet page has a "Print it, share it, link to it" box with a copyable link. Stores, libraries, teachers, and club organizers are who print these, and they link to what they print.
- **Guild certificate and register.** Every Expert Game Guide who opts in gets a register entry. Each entry has a "Copy link to this entry" button, and the certificate maker produces a ready-made link to paste into the reply, so guides can put it on their own sites, club pages, and store bios.
- **Tools.** Card Catalogue, RPG Demo Library, and Game Night Madness each get a one-paragraph "about" and a screenshot that bloggers can lift. Game Night Madness stays free of affiliate links (BGG terms).
- **Press page** at `/press/` (linked in the footer): quick facts, a paragraph to use as is, Levi's short bio, logos and photos to download, interview topics, and a contact email. Journalists and podcast hosts link to the page they used.

## 2. Answer journalists (start now: gift guide season)

Journalists post requests for sources daily. Answering with a quotable expert answer earns links from news and lifestyle sites, which are the most valuable links a young site can get.

- Sign up for a journalist-request service such as Qwoted or Featured and set alerts for: board games, game night, holiday gifts, family activities, team building, loneliness, friendship, third places, tabletop RPGs, Magic: The Gathering.
- November and December are peak season for gift guides and holiday-gathering stories. The site already has the matching articles to cite.
- Claude drafts each answer from the existing articles; Levi edits and sends. Aim for two or three answers a week.

## 3. Reach out to people who already care

Short, personal emails to people whose readers want the page. One email, one follow-up, then let it go.

Claude builds each list (name, site, the exact page to pitch, why it fits) and drafts the emails in Levi's voice.

## 4. Be the helpful person in the community

Reddit (r/boardgames, r/soloboardgaming, r/soloroleplaying, r/mtgcube), BoardGameGeek forums, and Discord servers. Answer the question fully first; link an article only when it's the best answer. Those links are mostly marked nofollow, so they don't pass ranking credit directly. They bring readers, and readers who run blogs and newsletters are where the real links come from.

Keep it to a few genuine answers a week. Never post the same link across many threads.

## 5. Podcasts and guest pieces

Board game, RPG, and loneliness or friendship podcasts need guests. Levi's angle, togethering and why people need a place to play, is a story hosts want. Every episode page links the guest's site. The malaphor book is a second hook for book and language shows.

Target one pitch a week; Claude builds the list and the pitch.

## 6. Make something worth citing (later, once the list is bigger)

Original data earns links from press for years. Candidates:

- **A State of Game Night survey** to newsletter subscribers: how often people play, with whom, what stops them. Publish the results as an article with charts.
- **Card Catalogue data:** which games score highest on Table Talk and the other feel dimensions, published as a ranking.
- **The Guild register:** how many Expert Game Guides, from where, teaching which games.

Run the survey once the newsletter has enough subscribers for the numbers to hold up.

## 7. Clean-up plays (low effort, ongoing)

- **Unlinked mentions:** set a Google Alert for "The Ludarium" and "theludarium". When someone mentions the site without a link, a friendly note usually gets one.
- **Book listings:** the malaphor book's Amazon author page, Goodreads, and any book blog reviews should link to `/malaphors/`.
- **Profiles:** BoardGameGeek, LinkedIn, any social accounts, and the Lemon Squeezy store all link back to the site.

## Measuring it

- Search Console, Links report: top linking sites and top linked pages. Check once a month and log new linking sites here.
- Watch referral traffic from each source, not only link counts. A link that sends readers is worth more than one that doesn't.
- Expect slow results: links build over months, and rankings follow them by weeks to months.
