# Indianapolis outreach

Play 3 in `docs/marketing/LINK-STRATEGY.md`. These are the stores, cafes, libraries, groups and outlets that the local articles already mention. That gives every email an easy, honest opener: "we wrote about you." What we offer: the article itself (a place a store can link from its site or socials), free teach sheets for their demo and family nights, and the lounge story, where stores sell and The Ludarium hosts.

Keep the lounge claim honest. There is no lounge yet. It is a plan with a waitlist, and partners help shape it.

Contacts checked 2026-11-04, and only ones each place publishes itself. Rows marked **check first** have a contact that couldn't be confirmed. Nothing has been sent.

### Game stores

Subject: We wrote about [store name]

> Hi [name or "there"],
>
> I'm Levi, and I run The Ludarium, a tabletop gaming site here in Indianapolis. We featured [store name] in our guide to [topic], and I wanted you to know: [article URL]. If anything is out of date, tell me and I'll fix it. If you'd like to share it, I'd be glad.
>
> I also wanted to offer something for your game nights. The Ludarium has free one-page teach sheets for [two or three games they run, e.g. Magic, Lorcana, and Pokémon]: [teach sheet URL]. They're free to print and hand out, and you're welcome to keep a stack at the counter.
>
> One more thing, for later. I'm planning a membership game lounge in Indianapolis: a place for clubs and groups to meet and play, with no retail. The idea is that stores sell and the lounge hosts, so it sends players your way rather than competing with you. It's early, and I'd love to hear what you'd want from a place like that: https://theludarium.com/lounges/indianapolis/
>
> Thanks for what you do for the scene here.
>
> Levi JA Wilson
> The Ludarium, theludarium.com
