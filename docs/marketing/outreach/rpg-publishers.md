# RPG Demo Library: publisher outreach

Play 3 in `docs/marketing/LINK-STRATEGY.md`. Each publisher below has a free, browser-playable intro adventure on theludarium.com built on their open license. The ask is small: "you're welcome to list it on your resources or community page." One email, one follow-up about ten days later, then let it go.

Research checked 2026-11-04. Contacts are only ones the publishers publish themselves. Nothing has been sent.

## Send order

Start with the four that have a real listing page or a submission path. Those are the ones most likely to link.

1. **Tomkin Press** (Ironsworn and Starforged): has a Community Resources page that lists apps and tools. Two demos.
2. **Blades in the Dark** (One Seven Design): has a Fan Creations page and asks for submissions through its feedback form.
3. **Skellies** (The Griffon's Saddlebag): its contact form has a "Third-Party Module Review" subject, and the site lists approved third-party content.
4. **24XX** (Jason Tocci): community board on itch.io, plus a 24XX itch collection with 200+ games.

Then the ones with no listing page, where the goal is a friendly note and maybe a share: Evil Hat (Fate), Monte Cook Games (Cypher), Ben Milton (Knave), John Harper (Lasers & Feelings), Dungeon World.

Skip Paizo. There is no listing for ORC work, and the demo deliberately avoids the Pathfinder name, so a pitch would gain nothing.

## Check before sending

These came up in the research. Each is a two-minute check of the demo's credits against the publisher's rules, so the first thing a publisher sees is a clean page.

- **Ironsworn and Starforged: checked 2026-11-04, clear to send.** Tomkin allows commercial use of the Ironsworn SRD, asset cards, and oracle tables (CC BY 4.0). The rest of the rulebook is non-commercial only (CC BY-NC-SA): the setting write-ups for the Ironlands and its regions, the foe and NPC entries, and the world truths.
  - The demo and the paid PDF *The Bell Beneath the Fen* use setting names: "the Ironlands", "the Flooded Lands", "the Havens". Those names all appear in CC BY text too: the Region oracle, the Ironlander Names oracle, and the adventure moves intro.
  - No wording from the non-commercial text appears in the book or the demo. A check of every five-word run in all 229 non-commercial passages against both found only the everyday phrase "all that is left of".
  - Starforged is clean: the whole Reference Guide is CC BY.
- **Blades:** don't use "Blades in the Dark" in a product title, and don't use the One Seven Design or Blades logos. The demo is titled "The Cinderhouse Score" under "Forged in the Dark", which fits.
- **Skellies:** CC BY-SA, so the demo text must stay CC BY-SA. The README already says it does.
- **Cypher:** the CSOL requires its notice text and rules out "overt political agendas". The README carries the notice.
- **Fate:** Evil Hat's preferred credit names "Fate Core System and Fate Accelerated Edition". The demo credits Fate Condensed, which is also CC BY. Fine as is.

## Draft emails

To: shawn@tomkinpress.com
Subject: A free browser intro for Ironsworn and Starforged

> Hi Shawn,
>
> I'm Levi, and I run The Ludarium, a tabletop gaming site in Indianapolis. I built two free solo intro adventures that run in the browser, one for Ironsworn and one for Starforged. The page plays the oracle and the moves, the player makes the choices, and every roll follows the rules from the SRD and the Reference Guide. Each takes about 20 minutes and ends with a link to your games.
>
> Ironsworn: https://theludarium.com/rpg-demo-library/games/ironsworn/
> Starforged: https://theludarium.com/rpg-demo-library/games/starforged/
>
> I made them for people who hear "solo RPG" and can't picture it yet. If you think they'd help your newcomers, you're welcome to add them to your Community Resources page. And if anything in them doesn't sit right with you, tell me and I'll fix it.
>
> Thanks for making a game that so many people play alone and still talk about together.
>
> Levi JA Wilson
> The Ludarium, theludarium.com
