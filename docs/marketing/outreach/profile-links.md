# Profile links to add this week

Play 7 in `docs/marketing/LINK-STRATEGY.md`. Each one takes a few minutes, and Levi does them himself. Most profile links are nofollow, so they won't move rankings much on their own. They help people (and Google) connect the profiles to the site, and they bring readers.

Menu paths were checked 2026-11-04 against each service's help pages. Sites change their menus often, so if a label is different, the field is usually close by. Lines marked "not confirmed" couldn't be checked without signing in.

## Amazon (the malaphor book)

Link: `https://theludarium.com/malaphors/`

Amazon no longer allows website links in the Author Central bio (since about March 2025), and it removed the blog feed. So the link goes in the book itself:

1. **Book description:** in KDP (kdp.amazon.com), open the paperback, then **Edit eBook details** or **Paperback details**, then the **Description** box. Add a closing line such as: "Send in your own mixed-up saying at theludarium.com/malaphors." Do it for both the paperback (B0HNTSXVS6) and Kindle (B0HNTT7KKV) editions. The edit needs Amazon's review again before it goes live, which usually takes a few days.
2. **Author Central bio:** author.amazon.com, **Profile** tab, "Your Biographies", **Edit**. No link, but name The Ludarium in plain text so readers can find it: "Levi JA Wilson founded The Ludarium, a tabletop gaming brand in Indianapolis."
3. The printed book already sends readers to /malaphors/.

## Goodreads (the malaphor book)

Link: `https://theludarium.com/malaphors/`

## Google Alerts

Signed in to the Google account that should get the emails, go to https://www.google.com/alerts.

1. Type `"The Ludarium"` with the quotes, then click **Show options**:
   - **How often:** At most once a day
   - **Sources:** Automatic
   - **Language:** English
   - **Region:** Any region
   - **How many:** All results (the default, "Only the best results", misses small blogs)
   - **Deliver to:** your email
2. Click **Create Alert**.
3. Repeat for `theludarium` and `"Levi JA Wilson"`.

When an alert shows a mention with no link, send a short note:

> Hi [name], thanks for mentioning The Ludarium in [piece]. If you're open to it, a link to theludarium.com [or the exact page they mentioned] would help readers find us. Either way, I appreciated it.
