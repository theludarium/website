# Course video script template

One 10 to 20 minute teach video per course on @theludarium, with Levi's own copy of the game on camera. Original footage only; no publisher art on screen beyond the physical game being played. Put the course link in the description and a pinned comment.

1. **Cold open (15 seconds).** The one thing this game makes people feel, said over the table. "By the end of this video you can teach it tonight."
2. **What it is (30 seconds).** Players, time, the goal in one sentence (the teach sheet's `goal`).
3. **Setup (1 to 2 minutes).** Build it on camera in the teach sheet's order.
4. **A turn (3 to 5 minutes).** Play two or three real turns, narrating the choices, not just the rules.
5. **Scoring (2 to 3 minutes).** Score a small example by hand, out loud.
6. **Three tips (2 minutes).** The teach sheet's three tips, each shown on the table.
7. **Where to go next (30 seconds).** "The free course walks you from here to your first tournament. Lesson one is in the description." End card to the course page.

**Two Shorts per course:** the 60-second teach (steps 2, 4 and 5 compressed) and one tip from lesson 3. Thursday Shorts link the week's Ludometer mover once the index runs.

**Description template:**

```
How to play GAME in N minutes, from The Ludarium.
Free course (8 short lessons by email): https://theludarium.com/academy/SLUG/
```
