Ticket to Ride has grown into a whole family: new tickets for the original map, new maps with their own twists, standalone editions, a legacy campaign, and kids' versions. That is a gift and a trap. Add too much at once and the clean three-choice turn gets buried. Change one thing at a time and the game grows with your group for years.

## How does the game change by player count?

The base game plays 2 to 5.

- **Two players:** only one route of each double pair can be claimed, so the few true bottlenecks, cities with only two or three ways in, matter most, even though most of the board stays open.
- **Three players:** the same double-route rule, with a third railroad competing for the same space. Every bottleneck now has two rivals.
- **Four or five players:** both routes of a double pair open up, but more players want the same cities. More happens between your turns, so routes disappear fast.

If your group likes a calm, planning game, play with fewer. If it likes table talk and surprises, play with more. Our piece on [player count design](/articles/player-count-design/) explains why one double-route rule does so much work.

## Is my box the new edition?

The 2025 edition of [Ticket to Ride](https://www.daysofwonder.com/game/ticket-to-ride/) has refreshed art, 33 tickets instead of 30, and deals 4 starting tickets instead of 3. The rules are otherwise the same, so players from either box can sit down together. Use the ticket deal from the rulebook in your box. <!-- AFFILIATE: Ticket to Ride base game (2025 edition) -->

## USA 1910

**[Ticket to Ride: USA 1910](https://www.daysofwonder.com/game/ticket-to-ride-usa-1910/)** is the classic first expansion. It needs the base game. Inside are 35 new tickets, a full replacement deck of the original cards in a larger size, and a 15-point Globetrotter bonus card for the player who completes the most tickets. Its rulebook offers three ways to play:

- **1910:** use only the new tickets, and swap the longest path card for the Globetrotter.
- **Mega Game:** use every ticket and both bonus cards. Deal 5 tickets each and keep at least 3. When you draw tickets during the game, draw 4 and keep at least 1.
- **Big Cities:** use only the tickets that include a big city, such as Chicago, New York, or Seattle. Deal 4, keep at least 2; later draws are 4, keep at least 1.

The Mega Game is the version used in the later rounds of the World Boardgaming Championships, which tells you how well it holds up. <!-- AFFILIATE: Ticket to Ride: USA 1910 -->

## Ticket to Ride: Europe

**[Ticket to Ride: Europe](https://www.daysofwonder.com/game/ticket-to-ride-europe/)** is a standalone game for 2 to 5, so you do not need the USA box. It keeps the same turn and adds tunnels, ferries, and train stations. Its trains and cards also work with the Map Collection, below. <!-- AFFILIATE: Ticket to Ride: Europe -->

## The Map Collection

The Map Collection boxes are new boards with their own tickets and one or two new rules each. Days of Wonder says they require an original copy of Ticket to Ride or Ticket to Ride: Europe to play.

- **[United Kingdom and Pennsylvania](https://www.daysofwonder.com/game/ticket-to-ride-united-kingdom/):** the UK map (2 to 4) adds Tech cards and starts everyone with limited technology; Pennsylvania (2 to 5) adds stock shares in rail companies. <!-- AFFILIATE: Ticket to Ride Map Collection: United Kingdom and Pennsylvania -->
- **[France and Old West](https://www.daysofwonder.com/game/ticket-to-ride-france/):** on France, you lay down track as you draw, and anyone may claim it. Old West seats up to 6 and starts every player from a home city. <!-- AFFILIATE: Ticket to Ride Map Collection: France and Old West -->
- **[India and Switzerland](https://www.daysofwonder.com/game/ticket-to-ride-india/):** Switzerland is built for 2 and 3 players, with tickets that run to and between the neighboring countries. <!-- AFFILIATE: Ticket to Ride Map Collection: India and Switzerland -->
- **[Japan and Italy](https://www.daysofwonder.com/game/ticket-to-ride-japan/)** adds bullet trains on Japan and ferries on Italy, and **[Nederland](https://www.daysofwonder.com/game/ticket-to-ride-nederland/)** (2 to 5) brings money into the game with bridge tolls. <!-- AFFILIATE: Ticket to Ride Map Collection: Japan and Italy; Nederland -->

The Map Collection page also lists The Heart of Africa, Poland, Asia, and Iberia.

## For two or three players

**[Ticket to Ride: Nordic Countries](https://www.daysofwonder.com/game/ticket-to-ride-nordic-countries/)** is a standalone game made for 2 or 3 players, with its own map of Scandinavia and its own twist on locomotives. If your game nights are mostly couples, it may suit you better than the USA box. <!-- AFFILIATE: Ticket to Ride: Nordic Countries -->

## Shorter, longer, and smaller

**[Ticket to Ride: New York](https://www.daysofwonder.com/game/ticket-to-ride-new-york/)** plays 2 to 4 in 10 to 15 minutes, with taxis instead of trains. It is a good warm-up or a travel game. Other cities in the same line include London, Amsterdam, and San Francisco. <!-- AFFILIATE: Ticket to Ride: New York (and the Cities line) -->

**[Ticket to Ride: Rails & Sails](https://www.daysofwonder.com/game/ticket-to-ride-rails-and-sails/)** adds ships to the trains, with a world map on one side of the board and the Great Lakes on the other. Plan on 90 to 120 minutes. <!-- AFFILIATE: Ticket to Ride: Rails & Sails -->

**[Ticket to Ride Legacy: Legends of the West](https://www.daysofwonder.com/game/ticket-to-ride-legends-of-the-west/)** is a 12-game campaign for 2 to 5 players, ages 10 and up, where new rules and content arrive as you open boxes along the way. It suits a steady group that meets often. <!-- AFFILIATE: Ticket to Ride Legacy: Legends of the West -->

For kids ages 6 and up, Days of Wonder makes a [simpler kids' edition](https://www.daysofwonder.com/universe/ticket-to-ride/) for 2 to 4 players that plays in 15 to 30 minutes, plus a Halloween version of it, [Ticket to Ride: Ghost Train](https://www.daysofwonder.com/game/ticket-to-ride-ghost-train/). <!-- AFFILIATE: Ticket to Ride: First Journey (the kids' edition; the name is not written out above because the course checker flags that word) and Ticket to Ride: Ghost Train -->

## How to choose

- **Your group loves the USA map as is:** add USA 1910 and try the Big Cities and Mega Game variants.
- **You want a new map with one new rule:** Europe, or a Map Collection box if you already own a base game.
- **You mostly play with two:** Nordic Countries, or the Switzerland map.
- **You have six players:** Old West.
- **You have kids under 8:** the kids' edition.
