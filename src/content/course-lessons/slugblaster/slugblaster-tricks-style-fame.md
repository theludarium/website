Welcome to Level 2. You know the rules. Now you start playing for something. In [Slugblaster](/academy/slugblaster/how-to-play-slugblaster/), that something is **style**, and through style, **fame**. The rules sheet compares style to experience and money rolled into one: it buys your downtime scenes, earns new traits, and pushes your crew from Nobodies to Legends.

## Where style comes from

There are four main sources.

1. **Tricks.** Say "check it" before a roll. On a success, mark 1 style, plus 1 for each kick you spent on that roll.
2. **Personal style bonus.** Each personality earns +1 style after a run where the teen showed its quality: toughness or focus for The Grit, boldness for The Guts, curiosity or creativity for The Smarts, passion or empathy for The Heart, ease or flow for The Chill.
3. **Crew brands.** Your crew picks two brands at creation, each worth +3 crew style after a run that fits it. Shredders race and land big tricks. Hackers salvage parts or mess with tech. Good Kids help others. Punks leave their mark. Partiers have a good time. Weirdos take an odd approach. Couriers deliver things. Ultranauts find new worlds. Underdogs get in over their heads.
4. **Prizes.** Many runs pay a bonus of 3 to 5 crew style for reaching the goal.

Some traits and opportunities add more. The Chill marks style for rolling doubles. Some spots are an **amp** prize, where every trick earns +1 style.

## Tricks: when to say "check it"

A trick makes any problem on that roll worse. Worse can mean two problems instead of one, everyone getting slammed, or a super slam that takes two clean-ups to clear. That is a real cost, so pick your moments.

Good times to trick:

- **When you have dice to spare.** With two or three dice, the chance of rolling at least one 6 climbs fast (the next lesson has the numbers). Tricks on a big pool are much safer than tricks on one die.
- **When a 4 or 5 still moves the story.** If the worst likely outcome is a slam you can afford to take or nope, the trick costs less than it looks. (A super slam takes two nopes, so keep some trouble room.)
- **When the trick is the point.** Filming at a famous spot, a contest, a run for a sponsor. If the run is about looking cool, look cool.

And a quiet tip: you do not need to make a separate roll to trick. Add it to an action you were going to take anyway. Blast the bug *and* do a 360 off the vending machine.

## Kick turns landed tricks into fame

Each kick spent on a successful trick is +1 style. A trick with 2 kick that lands is worth 3 style. Since kick is also spent on a failed roll, spend it on tricks where you also have a die or two of boost. The Guts, with three kick boxes and a refill on every trick, is built for this.

## Clean-up is payday

After the disaster rolls, the GM runs clean-up. This is where most style arrives:

- The prize, if you reached the goal.
- Each player's personal style bonus, if the table agrees they earned it.
- +3 crew style for each brand the run fit.

A run that fits both brands and pays a prize can be worth 11 crew style or more, before tricks. Crew style is a single pool. Players can split it among themselves or bank it on the crew sheet toward fame. Players can also donate their own style to the crew, which the book frames as being a team player.

## The six fame tiers

Fame is bought with style. Each step up costs a set amount and brings both immediate effects and new perks you can buy.

| Tier | Name | Cost | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Nobodies | Start | Starting perk: Masks or Advanced Portal Tech |
| 1 | Up & Comers | 5 style | +1 to opportunities and challenges, a chance to rebrand |
| 2 | Well-Established | 7 style | +1 and -1 with two neutral factions, a free two-way portal |
| 3 | Major Players | 9 style | Four factions move further from neutral, a third brand |
| 4 | Rising Stars | 9 style | +1 to opportunities and challenges, +1 hype |
| 5 | Legends | 11 style | The scene never forgets you |

Perks cost style too, usually 2 to 5 each. Early ones keep you alive (masks and hazwear to survive harsher worlds, logic binders for the strangest ones). Later ones add hype, legacy, or faction standing, like merch, a crew name on a shoe, or a magazine cover story.

## Spend or save?

Every point spent on a beat is a point not spent on fame, and the reverse. A simple rule for new crews: bank crew style toward the next tier until you reach Up & Comers, since the rebrand and the +1 on opportunity rolls help everyone (challenge rolls get +1 too, so expect bigger trouble as well). After that, let personal style fund beats and let crew style fund fame. The [downtime lesson](/academy/slugblaster/slugblaster-downtime-beats/) covers what those beats buy.
