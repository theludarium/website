Every teen in [Slugblaster](/academy/slugblaster/how-to-play-slugblaster/) starts with one of five personalities: The Grit, The Guts, The Smarts, The Heart, or The Chill. The book is clear on one point: a personality is not a class. Any slugblaster can shred, shoot, party, and rewire a fusion array. Your personality decides *how* you do it, and it shapes how you earn and spend your bonuses.

## What every personality sheet has

Each sheet has the same parts:

- **Attitude:** your personal boost and kick boxes, plus one rule for refilling them during a run. You describe how your grit, guts, smarts, or heart helps the action.
- **Traits:** special abilities. Pick one at the start and earn more with trait beats in downtime. One of your traits can come from another personality.
- **Style bonus:** +1 style after any run where you showed your personality's quality.
- **Extra gear:** one special item that fits the personality, plus a second you can take in place of a regular piece of gear.
- **Its own beats:** a trait beat and a four-step arc you can play through in downtime.

The attitude counts below come from the 1.33 playsheets.

## The Grit

Focus, patience, resilience. The kid who practiced one trick for a month. Attitude holds **3 boost and 1 kick**, the most boost boxes of any sheet, and it refills 1 whenever you fail any action. That makes The Grit the steady one: every failure loads the next attempt.

Traits lean into toughness. One lets you take a slam you cannot nope for +2 dice. Another gives you an extra slam box and a style each time you are slammed. Style bonus: show toughness or focus.

**Play The Grit if** you like being dependable, taking hits for the crew, and grinding toward a goal.

## The Guts

Confidence, boldness, sass. Attitude holds **1 boost and 3 kick**, so The Guts hits hard rather than safely. It refills 1 whenever you attempt a trick, even if you fail. The more you show off, the more fuel you have.

Traits include an extra die on every trick, going first whenever there is a question of who goes first, a bigger version of the dare, and an extra die on disaster rolls plus the option to take a disaster for a willing friend. Style bonus: show boldness or risk-taking.

**Play The Guts if** you want to say "check it" constantly and play the loose cannon.

## The Smarts

Curiosity, not grades. Attitude holds **2 boost and 1 kick**, and it refills when you or a nearby teammate fails an action. The book notes this is the one rule it changed in this edition, so older copies may differ.

Traits reward clever play: style for creative tricks even on a fail, technobabble for a bonus die, a second signature device, and swapping mods on the fly. Style bonus: show curiosity or creativity.

**Play The Smarts if** you love asking questions, adding to the world, and collecting device mods.

## The Heart

Integrity, empathy, passion. Attitude holds **1 boost and 2 kick**, and it refills whenever you, or a teammate you are helping, succeed on any action. The Heart thrives on teamwork.

Traits include style for taking a slam for someone else, a once-per-run pep talk that refills crew hype or clears a friend's slam, and a once-per-run automatic 6. Style bonus: show passion or empathy.

**Play The Heart if** you want the drama, the relationships, and the big moment of standing up for your friends.

## The Chill

Effortless, lucky, going with the flow. The Chill's attitude works differently: you get **+1 die on every action**, always, with no boxes to mark and no refill to track. It cannot be shared with teammates, and it gives no kick.

Traits are about luck and noticing: style when you roll doubles, spotting what everyone else missed, a once-per-run stroke of dumb luck, and using locked device mods by mashing buttons. Style bonus: show ease or flow.

**Play The Chill if** you want to crack jokes, eat snacks, and still roll two dice on every action.

## The trait every sheet shares

Each personality has a version of the same flexible trait: Skill for The Grit, Raw Talent for The Guts, Know-How for The Smarts, Passion for The Heart, Quirk for The Chill. You fill in a blank, like flip tricks or xenobiology, and when it helps an action you can mark 1 trouble to turn a 1 to 3 into a 4 or 5. It is a solid first trait for any new player.

## Picking your signature device

Your signature is the device you love and keep modifying: a hoverboard, a raygun, sneakers, a backpack, a robot friend, and more. Each has a **function** (what it lets you do), **turbo** (its own boost and kick), and **mods**. You start with one mod that costs 2 components. [Level 3 covers devices in depth](/academy/slugblaster/slugblaster-signature-devices-mods/). For now, pick the one whose function makes you grin.

## A balanced crew

You do not need all five personalities. A crew of three Guts is chaos in the best way. But mixing refill triggers helps: a Smarts and a Heart refill on opposite results, so someone is always topping up.
