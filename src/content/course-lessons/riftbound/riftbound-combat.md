Combat decides who holds the battlefields, and battlefields decide the game. The rules for it are precise, and that is good news: once you know them, a fight stops being a surprise and becomes a sum you can work out before you commit. This lesson walks through a combat in the order the Core Rules run it.

## When does a combat start?

A combat starts when units of two different players are at the same battlefield. Usually that happens because you move units onto a battlefield where your opponent has units. The player whose units arrived is the **attacker**. The other is the **defender**.

Only two players can ever fight at one battlefield. That matters more in multiplayer, which a Level 2 lesson covers.

## Step 1: the showdown

Every combat opens with a **showdown**, a window where players take turns playing cards with Action or Reaction.

- The attacker gets **focus** first. With focus, you may play an Action or Reaction card or use such an ability, or pass.
- When a card is played, it goes on the **Chain**. Players can answer it with Reaction cards before it resolves.
- Once the Chain resolves, focus passes to the next player.
- When every player passes in a row without playing anything, the showdown closes and damage happens.

Two habits help here. Say "pass" out loud, so both players know where the showdown stands. And remember that passing is not final until your opponent also passes: if they play a card, you get focus back.

## Step 2: damage

1. **Add up the Might** of all attacking units, then of all defending units. Use current Might, including Assault on attackers and Shield on defenders.
2. **The attacker assigns** their total as damage among the defending units, then the defender does the same to the attackers.
3. **Lethal first.** You must assign a unit enough damage to kill it before moving on to the next unit. You can't spread 5 damage across four units as 2, 1, 1, 1. And you can't put more than lethal on one unit while another is still waiting, unless no units are left to take it.
4. **Tank** units must be assigned lethal damage before any non-Tank unit with the same controller. A unit with **Backline** must be assigned damage last.
5. **All damage is dealt at once.** A unit with damage equal to or greater than its Might is killed.

Damage never lowers Might. A unit with 4 Might and 3 damage on it still deals 4.

## Step 3: resolution

After damage, every unit heals. Then:

- **Only attackers left:** the attacker takes control, which is a conquer if they have not scored that battlefield this turn.
- **Any defenders left:** surviving attackers are sent back to their base. Being sent back does not ready them, so units that moved there stay exhausted.
- **Nobody left:** the battlefield becomes uncontrolled. The defender loses it even though the attacker did not win it.

If the defender loses control, any facedown card the defender had hidden there is removed too.

## Three worked fights

**An even trade.** You attack with two 3 Might units, 6 in total. The defender has one 4 Might unit. You assign 4 to it, lethal, and the extra 2 can go on it too since nothing else is there. The defender assigns 4: lethal 3 to one of your units first, then 1 to the other. Result: their unit dies, one of yours dies, and the other survives, heals, and conquers.

**The Tank wall.** You attack with a 5 and a 2, total 7. The defender has a 4 Might unit with Tank and a 3 Might unit, also total 7. You must kill the Tank first: 4 to it, then 3 to the other unit. Both die. They assign 5 to your 5 Might unit and 2 to your 2. Both die. Nobody is left, so the battlefield becomes uncontrolled. A fair trade on Might, but if that was their battlefield, you have stopped their hold.

**Assault and Shield.** Your 3 Might unit has Assault 2, so it attacks at 5. Their 4 Might unit has Shield, so it defends at 5. Each deals 5 to the other and both die. The same two units the other way round, with theirs attacking and yours defending, fight 4 against 3: theirs survives and yours dies.

## Reading the fight before you start it

Before any move onto an occupied battlefield, ask four questions:

- **What is the Might on both sides right now?**
- **How many runes does my opponent have ready,** and how many cards in hand? Ready runes plus cards mean a possible trick.
- **Is there a facedown card at that battlefield?** A hidden card can be played there as a Reaction, without paying its normal cost.
- **If the fight goes badly, what do I still hold?** A fight you can afford to lose is a fight you can take.
