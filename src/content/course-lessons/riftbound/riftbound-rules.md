Riftbound looks busy at first: two decks per player, cards in a dozen places, and words like channel and recycle. Underneath, a turn has a calm rhythm. Four quick steps get you ready, then you do whatever you like with what you have. This lesson walks through a two-player game, the mode the rules call a Duel, using the terms from the official Core Rules.

## How do you set up?

![One player's side of the play area: two battlefields in the middle with a facedown slot each, the Legend and Chosen Champion on the left, the base with units and runes in the center, and the Main Deck, Rune Deck, trash, and score on the right](/academy/riftbound/setup.svg)

1. Put your **Champion Legend** in your Legend zone and your **Chosen Champion** beside it in your Champion zone, both face up.
2. Shuffle the other 39 cards of your Main Deck. Shuffle your 12-card Rune Deck separately.
3. Each player picks 1 of their 3 battlefields at random. Both go in the middle of the table. The rest stay out of this game.
4. Choose a first player at random.
5. Each player draws 4. Then, in turn order, each player may **mulligan**: set aside up to 2 cards, draw that many, then put the set-aside cards on the bottom of the deck.

Leave room in front of you for your **base**, where your units, gear, and runes live, and for your **trash**.

## The four steps that start every turn

Every turn opens the same way. The quick start names them A, B, C, D.

- **Awaken.** Ready everything you control: turn your units, runes, and Legend upright.
- **Beginning.** First, any "start of turn" effects. Then you **hold**: score 1 point for each battlefield you control.
- **Channel.** Put the top 2 runes of your Rune Deck onto the table, ready. On their very first turn, the second player channels 3 instead. That extra rune is how the game makes up for going second.
- **Draw.** Draw 1 card. There is no maximum hand size.

## How do you pay for cards?

Runes make two kinds of resource. The number in a card's upper left corner is its **energy** cost. The symbols under it are its **power** cost.

- **Energy:** exhaust a rune, turning it sideways, to add 1 energy.
- **Power:** recycle a rune, putting it on the bottom of your Rune Deck, to add 1 power of that rune's domain.

You can recycle a rune you already exhausted this turn, so one rune can pay a little of both. Unspent energy and power vanish at the end of the turn, so spend them or lose them.

## What can you do in your action phase?

Anything below, in any order, as many times as you can afford it:

- **Play cards.** Units enter **exhausted**, at your base or at a battlefield you already control. Gear enters ready at your base. Spells do what they say, then go to the trash. Your Chosen Champion can be played straight from its zone.
- **Use abilities,** including your Legend's.
- **Move units.** Exhaust a unit to move it from your base to a battlefield, or from a battlefield back to base. You can move several at once if they all go to the same place.

Because units enter exhausted, a unit you play this turn usually cannot move until next turn. That one rule shapes the whole game.

## Conquering an empty battlefield

Move a unit to a battlefield with no units on it. A short showdown opens first, so your opponent can answer with an Action or Reaction card. If your units are still the only ones there when both players pass, you **conquer** it: you take control and score 1 point. You keep control for as long as you have a unit there.

## When units meet: showdown, then combat

Move onto a battlefield where your opponent has units, and a **combat** begins. You are the attacker. First comes a **showdown**: starting with the attacker, players take turns playing cards with the Action or Reaction keyword, or passing. When both pass in a row, the fight happens.

1. Each player adds up the **Might** of their units there.
2. Starting with the attacker, each player assigns that much damage to the other side's units. You must assign a unit lethal damage before moving to the next one, and units with **Tank** must be assigned damage first.
3. All damage lands at once. Any unit with damage equal to or more than its Might is killed.
4. Surviving units heal.

If only the attacker's units are left, the attacker conquers and scores. If any defenders are left, the surviving attackers go back to base and nothing is scored. Damage never lowers Might, so a battered 6 Might unit still hits for 6.

## How do you win?

First to **8 points**, with more points than anyone else, wins. One catch: the final point cannot come from just one conquer. A conquer only gives you the final point if you have scored every battlefield that turn, by holding or conquering. Holding always counts. If a conquer would give you the winning point without that, you draw a card instead.

One more way to score: if a player must draw from an empty Main Deck, they **burn out**. They shuffle their trash back into their deck, an opponent of their choice gains 1 point, and then they draw.

## The end of the turn

When you are done, say so. End of turn effects happen, every unit heals, and "this turn" effects end. Then it is the next player's turn.
