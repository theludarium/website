A Riftbound card packs a lot into a small space. Most of it you can learn in one sitting, and the official rules say exactly where each piece lives. The card below is one we invented for this lesson, laid out the way a unit card is. Its name, art, and text are made up, so you will not find it in any pack.

![An invented unit card, Hearthside Tactician, with every part labeled: the energy cost and power symbol in the upper left, Might in the upper right, the art, the name, the type and tags, the rules text with a keyword, flavor text, and the domain symbol in the lower right](/academy/riftbound/mock-card.svg)

## Cost: the upper left corner

The cost has two parts, and not every card has both.

- **Energy** is the number. Pay it by exhausting that many runes.
- **Power** is the row of symbols under the number. Each symbol needs 1 power of that domain, paid by recycling a matching rune.

Our invented card costs 3 energy and 1 power. To play it, exhaust 3 runes and recycle 1 rune of its domain. That recycled rune can be one of the three you just exhausted.

## Might: the upper right corner

Units show their **Might** here. It does two jobs. In combat, it is how much damage the unit deals. It is also how much damage the unit can take: a unit dies when the damage on it reaches its Might. A unit with 5 or more Might counts as **Mighty**, which some cards check for.

Spells and gear have no Might, because they never fight.

## Name, type, and tags

The name sits in the middle of the card. Some names have a short name and a subtitle, like a champion and a description of them. For deck building, the whole thing is the name, so two versions of the same champion with different subtitles are different cards. You can run 3 of each.

Below the name is the **type**: unit, spell, or gear. Some cards add a **supertype** in front, like Champion or Signature. **Tags** come after the type. A tag has no rules of its own, but other cards can point at it, and the champion tag is what links your Legend to the champion units and signature cards your deck can use.

## Rules text and keywords

The text box tells you what the card does. Most of it is plain instructions, but bold keywords stand in for whole rules. The common ones from the quick start:

- **Accelerate:** pay extra as you play this unit, and it enters ready instead of exhausted.
- **Assault:** extra Might while attacking. **Shield:** extra Might while defending.
- **Tank:** must be assigned combat damage first.
- **Ganking:** can move straight from one battlefield to another.
- **Deathknell:** something happens when this dies.
- **Deflect:** opponents pay extra power to target this.
- **Legion:** a bonus if you already played another card this turn.
- **Vision:** when played, look at the top card of your deck and choose whether to recycle it.
- **Hidden:** pay 1 power of any domain to place this facedown at a battlefield you control. Starting on the next turn, even your opponent's, you can play it as a Reaction without paying its normal cost.
- **Action** and **Reaction:** permission to play at other times. Action cards work during showdowns. Reaction cards can also answer a spell before it resolves.

Our invented card has Tank and a small trigger. Flavor text, in italics at the bottom of the text box, has no effect on play. Neither does the art.

## Domain: the lower right corner

The symbol in the lower right is the card's **domain**. There are six, each with a color: Fury (red), Calm (green), Mind (blue), Body (orange), Chaos (purple), and Order (yellow). Your Legend's domains, shown in its upper left corner, set your deck's **domain identity**. A card with one domain fits any deck that includes it. A card with two fits only a deck that has both. A card with no domain fits any deck.

## The other card shapes

Not everything in your deck is a unit.

- **Legends** are not part of your Main Deck. They sit in the Legend zone all game, can't be killed or moved, and usually have an ability of their own.
- **Battlefields** are wide cards. They start on the table and never move. Many have text that rewards whoever conquers or holds them.
- **Runes** have a different back from your Main Deck cards. Every basic rune can exhaust for 1 energy or recycle for 1 power of its domain.

## When a card and the rules disagree

The Core Rules open with a golden rule: card text beats rules text. If a card says it can do something the rules normally forbid, the card wins. The silver rule is the other half: card text uses shorthand, so read it through the rules, not as a separate rulebook.

Starting with Legacy in January 2027, Riot is updating how cards are worded. Tokens will be "created" rather than "played," "When you play me" becomes "When I enter," a grey backing will mark targets, and an arrow will split a trigger from its effect.
