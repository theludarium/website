Dominion looks like a quiet card game, and it is one. It also has a long competitive life: a championship event at the World Boardgaming Championships since 2009, official online championships, team events between countries, and ranked ladders you can join tonight. You do not need to win any of them to enjoy them. Knowing how they work changes how you play at home.

## How tournament Dominion differs from home play

Tournament games keep the rules you know and add a few things:

- **Random kingdoms.** Nobody picks the cards. You read ten new ones and plan on the spot.
- **A time limit.** Games run on a clock, so you need your opening ready fast.
- **Set limits.** Events choose which expansions are allowed, so everyone knows the card pool.
- **Seat rules.** Going first can matter, so some rounds ask players to bid points for their seat.

The tiebreak is the one from the rulebook: on a tie, the player with fewer turns wins.

## The World Boardgaming Championships

The [World Boardgaming Championships](https://www.boardgamers.org/) (WBC) is an annual tournament convention run by the Boardgame Players Association. Many games crown a champion there each summer. WBC 2026 ran from July 24 to August 1, 2026, at Seven Springs Mountain Resort in Pennsylvania.

Dominion has been a WBC event since 2009. The [event history](https://www.boardgamers.org/eventhistory/dom.html) shows 236 players in the first year and 145 in 2025. The 2026 [results page](https://www.boardgamers.org/wbc_results.html) lists Chris Wildes as Dominion champion.

The format, from the [2026 event preview](https://www.boardgamers.org/wbc26/Previews/dom.html):

- **Three heats.** Win any heat and you advance to the quarterfinal.
- **A bracket.** 64 quarterfinalists, then 16 semifinalists, then a final of 4.
- **One hour per round.**
- **Allowed sets:** the base game, Intrigue, Seaside, and Prosperity, in first or second edition, with Prosperity's Colony and Platinum left out.
- **Random kingdoms**, drawn with the randomizer cards or a free kingdom app.
- **A hand-picked final.** The game master chooses the final's ten Kingdom cards and may add special rules.
- **Turn-order bidding** in the semifinal and final. Players bid for their seat in half-point steps, and the winning bids come off their final scores.
- **Ties:** fewer turns wins. If still tied, both players win in a heat. In later rounds, the later seat wins.

The event is marked as welcoming beginning players. The preview also asks players to bring a copy of the game, so nobody is left out of a heat.

## Online play

Online is the fastest way to get many games in, and to see kingdoms you would never set up at home.

- **The official app.** [Dominion by Temple Gates Games](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1131620/Dominion/) is the officially licensed digital edition. On Steam it lists the base set as free to play, with ranked and unranked matchmaking, online games for up to 6, and computer opponents.
- **Dominion Online.** [Dominion Online](https://dominion.games/) has been the licensed way to play in a browser, and Shuffle iT, the team behind it, has hosted an annual official Dominion Online Championship. In 2021 it was a single-elimination event open to Gold subscribers that [started with 356 players](https://dominionstrategy.com/2021/10/28/dominion-online-2021-championship-final-8/).
- **Community events.** The [Dominion Strategy](https://dominionstrategy.com/) site covers the online scene. Its reports include a team event, the Dominion World Cup, whose 2022 edition had [84 players from 17 countries](https://dominionstrategy.com/2022/03/08/germany-wins-dominion-world-cup-2022/) and was won by Team Germany A. The community also runs the Dominion League, which had reached at least its 47th season by 2021.

## Finding local play

You do not need a tournament to find good opponents.

- **Your local game store.** Many run open game nights, and Dominion is easy to bring because it fits in one box. Ask the staff.
- **Your library or community center.** Many host board game meetups.
- **Start your own.** Our guide on [how to start a play group](/articles/how-to-start-a-play-group/) walks through it, and our [league format guide](/articles/board-game-league-format/) shows how to run a season of one game, Dominion included.

## How to practice like a competitor

- **Play two-player games with random kingdoms.** That is where reading a kingdom gets fast.
- **Name your plan before turn 1.** Opening, money or engine, and the card you want at $5. Check afterward whether it held up.
- **Race the benchmark.** The Dominion Strategy site [reports](https://dominionstrategy.com/big-money) that plain Big Money averages 4 Provinces by around turn 17. Play a kingdom alone and see whether your plan gets there sooner.
- **Review your losses.** Where did your opponent pull ahead: the opening, the first Gold, or the last few buys?
