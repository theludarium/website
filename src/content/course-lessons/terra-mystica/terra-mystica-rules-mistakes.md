Terra Mystica has no hidden information and no dice, so a rules slip changes the game in a way nobody notices until the scores look strange. This lesson collects the common ones, each checked against the current English rulebook from Capstone Games.

## Income and Power

**Mistake: skipping round 1 income.** You take your starting resources at setup and then your full income in round 1 as well.

**Mistake: moving Power straight into Bowl III.** When you gain Power, tokens move from Bowl I to Bowl II first. Only when Bowl I is empty do they move from II to III. Once everything is in Bowl III, further Power is lost.

**Mistake: taking part of a leech.** When an opponent builds or upgrades next to you, you may gain Power equal to the Power value of your buildings directly next to it (Dwelling 1, Trading house and Temple 2, Stronghold and Sanctuary 3). You lose Victory points equal to one less than the Power gained. You take all of it or none of it. The only exceptions: if your bowls cannot hold it all, you take what fits and pay for that; and you never go below 0 points.

**Mistake: leeching across a river.** Only direct adjacency counts for Power: a shared hex edge, or a Bridge. Shipping range does not.

**Mistake: burning Power for free.** If Bowl III is short, you may move tokens from Bowl II to Bowl III, but for each one you move, another token leaves Bowl II and the game for good. You cannot do this with only one token in Bowl II.

## Spades and building

**Mistake: saving Spades.** Spades must be used at once. You cannot bank a Spade for a later turn.

**Mistake: building after a cult bonus Spade.** Spades from a round's cult bonus come at the end of the round. You can transform with them, but you cannot build a Dwelling then, and you cannot buy extra Spades with Workers.

**Mistake: transforming anywhere.** Even when you only transform and do not build, the space must be directly or indirectly adjacent to one of your buildings.

**Mistake: leaving a dug space safe.** If you stop partway around the ring, that space is unoccupied, and an opponent whose Home terrain it now is can build there.

**Mistake: paying full price next to a neighbor.** A Trading house costs 2 Workers and 3 Coins instead of 6 if an opponent's building is directly next to the Dwelling you upgrade.

## Towns, Bridges, and Shipping

**Mistake: counting Shipping toward a Town.** A Town needs at least four of your buildings that are directly adjacent, with a total Power value of 7 or more (or three buildings, if one is your Sanctuary). Bridges count as direct adjacency. Shipping does not. The Mermaids are the exception: they may skip one river space when founding a Town.

**Mistake: forgetting Bridges are limited.** You have three Bridges for the game, and they stay where you build them.

## Cults and Favor tiles

**Mistake: climbing to space 10 without a key.** Each Town gives one key, and each key lets you reach space 10 of one cult track. Without a key you stop at 9, and only one player can ever occupy space 10 of a track.

**Mistake: getting Priests back.** A Priest placed on a cult order space (worth 3 or 2 steps) stays there for the rest of the game. If you want him back, you advance only 1 step and return him to your supply. You have only 7 Priests in total.

**Mistake: spending cult steps for a bonus.** The cult bonus on a Scoring tile only checks your position. You do not move back down.

**Mistake: taking a duplicate Favor tile.** You may hold only one tile of each type.

## Passing and the last round

**Mistake: taking back the card you returned.** When you pass, you return your Bonus card and take one of the others. You cannot take the one you just put back.

**Mistake: forgetting points on the way out.** Three Bonus cards score when you return them: per Dwelling, per Trading house, or for your Stronghold and Sanctuary.

**Mistake: taking a Bonus card or cult bonus in round 6.** In the last round nobody takes a new Bonus card when passing, and the Game end token covers the round 6 cult bonus.

**Mistake: letting the Spade tile land late.** In the variable setup, the Scoring tile that pays for Spades cannot go on rounds 5 or 6. If you draw it there, draw again and shuffle it back in.

## One habit that prevents most of these

Say each leech offer out loud: who built, which of your buildings touch it, how much Power that is, and what it costs in points. It slows round 1 by a minute and saves an argument in round 4.
