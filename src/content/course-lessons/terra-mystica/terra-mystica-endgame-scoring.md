A close Terra Mystica game is often decided after the last action, in the final scoring. All of that scoring is open information. This lesson walks through each part, using the current English rulebook from Capstone Games, then turns it into a round 6 checklist.

## The four parts of final scoring

**1. Cult majorities.** Each of the four tracks pays 8, 4, and 2 points to the top three. Space 0 scores nothing. Tied players split the points of their places, rounded down. Two players tied for first on a track each get 6 (8 plus 4, split).

**2. Area scoring.** Count your largest group of connected buildings, directly or indirectly adjacent. Shipping counts here, so a Shipping value of 1 links groups across a single river space, and 2 across two. Tunneling and Carpet Flight count for the Dwarves and Fakirs. The biggest group scores 18, then 12, then 6, with the same split rule for ties.

**3. Fire & Ice final tile (if used).** One more category, scored like area at 18, 12, and 6.

**4. Leftover resources.** Convert what you can into Coins (Power to Coins 1 for 1, Priests to Workers, Workers to Coins) and score 1 point per 3 Coins. The Alchemists score 1 point per 2.

## Why the swings are bigger than they look

Final places move in steps of points, not in steps of effort. Two examples:

- **One Dwelling** that links your second cluster to your first can move you from third to first in area: 6 points becomes 18.
- **One cult step** that breaks a tie for first turns 6 into 8 for you and 6 into 4 for your rival. A 2-point gain for you is a 4-point swing between you.

Compare those with ordinary round 6 actions. A Dwelling on a Dwelling-scoring tile earns 2 points. The same Dwelling, placed where it links your area, might earn 12.

## Towns and keys

A Town forms the moment you have at least four directly adjacent buildings with a total Power value of 7 (three, if one is a Sanctuary). Bridges count; Shipping does not. Each Town gives:

- **Points and a reward from a Town tile.** The five tiles pay 5 points and 6 Coins, 6 points and 8 Power, 7 points and 2 Workers, 8 points and one step on every cult track, or 9 points and a Priest. There are two of each, and only 10 Towns in a game.
- **Extra points** if the current Scoring tile pays for Towns (5 each), plus 5 more for the Witches and 3 Workers for the Swarmlings.
- **A key** to space 10 of one cult track.

Late Towns matter twice. The Town tile with one step on every track can win a majority by itself, and a key is the only way to space 10, which also pays 3 Power. When a rival sits on space 9 waiting for a key, a Town of your own that lets you reach space 10 first takes the space from them for good, since only one player can stand there.

## Round 6 rules to remember

- Nobody takes a new Bonus card when passing.
- There is no cult bonus at the end of the round.
- Shipping and the exchange rate upgrade still score their points when bought, which the rulebook points out as a reason to take them late.
- The Bonus card that adds 1 Shipping for the round does not count for final area scoring if you hold it in round 6.

## A round 6 checklist

Before your first action of the last round, count:

1. **Area.** Your largest group and each rival's. Which single build or Shipping step changes a place?
2. **Each cult track.** Your place and the gap to the players above and below. Which step wins or ties a place?
3. **Spare resources.** What will your leftovers be worth at 3 Coins per point? Spending them on something worth more is better.
4. **This round's tile.** What does it pay for, and can those builds also serve area or a Town?
5. **Turn order.** Which Power actions will be gone before your second turn? Take the contested one first.

Then play the moves that change places first, while they are still available, and the moves that only score a few points last.

## Counting earlier

The best time to win the endgame is round 4 or 5. Look at your area and cult places then, and plan the round 5 Sanctuary or Town that sets up the round 6 swings. The [cult tracks lesson](/academy/terra-mystica/terra-mystica-cult-tracks-favor-tiles/) covers how to win majorities without wasting Priests.
