Welcome to Level 3. The base game has 14 factions on seven double-sided boards, two for each terrain. Each has a starting ability and a Stronghold ability (the Auren have only the second). All abilities below come from the current English rulebook's faction appendix. The plans are widely shared community advice, not rules.

## Feuerland's balance table

Feuerland, the original publisher, posted a table in September 2020 that replaces the flat 20 starting points with a number per faction for each map. On the base map it gives:

| Starting points | Factions |
| --- | --- |
| 30 | Fakirs |
| 25 | Auren, Alchemists, Giants |
| 20 | Witches, Nomads, Dwarves, Mermaids, Swarmlings, Halflings, Chaos Magicians |
| 15 | Engineers, Cultists, Darklings |

Read it as the publisher's view of strength: fewer starting points means a stronger faction. Use it in mixed-experience games, or ignore it and simply know who is favored.

## Plains

**Halflings.** One extra point for every Spade they use, and cheaper Spade upgrades. Their Stronghold gives 3 Spades at once, with one Dwelling allowed on them. Plan: an early Stronghold, lower the exchange rate, and dig a lot, especially in Spade-scoring rounds.

**Cultists.** When any opponent takes Power from their building, they climb one cult step (if every opponent declines, they gain 1 Power instead). The Stronghold is worth 7 points once. Plan: build right next to busy neighbors and turn every leech into cult majorities.

## Swamp

**Alchemists.** At any time they can trade 1 point for 1 Coin, or 2 Coins for 1 point, and they score 1 point per 2 Coins at the end. Their Stronghold gives 12 Power once and then 2 Power for every Spade, and it pays 6 Coins of income instead of Power. Plan: a Coin engine that becomes points, with Spades to fuel Power.

**Darklings.** They pay Priests, not Workers, for Spades and score 2 points per transform step. Their Sanctuary gives 2 Priests of income, and their Stronghold lets them trade up to 3 Workers for Priests once. Plan: Temples and an early Sanctuary for Priests, then dig for points.

## Lakes

**Mermaids.** They start at Shipping 1 and can ignore one river space when founding a Town. Their Stronghold gives a free Shipping step. Plan: grow along rivers and found Towns other factions cannot.

**Swarmlings.** Every Town gives them 3 extra Workers. Their Stronghold gives a Special action: one free Trading house upgrade per round. They pay more for buildings, and their Sanctuary gives 2 Priests. Plan: Towns early and often, with the free Trading house each round.

## Forest

**Witches.** Each Town scores them 5 extra points. Their Stronghold gives Witches' Ride: once per round, a free Dwelling on any unoccupied Forest space, ignoring adjacency. Plan: an early Stronghold, then Forest Dwellings across the map and Town after Town.

**Auren.** No starting ability. Their Stronghold gives a Favor tile once and a Special action to climb 2 cult steps each round. Plan: Temples and Favor tiles toward cult majorities, with the Stronghold as a steady cult engine.

## Mountains

**Dwarves.** No Shipping. Instead they tunnel past one space for 2 extra Workers (1 after their Stronghold), scoring 4 points each time. Plan: tunnel for points and reach, and lower the exchange rate to afford it.

**Engineers.** Cheaper Dwellings, Trading houses, and Temples, and they can build a Bridge as an action for 2 Workers. After their Stronghold, they score 3 points per Bridge linking two of their buildings each time they pass. Plan: a tight, bridged cluster with Towns and steady passing points.

## Wasteland

**Chaos Magicians.** One starting Dwelling, placed after everyone else. They take 2 Favor tiles for each Temple and Sanctuary, and their Stronghold gives a double turn once per round. Plan: Temples early for Favor tiles, then use the double turn to grab contested Power actions and spaces.

**Giants.** Every transform costs exactly 2 Spades, even from a neighboring terrain. Their Stronghold gives 2 free Spades once per round. Plan: an early Stronghold, then a Dwelling almost every round from the free Spades.

## Desert

**Fakirs.** No Shipping. Carpet Flight lets them skip one space for an extra Priest, scoring 4 points; the Stronghold extends the range. Their Stronghold pays a Priest instead of Power. Plan: Priest income, long flights, and a large area for end scoring.

**Nomads.** They start with three Dwellings. Their Stronghold gives Sandstorm: once per round, turn a space next to their buildings into Desert without Spades, then build there by paying the Dwelling's cost. Plan: use the three-Dwelling start for early Trading houses, then Sandstorm every round.

## Picking a faction

Pick after you see the Scoring tiles, Bonus cards, and the other choices. A digging faction loves Spade rounds. A Town faction loves Town rounds. Factions that need the same terrain colors near each other compete for space, and only one faction per terrain can play.

The [beginner plan](/academy/terra-mystica/terra-mystica-beginner-strategy/) is a good template for any of them.
