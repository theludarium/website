New Terra Mystica players often play the turn in front of them: build what is affordable, take what is offered, and pass when the money runs out. That plays a full game, but it scores far below what the same faction can do with a plan. The plan does not need to be clever. It needs to come from the Scoring tiles.

## Step 1: read all six Scoring tiles

Every round has a Scoring tile, face up from the start, and each one pays points for one thing during that round's Action phase:

| Tile pays for | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Each Dwelling built | 2 |
| Each Trading house built | 3 |
| Stronghold or Sanctuary built | 5 each |
| Each Spade used | 2 |
| Each Town founded | 5 |

The right half of each tile pays a cult bonus at the end of the round to players high enough on one track: Priests, Power, Spades, Workers, or Coins. Round 6 has no cult bonus.

The rulebook names the dilemma: your faction wants certain buildings early, and the tiles pay for certain buildings in certain rounds. A first plan is simply a rough answer to "what do I build in each round?"

## Step 2: settle near your neighbors

New players tend to hide in a quiet corner. The rules push the other way. A Trading house costs 3 Coins instead of 6 when an opponent's building sits right next to your Dwelling, and every time a neighbor builds next to you, you are offered Power. The same is true for them, so the trade is fair, but the isolated player gets none of it.

The other side of the coin is space. You need room for Dwellings that form one connected area by the end. A good first spot touches one or two neighbors and still has open terrain one or two Spades away.

## Step 3: decide Stronghold first or Temple first

After your first Trading house, you face the game's main fork.

- **Stronghold first** if your faction's Stronghold ability is its engine: a Special action you can use every round (Witches, Nomads, Giants, Swarmlings, Chaos Magicians) or a one-time boost that shapes your opening (Halflings' three Spades, Mermaids' free Shipping). An ability you get in round 2 works for five rounds; one you get in round 5 works for two.
- **Temple first** if Priests and Favor tiles matter more to your faction: Priests buy cult steps, Shipping, and cheaper Spades, and the Favor tile arrives immediately.

The introductory factions lean toward early Strongholds, and that is a sound first game: build the Stronghold in round 1 or 2, then add Temples.

## Step 4: spend your Power every round

Power left in Bowl III at the end of a round is usually a missed action: the Power actions reset each round, and once every token sits in Bowl III, new Power has nowhere to go. Before you pass, check Bowl III and spend it on a Power action (7 Coins, 2 Workers, a Priest, a Bridge, or Spades) or on conversions. Lesson 5 shows the math.

## Step 5: pick Bonus cards on purpose

When you pass, you swap your Bonus card for one of the unused ones, and unused cards collect a Coin each round. Ask two questions:

1. **What do I need next round?** Workers, Coins, a Priest, Power, a free Spade, or extra Shipping.
2. **What scores when I pass next?** The Dwelling, Trading house, and Stronghold and Sanctuary cards pay points when returned. Take the Trading house card in a round where you already have several Trading houses, for example.

Passing first also makes you the Starting player for the next round and gives you the first pick of the Power actions. Passing early with a good card is often worth more than one weak last action.

## A first-game round plan

This is a starting shape, not a script:

- **Round 1:** Dwellings or a Trading house next to a neighbor. Spend all your Power.
- **Round 2:** Stronghold, especially if a tile pays for it this round or next.
- **Rounds 3 and 4:** Temples, a Favor tile or two, and more Dwellings toward a Town.
- **Round 5:** Your Sanctuary or a second Town, depending on the tiles.
- **Round 6:** Build whatever the tile pays for, climb cult tracks for the final majorities, and connect your area.

Adjust it to your faction. Lesson 7 has a plan for each of the 14, and the [first lesson](/academy/terra-mystica/how-to-play-terra-mystica/) has the full rules if you need a refresher.
