Terra Mystica is a game about fourteen magical peoples who reshape the land to suit themselves. Each faction builds only on its own Home terrain, so it transforms the land around it. There is no luck once the game starts, and everything is open information.

This lesson follows the current English rulebook from Capstone Games and uses its terms.

## What is the goal?

Score the most Victory points. Everyone starts on 20. You score during the game, mostly for what each round's Scoring tile rewards, and at the end for cults, area, and leftovers.

## How do you set up?

![An abstract Terra Mystica setup: a hex map with a river and two Dwellings per player, Scoring tiles, Power actions, and Bonus cards below it, and the Cult board, Favor tiles, Town tiles, and a Faction board to the right](/academy/terra-mystica/setup.svg)

The rulebook has two setups. For the first game, use the **introductory game**: fixed factions (Witches, Nomads, Halflings, and Mermaids at four players), fixed Scoring tiles and Bonus cards, and printed starting spots for every Dwelling.

1. **Faction board.** Take your board and everything in your color. Buildings go on the board; the 7 Priests and 3 Bridges go beside it as your supply.
2. **Starting resources.** Take the Workers and Coins (and any Priests) shown under your faction's name.
3. **Markers.** One on 0 of your Shipping track, one on the Exchange track at 3 Workers per Spade, one on 20 of the score track, and one on 0 of each cult track. Then move up the cults your board shows.
4. **Power.** Put 12 Power tokens into Bowls I and II as your board shows.
5. **Shared pieces.** Lay out the Favor tiles, the 10 Town tiles face up, and an Action token on each of the six Power action spaces.

In later games, Scoring tiles and Bonus cards are random, players choose factions, and players place their own first Dwellings: one each clockwise, then a second each back counterclockwise. Then, starting with the last player, everyone takes a Bonus card.

## How does a round work?

There are six rounds, each with three phases.

**Income.** Each building track shows income in the spaces your buildings have left empty: Dwellings give Workers, Trading houses Coins and Power, the Stronghold usually Power, and Temples and the Sanctuary Priests. Add your Bonus card and Favor tiles. Round 1 income comes on top of your starting resources.

**Actions.** Starting with the Starting player, everyone takes one action at a time until everyone passes.

**Cult bonuses and clean-up.** Players far enough up the cult shown on the Scoring tile take its bonus. Then each unused Bonus card gets a Coin, and the Scoring tile flips face down.

## What are the eight actions?

1. **Transform and build.** Change one terrain space toward your Home terrain using Spades, then build a Dwelling there for its cost (1 Worker and 2 Coins for most factions). The space must connect to your buildings.
2. **Advance Shipping.** Reach across rivers. It costs a Priest and 4 Coins and scores the points on the track.
3. **Lower the Spade exchange rate.** From 3 Workers per Spade to 2, then 1. Each step scores 6 points.
4. **Upgrade a building.** Dwelling to Trading house, Trading house to Temple or Stronghold, Temple to Sanctuary. Temples and the Sanctuary each bring a Favor tile. The Stronghold gives your faction a new ability.
5. **Send a Priest to a cult.** Climb 3 or 2 spaces and leave the Priest there for good, or climb 1 and take him back.
6. **Power actions.** Spend Power from Bowl III on a shared action like 7 Coins or 2 Spades. Each one can be taken once per round, by anyone.
7. **Special actions.** Actions from your Stronghold, a Bonus card, or a Favor tile, each once per round.
8. **Pass.** Swap your Bonus card for an unused one. The first to pass starts the next round.

## Terraforming in one paragraph

Your Faction board shows the seven terrains in a ring. Each step around it costs one Spade, from 1 for a neighbor to 3 for the farthest. Spades come from Workers, Power actions, a Bonus card, or cult bonuses, and cannot be saved.

## Power and your neighbors

Your 12 Power tokens flow between three Bowls: gaining moves them from Bowl I to II, then II to III, and you spend only from Bowl III. When an opponent builds next to you, you may gain Power, paying one point fewer than the Power you take. Lesson 5 covers this in depth.

## Cults and Towns

The Cult board has four tracks: Fire, Water, Earth, and Air. Climbing them gives Power, round bonuses, and points at the end. A **Town** forms when you have four connected buildings worth at least 7 Power value (or three, if one is your Sanctuary). It scores points, gives a one-time reward, and gives a key that lets you reach the top space of a cult track.

## How does the game end?

After round 6's Action phase, score each cult track (8, 4, and 2 points for the top three), your largest connected group of buildings (18, 12, and 6), and 1 point per 3 Coins left over.
