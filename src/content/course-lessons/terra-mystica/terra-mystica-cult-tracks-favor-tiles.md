The Cult board looks like a side game, and new players often ignore it until round 5. That leaves a lot of points on the table. Each of the four tracks, Fire, Water, Earth, and Air, pays out three different ways. All rules here come from the current English rulebook from Capstone Games.

## How a track pays

**Power on the way up.** You gain 1 Power reaching space 3, 2 at space 5, 2 at space 7, and 3 at space 10, once each, even if you jump past them.

**Round bonuses.** The right half of each Scoring tile pays a bonus at the end of rounds 1 to 5 to players high enough on one track, for example 1 Spade for every 4 steps on Air, or 1 Coin for every step on Earth. You keep your position; nothing is spent.

**Final majorities.** At the end, each track pays 8 points for first, 4 for second, and 2 for third. Ties split the points of the tied places, rounded down. Nobody scores from space 0.

## How to climb

- **Priests to an order.** Each track has four order spaces: one moves you 3 steps and three move you 2. The Priest stays there for the rest of the game. Or advance 1 step and return the Priest to your supply.
- **Favor tiles.** Every Temple and Sanctuary gives one (the Chaos Magicians get two). Each tile moves you up 1, 2, or 3 steps.
- **Special actions.** One Bonus card and one Favor tile each give a 1-step cult move once per round. The Auren Stronghold gives 2 steps.
- **Town tile.** One Town tile moves you up 1 step on all four tracks.
- **Cultists.** They climb a step when opponents take their leech.

Space 10 needs a key, and each Town gives one key. Only one player can reach space 10 on each track.

## The 12 Favor tiles

You may hold only one of each type. Four tiles give 3 cult steps and nothing else. The other eight give 1 or 2 steps plus an ability, with three copies of each.

| Steps | Ability |
| --- | --- |
| 3 on one track | Nothing more (one tile per track) |
| 2 Fire | Towns need a Power value of 6 instead of 7 |
| 2 Water | Special action: 1 cult step of your choice each round |
| 2 Earth | Income: 1 Worker and 1 Power |
| 2 Air | Income: 4 Power |
| 1 Fire | Income: 3 Coins |
| 1 Water | 3 points per Trading house built |
| 1 Earth | 2 points per Dwelling built |
| 1 Air | Points when passing, by Trading houses on the map: 2, 3, 3, or 4 for 1 to 4 |

## Which tiles are worth taking

Each tile has a clear payoff window, so judge it by the rounds left:

- **Income tiles** pay every round from now on. Taken in round 2, the 3-Coin tile pays four times, in rounds 3 to 6. In round 5, once.
- **Building-point tiles** pay per building, so the Dwelling tile is strongest with many Dwellings still to build, and weakest after your area is mostly done.
- **The Town tile** (Power value 6) matters most to Town factions like the Witches and Swarmlings, and when a key is the only way to reach space 10.
- **The 3-step tiles** are pure cult. They are best when they decide a majority or reach a Power space.

## Winning majorities without wasting Priests

A track is worth 8, 4, and 2 points, 14 points in all. Moving from fourth to third is worth 2; from second to first, 4. That makes the ladder clear:

1. **Look for uncontested tracks.** If nobody else is climbing Earth, a few steps there can take first place cheaply.
2. **Concentrate.** Four steps on one track usually beat one step on four tracks, because the points come from places, not steps.
3. **Use ties.** Tied players split the points. Sometimes matching a rival is enough.
4. **Time the last climbs.** Opponents react to visible moves. Climbing in the last round leaves them fewer turns to answer.

A Priest sent to an order is gone for good, and you have only 7. A Priest is also worth Shipping, cheaper Spades, or simply a Worker if you convert it. Send one when it moves you past a Power space, wins a place, or sets up a round bonus you will collect more than once.

## Reading the round bonuses

Look at all five cult bonuses at setup. If three rounds pay for Water, early Water steps pay three times. That is often reason enough to put your first Favor tile or Priest there, long before the final majority is in sight.
