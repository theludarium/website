Terraforming is what makes Terra Mystica different from other building games. You do not find land that suits you. You make it. This lesson puts numbers on that, using the current English rulebook from Capstone Games.

## The terrain ring

Your Faction board shows the seven terrains in a ring, in this order: Plains, Swamp, Lakes, Forest, Mountains, Wasteland, Desert, and back to Plains. Each step around the ring, in either direction, costs one Spade.

- A terrain next to yours on the ring costs **1 Spade**.
- Two steps away costs **2 Spades**.
- The two terrains on the far side cost **3 Spades**, the most any transform can cost.

So for the Witches (Forest), Lakes and Mountains are 1 Spade, Swamp and Wasteland are 2, and Plains and Desert are 3. The Giants are the exception: they always pay exactly 2 Spades, whatever the terrain.

You may also stop partway. Stopping still costs the Spades you used, and the space becomes open land that any faction with that Home terrain can build on.

## What a Spade costs

The most common source is Workers, at the rate on your Exchange track. Everyone but the Darklings starts at 3 Workers per Spade.

| Spades needed | At 3 Workers each | At 2 Workers each | At 1 Worker each |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 3 | 2 | 1 |
| 2 | 6 | 4 | 2 |
| 3 | 9 | 6 | 3 |

Add the Dwelling's own cost (1 Worker and 2 Coins for most factions), and a 2-Spade Dwelling at the starting rate costs 7 Workers and 2 Coins. That is why early expansion goes toward 1-Spade spaces.

Other sources:

- **Power actions:** 1 Spade for 4 Power, or 2 Spades for 6 Power. If those are not enough, you may pay the missing Spades in Workers at your current rate. If you need only one of the two Spades, the other can transform a second space, but you cannot build on that second space.
- **Bonus card:** one card gives a free Spade as a Special action once per round.
- **Cult bonuses:** some Scoring tiles give Spades at the end of the round to players high enough on a cult. You can transform with them but not build that round.
- **Factions:** the Halflings get 3 Spades when they build their Stronghold, and the Giants gain a Special action for 2 free Spades.

## When to lower the exchange rate

Lowering the rate is an action that costs 2 Workers, 5 Coins, and 1 Priest (the Halflings pay fewer Coins), and it scores 6 points at once.

The first step, from 3 to 2, saves 1 Worker per Spade. If you will dig 8 more Spades with Workers, it saves 8 Workers for 2 Workers, 5 Coins, and a Priest, plus 6 points. If you plan to dig mostly with Power actions, it saves much less. Count your remaining Dwellings and their likely Spades before you buy it.

Because it scores 6 points, the upgrade can also be a good late action even when the savings are small, as the rulebook notes.

## Shipping and Bridges

Rivers block expansion. Two tools cross them.

**Shipping** costs 1 Priest and 4 Coins per step and scores the points printed on your track. A Shipping value of 1 lets you reach land across one river space, 2 across two. It counts for building and for the final area scoring, but not for Towns or for leech.

**Bridges** cost 3 Power as a Power action (the Engineers can also build them for 2 Workers). A Bridge needs your building on one end, and it makes the two spaces directly adjacent, which does count for Towns and leech. You have only three.

The Mermaids start with Shipping 1. The Dwarves and Fakirs have no Shipping at all. They tunnel or fly across one space instead, paying extra Workers or a Priest and scoring 4 points each time.

## Choosing where to grow

With costs in hand, a good spot usually has three things:

1. **Cheap land:** several spaces 1 Spade from your Home terrain, or already your Home terrain.
2. **A neighbor:** an opponent's building beside your future Trading house, for the cheaper upgrade and the Power flow.
3. **A Town shape:** room for four connected buildings worth 7 Power value, ideally without crossing a river.

When two of those three line up, build. When only one does, look again, or use the land as a later area-scoring link instead of an early engine.

Last, watch your opponents' rings. Digging a space halfway can hand it to someone else. Digging toward an opponent's Home terrain right beside their Stronghold is an invitation.
