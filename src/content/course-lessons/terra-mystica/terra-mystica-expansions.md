Welcome to Level 4. Before you go deeper on scoring, it helps to know the boxes and options that tournament players and online sites use. This is also the one lesson that names products to buy.

## How player count changes the game

The base game plays 2 to 5. The number of Bonus cards is the player count plus three: 5 at two players, 6 at three, 7 at four, 8 at five. The map stays the same size.

- **Two players:** lots of room. Leech offers are rarer, Trading houses next to an opponent are harder to set up, and the shared Power actions are less contested.
- **Three players:** a balance of room and pressure.
- **Four players:** the count the online tournaments use (see lesson 11). The map fills and every Power action is contested.
- **Five players:** a tight map, many leech offers, and long waits between turns.

The rulebook's introductory game has a set of factions for each count: Witches and Nomads at two, plus Alchemists at three, plus Halflings and Mermaids (instead of Alchemists) at four, and Giants added at five.

## Balance: Feuerland's starting points

In 2020 Feuerland posted [a table of starting points per faction and map](https://www.feuerland-spiele.de/fileadmin/game/Terra_Mystica/TM_new_starting_VP_09_2020.pdf) (in German), replacing the flat 20. It covers the base map, the variant map on the back of the Fire & Ice board, the Fire & Ice map, and both Merchants of the Seas maps. Lesson 7 has the base map numbers. Merchants of the Seas also prints its own per-map tables.

## Fire & Ice

[Terra Mystica: Fire & Ice](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08JD4GL53?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) is the first expansion and the one most groups add. It needs the base game. It includes:

- **Six factions.** Ice Maidens and Yetis live on Ice, Acolytes and Dragonlords on Volcanoes. Ice and Volcano spaces can never be transformed again. The Shapeshifters and Riverwalkers break the one-faction, one-terrain rule.
- **A new double-sided board.** One side is a new map; the back is a variant of the base map.
- **Final Scoring tiles.** One is drawn at setup and scored after area scoring, at 18, 12, and 6 points: Greatest distance, Stronghold and Sanctuary distance, Outposts on the map edge, or number of Settlements.
- **Variable turn order.** Passing order sets the next round's play order.
- **Faction auction.** Draw factions at random, then bid points for them from 40. Many competitive games use a bidding system like this.

## Merchants of the Seas

[Terra Mystica: Merchants of the Seas](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07YXKXR71?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) is the second expansion. It adds a double-sided board with two maps, the Lakes and the Fjords, plus Ships, Shipyards, new Favor tiles, Scoring tiles, Town tiles, and Bonus cards. The Fakirs and Dwarves use traveling Merchants and Markets instead of Ships. It works with or without Fire & Ice.

## The Big Box, the solo box, and smaller add-ons

- [Terra Mystica: Big Box](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09TWMLR4B?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) collects the base game, Fire & Ice, Merchants of the Seas, and a solo mode by Automa Factory. If you are buying new and know you like the game, it is the simplest way to get everything.
- [Terra Mystica: Automa Solo Box](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09VCQR937?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) adds solo play against automated factions and a variant that adds a neutral third faction to two-player games.
- The **mini expansion** adds a Bonus card that scores for Shipping, four Town tiles, and a Scoring tile for Temples. Capstone's English rules for it also include Special Terrain tiles, one per faction. The tmtour.org league uses the Town tiles, the Bonus card, and the Scoring tile.
- **Fan Factions** adds 20 fan-designed factions, 14 for the base game and 6 for Fire & Ice, that work with the base game and both expansions.

## The sister games

**[Gaia Project](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08JCCV2VM?tag=cubecommonw0a-20)** moves the system into space. Terraforming becomes planet types, the cults become research tracks, and the factions and maps are variable. It is a full game, not an expansion, and many players find it a longer, heavier step up from Terra Mystica.

**[Age of Innovation](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C31K7XMD?tag=cubecommonw0a-20)** is a standalone game in the Terra Mystica world, published in English by Capstone Games. It keeps terraforming and the Power bowls and adds more variable setup, such as palace abilities and innovations.

If your group loves the base game, start with Fire & Ice. If your group loves the system but wants a new puzzle, try a sister game.

For the base game itself, the [Z-Man Games edition on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00APPE4HK?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) is the listing most players will find.
