Welcome to Level 2. Now you put numbers under the habits from Level 1. Every number in this lesson comes from the rulebook's component list and simple counting. Check any of it with a pencil.

## The clock: 45 Landscape tiles

The box has 48 Landscape tiles. Three go back unseen at setup, so 45 enter play. You only reveal a Landscape tile when 3 Task tiles with markers sit on the map (or when the Task tiles have run out), so every Landscape tile is one tick of the clock. When you need one and there are none, the game ends.

Task tiles do not tick the clock. There are 25. The first 3 come out on the first 3 turns. After that, one more comes out each time a marker leaves the map, completed or cancelled. So:

**Task tiles revealed = 3 + markers that left the map**, up to the 25 in the box.

To see all 25, you need 22 markers to leave the map before the Landscape stack runs out (or in the run of Task tiles the last Landscape tile can start). Every task you finish quickly buys you another tile for free.

## Count the stacks

The rules let you make the stacks any size. Make them easy to count: five stacks of 9 Landscape tiles is 45. When one empties, you know exactly where you are.

## What will the next marker be?

Each Task type has five markers: one 4, two 5s, and two 6s. They sit facedown in their own color pile, and you take one at random.

| Markers left in a pile | Chance of a 4 | Chance of a 5 | Chance of a 6 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| All five (4, 5, 5, 6, 6) | 20% | 40% | 40% |
| The 4 is gone (5, 5, 6, 6) | 0% | 50% | 50% |
| A 6 is gone (4, 5, 5, 6) | 25% | 50% | 25% |
| A 5 is gone (4, 5, 6, 6) | 25% | 25% | 50% |

Completed markers sit in your Completed Task area, so you can see which numbers are gone. Note cancelled markers as they go back to the box. The average fresh marker needs 5.2 tiles. Plan for 5 or 6, and treat a 4 as a gift.

## The best size for a waiting area

Here is where the counting pays. A new Task tile adds 1 tile to the area it joins. So the size of an open area decides which markers can go there:

| Area size before the Task tile | A 4 | A 5 | A 6 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 tiles | Needs 1 more | Needs 2 more | Needs 3 more |
| 3 tiles | Completes at once | Needs 1 more | Needs 2 more |
| 4 tiles | Not allowed | Completes at once | Needs 1 more |
| 5 tiles | Not allowed | Not allowed | Completes at once |

An open area of exactly **3 tiles** of a type is the best landing spot: it accepts every marker, completes a 4 at once, and leaves a 5 or 6 one or two tiles away. An area of 4 still takes 80% of fresh markers. An area of 5 only takes a 6.

So when you grow an area with no task on it yet, slow down at 3. That is a waiting area: a Task tile of that type has a home that pays out fast, and a task that completes on landing frees its slot for another Task tile.

## Task types come in fives

There are 5 Task tiles of each type. As the game goes on, count the Task tiles already out. If four Track Task tiles have appeared, only one remains, and building a waiting area for Track tasks is less useful than one for Village.

## How often a Flag sits out

The 3 returned Landscape tiles are random. The chance a given Flag tile is among them is 3 in 48, about 6%. The chance that all three Flags are in play is (45 × 44 × 43) ÷ (48 × 47 × 46), about 82%. So in nearly 1 game in 5, at least one Flag never appears.

Later in the game, it gets sharper. If a Flag has not shown up and some Landscape tiles remain, the missing Flag is equally likely to be in any unseen spot: the stack or the 3 in the box.

| Landscape tiles left in the stack | Chance the missing Flag is still coming |
| --- | --- |
| 15 | 83% (15 of 18) |
| 9 | 75% (9 of 12) |
| 6 | 67% (6 of 9) |
| 3 | 50% (3 of 6) |

With a third of the stack left, a missing Flag is still likely. With a handful left, it is a coin flip and would arrive too late to grow and close anyway. Stop saving space for it.

## What the numbers leave out

The rulebook does not list how many Landscape tiles show each terrain, so this lesson does not guess. Watch what has come out instead.
