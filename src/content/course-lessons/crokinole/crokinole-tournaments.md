Welcome to Level 5. Crokinole has a real competitive scene, with a tour, rankings, and a world championship that has run since 1999. It is also one of the most open: no qualifying, recreational divisions, and organizers who want new players. This lesson covers where competitive crokinole happens and how to join in.

## The World Crokinole Championship

The [World Crokinole Championship](https://worldcrokinole.com/) is held in Tavistock, Ontario, at the Tavistock Arena, on the first Saturday of June. According to the championship's [February 2026 news post](https://www.worldcrokinole.com/headline%20news/tavistocktradition.html):

- The first one was played in 1999. Apart from three years skipped during the pandemic, it has run every year since.
- The 2025 event had more than 400 players, from as far as the Netherlands, the UK, and Japan.
- It is free and open to spectators.
- Tavistock sits near Sebastopol, where the oldest known board was built by Eckhardt Wettlaufer in 1876.

The 25th championship was held on June 6, 2026. Devon Fortino of Astorville, Ontario, beat Shawn Hagarty in the final, a rematch of the 2025 final, and finished with a perfect round of eight 20s. Ryan Buhr of Fort Wayne, Indiana, took third in singles and won the doubles with Hagarty, according to the championship's [2026 report](https://worldcrokinole.com/headline%20news/winner2026.html).

### Divisions

The [rules page](https://worldcrokinole.com/thegame.html) lists the categories and disc counts:

| Category | Discs per player per round |
| --- | --- |
| Adult and recreational singles | 8 |
| Competitive and recreational doubles | 6 |
| Cue singles | 6 |
| Cue doubles | 5 |
| Junior and intermediate | 6 |

Games are four rounds, scored 2 points for a round win and 1 for a tie. The championship matches at the end of the day play to a target score instead.

### Entering

The championship's [registration page](https://worldcrokinole.com/register.html) says registration for the 2027 event opens December 15, 2026. Its [FAQ](https://worldcrokinole.com/faq.html) answers the big question: anyone can enter. Players who are new or less competitive may choose a recreational category, and the FAQ suggests choosing competitive if you want to play the best or take the day somewhat seriously, recreational if you are there mainly to have fun and be social.

## The NCA Tour

The [National Crokinole Association](https://nationalcrokinoleassociation.com/) writes the rules most events use and runs the NCA Tour. Per its [tournaments page](https://nationalcrokinoleassociation.com/?p=147):

- The Tour is a set of independently run tournaments where players earn ranking points.
- The 2026 to 2027 season began in June 2026 and ends at the World Crokinole Championship in June 2027.
- A player's ranking counts their best five finishes of the season.
- Events are graded Tier 1, 2, or 3. The schedule includes events in Ontario, across the United States, and in Hungary, Germany, Austria, and the UK.

Upcoming stops on the 2026 to 2027 schedule include the Cleveland Holiday Classic singles (December 12, 2026), the Iowa Flickin' City Regionals in Iowa City (January 9, 2027), the Elmira Winter Crokinole Classic in Ontario (January 16, 2027), and the CrokinOlympics doubles and singles in Charleston, South Carolina (March 13 and 14, 2027). Schedules change, so check the NCA page before you book anything. The NCA site also lists clubs, which are the easiest way to find regular play.

## Rules you will meet at an event

Events play the NCA or World Championship rules, so a few things will be new if you learned at home:

- **Posture.** Part of your seat on the chair, all chair legs on the floor (NCA rules 7k to 7l).
- **No coaching from the crowd.** Spectators may not tell you the score, the shots left, or who has the hammer. Only players at the table may advise you, so doubles partners may talk (NCA rule 8a).
- **Wax by the officials.** Powder goes in the ditch; only officials treat the playing surface (NCA rule 8b).
- **A clock.** The World Championship starts and ends games with a buzzer. When time runs out, one more shot may be allowed to even the shot count.
- **Scorecards.** At the World Championship, you mark your own card in ink, record points and 20s, and swap with your opponent to check.
- **Tiebreaks use 20s.** In the World Championship's preliminary rounds, more 20s in the match breaks a tie in points. A final tie goes to a shoot-out of 12 shots at the 20 hole (6 each in doubles).

That last rule is one reason serious players drill 20s so much. Lesson 12 starts there.

## Watching online

The 2026 report says the World Championship playoff matches go up on the Tracey Boards YouTube channel, with more matches on the USA Crokinole channel. Watching a final is the fastest way to see hit and stick, hiding, and hammer play at full speed.
