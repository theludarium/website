A prerelease is the party at the start of every set. Stores open the new cards about a week before they reach big retailers, and everyone plays with them for the first time together. It is one of the best events for a new player, because nobody has an advantage from a big collection. Everybody starts from the same sealed product.

The official rules list prereleases as an example of an entry-level event: relaxed rules, fun first, and organizers responsible for welcoming newer players.

## Sealed or Pack Rush?

Stores choose between two formats for prerelease.

**Sealed** is the longer, more structured option. You open six packs and build a deck from what you open:

- At least **40 cards**, not 60.
- **Any number of inks.** The two-ink limit does not apply.
- **Any number of copies.** Open five of the same card and you may play all five.
- Otherwise, the normal rules: race to 20 lore.

**Pack Rush** is the faster, beginner-friendly option. You use two packs, shuffled together without looking, as a 24-card deck. The marketing cards go into your inkwell as 2 ready ink, you draw 5 cards instead of 7, the first to 15 lore wins, and if your deck runs out you shuffle your discard into a new one. For Hyperia City, stores were told to run two rounds of Pack Rush, one game each.

## Building a Sealed deck in under half an hour

You will have a deck-building window. Here is a simple method.

**1. Sort by ink.** Lay your cards out in six piles, one per ink. Dual-ink cards go between their two piles.

**2. Pick two inks.** You may use more, but two inks usually give the steadiest deck. Pick the two deepest piles, with a lean toward the pile holding your strongest cards.

**3. Count your characters.** Characters quest, and questing wins. Most sealed decks want a lot of them.

**4. Check your ink.** Make sure most of your cards have the inkwell swirl. A sealed deck with too many uninkable cards stalls.

**5. Spread your costs.** Aim for several plays at 1 and 2 ink, a good number at 3 and 4, and only a few at 6 or more.

**6. Keep your answers.** Any card that banishes, deals damage, or wins challenges goes in.

**7. Trim to 40 or a little over.** Extra cards make your best ones show up less often.

Some Hyperia City cards also give you **ink drops**, a temporary ink. If your pool has several cards that make or use them, they can let you play big cards a turn early.

## During the games

- Rounds are usually best of one or best of three, set by the store. Ask at the start.
- Read your opponent's new cards. Nobody knows the set yet, so asking "what does that do?" is normal.
- Track damage with dice or counters. Bring your own in case the box has none.
- Say your lore total out loud as it climbs. Twenty comes faster than you expect.

## What to bring

- A way to track lore: a tracker, pen and paper, or the Companion app.
- Sleeves if you want them; you can sleeve your deck after you build it.
- Something to carry your cards home. The packs are yours, so everything you open goes home with you.

## After the prerelease

Your sealed pool is the start of a collection. Keep your favorite cards from the new set together; many will be playable in Core for years. Then take what you learned back to lesson 5 and start shopping for the singles your weekly deck needs.
