Duel is a good tournament game. It plays in about 30 minutes, every game has a clear winner, and skill shows over a handful of games even though the layout is random. You do not need to win anything to enjoy competing, but knowing how events run changes how you play at home.

## How competitive play differs from home

- **Base game, by default.** Expansions are usually optional or left out.
- **Both seats.** At the WBC, each heat has you play once as first player and once as second.
- **A balanced start.** The WBC lets the second player decide who begins each Wonder draft in elimination games, to offset the first player's edge.
- **A clock.** Rounds have time limits, so slow play has a cost.

## The World Boardgaming Championships

The [World Boardgaming Championships](https://www.boardgamers.org/) (WBC) is an annual tournament convention run by the Boardgame Players Association, where many games crown a champion. 7 Wonders Duel has its own event, marked **Class B, beginning players welcome**, which makes it a friendly first tournament.

Everything below comes from the [2026 Duel event preview](https://www.boardgamers.org/wbc26/previews/7WD.html). Formats can change each year, so read the current preview before you go.


### Heats

- **Two heats,** with rounds of 2 hours. You may play one heat or both.
- **Two games per heat:** one as first player, one as second.
- **Win at least two heat games** to advance to the elimination rounds.
- **No expansions by default.** You can ask for them in the heats and be seated with others who want the same.

### Elimination rounds

- **Single elimination:** quarterfinal (8 players), semifinal (4), and final (2), played continuously.
- **First player** is chosen at random.
- **The balanced draft.** After each set of four Wonders is dealt, the second player decides who starts the 1-2-1 draft for that set.

### Ties and seeding

- **Ties** go to blue-card points, as in the rulebook. If still tied, the game is a draw.
- **A draw** counts as a win for both players in the heats. In elimination rounds, it eliminates the first player.
- **Seeding** for byes uses most wins, then most wins by supremacy, then point ratio in games decided on points.

Players are asked to bring a copy of the game. The preview notes that bringing one guarantees you a spot, since players can be turned away if there are not enough copies.

## What those rules change at the table

**The second seat's draft choice.** Look at the four Wonders before deciding. If one is clearly best, start the draft and take it. If two or three are close, let your opponent start: you then take 2 of the remaining 3 and choose which one they get. The [Wonders lesson](/academy/7-wonders-duel/7-wonders-duel-wonders/) covers both seats.

**The draw rule.** In an elimination game, a tie is a loss for the first player and a win for the second. As first player late in Age III, count blue-card points for both of you and play for a clear margin. As second player, a tie is enough.

**Supremacy is worth more in the heats.** Wins by supremacy are the second seeding tiebreak. When a clean military or science finish is on, take it rather than playing on for points.

## Official championships

We found no official world championship or qualifier circuit for Duel from Repos Production. Watch the publisher's [Duel page](https://www.rprod.com/en/games/7-wonders-duel) for news.

## Online play

[Board Game Arena](https://en.boardgamearena.com/gamepanel?game=sevenwondersduel) has Duel in your browser, with Pantheon and Agora as options. The publisher also has an official [7 Wonders Duel app](https://www.rprod.com/en/games/7-wonders-duel-app) for iOS and Android. Online play is the fastest way to get many games in from both seats.

## Finding local rivals

You only need one opponent, which makes Duel easier to practice than most tournament games.

- **Your local game store.** Many run open game nights, and a two-player game fits on any spare corner of a table. Ask the staff.
- **BoardGameGeek.** Its forums and local guild listings point to game groups near you.
- **Run your own bracket.** Duel is short enough for an evening tournament with eight players. Our guide on [how to run a board game tournament](/articles/how-to-run-a-board-game-tournament/) covers brackets and seeding.
