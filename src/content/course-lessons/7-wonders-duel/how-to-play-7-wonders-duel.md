7 Wonders Duel is a two-player card game by Antoine Bauza and Bruno Cathala. Each of you builds an ancient city over three Ages, taking turns to choose cards from a shared layout. It plays in about 30 minutes, and it can end on any turn: push your army into the rival capital, or collect six different science symbols, and you win at once.

If you know the big 7 Wonders, the card colors will feel familiar. Almost everything else is new: there is no passing, no neighbors, and you buy resources from the bank. This lesson covers the whole game from the current [7 Wonders Duel rulebook](https://cdn.svc.asmodee.net/production-rprod/storage/downloads/games/7wonders-duel/en/7du-rules-us-15990558193s5I6.pdf), which Repos Production posts on its [Duel page](https://www.rprod.com/en/games/7-wonders-duel) with a helpsheet and an online FAQ.

## Setup

![7 Wonders Duel setup: the board with the Conflict pawn in the middle, Military tokens and 5 Progress tokens, each player's 7 coins and 4 Wonders, and the Age I layout of 20 cards](/academy/7-wonders-duel/setup.svg)

1. Put the board to one side of the table, between you. Place the Conflict pawn on the middle space and the 4 Military tokens face up on their spaces.
2. Shuffle the 10 Progress tokens and place 5 face up on the board. The other 5 go back in the box.
3. Each player takes 7 coins from the bank.
4. Draft Wonders (below), so each player has 4.
5. Return 3 cards from each Age deck to the box without looking. Draw 3 of the 7 guilds at random and shuffle them into Age III unseen.
6. Lay out Age I, following the diagram on the back of the rulebook.

## The Wonder draft

Choose a first player. Shuffle the 12 Wonders and deal 4 face up. The first player takes 1, the second player takes 2, and the first player takes the last. Deal 4 more and repeat, but this time the second player picks first. Put your 4 Wonders in a column beside your city.

For a first game, the rulebook skips the draft and gives fixed sets. Player 1 takes The Pyramids, The Great Lighthouse, The Temple of Artemis, and The Statue of Zeus. Player 2 takes Circus Maximus, Piraeus, The Appian Way, and The Colossus.

## The layout

Each Age, shuffle that Age's deck and lay out 20 cards in a set shape, with some rows face up and some face down. Age I is a pyramid: 2 cards at the top, 6 at the bottom. Age II flips it, 6 at the top and 2 at the bottom. Age III is a taller shape that narrows and widens again. The last page of the rulebook shows all three.

A card is **accessible** when no other card covers any part of it. Only accessible cards can be taken. At the start of Age I, that is the bottom row of 6.

## Your turn

Take one accessible card and use it in one of three ways:

- **Build it.** Pay its cost and place it in your city. Sort your cards by color so both of you can read them.
- **Sell it.** Discard it face down and take 2 coins, plus 1 coin for each yellow card in your city.
- **Build a Wonder.** Pay the cost of one of your Wonders, not the card's, and tuck the card face down under it. The Wonder's effect happens now.

Then flip face up any face-down card your pick uncovered. Play passes to your opponent.

**Only seven Wonders.** Eight Wonders start the game, but only seven can be built. When the seventh goes up, the last unbuilt Wonder goes back to the box.

## Paying costs

Some cards are free. Some cost coins, paid to the bank. Most cost resources. Your brown and grey cards produce resources every turn, and they are never used up.

Missing a resource? Buy it from the bank. Each one costs **2 coins plus 1 for every matching symbol on your opponent's brown and grey cards.** If your opponent has two stone symbols on brown cards, stone costs you 4 coins each. The coins go to the bank, not to your opponent. Resources from yellow cards and Wonders never raise prices, and some yellow cards fix a resource's price at 1 coin for you.

From Age II on, some cards show a white chain symbol under their cost. If you already built the card that shows that symbol, you build the new one for free.

## Military and science

**Shields** on red cards and some Wonders move the Conflict pawn one space toward your opponent's capital per shield. When the pawn enters a zone that holds a Military token, the opponent loses 2 or 5 coins, and the token leaves the game.

**Science symbols** are on green cards. There are seven symbols in all, six on cards and one on the Law Progress token. Each time you collect a pair of identical symbols, take one Progress token from the board and keep it.

## Three ways to win

- **Military supremacy.** The pawn reaches your opponent's capital. You win at once.
- **Scientific supremacy.** You hold 6 different science symbols. You win at once.
- **Civilian victory.** If nobody wins early, score after Age III: the pawn's zone (0, 2, 5, or 10 points), points on your cards and Wonders, Progress tokens, and 1 point per 3 coins. Most points wins. A tie goes to the most points on blue cards, then a shared win.

## Between Ages

An Age ends when all 20 cards are taken. Lay out the next Age. The player with the weaker military, the one the pawn leans toward, chooses who starts it. If the pawn is on the middle space, the player who took the last card chooses.

Two rulings catch new players: a play-again effect on the last card of an Age is lost, and a Military token that asks for more coins than you have takes all you have. The [rules mistakes lesson](/academy/7-wonders-duel/7-wonders-duel-rules-mistakes/) covers the rest.
