You can now draft a pack, read a card, build a deck, read signals, plan a mana base, draft aggro or control on purpose, and build, curate, and host a Cube of your own. That is the whole ladder. This last lesson is a map of where to take it next. Cube has no official championship to climb toward; it widens instead.

## New ways to draft the same box

The eight-player booster draft is the classic, not the only way. Each of these uses the same Cube and changes the night:

- **Two players:** [Winston, Grid, and Winchester draft](/articles/two-player-cube-formats/) turn a quiet evening for two into a real draft.
- **Three or four players:** [Grid draft](/articles/grid-draft-four-players/) makes every pick a choice of one full row or column.
- **Teams:** [3v3 team draft](/articles/team-draft-cube/) and [Two-Headed Giant Cube](/articles/two-headed-giant-cube/) put friends on the same side.
- **Open information:** [Rochester draft](/articles/rochester-draft-cube/) lays every pack face up for the whole table.
- **Something different:** [Glimpse draft](/articles/glimpse-draft/), plus [rotisserie draft and Cube sealed](/articles/cube-sealed-rotisserie/), change how the cards reach you.
- **Multiplayer:** [Commander Cube](/articles/commander-cube/) drafts decks for four-player games.

## New kinds of Cubes

Every Cube plays differently. Once you know one well, try another:

- **Vintage Cube**, the most powerful environment in Magic. Our guide to [drafting Vintage Cube](/articles/how-to-draft-vintage-cube/) covers how it rewards discipline.
- **Pauper and Peasant Cubes**, where commons and uncommons carry the game. Compare them in [Pauper Cube vs Vintage Cube](/articles/pauper-cube-vs-vintage-cube/).
- **Set Cubes**, which keep a favorite set draftable forever. See [Set Cube](/articles/set-cube/).
- **Themed Cubes**, built around a creature type, a card type, or an idea. Start with [themed Cube ideas](/articles/themed-cube-ideas/).
- **Jumpstart Cubes**, with prebuilt half-decks for a fifteen-minute setup. See [Jumpstart Cube](/articles/jumpstart-cube/).

## Keeping up with new cards

New Magic sets bring new candidates for Cubes. Reality Fracture came out on October 2, 2026, and the official products page lists Magic: The Gathering | Star Trek next, on November 13, 2026, with Nauctis: The Sunken Realm on February 5, 2027.

You do not need to buy any of them to keep drafting. A Cube changes when its owner decides. If you own one, our [method for reviewing a new set](/articles/cube-set-review-method/) and our review of [Reality Fracture for Cube](/articles/cube-set-review-reality-fracture/) show how owners decide what earns a slot.

## Drafting online

When you cannot gather a table, Cube still works on a screen:

- **Magic Online** runs Vintage Cube in limited-time leagues. Our explainer on [MTGO Vintage Cube](/articles/mtgo-vintage-cube-explained/) covers what it is, and [When Is Vintage Cube on MTGO?](/articles/when-is-vintage-cube-on-mtgo/) tracks the current schedule.
- **CubeCobra** lets you draft any posted Cube against computer drafters, and its playtesting includes drafting with other people online.
- **SpellTable**, Wizards' webcam tool for playing paper Magic at a distance, is listed among the official ways to play Booster Draft.
- **MTG Arena**, Wizards' digital game, is on that same list of official ways to play Booster Draft. <!-- VERIFY: whether MTG Arena is running a Cube event this season (Arena Cube or similar) and when. -->

## Local play and the official path

For in-store play, the [Wizards Store and Event Locator](https://locator.wizards.com/) finds stores near you. Two official events are worth knowing:

- **Magic Academy** is Wizards' beginner program at local stores, with events that teach the rules and deckbuilding. The official page says everything you need is provided.
- **Friday Night Magic** is the Friday night store event, open to players of all levels, and Booster Draft is one of its formats. It is a store draft with packs rather than a Cube, but it is the same skill, and the table is full of people who might want to draft your Cube.

If your aim is competitive play, a store's booster drafts are the place to start. Ask your store which events it runs and how they are structured. <!-- VERIFY: whether any current Wizards organized-play events use Cube, and the path from store play to Regional Championships this season. -->

## Pass it on

The best next step is the one that brings someone else in. Teach a friend to draft. Lend them the teach sheet. Save them a seat. Every regular at every Cube table was a first-timer once, and most of them remember the person who showed them how.

The game is the reason. The people are the point.
