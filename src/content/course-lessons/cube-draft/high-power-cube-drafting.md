Some Cubes hold the most powerful cards ever printed. The best known is the Vintage Cube on Magic Online, a 540-card list that includes the Power Nine, a group of famous early cards such as Black Lotus and Ancestral Recall. Some owners build paper versions too, often with playtest cards standing in for the rarest cards, as the [building lesson](/academy/cube-draft/how-to-build-a-cube/) discusses.

Drafting these Cubes uses everything from Levels 1 to 3, but a few priorities flip. This lesson covers what changes.

## Why speed rules

In a typical Cube, the strongest cards are strong threats or answers. In a high-power Cube, the strongest cards make mana: artifacts that cost zero or one and add mana every turn, or once in a burst.

A deck that plays a three-mana card on turn one, or a five-mana card on turn two, skips a turn or two of the game. Few single threats are worth that much. That is why experienced drafters often take fast colorless mana over a powerful creature or spell. Our piece on [fast mana in Cube](/articles/fast-mana-in-cube/) goes through these cards one at a time.

Colorless fast mana has a second advantage: it fits any deck. Taking it early keeps all your options open.

## Lanes, not colors

In a typical Cube, you draft two colors. In a high-power Cube, decks are often defined by an engine rather than a color pair. A deck that puts big creatures into play early, a deck built around artifacts, a fast aggressive deck, a blue control deck full of the best card draw ever printed.

So when you settle, settle on a lane: what your deck does, not only its colors. Each lane has key pieces, and you need enough of them to work. Our guide to [drafting Vintage Cube](/articles/how-to-draft-vintage-cube/) lays out the main lanes in the current Magic Online list and what each needs by the end of pack one.

## Discipline: the main skill

Power is everywhere in these Cubes, so the hardest picks are about turning it down.

**Do not take power for a deck you are not building.** A card that is a first pick in one lane can be close to useless in another. After the first several picks, judge each card by what it does in your deck.

**Do not play everything because the fixing allows it.** High-power Cubes often include lots of dual lands. That makes four-color decks possible, and they often lose to a focused two-color deck that curves out. Two colors and a light splash is a sound ceiling for most decks.

**Respect cheap interaction.** Fast decks punish slow hands. Cheap removal and cheap counterspells hold more value here than in a lower-power Cube, because the games are decided earlier.

**Aggro is still a deck.** Cheap, efficient creatures and burn can race the most powerful cards in Magic. The [aggro lesson's](/academy/cube-draft/drafting-aggro-in-cube/) density rules still apply.

## Mana and building

Fast mana changes the [mana math](/academy/cube-draft/cube-mana-base/). A deck with several mana artifacts can play fewer lands, but each artifact you count as a land is a card that can be destroyed. Our [Vintage Cube guide](/articles/how-to-draft-vintage-cube/) suggests sixteen or seventeen lands, dropping to fifteen only with three or more of the zero-cost artifacts. Treat it as a judgment, and test it.

## Playing high-power games

Games are faster and swingier. Three habits help:

- **Mulligan for speed and action.** A hand that does nothing for three turns is often worse here than a mulligan.
- **Count the opponent's possible mana,** not just their lands. Artifacts can let them cast something much bigger than you expect.
- **Do not panic at power.** Opponents will draw broken starts sometimes. Variance is part of the format, and a best-of-three match evens it out more than one game does.

## Where to draft one

Few people own a powered paper Cube. Two common ways in:

- **Magic Online** runs the Vintage Cube in limited-time leagues. Our explainer on the [MTGO Vintage Cube](/articles/mtgo-vintage-cube-explained/) covers how it is curated, and [When Is Vintage Cube on MTGO?](/articles/when-is-vintage-cube-on-mtgo/) tracks when it runs.
- **CubeCobra** lists many high-power Cubes you can draft against computer drafters, for practice at any hour.

For the step down in power, the [MTGO Legacy Cube](/articles/mtgo-legacy-cube/) drafts without the Power Nine, and our guide to [Cube power levels](/articles/cube-power-levels-explained/) compares the bands.
