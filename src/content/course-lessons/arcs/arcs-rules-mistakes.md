Welcome to Level 1. Arcs has a short rulebook, and that is part of the problem: each rule carries a lot of weight, and a small slip changes the whole game. This list collects the common ones, each checked against the rulebook dated April 11, 2024.

One rule about rules first. Cards beat the rulebook, but any rule that says **cannot** is absolute unless a card says **ignore**.

## Control and building

**Mistake: counting all your pieces for control.** You control a system only if you have more **fresh** ships there than each Rival. Damaged ships and buildings do not count. On a tie, nobody controls it.

**Mistake: building at full strength in an enemy system.** If anyone other than you controls the system, whatever you build there is placed damaged.

**Mistake: one starport, many ships.** Each starport builds only one ship per turn, even if you have pips to spare.

## Moving

**Mistake: Catapulting from anywhere.** Catapult moves only start from a system with your own starport, and not from a Rival starport you control. The ships keep moving until they enter a gate someone else controls or any planet. Check control before you move in, not after.

**Mistake: moving through closed clusters.** Out-of-play clusters cannot be entered at all. The path marker on a closed gate simply makes the two neighboring gates adjacent.

## Card play

**Mistake: Surpassing every card on the table.** To Surpass, you only need a higher number than the lead card, not than other followers. A Pivot does not change the lead suit.

**Mistake: forgetting what Copy gives.** Copy is one action from the **lead** card. Pivot is one action from **your** card. Only Surpass gives your full pips.

**Mistake: declaring halfway through a turn.** Declare an ambition before taking any actions, including Prelude actions. Declaring sets your card number to 0 but leaves its pips alone. You can declare an ambition that is already declared by adding a second marker, but not once all three markers are out.

**Mistake: seizing late, or twice.** You seize when you play your card, before any actions. The player who already has initiative cannot seize, and only one player can seize each round. Once it is seized, a 7 can still Surpass but no longer seizes.

## Resources and the Prelude

**Mistake: spending resources after your first action.** Your Prelude ends as soon as you spend a pip. Spent resources return to the supply at the end of the Prelude, so you cannot regain them until the Prelude is over.

**Mistake: reading Weapons as a free battle.** A Weapon does not give a Battle action. It lets you spend this turn's card pips on Battle, whatever the suit. It cannot turn other resources' actions into battles.

**Mistake: using a card you just secured.** You cannot use Prelude actions on a card secured in the same Prelude.

## Battle

**Mistake: rolling raid dice at anything.** You can collect raid dice only if the defender has buildings in the battle system, or has no buildings anywhere on the map.

**Mistake: resolving hits in the wrong order.** Self-hits come first, then the intercept (once per battle, one hit per fresh defending ship), then your hits on ships, then building hits, then raiding. The attacker resolves everything.

**Mistake: making the defender pay for Outrage.** When you destroy a city, **you**, the destroyer, Provoke Outrage: discard all your resources and Guild cards of that planet type, and cover that resource's Outrage icon with an agent. Then you Ransack the Court: secure one Court card that holds any of the defender's agents, and take all Rival agents on it as Trophies, not Captives.

**Mistake: thinking Outrage locks a resource away.** Outraged resources still count for ambitions, and you can still tax them. You just cannot spend them for their Prelude action.

## Scoring

**Mistake: splitting first place.** On a tie for first, every tied player takes **second** place Power. On a tie for second, the tied players get nothing.

**Mistake: scoring with nothing.** You cannot gain Power from an ambition if you have none of what it counts. If Warlord is declared and nobody has Trophies, nobody scores it.

**Mistake: counting Weapon cards.** Weapon Guild cards add to no ambition. Material and Fuel cards add to Tycoon, Relic cards to Keeper, Psionic cards to Empath.

**Mistake: clearing Trophies every chapter.** Trophies return only if Warlord was scored that chapter, and Captives only if Tyrant was.

**Mistake: claiming city bonuses on a tie.** The +2 and +3 city bonuses apply only when you win first place outright, and only once per ambition however many markers are on it.

**Mistake: ending the game mid-chapter.** The Power target is checked only after a chapter's scoring.

## A table habit

Discard played cards face down, as the rules ask, and never show your hand. When a question comes up, look it up in the rulebook's index rather than voting.
