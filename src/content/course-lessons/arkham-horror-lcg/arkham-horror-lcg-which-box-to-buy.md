Arkham Horror: The Card Game is a Living Card Game. Every box has fixed contents, with no random packs, so you always know what you are buying. The hard part is the number of boxes. This lesson sorts them out using Fantasy Flight's own product pages and its 2026 announcements. It names products only; no story spoilers.

## The short answer

Buy the [2026 core set](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GK349WJ7?tag=cubecommonw0a-20). Fantasy Flight calls it the only product new players need. It plays 1 to 4 players from one box, with five investigators and the three-scenario Brethren of Ash campaign. You do not need two copies.

Play its campaign at least once before buying more. Then pick by what you want next.

## Chapter One and Chapter Two

In 2026, Fantasy Flight started what it calls Chapter Two. The new core set replaced the 2021 Revised Core Set as the evergreen starting box. Fantasy Flight is clear that this is not a new edition: all earlier content is compatible with the new cards.

What changed is the recommended card pool. The new core set and current releases form a **current environment**, a smaller pool that new campaigns are tuned for. Playing with everything ever released is the **legacy environment**, and it is fully supported too.

## The product types now

Fantasy Flight's 2026 plan describes these formats:

- **Investigator decks.** Five evergreen decks, one per class, each a ready-to-play investigator with upgrade cards. They stay in print.
- **Small campaign expansions.** Short campaigns of about three scenarios.
- **Campaign expansions.** Five-scenario campaigns, no longer tied to a matching investigator box.
- **Investigator expansions.** New investigators and player cards.
- **Deckbuilding expansions.** Mostly player cards for all five classes, usually with no new investigators, alternating with investigator expansions.
- **Scenario packs.** A single story you can play standalone or add to a campaign as a side story, about one a year.

Expansions are printed for the current environment, then rotate out of it into the legacy environment later.

## What to buy for each reason

**You want more investigators.** Start with the investigator decks: [Tommy Muldoon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GF9MSFGB?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) (Guardian), [Carolyn Fern](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GF9SLPHW?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) (Seeker), [André Patel](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GF9RKRNC?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) (Rogue), [Marie Lambeau](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GF9YQX5W?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) (Mystic), and [Miguel de la Cruz](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GF9PMD6D?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) (Survivor). Pick the class your group plays most, or the one nobody has tried. They all count toward the current environment.

**You want another campaign.** [Children of Blood](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H8YNG1QQ?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) is the first Chapter Two small campaign expansion: three scenarios set in Arkham, for 1 to 4 investigators. It is the natural next story after the core set.

**You want a one-night game.** Scenario packs are single stories. [Traces to Nowhere](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0HK2CZCDX?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) follows a series of disappearances, and Fantasy Flight says it needs only a core set. [Film Fatale](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0FKKBK146?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) sends investigators onto three troubled film sets. It came out before Chapter Two, and its product page lists the Revised Core Set as required.

**You want the classic campaigns.** Chapter One ran from 2016 to 2025. Its later cycles were sold as a pair of boxes: an investigator expansion with the player cards and a campaign expansion with the scenarios, such as The Drowned City. Older cycles were reissued in the same two-box format. These campaigns were designed around encounter sets from the earlier core sets. The Arkham Grimoire lists swaps from the 2026 core set that let you play most of them anyway, as an alternate experience, and Fantasy Flight offers the original core encounter sets as free print-and-play files. For the intended version, also pick up the [Revised Core Set](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0999D3P8S?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), home of the Night of the Zealot campaign and the classic five investigators.

## A simple buying order

1. **2026 core set.** Play Brethren of Ash.
2. **One investigator deck** for the class you liked most.
3. **Children of Blood** for your second campaign.
4. **A scenario pack** for nights when the group cannot commit to a campaign.
5. **Then choose a lane:** stay current with new Chapter Two releases, or go back into Chapter One with the Revised Core Set and a classic campaign.

## Things to skip, at first

- **A second core set.** One box supports four players.
- **Every investigator deck at once.** Each is a full deck for one investigator. Buy them as you want to play them.
- **Old mythos packs.** Chapter One's original format was a deluxe box plus six small packs per cycle. The reissued two-box versions hold the same cards and are simpler to buy.

## How many players, and how long

Every product here plays 1 to 4. A scenario usually takes 1 to 2 hours, longer for a first game. A three-scenario campaign fits a few evenings; a five-scenario campaign with interludes takes longer.
