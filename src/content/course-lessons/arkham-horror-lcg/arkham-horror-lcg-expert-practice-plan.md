Arkham Horror: The Card Game is cooperative: you play against the game, not other players. So the top of this course is not a championship. It is about winning harder games: full campaigns on Expert, with Ultimatums on, and a record of how you got there.

This lesson collects the challenges the rules offer, the tools players use, and a six-week plan.

## What counts as mastery

Set your own ladder. These goals come straight from the rules in the 2026 rulebook and the Arkham Grimoire v1.1, and each is harder than the last:

1. **Win the core campaign on Hard.**
2. **Win it on Expert.**
3. **Win on Expert with one or more Ultimatums,** such as Dread or Broken Promises.
4. **Play Epic Campaign Mode.** Carry one investigator through campaign after campaign, keeping trauma and rewards, until they fall. Your score is the total experience they earned.
5. **Win on Expert solo,** one investigator, where a single defeat ends the scenario.

You do not need to do them in order. Pick the one that sounds most fun and work toward it.

## The skills that matter on Expert

**Counting the bag.** On Expert at a margin of 2, at worst you pass about a third of the time. Players who win on Expert test less often and test bigger. The [chaos bag lesson](/academy/arkham-horror-lcg/arkham-horror-lcg-chaos-bag-odds/) has the numbers.

**Action efficiency.** Every action you spend drawing or gaining a resource is an action not spent on clues or enemies. Expert decks lean on cards that do two things at once, or that let one action find two clues or deal extra damage.

**Respecting the agenda.** Doom is a clock. On Expert, scenarios often end on the agenda, so plan from the agenda's threshold backward: how many rounds do you have, and how many clues do you need per round?

**Knowing when to resign.** Many scenarios offer a way out. Leaving with some progress beats losing an investigator to trauma.

**Deck focus.** At high difficulty, each investigator needs to do their job almost every game. Cut cards that are fun but rarely matter. The [deckbuilding lesson](/academy/arkham-horror-lcg/arkham-horror-lcg-deckbuilding/) covers the checklist.

## Online tools

**ArkhamDB** (arkhamdb.com) is a long-running fan site with a card database, a deckbuilder that checks your deck and experience, a collection manager for the packs you own, and shared decklists. It includes Chapter Two products and has an option to apply the Taboo List. It is not made, endorsed, or supported by Fantasy Flight Games. Check legality against the Grimoire if a card looks off.

**Fantasy Flight's own documents** are the authority: the core rulebook, the Arkham Grimoire for 2026 products and later, and the retired FAQ for anything before 2026. Campaign logs are free to download from the Fantasy Flight site.

**BoardGameGeek's forums** for the game are the busiest place for rules questions and campaign talk. Mind the spoiler tags.

## Finding other players

Arkham is easy to play solo or with a steady group, and hard to drop into as a stranger mid-campaign. Good options:

- **A local game store.** Ask whether anyone runs campaign nights, or post on the store's board.
- **BoardGameGeek** and other forums, to find players near you.
- **Standalone scenarios** as a way to meet new players without a campaign commitment.

The Ludarium is building toward a game lounge in Indianapolis. Join the [Indianapolis list](/lounges/indianapolis/) for first word on local learn-to-play nights as The Ludarium grows.

## A six-week plan to an Expert win

**Week 1: Baseline.** Play the first core scenario on Hard with your best group or two-handed. Log the result and the single biggest mistake.

**Week 2: Rules audit.** Reread the [rules mistakes lesson](/academy/arkham-horror-lcg/arkham-horror-lcg-rules-mistakes/) and the Grimoire's glossary entries for attack of opportunity, engagement, and the mythos phase. Replay the same scenario in standalone mode on Hard.

**Week 3: Deck tuning.** Rebuild each deck with the job, weak skill, and resources checklist. Count how many cards can do your job, and aim to see one in your opening hand most games. Play a second scenario on Hard.

**Week 4: Expert standalone.** Play your best scenario on Expert in standalone mode. Before each test, say the token count you can survive. Notice which tests you should not have taken.

**Week 5: Start the campaign.** Begin a full campaign on Expert. Spend experience on reliability first. Keep the log.

**Week 6: Finish and review.** Finish the campaign. Win or lose, read your log and write three lessons for next time. Then add one Ultimatum and start again.

## Where to go from here

Every new scenario pack and campaign is a fresh puzzle on Expert, and Epic Campaign Mode can run as long as your investigator survives.
