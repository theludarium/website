Arkham Horror: The Card Game is a cooperative card game for 1 to 4 players from Fantasy Flight Games. Each player is one investigator with their own deck, and together you work through scenarios in a horror story set in and around the town of Arkham.

This lesson follows the 2026 core set, the one Fantasy Flight calls the start of Chapter Two, and its living rules document, the Arkham Grimoire v1.1. The rules are the same game players have known since 2016, with a few updates noted in the rulebook. It stays spoiler-free: nothing here goes past the setup of the first scenario.

## What is in the box?

The 2026 core set has five investigators, each with a ready-to-play starter deck: Daniela Reyes (Guardian), Joe Diamond (Seeker), Trish Scarborough (Rogue), Dexter Drake (Mystic), and Isabelle Barnes (Survivor). It also has a three-scenario campaign called Brethren of Ash, the chaos bag and its tokens, and a rulebook. One box plays one to four people.

The rulebook teaches the game; the free Arkham Grimoire answers everything else.

## How do you win?

Each scenario has two decks that tell its story.

- The **act deck** is your progress. You usually advance it by spending clues, as a group.
- The **agenda deck** is the enemy's progress. It advances when enough doom is in play.

Reaching the end of the act deck usually means a good resolution. Letting the agenda run out usually means a worse one. Either way, the story continues.

## How do you set up?

![A two-player Arkham Horror LCG table at setup: agenda, act, scenario reference card, and encounter deck along the top, the starting location and chaos bag in the middle, and each player's investigator, assets, deck, discard, and threat area along the bottom](/academy/arkham-horror-lcg/setup.svg)

1. Each player picks a different investigator, takes that starter deck, and shuffles it.
2. Choose a **lead investigator**. They break ties and settle choices for the group.
3. Put the damage, horror, clue/doom, resource, and connector tokens in a pool.
4. Fill the **chaos bag** with the tokens listed for your difficulty. Your first game uses the 16 tokens the rulebook shows.
5. Each investigator takes 5 resources and draws 5 cards. Set aside any weakness drawn and draw again. Each player may mulligan once.
6. Follow the campaign guide for the scenario: build the encounter deck, put the starting location into play, and stack the agenda and act decks in order.

## How does a round work?

A round has four phases. **Skip the first one in round one.**

1. **Mythos phase.** Place 1 doom on the agenda. If the doom in play meets the agenda's threshold, it advances. Then each investigator draws an encounter card: an enemy or a treachery.
2. **Investigation phase.** Each investigator takes a turn of three actions, in any order the group chooses.
3. **Enemy phase.** Hunter and patrol enemies move. Each ready enemy engaged with someone attacks, then exhausts.
4. **Upkeep phase.** Ready everything, draw 1 card, gain 1 resource, and discard down to 8 cards.

## What can you do with an action?

Draw a card. Gain a resource. Play an asset or event (fast cards cost no action). Activate an ability. Move to a connecting location. **Investigate** your location, which is an intellect test against its shroud; success discovers a clue. **Engage**, **Fight**, or **Evade** an enemy. Some cards also give **Parley** or **Resign** actions. You can repeat an action.

## How do skill tests work?

Most things you do are skill tests. You have four skills: willpower, intellect, combat, and agility. Compare the right skill to the test's difficulty, then:

1. Commit cards from your hand with matching skill icons. Each icon adds 1. Another investigator at your location may commit one card to help.
2. Pull one token from the chaos bag and apply it. A number modifies your skill. A symbol sends you to the scenario reference card, or to your own investigator card for the Elder Sign. The autofail token fails the test.
3. If your total meets or beats the difficulty, you succeed.

Committed cards are discarded afterward, and the token goes back in the bag. ## What about enemies?

An enemy you draw spawns **engaged** with you, in your threat area, unless its text says otherwise. While a ready enemy is engaged with you, any action except Fight, Evade, Parley, or Resign lets it make an **attack of opportunity**. A successful fight deals 1 damage unless a card adds more. A successful evade exhausts the enemy and breaks the engagement.

Damage and horror pile up on your investigator and on assets that can soak them. Take damage equal to your health or horror equal to your sanity and you are defeated: you are out of the scenario, and in a campaign you also suffer trauma.

## When does it end?

When a card sends you to a resolution, such as "R1," the scenario is over. Read that resolution in the campaign guide, record what it says, earn experience, and set up the next one.
