Finishing Jaws of the Lion leaves most groups with the same question: what next? Cephalofair makes three other games on the same core system, and each answers that question differently. This lesson compares them using the publisher's own product pages, with no spoilers for any of them.

What carries over is the part you have spent this whole course learning: the two-card turn, initiative, the attack modifier deck, resting and exhaustion, elements, and monster focus. Those transfer directly, and they are the hard part of learning any Haven game.

## Gloomhaven: the full campaign

[Gloomhaven](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B01LZXVN4P?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), now in its second edition, is the big box Jaws of the Lion was built to lead into.

- **Players and time:** 1 to 4 players, one to two hours or more per scenario, per Cephalofair.
- **Size:** Cephalofair says over 90 sessions are built into the base game.
- **What is new to you:** six starting classes, each with a personal quest. When a character completes its quest, it retires and opens up a new class, with 12 new classes to find in all. Scenarios are built from map tiles instead of a scenario book, so setup takes longer, and Cephalofair rates the setup time as high.

**Choose it if** your group loved the campaign and wants a much longer one with more characters to discover. Expect a year or more of regular play. Our comparison, [Gloomhaven vs Frosthaven vs Jaws of the Lion](/articles/gloomhaven-vs-frosthaven/), goes deeper on the commitment.

## Frosthaven: the biggest

[Frosthaven](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08FK7B2SC?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) is Cephalofair's largest game.

- **Players and time:** 1 to 4 players, two to three hours per session, with about an hour of setup, per Cephalofair.
- **Size:** a campaign of more than 130 scenarios, with seventeen new characters and more than twenty new enemies, per Cephalofair.
- **What is new to you:** much more to do outside of combat, including a town you build up, a seasonal event system, and mysteries to solve. Cephalofair says characters and items from Gloomhaven work in Frosthaven and the other way around.

**Choose it if** your group wants the biggest version and the town-building between fights. Cephalofair itself recommends Frosthaven only after playing both Jaws of the Lion and Gloomhaven, which is a fair warning about its weight. Our article on [board game weight](/articles/board-game-weight-explained/) explains what that jump feels like.

## Buttons and Bugs: the tiny solo game

[Gloomhaven: Buttons & Bugs](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CZSPMHJ8?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) goes the other way.

- **Players and time:** 1 player, about 20 minutes per scenario, per Cephalofair.
- **Size:** 22 scenario cards with small battle maps, and six mercenaries shrunk to micro miniatures, each with 8 double-sided ability cards.
- **What is new to you:** a smaller, streamlined version of the system that fits on a coffee table. Cephalofair says it keeps the same deep strategy combat in a fraction of the space.

**Choose it if** you loved the system but your group is done, you travel, or you want a Haven puzzle on a weeknight in under half an hour. It is also a gentle way to meet characters from the big box before buying it.

## How to choose

| If your group... | Try |
| --- | --- |
| Wants more of the same, much longer | Gloomhaven |
| Finished Gloomhaven too, and wants the most | Frosthaven |
| Is breaking up, or one player wants more | Buttons and Bugs |
| Is not sure about another long campaign | Replay Jaws of the Lion on Hard (Lesson 12) |

A practical test from our [Is Gloomhaven worth it?](/articles/is-gloomhaven-worth-it/) article still applies: a group that finished this campaign has shown it has the habit. That is the best predictor that a bigger box will get played rather than sit on a shelf.

## Before you switch

A few habits from Jaws of the Lion make the move smoother:

- **Keep the rulebook open for the first scenario.** Each game adds its own rules on top of the shared core, and a quick check beats a wrong habit.
- **Bring your focus skills.** Monster focus works the same way, and it decides more scenarios than anything else.
- **Expect a new cast.** Each game has its own starting characters, so let each player try a role they did not play this time.
