A One Piece card packs a lot into a small space. The good news is that the layout is the same on every card of a category, so once you can read one Character, you can read them all.

The card below is invented for this lesson. The name, art box, and text are our own, not a real card. It shows where each part sits on a Character card.

![An invented One Piece style Character card with every part labeled: cost in the upper left, power and attribute in the upper right, the Counter value along the left edge, the effect text box with a Trigger line, the card category, name, and type at the bottom, and the color hexagon in the lower left](/academy/one-piece-card-game/mock-card.svg)

## Cost, power, and Counter: the three numbers

**Cost**, in the upper left corner, is how many active DON!! you rest to play the card. Characters, Events, and Stages have a cost. Leaders do not.

**Power**, at the top right, is the card's strength in battle. Only Leaders and Characters have power. An attacker wins if its power is equal to or higher than the target's.

**Counter**, along the left edge, is the power this card adds when you trash it from your hand during the Counter Step of your opponent's attack. Only Characters have it, and not every Character does. A Character without a Counter value cannot be used that way.

That last number is easy to overlook, and it is half of the card's value. Every Character in your hand is both a crew member you might play and a shield you might throw.

## Color, type, and attribute

**Color** shows in a small hexagon in the lower left, split into six sections. A card can have one color or two. A two-color card counts as both colors, so it fits a deck whose Leader has either one.

**Type** sits under the name, such as a crew or organization. Some cards have more than one, split by a slash. Card text often refers to a type in curly brackets, like "your {Lamplight Crew} Characters."

**Attribute** is shown near the power: Slash, Strike, Ranged, Special, Wisdom, or a question mark. Only Leaders and Characters have one. It does nothing on its own, but some effects check it.

## The text box: read the brackets first

Card text starts with a timing word in square brackets. It tells you **when** the effect happens, which matters more than what it does. The common ones:

- **[On Play]** happens when you play the card.
- **[When Attacking]** happens when this card declares an attack.
- **[Activate: Main]** you may use during your Main Phase, outside battle.
- **[DON!! x1]** works only while this card has at least 1 DON!! given to it. The number can be higher.
- **[Once Per Turn]** limits an effect to one use each turn.
- **[Your Turn]** and **[Opponent's Turn]** limit when an effect is on.

You will also see **DON!! −1** or a higher number as a cost. It means return that many DON!! from your field to your DON!! deck. It is a real price, because those DON!! will not help you again until they are added back.

When card text and the rules disagree, the card text wins.

## Triggers

Some cards have a **[Trigger]** line at the bottom of the text box. When your Leader takes damage and the Life card you would add to your hand has a Trigger, you may reveal it and use the Trigger instead. After the Trigger resolves, the card goes to the trash unless it says otherwise. Triggers are the surprise in every game: a hit on your Leader might draw a card, stop an attacker, or bring a Character into play.

## Keywords worth knowing first

- **Rush:** this Character can attack on the turn it is played.
- **Blocker:** during your opponent's attack, rest this card to make it the new target.
- **Double Attack:** a Leader hit by this card takes 2 damage, not 1.
- **Banish:** a Leader hit by this card trashes the Life card instead of adding it to hand. The Trigger is lost too.
- **Unblockable:** your opponent cannot use Blocker against this card.

## Leaders, Events, and Stages

**Leader cards** have power, Life, color, type, and attribute, but no cost and no Counter. The Life number sits in the lower right.

**Event cards** have a cost and an effect. You trash the card as you use it. **[Main]** Events are played in your Main Phase. **[Counter]** Events are played during your opponent's attack, after you pay their cost.

**Stage cards** have a cost and an effect that stays on the table. You can have one Stage at a time.

**DON!! cards** have no text you need to read in a first game. They pay costs and add power.

## The small print at the bottom

The card number, such as a set code and a number, sits in the lower right corner with the rarity and the block symbol. The card number is what the 4-copy limit counts. Rarity does not change how a card plays. The block symbol does not affect play either, but it tells you which group of sets a card belongs to, which matters for which cards are legal in the main tournament format.
