If you played years ago, you may remember new-set weekends where everyone opened packs and built a deck on the spot. Today's new-set events work differently, and each store runs them its own way. This lesson covers what Konami's official pages describe now, and how to get the fun of building from fresh packs anyway.

## What a Premiere! event is today

Konami's **Core Booster Premiere!** events let players get cards from a new set before its official release. The most recent was for **Beyond the Brave**, run at participating stores from Friday, October 2, to Sunday, October 4, 2026, ahead of the set's October 9 launch.

As Konami's [Premiere! page](https://www.yugioh-card.com/en/events/core-booster-premiere/) and its North America FAQ described it:

- Entry meant buying 5 packs of the new set and registering in NEURON with your CARD GAME ID.
- Every entrant got a Field Center Card and a shot at prizes such as a Game Mat and a promo card, while supplies lasted.
- A store could also run a Premiere! Event Tournament at no extra cost, in Advanced or Genesys. You add your new cards to a constructed deck you bring and try them a week before they are tournament legal.
- Some stores could host a Release Celebration instead, so Konami told players to check with their store first.

Konami calls Premiere! events primarily non-competitive, and none of this is Sealed: nobody builds a deck from only the packs they opened. Ask your store what it has planned.

## What else happens around a release

- **OTS launch.** Official Tournament Stores get new sets a couple of days early. Beyond the Brave reached stores on October 7, two days before its general launch.
- **Yu-Gi-Oh! Day.** In June 2026, the entry fee for this one-day store tournament included three packs of Battles of Legend: Glorious Gallery, released that month. Players brought their own Advanced or Genesys decks.

## The next sets on the calendar

## Sealed play: build from what you open

Sealed is the oldest way to play with fresh packs: everyone opens the same number of packs and builds a deck from only what they pulled. Konami's Tournament Policy still has rules for Sealed events: a Main Deck of at least 20 cards, and no 3-copy limit as long as every copy came from your own product. None of the Premiere! pages above run it, though, so treat it as a house game with friends, or ask your store whether it runs one.

**Agree on house rules first.** A few packs rarely give anyone 40 playable cards, so borrow the policy's 20-card minimum. Keep the normal rules for everything else: 8000 Life Points, 5-card opening hands, the usual phases.

## How to build a Sealed deck in ten minutes

1. **Sort into piles:** monsters, Spells, Traps, and Extra Deck cards.
2. **Count your easy monsters.** Monsters of Level 4 or lower can be Normal Summoned without Tributes. They are the backbone of a pack deck. Aim for at least half your deck.
3. **Add a few big hitters.** One or two Level 5 or higher monsters are fine. More than that and you will get stuck holding them.
4. **Take every card that stands alone.** In Sealed, a card that works by itself beats a card that needs three specific partners you did not open.
5. **Grab your removal.** Spells and Traps that destroy or stop monsters win Sealed games. Play all of the good ones.
6. **Check the Extra Deck cards.** Keep a Synchro or Xyz Monster only if your monsters can reach its materials: a Tuner for Synchro, matching Levels for Xyz.
7. **Cut to your agreed size.** Fewer, better cards beat a bigger pile.

## Opening packs without regret

Packs are fun. A few habits keep them fun:

- **Decide before you open.** Playing or collecting? It changes what you do with the cards next.
- **Sleeve anything valuable right away.** Pack-fresh foils scratch easily.
- **Look up unfamiliar cards** in Konami's official card database before you trade them.
- **Set a pack budget for the weekend,** and buy the singles you need for your deck after. Lesson 8 explains why.
