Marvel Champions has been growing since 2019, and the full list of products is long. You do not need most of it. Because it is a Living Card Game, every box has fixed contents, so you can buy exactly what you want. The question is what to want.

## The three kinds of box

**Campaign expansions** are the big boxes. Each one adds two heroes with pre-built decks, several villains, new modular encounter sets, and a campaign: a series of scenarios linked by a story and a campaign log. You can also play each scenario on its own.

**Hero packs** are small. Each one adds one hero with their identity-specific cards, a nemesis set, and enough aspect and Basic cards for a pre-built deck. They add player cards, not villains.

**Scenario packs** add one villain scenario and its encounter cards, with no heroes.

There is also a newer type: the Civil War box is a custom scenario expansion rather than a campaign.

## The order most players follow

After the core set, a campaign box usually adds the most, because the core set's three villains wear thin before its five heroes do. Once you have a few campaigns, hero packs keep deckbuilding fresh.

Every product needs the [core set](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07W1BF6D5?tag=cubecommonw0a-20). If you only have one core set, everything below works with it.

## Current picks

These are recent or long-running boxes that are widely sold. Check that a box is in stock before you set your heart on it, since older products go in and out of print.

**Fear No Evil.** The ninth campaign expansion, from 2026, set in the streets of New York. It adds Daredevil and Echo as heroes, with a crime-wave campaign built around Kingpin and six scenarios. Rules Reference 1.8 lists it in the Current Environment, if you build by the game environment rules. [Fear No Evil on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H51YHWHV?tag=cubecommonw0a-20).

**Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.** The eighth campaign expansion. Maria Hill and Nick Fury join as heroes, and five scenarios face A.I.M. and a traitor inside S.H.I.E.L.D., with villains including Batroc and M.O.D.O.K. [Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DK7T35ZF?tag=cubecommonw0a-20).

**Mutant Genesis.** The fifth campaign expansion, an X-Men campaign with Colossus and Shadowcat as heroes, and villains including Sabretooth, the Sentinels, and Magneto across five scenarios. [Mutant Genesis on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0B1L3PH1C?tag=cubecommonw0a-20).

**The Rise of Red Skull.** The first campaign expansion, with Hawkeye and Spider-Woman as heroes and five villains: Crossbones, Absorbing Man, Taskmaster, Zola, and Red Skull. Its scenarios are a steady step up from the core set.

## How to choose

**You play solo or two-handed.** Campaign boxes give the most game per box, since you get both heroes and villains, and the campaign log gives solo play a story to follow.

**You love one character.** Buy their hero pack or the campaign they come in. Playing a hero you care about is the best reason to learn their deck.

**You love deckbuilding.** Hero packs add aspect and Basic cards as well as a hero, so each one widens every deck you build.

**You want a harder game.** A campaign box adds villains with more stages and more complex encounter decks. Expert mode on those villains will last you a long time.

**You teach new players a lot.** Stick with the core set and one campaign. A smaller collection is easier to pick from, and new players feel less lost.

## A note on player count

A single core set supports up to four players, but it has one set of each aspect's cards, so four players must each take a different aspect. Adding a campaign box gives each player more to build with. A second core set mostly adds duplicate player cards, and most players buy expansions first.
