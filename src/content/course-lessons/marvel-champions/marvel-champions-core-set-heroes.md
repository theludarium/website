The core set has five heroes. Each comes with an identity card and a set of identity-specific cards that always go in that hero's deck, plus a nemesis set and an obligation that go into the encounter deck. The Learn to Play pairs each one with an aspect for a pre-built deck: Spider-Man with Justice, Captain Marvel with Leadership, She-Hulk with Aggression, Iron Man with Aggression, and Black Panther with Protection.

One catch: one core set has only one set of Aggression cards, so She-Hulk and Iron Man cannot both use their pre-built decks in the same game. Swap one of them to another aspect.

All numbers below come from the printed cards, as listed on the fan database MarvelCDB.

## Spider-Man: the careful striker

**Hero:** 10 hit points, ATK 2, THW 1, DEF 3, hand size 5. **Alter-ego:** Peter Parker, hand size 6, REC 3.

Spider-Man's identity draws a card each time the villain initiates an attack against him. His DEF of 3 is the best in the box, so he blocks well and gets paid for it. Peter Parker can generate a mental resource once per round, and that matters, because Swinging Web Kick costs 3 and deals 8 damage.

**Plan:** stay in hero form most of the time. Defend with Spider-Man's DEF, draw from Spider-Sense, and save resources for Swinging Web Kick. Backflip prevents all damage from one attack, and Enhanced Spider-Sense cancels a treachery's "When Revealed" effect, so keep one in hand on scary turns. His pre-built Justice deck covers his weak THW.

## Captain Marvel: the steady all-rounder

**Hero:** 12 hit points, ATK 2, THW 2, DEF 1, hand size 5. **Alter-ego:** Carol Danvers, hand size 6, REC 4.

Her Rechannel ability lets her spend an energy resource and heal 1 damage to draw a card, once per round. Cosmic Flight gives her the Aerial trait, which makes Crisis Interdiction remove threat from two schemes and her helmet give +2 DEF.

**Plan:** play Cosmic Flight early. Feed energy into Rechannel and Energy Channel. Her THW 2 and ATK 2 mean she can cover whichever clock is losing. Her Leadership deck adds allies, so she plays well as a team's flexible anchor.

## She-Hulk: the heavy hitter

**Hero:** 15 hit points, ATK 3, THW 1, DEF 2, hand size 4. **Alter-ego:** Jennifer Walters, hand size 6, REC 5.

She-Hulk deals 2 damage to an enemy each time she changes to hero form. Her ATK 3 is the highest in the box, and One-Two Punch readies her after a basic attack, so she can attack twice. Gamma Slam deals damage equal to the damage she has sustained, up to 15.

**Plan:** flip often. Start your turn in alter-ego, use Jennifer Walters, then flip for 2 free damage. Her THW is weak, but Legal Practice lets Jennifer discard cards to remove threat, and Jennifer can prevent 1 threat once per round. With 15 hit points, she can afford to take hits before Gamma Slam.

## Iron Man: the slow builder

**Hero:** 9 hit points, ATK 1, THW 2, DEF 1, hand size 1. **Alter-ego:** Tony Stark, hand size 6, REC 3.

Iron Man's hero hand size starts at 1 and grows by 1 for each tech upgrade he controls, to a maximum of 7. His cards are mostly tech upgrades: Mark V Armor (+6 hit points), Powered Gauntlets, Rocket Boots, Arc Reactor, and the helmet. Rocket Boots make him Aerial until the end of the phase, which doubles several of his effects. Supersonic Punch deals 8 damage instead of 4 if he is Aerial.

**Plan:** spend the first few rounds in alter-ego form, building. Tony Stark's Futurist ability digs for the right card. Flip to hero only once you have enough tech upgrades to make the hand worth it. Iron Man is strong late and fragile early, so he is a better second or third hero than a first.

## Black Panther: the combo engine

**Hero:** 11 hit points, ATK 2, THW 2, DEF 2, hand size 5. **Alter-ego:** T'Challa, hand size 6, REC 4.

Black Panther has retaliate 1. His deck builds four Black Panther upgrades, each with a "Special" ability, and Wakanda Forever! resolves every one in a sequence. Whichever upgrade goes last gets the bigger effect. T'Challa searches for one Black Panther upgrade at setup.

**Plan:** get the upgrades out, then play Wakanda Forever! with Panther Claws or Energy Daggers last for damage, or Tactical Genius last for threat. Five copies of Wakanda Forever! make the engine reliable. His Protection deck keeps him alive while it assembles.

## Pairing heroes in a two-player game

Pair a hero who handles threat with one who deals damage. Captain Marvel with She-Hulk makes an easy first pairing: one covers both clocks, the other hits. Spider-Man and Black Panther are both comfortable in hero form, so the table spends fewer turns resting.
