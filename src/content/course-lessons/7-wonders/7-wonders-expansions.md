7 Wonders plays 3 to 7 players, and every expansion keeps that same range, so 7 is the most the box seats. For two players, the publisher makes a separate game, 7 Wonders Duel, and there is no official solo mode in the base game.

7 Wonders has a small, well-chosen set of expansions. That makes choosing easier than for games with dozens of boxes. This lesson starts with what changes at different player counts, then the free variants already in your box, then each expansion and spin-off worth knowing.

## How many players

The current edition plays 3 to 7, and because everyone picks at once, the publisher lists about 30 minutes at any count. Setup changes with the count: each Age card shows a minimum number of players (3+ up to 7+), and you keep only the cards at or below your player count. The guild deck in Age III gets the number of players plus 2.

| Players | What changes |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | Not supported. Play 7 Wonders Duel instead. |
| 3 | Only 3+ cards. Both other players are your neighbors, so you trade with and fight everyone. Your first hand comes back on the fourth turn of each Age, minus three cards. |
| 4 to 5 | Adds the 4+ and 5+ cards. One or two players are not your neighbors, so you start to ignore part of the table. |
| 6 to 7 | Adds the 6+ and 7+ cards, including second copies of some, so resources are easier to find. You never see a hand twice in an Age. |

If your group likes planning and close rivalry, play at 3 or 4. If it likes a big, lively table where everyone plays at once, 6 or 7 is where 7 Wonders shines. Our list of the [best games for 6 players](/articles/best-board-games-for-6-players/) shows where it sits among other big-table options.

## Two players? Play Duel

The second edition rulebook covers 3 to 7 players only. For two, the publisher's answer is 7 Wonders Duel, a separate game with its own cards and board, built for exactly two. It has [its own course](/academy/7-wonders-duel/) here.

**[7 Wonders](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08F65MX4L?tag=cubecommonw0a-20)** and **[7 Wonders Duel](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B014DMSTXK?tag=cubecommonw0a-20)** each stand alone, and owning both covers any count from 2 to 7.

## Variants already in the box

- **Night sides.** Every Wonder board has a Night side with different stages. The rulebook suggests Day sides for your first few games, then mixing freely.
- **Choose your Wonders.** The rulebook allows choosing boards instead of dealing them at random. It is a good way to let a newer player take a simpler board.

The publisher's 7 Wonders page also posts rules for promo extras, such as additional guild cards and a Manneken Pis Wonder board.

## 7 Wonders expansions

All four of these add to the base game, and each plays 3 to 7.

**[Leaders](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08FRTVGFV?tag=cubecommonw0a-20)** adds 49 Leader cards, famous figures who give each player a starting strategy, plus two new Wonders, Rome and Abu Simbel. The publisher calls it very simple to add, which makes it the natural first expansion.

**[Cities](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08F5MD2Y6?tag=cubecommonw0a-20)** adds 42 black Cities cards with mercenaries, spies, and diplomats, two Wonders (Byzantium and Petra), debts, and diplomacy. It also adds an option to play in two teams at 4 or 6 players. It suits groups who want more interaction.

**[Armada](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08FRSYGXM?tag=cubecommonw0a-20)** adds naval play: Shipyard boards with four naval tracks, Armada cards added to the Age decks, Island cards, and a new Wonder, Siracusa. It has a team option at 4 and 6 too. The publisher describes it as changing the game a lot, so save it for a group that knows the base game well.

**[Edifice](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0BSR43PY1?tag=cubecommonw0a-20)** adds three shared projects, one per Age, that players can join for rewards, with a penalty for those who stay out. It brings two Wonders, Ur and Carthago, and the publisher says it adds no playing time.

One caution: the publisher's FAQ says not to mix components from the first and second editions. Check which edition you own before buying an expansion.

## Duel expansions

Duel has two expansions of its own, Pantheon and Agora, and they do not mix with the ones above. The [Duel expansions lesson](/academy/7-wonders-duel/7-wonders-duel-expansions/) compares them.

## Spin-offs

**[7 Wonders Architects](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09FLT9JMM?tag=cubecommonw0a-20)** is a lighter family game for 2 to 7 players, ages 8 and up, in about 25 minutes. Each player builds one Wonder, stage by stage. It is a good bridge for younger players.

**[7 Wonders Dice](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0FS1B5LC4?tag=cubecommonw0a-20)** is a shake-and-write take (the publisher's term) for 2 to 7 players, with a shared dice box and a city board for each player.

## How to choose

- **Your group plays at 3 to 7 and loves the base game:** Leaders.
- **You want more fights between neighbors:** Cities.
- **You want a bigger game with a new board to manage:** Armada.
- **You play mostly as a couple:** Duel, then its own expansions.
- **You have kids or new players:** Architects.
