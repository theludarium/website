Welcome to Level 1. New Scythe tables rarely miss the big ideas. The slips are in the small rules, the ones that come up every few turns and quietly change the result. This lesson collects the common ones, checked against Stonemaier's rulebook and its official FAQ page.

## The turn

**Mistake: choosing the same section twice in a row.** You must pick a different section of your player mat each turn. Rusviet's faction ability is the only exception, and the FAQ adds that even Rusviet cannot use it to place its token on the Factory card on consecutive turns.

**Mistake: forgetting the bottom-row coins.** Each bottom-row action pays the coins printed beside it. Stonemaier's tournament guidelines suggest letting a player claim missed coins if they remember before their next turn. That only works if someone remembers.

**Mistake: forgetting your neighbors' recruit bonuses.** When a player takes a bottom-row action, the players to their left and right gain the ongoing bonus of any recruit they enlisted from that same section. Announce your bottom-row action out loud so neighbors can check. Top-row actions and Factory card actions do not trigger these bonuses.

## Movement

**Mistake: moving back into a home base.** By default, no unit can move from the board into any home base, even your own. Units only go home by retreating after a lost combat or being forced out as workers.

**Mistake: letting the character carry workers.** Only mechs carry workers, and they can carry any number. Any unit can carry resources. A carried worker costs no movement of its own.

**Mistake: workers walking into enemy territory.** Your workers alone cannot move into a territory controlled by enemy workers, and cannot move into or out of a territory with an enemy character or mech.

**Mistake: forgetting the popularity cost.** When your character or mech moves into a territory held only by enemy workers, its movement ends, those workers retreat home, and you lose 1 popularity for each one. The same cost applies when you win a combat as the attacker and force workers out. Polania's Camaraderie mech ability removes the cost after a combat win. You can still force workers out at 0 popularity.

**Mistake: mech abilities for mechs only.** Once you deploy a mech, its ability applies to your character and all your mechs. It never applies to workers.

## Combat

**Mistake: dialing more than you have.** The power dial goes to 7, but you can never choose more than your current power on the Power Track.

**Mistake: playing a stack of combat cards.** You may add one combat card per character or mech of yours in the fight. Workers do not add card slots, except with Rusviet's People's Army.

**Mistake: forgetting the loser's consolation.** The loser retreats all of their units in the territory to their home base. If they revealed at least 1 power, on the dial or through combat cards, they also gain a combat card.

**Mistake: stopping for an encounter mid-move.** A character that moves onto an encounter token stops there. The encounter resolves only after the whole Move action and any combats are done.

## Stars and objectives

**Mistake: a third combat star.** Each player can earn at most two stars from combat wins. Saxony is the exception: no cap, and Saxony must place a star after a win if it has one left.

**Mistake: completing both objectives.** You can earn only one objective star. When you reveal a completed objective, discard both cards. Saxony can complete both. You may reveal an objective before or after an action on your turn, but not in the middle of one.

**Mistake: losing a star.** Stars are permanent. If your power drops below 16 after you place the power star, the star stays.

**Mistake: skipping a star.** When you reach a goal, you must place a star on it. You cannot save the goal for later.

## The end

**Mistake: finishing the round.** The game ends the instant a player places their sixth star. Nobody takes another turn. If the sixth star comes from a bottom-row action, that player first takes the action's benefit, its coins, and any recruit bonuses, then places the star.

**Mistake: counting resources you do not control.** Resources only score if they sit on territories you control. Resources left on a territory you lost belong to whoever controls it now.

**Mistake: scoring the Factory as one territory.** The Factory counts as three territories for the player who controls it.

## A rules habit for the table

Keep the rulebook nearby and look questions up instead of voting. Stonemaier's [Scythe FAQ page](https://stonemaiergames.com/games/scythe/faq-scythe/) covers the questions that come up most, and it links a community list of commonly overlooked rules on BoardGameGeek.
