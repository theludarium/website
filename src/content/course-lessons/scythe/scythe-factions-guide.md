Welcome to Level 2. Each base faction in Scythe has one faction ability and four mech abilities. Every faction's first mech ability is a version of Riverwalk, and every faction's fourth is Speed. The two in the middle are what make each faction distinct. This lesson covers all five, with the abilities in our own words from the rulebook, and a plan for each built on well-known community advice.

Starting power and combat cards also differ by faction. Your faction mat shows yours, so check both before your first move: together they shape how soon you can afford a fight.

## The shared mechs

- **Riverwalk:** your character and mechs can cross rivers, but only onto the two terrain types your faction's version names. Each player has a Riverwalk card as a reminder.
- **Speed:** your character and mechs can move one extra territory per Move. Speed mechs can also pick up and drop off workers and resources partway through a move.

Rivers hem in most home bases at the start, so many players deploy Riverwalk early to get out. Pick whichever ability your plan needs most.

## Nordic Kingdom

**Faction ability, Swim:** Nordic workers can move across rivers onto any terrain except lakes. Only workers, not the character or mechs.

**Mechs:** Riverwalk onto forests and mountains. **Seaworthy:** move onto and off lakes, which become usable territory. **Artillery:** before combat, pay 1 power to make the opponent lose 2 power, once per combat. Speed.

**The plan:** Nordic workers spread across the map without waiting for a mech. Use that head start for an economy plan: workers on productive land early, an early worker star, and mechs deployed for coverage rather than conquest. Artillery makes Nordic an unwelcome target, which often means the board leaves them alone.

## Rusviet Union

**Faction ability, Relentless:** Rusviet may choose the same section as on the previous turn. This does not apply to the Factory card.

**Mechs:** Riverwalk onto farms and villages. **Township:** villages you control and the Factory count as adjacent for Move actions. **People's Army:** if at least one of your workers is in a combat, you may play one extra combat card (you still need a character or mech there). Speed.

**The plan:** Relentless lets Rusviet repeat its best section, so pick one strong section and take it again and again. Township gives fast access to the Factory and quick reinforcement from villages. Many experienced players rate Rusviet among the strongest base factions. Stonemaier bans its pairing with the Industrial mat because that combination can finish too fast in experienced hands.

## Polania Republic

**Faction ability, Meander:** for each encounter card, Polania may choose up to two different options, in any order. Only one card is drawn.

**Mechs:** Riverwalk onto villages and mountains. **Submerge:** move onto and off lakes, and from any lake to any other lake. **Camaraderie:** no popularity loss for forcing workers to retreat after winning combat as the attacker. Speed.

**The plan:** Polania is the encounter faction. Two options per card make each encounter roughly twice as valuable, so the character should travel the map collecting them. Submerge makes the lakes a private highway. Camaraderie means Polania can push enemy workers off land without the popularity cost that holds other factions back.

## Crimean Khanate

**Faction ability, Coercion:** once per turn, Crimea may spend one combat card as if it were any one resource. Combat cards are still worth nothing at the end.

**Mechs:** Riverwalk onto farms and tundra. **Wayfare:** your character and mechs can move from a territory or home base to any inactive faction's home base, or your own, regardless of distance. **Scout:** before combat, take one random combat card from your opponent. Speed.

**The plan:** Coercion turns Bolster into a resource engine: a combat card can stand in for the missing oil, metal, wood, or food that would have cost you a turn. Crimea is flexible and quick, and Stonemaier bans its pairing with the Patriotic mat for the same reason of speed. Scout punishes opponents who hold combat cards, so Crimea fights from a position of strength.

## Saxony Empire

**Faction ability, Dominate:** Saxony can complete both objectives, earning a star for each, and has no limit on combat stars. If Saxony wins a combat and has stars left, it must place one.

**Mechs:** Riverwalk onto forests and mountains. **Underpass:** mountains you control and all tunnels count as adjacent for Move actions. **Disarm:** before combat in a territory with a tunnel or your Mine, the opponent loses 2 power, once per combat. Speed.

**The plan:** Saxony has more paths to six stars than anyone else, because it can take three, four, or more stars from combat and two from objectives. Underpass and Disarm make the tunnels its home ground. Plan fights in tunnel territories, and keep both objectives in mind from the first turn.

## Reading the table by faction

When you sit down, look at your neighbors' factions. Rusviet and Crimea tend to race. Polania will chase encounters near you. Saxony has reason to fight. Nordic will spread workers quickly and may reach your borders first. Knowing that tells you which side of your home base needs a mech.
