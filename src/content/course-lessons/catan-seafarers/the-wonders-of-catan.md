The Wonders of Catan is a race. There is no pirate, and beside the board sit five wonder tiles, each a four level building that can win the game. The twist: you rarely need to finish one.

## Setup and special rules, in short

Your starting settlements go on the large island, but not on an intersection with a Great Bridge or Great Wall marker, and not on the "X" markers next to the Great Bridge markers. The robber starts on a desert, and the pirate stays in the box. Each player takes the wonder marker in their color, and the five wonder tiles go beside the board.

The first time you settle each small island, you earn **1 bonus point**, as in the earlier exploring scenarios, though here it is 1 point rather than 2.

## The five wonders

Each tile shows a requirement. Meet it and you may claim the wonder by taking the tile. Once claimed, no one else may build it, and you may only ever claim one.

| Wonder | Requirement to begin |
| --- | --- |
| Great Wall | A building at one of the Great Wall markers |
| Great Bridge | A building at one of the Great Bridge markers |
| Grand Theater | 2 cities |
| Grand Castle | 1 city and 6 or more points |
| Grand Monument | 1 city at a port, and a trade route of 5 or more roads and ships |

To build, pay the cost shown at the top of your tile and move your marker up one space. Each of the four levels costs the same 5 resources, and you may build as many levels in one turn as you can pay for. Level 4 completes the wonder. The resources differ from tile to tile, so read them before you choose. <!-- VERIFY: the cost icons on each wonder tile (the rulebook text does not list them, and this lesson does not name them), and that the first payment counts as completing level 1, so four payments of 5 finish a wonder. -->

## How you win

On your turn, you win if you either:

1. **finish your wonder** (all four levels), or
2. **have 10 or more points and a higher completed wonder level than every other player.**

The second path is the one that decides most games. It only needs a strict lead in levels. If no one else has started a wonder, a single level is enough. If a rival sits on level 2, you need level 3.

## What the map rewards

Count the cost. Finishing a wonder is four payments of 5, or 20 resources, on top of whatever it took to qualify. The level race is much cheaper: one level beyond the next player. So the wonder is less a project to finish than a lead to hold while you reach 10 points.

That makes your choice of wonder a production question. A wonder whose five resources match your best numbers turns each good roll into progress. Use [the dots](/academy/catan/number-probability/) to total your pips for each resource on the tile.

The requirements set the timing:

- **Great Wall and Great Bridge** can be claimed early, as soon as you build at a marker. The markers sit at fixed places on the map, so they reward a route planned from your first placement.
- **Grand Theater** needs two cities, a classic ore and grain plan.
- **Grand Castle** waits until 6 points and a city, so it is a midgame claim.
- **Grand Monument** needs a city at a port and a 5 piece route, which suits a player who already builds toward ports and small islands.

## Opening plan

Pick your wonder before you place. Aim one settlement at its requirement (a marker, a port, or a strong ore and grain spot for early cities) and the other at the resources on its tile. If two players want the same early wonder, the first to meet its requirement takes it, so a clear plan to reach a marker in two or three turns is worth a slightly weaker start.

## Midgame plan

Claim as soon as you qualify. Claiming is free, and it takes that wonder away from everyone else.

Then build levels in bursts. Because you can buy several levels in one turn, a level lead can flip in a single turn. Saving 10 cards for a two level jump is strong, but a 7 makes you discard half of a hand over 7 cards. Hold more than 7 only when you plan to spend it on your next turn.

Each first settlement on a new small island is 2 points in one build: the settlement and the bonus. See [exploring for the bonus points](/academy/catan-seafarers/exploring-for-bonus-points/) for how to time those trips.

## Endgame: the level check

Every turn, write down three numbers: your points, your wonder level, and the highest level anyone else has. When you are at 8 or 9 points, the race is on. If you can reach 10 with a level lead in the same turn, do it in one go, so no one gets a turn to answer. If a rival has 10 points and only needs a level, the robber on their wonder's key resource is your best defense.

## The trap to avoid

Finishing the wonder for its own sake. Twenty resources is a lot of cards that could have been cities. Claim, lead, reach 10. Build the fourth level only if it is the fastest finish on the board.
