This lesson stretches Seafarers two ways: more people at the table, and more game on it. Both are official.

## At 3 and 4 players

The [Seafarers](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DYK3S5MJ?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) box plays 3 or 4 with [base Catan](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DYK1ZH2D?tag=cubecommonw0a-20). The rulebook gives different maps and component lists for 3 and 4 players, so set up for your actual count. A few scenarios change more than the map. In The Pirate Islands, a 3 player game skips one set of fortress and beachhead locations and removes the Victory Point cards from the deck. Check the scenario page before you set up.

## At 5 and 6 players: which boxes

To seat 5 or 6, you need four boxes: base Catan, the [CATAN 5 to 6 Player Expansion](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DYK1LYDT?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), Seafarers, and the [Seafarers 5 to 6 Player Expansion](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DYK1QR6W?tag=cubecommonw0a-20). The Seafarers 5 to 6 rulebook says you need most of the components from the other three. It adds sea, gold, and desert hexes, two frame pieces, ships and markers for two more colors, and two new wonders. Note one swap: the Seafarers number discs go back in the box, and you use the discs from base Catan and the base 5 to 6 extension instead.

Our [best Catan expansions guide](/articles/best-catan-expansions/) covers which order to buy them in.

## The paired turn

The 2025 rules replace the old special building phase with **paired players**. Each turn, two people act:

1. **Player 1** takes a full Seafarers turn: production, then actions. If player 1 meets the scenario's win condition, the game ends at once.
2. **Player 2**, the third player to the left of player 1, then takes an Action phase: build, move 1 ship, play 1 development card, and trade with the supply. **Player 2 may not trade with other players.**

Then the dice and both player markers pass left. A development card bought as player 2 can be played as player 1, and the reverse. Player 2 can also win on their Action phase.

In one full lap of a 6 player game, six turns, you act twice: once with a roll, once without.

For strategy, treat your player 2 action as a second build window. Plan your trades as player 1 so the cards you need are already in hand, because as player 2 only the bank and your ports will trade with you.

## What changes in each scenario at 5 to 6

The 5 to 6 rulebook prints its own maps and notes these changes:

- **The Six Islands** replaces The Four Islands.
- **Cloth for Catan** has 12 villages on six small islands, each starting with 5 cloth, plus a general supply of 10. <!-- VERIFY: whether the 5 or more empty villages ending changes with 12 villages; the 5 to 6 rulebook does not say, so the Seafarers rule appears to stand. -->
- **The Pirate Islands** skips one set of piece locations in a 5 player game, and the fleet does not attack when it stops on the hex marked "!".
- **The Wonders of Catan** uses 7 wonders: the original five plus the Lighthouse (a building at a lighthouse marker) and the Great Library (2 cities).
- **New World** has its own hex list for the bigger frame.

## Seafarers with Cities & Knights

There is no separate combination booklet for the 6th edition on the official rules page. The rules live in the [Cities & Knights rulebook](https://www.catan.com/sites/default/files/2025-03/CN3087%20CATAN%E2%80%93Cities%26Knights_%20Rulebook.pdf) itself, on its page about combining with Seafarers. You need [Cities & Knights](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DY87W8SS?tag=cubecommonw0a-20); if C&K is new to you, start with [how to play Cities & Knights](/academy/catan-cities-and-knights/how-to-play-cities-and-knights/).

**Which scenarios.** The rulebook steers you away from scenarios that explore hidden board (The Fog Islands) or many small islands (The Four Islands), because they noticeably increase the impact of the barbarians. It names Heading for New Shores and Through the Desert as good fits.

**The added rules,** in short:

- Rules for roads in Cities & Knights apply to ships too.
- The pirate starts on the last space of the barbarian track and enters play only after the first barbarian attack, then follows the scenario's placement.
- Barbarians attack all islands at once, counting every city and knight on the board.
- Gold fields produce resources only, never commodities, and the merchant may not go on gold.
- Knights move along your connected roads and ships, and may end on an empty intersection between sea hexes. You may not move a ship if it would cut a knight off from your route.
- A knight at sea may chase away the pirate the way land knights chase the robber.
- Taxation moves only the robber. Diplomacy removing a road lets you build only a road; removing a ship, only a ship.
- **Add 2 points** to the scenario's target.

The barbarian clock matters even more on an open map, because every ship you build is a resource not spent on knights. The C&K course's [barbarian timing math](/academy/catan-cities-and-knights/barbarian-timing-math/) applies directly.

## All of it at once

Seafarers, Cities & Knights, and both 5 to 6 extensions together is possible on paper. You would add the [Cities & Knights 5 to 6 Player Expansion](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DY87CXSQ?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) as well. But neither the Cities & Knights rulebook nor the Seafarers 5 to 6 rulebook gives combined rules for that table, so you would be making house rules. <!-- VERIFY: check catan.com's rules page for a later 6th edition document that combines Seafarers, Cities & Knights, and 5 to 6 players. --> Learn each layer on its own first.
