The Fog Islands is the third scenario in the Seafarers book. Two known islands sit on either side of the map, and between them lies a band of empty spaces: the fog. You do not know what is out there until someone sails into it. It is the most surprising of the early scenarios, and the one where a single discovery can swing the game.

## Setup and special rules

- **The map.** Two islands of land are set out face up. The white spaces on the map stay empty at the start. The hexes for the fog go into a face-down stack beside the board, and a set of number discs goes into a face-down pile.
- **Where you start.** Your two starting settlements, each with a road or ship, may go on one island or on two different islands.
- **The robber** starts on the hex with the 12.
- **Discovery.** When you place a road or ship next to an intersection that touches an empty space, you discover it. Flip the top hex from the stack into that space. If it is land, draw a random number disc for it and take 1 resource card of the type that hex produces. If it is sea, it gets no number and you take nothing. <!-- VERIFY: the rulebook does not say what a discovered gold field pays on discovery; check the official FAQ before saying it pays a card of choice. -->
- **No island bonus.** Unlike most early scenarios, this one has no bonus points for settling new islands. The component list has no victory point tokens at all.

## How you win

The first player to have 12 or more victory points during their turn wins. That is the lowest target among the first four scenarios, so the game moves quickly.

## What the map rewards

Look at what the face-up land holds, and what the fog holds. In the 3-player game, the two known islands have 4 forests and 4 pastures, but only 2 hills, 2 fields, and 2 mountains. The fog stack is the mirror image:

| Fog stack, 3 or 4 players | Count |
| --- | --- |
| Sea | 2 |
| Gold fields | 2 |
| Hills | 2 |
| Fields | 2 |
| Mountains | 2 |
| Forest | 1 |
| Pasture | 1 |

So 6 of the 10 land hexes in the fog make brick, wheat, or ore, and 2 more are gold. The known land fuels ships. The fog builds settlements and cities. The 4-player known land is a little more even, but the fog stack is the same.

Two more numbers help:

- **Each fog space is land 10 times in 12** at the start. Most discoveries pay a card.
- **The fog's number discs include one 6 and one 8** in both player counts. Somewhere out there are two red numbers. Nobody knows where.

## Opening plan

1. **Take your wood and wool from the known land.** That is where it is, and you will need it for every ship. Use the [Seafarers openings](/academy/catan-seafarers/seafarers-openings/) habit of counting forest and pasture pips separately.
2. **Grab what brick, wheat, and ore you can.** They are scarce on the known islands, so a decent number on any of them is worth more than usual. Expect the robber to visit the best ore hex.
3. **Put one settlement on the coast facing the fog,** with a ship as its starting piece. You want to be the one who reveals the fog nearest you.
4. **Consider splitting across both islands** in a 4-player game. Two launch points face two parts of the fog, and you are less likely to be boxed in.

## Midgame plan

- **Reveal where you can settle.** A revealed hex belongs to no one. If you flip a 6 next to a rival's ship, you have just found their next settlement. Sail into fog spaces that your next ship or road can reach a legal corner of, not spaces that sit next to someone else's fleet.
- **Count discovery as income.** Each new piece that reveals a land hex pays back 1 of its 2 cards. A ship that discovers is half price. A ship that discovers nothing still extends your reach.
- **Land fast on good numbers.** Once a strong hex appears, every player at the table can see it. The race starts the moment it flips.
- **Turn fog ore and wheat into cities.** With a 12-point target and no island bonus, cities on good fog hexes are the fastest finish. See [cities first?](/academy/catan/cities-first/) for the tempo math.
- **Do not forget Longest Route.** Lines that sail through the fog grow long naturally, and 2 points is a sixth of the target. See [the longest route at sea](/academy/catan-seafarers/longest-route-at-sea/).

## The trap

**Exploring for the table.** The player who reveals the most fog often does not win. Every discovery pays one card to the discoverer and a new option to everyone. If you push a single long ship line into the fog, flipping a hex each turn, you will hand rivals a tour of the best spots while you pay for the boat. Explore in short steps that end next to a corner you can claim, and let others reveal the fog near their own fleets.

The other trap is the opposite: playing base Catan on the known islands and ignoring the fog. The known land is short of what cities need. A player who never sails will run out of ore and wheat long before 12 points.
