War of the Ring is a two-player strategy game about the whole of The Lord of the Rings. One player leads the Free Peoples: Gondor, Rohan, the Elves, the Dwarves, the North, and the small Fellowship carrying the Ring. The other leads the Shadow: Sauron, Saruman's Isengard, and the Southrons and Easterlings. The Shadow has far bigger armies. The Free Peoples have a secret: a few hobbits walking toward the one place nobody is watching.

This lesson follows the Ares Games Second Edition rulebook, version 2.3. The deluxe Anniversary Release uses the same rules.

## How do you set up?

![A schematic War of the Ring setup: the map with the Ring-bearers in Rivendell, the Fellowship and Political tracks, the Hunt Box and Hunt Pool, the Mordor Track, and each side's decks and dice](/academy/war-of-the-ring/setup.svg)

1. **The Fellowship.** Put the Ring-bearers figure (Frodo and Sam) in Rivendell. Put the Fellowship Progress counter on step 0 of the Fellowship Track, Hidden side up, and the Corruption counter on 0.
2. **The Companions.** Stack the seven Companion cards in the Guide box with Gandalf the Grey on top, and put their figures in the Fellowship box. Set aside Aragorn, Gandalf the White, Gollum, and the three Shadow Minions.
3. **The Elven Rings.** The Free Peoples take the three Elven Ring counters, Ring side up.
4. **The cards.** Each side has two Event decks, Character and Strategy. Shuffle all four.
5. **The Hunt Pool.** Put the standard (beige) Hunt tiles in a cup. Set the blue and red special tiles aside.
6. **The dice.** The Shadow takes seven red action dice. The Free Peoples take four blue ones.
7. **The Political Track.** Place each Nation's counter on its marked step. Every Free Peoples Nation starts passive except the Elves.
8. **The armies.** Place every Nation's starting units and Leaders from the setup chart in the rulebook.

## What happens in a turn?

Each turn has six phases.

1. **Recover dice and draw.** Take back your dice and draw one card from each of your two decks. Your hand limit is six.
2. **Fellowship phase.** The Free Peoples may declare where the Fellowship is and may change its Guide.
3. **Hunt allocation.** The Shadow may set dice aside in the Hunt Box, up to one per Companion still in the Fellowship.
4. **Action roll.** Both sides roll their remaining dice. Every Shadow die showing the Eye goes to the Hunt Box too.
5. **Action resolution.** Starting with the Free Peoples, players take turns using one die each.
6. **Victory check.** See if either side has won a military victory.

## What do the action dice do?

Each die face is a kind of action. **Character** moves Leaders' armies, Companions, Minions, or the Fellowship. **Army** moves two armies or makes one attack. **Muster** recruits troops or moves a Nation along the Political Track. **Event** draws a card or plays any card.

The sides' dice differ. The Shadow's special face is the **Eye**, which feeds the Hunt. The Free Peoples' special face is **Will of the West**, which becomes any result they want.

## How does the Fellowship move?

The Fellowship moves in secret. Each time the Free Peoples use a Character die to move it, the Progress counter goes up one step, and the figure stays where it was last seen. The number on the track is how many regions it might have traveled.

Every move triggers the **Hunt**. The Shadow rolls one die per die in the Hunt Box, up to five, and each 6 is a success. Each earlier Fellowship move that turn adds 1 to every Hunt die. On a success, the Shadow draws a Hunt tile, and its damage kills a Companion or adds Corruption. Some tiles also reveal the Fellowship, which must hide again before it can move.

Once the Fellowship reaches Minas Morgul or the Morannon, it can enter the Mordor Track. From there, each move draws a tile with no roll at all, until the Ring-bearers reach the Crack of Doom.

## How do battles work?

An army is everything one side has in a region, up to 10 units. To attack, roll one combat die per unit, up to five, and hit on 5 or 6. Leaders, Nazgûl, and Characters let you re-roll misses. Each hit removes a Regular or turns an Elite into a Regular. Cities, Fortifications, and Strongholds favor the defender.

## What is the Political Track?

A Nation can only muster with dice, attack, or cross another Nation's border once it is **At War**. Muster dice move a Nation down the track, and so does being attacked. Passive Free Peoples Nations must be activated first, for example by an enemy army entering their land.

## How do you win?

| Side | Ring victory (ends the game at once) | Military victory (end of turn) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free Peoples | Ring-bearers reach the Crack of Doom with under 12 Corruption | Hold Shadow Cities and Strongholds worth 4 points |
| Shadow | Ring-bearers reach 12 Corruption | Hold Free Peoples Cities and Strongholds worth 10 points |

A City is worth 1 point and a Stronghold 2. The [four ways to win](/academy/war-of-the-ring/war-of-the-ring-victory-conditions/) shape every plan, so Level 1 gives them a lesson of their own.
