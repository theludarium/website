The Shadow has more of everything: more dice, more units, and units that come back when they die. That is exactly why the Shadow can lose. A Shadow player who spreads that strength evenly across the map, or spends it all chasing the Ring, gives the Free Peoples the turns they need. This lesson turns the Shadow's size into a plan.

## Your two clocks

The Shadow runs two clocks. The military clock counts toward 10 points of Free Peoples Cities and Strongholds. The Corruption clock counts toward 12. The Free Peoples have one clock, the Fellowship's steps, and they try to finish it before either of yours.

Most of your dice go to the military clock, because Shadow losses return to the reinforcements and the Free Peoples' do not. The Hunt is how you slow the Free Peoples' clock. A Fellowship that has to stop and heal, or lose a Companion, or spend a die hiding, is a Fellowship that is not walking.

## Waking your Nations

Sauron and Isengard start one step from At War, and the Southrons and Easterlings start two steps away. A Muster die moves one Nation one step. Many Shadow players spend early Muster dice getting Sauron and Isengard to At War on the first turn, because a Nation that is not At War cannot recruit with Muster dice, attack, or cross into another Nation.

The Southrons and Easterlings are a large reserve; bring them in when you know where you want them.

## The three Minions

Each Minion adds one action die from the next turn on, so each pays you back every turn after it arrives.

- **Saruman** enters with a Muster die once Isengard is At War, placed in Orthanc. He never leaves the Orthanc region.
- **The Witch-king** needs Sauron and at least one Free Peoples Nation At War. Use a Muster die to place him with a Shadow army that includes a Sauron unit. When he enters, **every Free Peoples Nation is activated.** That wakes every passive Nation at once, so time him for when that no longer hurts you.
- **The Mouth of Sauron** comes late. He enters with a Muster die once the Fellowship is on the Mordor Track or every Free Peoples Nation is At War. <!-- VERIFY: the Mouth of Sauron's placement on his Character card (believed to be any Shadow Stronghold) -->

Saruman early is a common choice, because one Muster die puts Isengard At War and the extra die starts paying the turn after he arrives.

## Pick a front and count to 10

A City is worth 1 point and a Stronghold 2. Some of the big targets:

| Region | Settlements | Points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gondor | Minas Tirith and Dol Amroth (Strongholds), Pelargir (City) | 5 |
| Rohan | Helm's Deep (Stronghold), Edoras (City) | 3 |
| The North and the Dwarves | Erebor (Stronghold), Dale (City) | 3 |
| The Elves | Rivendell, Lórien, the Woodland Realm, the Grey Havens (Strongholds) | 2 each |

Gondor and Rohan together make 8. Add one Elven Stronghold, or two Cities, and you are there. That arithmetic is why Gondor and Rohan see so many sieges. Elven Strongholds are worth as much as Gondor's, but the Elves start active, so their Muster dice can take them to At War without waiting to be woken.

The common mistake is to attack everywhere a little. Spread armies make slow progress and hand the Free Peoples Elite units to trade efficiently. Mass on one front, take it, then roll the armies to the next.

## Sieges and time

A siege is slow on purpose. You hit only on 6, and each extra round costs you an Elite. Bring Nazgûl, which count as Leaders and fly in with one Character die. Bring cards that help sieges. And bring more units than you think, because only five dice roll but your extra units let you keep going.

## Budgeting the Hunt

Every die you put in the Hunt Box is a die not spent on the war. A common rhythm:

- **Early:** one or two dice in the Hunt, plus whatever Eyes you roll. The Fellowship is far from Mordor and has Companions to absorb damage.
- **When the Fellowship moves fast:** more dice. A Free Peoples player who moves two or three times a turn is betting the Hunt misses, so make that bet expensive.
- **When it enters Mordor:** as many as you can afford. In Mordor every move draws a tile, and an Eye tile deals damage equal to the dice in the Hunt Box.

Use your Nazgûl too. A Nazgûl in the Fellowship's region grants a Hunt re-roll, and so does an army there.

## Guard your own Strongholds

The Free Peoples only need 4 points. Shadow Strongholds near Free Peoples armies, such as Dol Guldur near the Elven lands and Orthanc near Rohan, can fall if you empty them for an offensive. Leave enough at home that a Free Peoples raid would cost them more than it is worth.

## Read the race

Each turn, compare your two clocks with theirs. If your armies are three turns from 10 and the Fellowship is five turns from the Crack of Doom, push the war. If the Fellowship is closer, move dice into the Hunt and bring armies to its likely path. The [Hunt odds](/academy/war-of-the-ring/war-of-the-ring-hunt-for-the-ring/) tell you how much each die buys.
