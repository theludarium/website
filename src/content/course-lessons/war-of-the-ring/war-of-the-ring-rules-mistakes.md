War of the Ring is long and its rulebook is thorough, so every group plays a few rules wrong at first. Most of those slips favor one side without anyone noticing. This lesson collects the common ones, each checked against the Second Edition rulebook, version 2.3.

## The Hunt

**Mistake: forgetting the bonus for moving twice.** Each die the Free Peoples use to move the Fellowship goes into the Hunt Box after that move's Hunt is resolved. Every Free Peoples die there adds 1 to each Hunt die for later moves that turn. The first move needs 6s. The second needs 5 or 6. The third needs 4, 5, or 6. A 1 always fails.

**Mistake: the Shadow skipping the Hunt for free.** If any Free Peoples die was in the Hunt Box at the end of the last turn, the Shadow must allocate at least one die to the Hunt this turn. The most the Shadow can allocate is one die per Companion still in the Fellowship, though one die is always allowed. Eyes rolled in the action roll go in on top of that, with no limit.

**Mistake: moving a revealed Fellowship.** A revealed Fellowship cannot move until it is hidden again. Hiding takes a Character die, does not move the Fellowship, and that die does not go into the Hunt Box.

**Mistake: re-rolling every miss.** Hunt re-rolls come from the region the Ring-bearers figure is in. The Shadow gets one re-roll of a failed die for each condition present: a Shadow-controlled Stronghold, Shadow army units, and Nazgûl. That is three re-rolls at most, not one per unit.

**Mistake: wounding a Companion.** When Hunt damage is taken as a casualty, the Free Peoples choose either the Guide or a random Companion, and that Companion dies whatever the damage. If the damage is higher than his Level, the rest becomes Corruption. A Level 3 Gandalf can absorb a 3 completely. A Level 1 hobbit taking a 3 leaves 2 Corruption.

**Mistake: hiding inside a friendly Stronghold after a reveal.** When the Hunt reveals the Fellowship, the Free Peoples move the figure up to the number on the Progress counter, but it cannot end in a Free Peoples City or Stronghold they control. If the path touches a Shadow Stronghold, the Shadow draws an extra tile for each one.

## Nations and armies

**Mistake: attacking with a Nation not At War.** Units of a Nation that is not At War cannot attack, cannot be mustered with Muster dice, and cannot cross into another Nation's borders, even a friendly one. They can still defend and can move outside their own borders into land that belongs to no Nation. These limits apply even when they share a region with units of a Nation that is At War, so an attacking army must leave them behind.

**Mistake: moving a passive Nation to At War.** A passive Free Peoples Nation can advance on the Political Track but can never step onto At War until it is activated. Activation happens when an enemy army enters its land, when one of its armies is attacked, when the Fellowship is declared in one of its Cities or Strongholds, or when a Companion who can activate it ends a move in one.

**Mistake: forgetting that attacks wake Nations up.** Each battle against a Nation's army moves that Nation one step toward At War, and so does each capture of one of its Settlements. Attacking a sleeping Nation can be the push that brings it into the war.

**Mistake: one Army die, two attacks.** An Army die can move two different armies, but it can only attack with one.

## Battles

**Mistake: rolling more than five.** Combat Strength is the number of units, and you roll that many dice up to five. Leadership lets you re-roll that many misses, also up to five. No modifier breaks the cap.

**Mistake: hitting on 5 in a City.** When the defender is in a City or Fortification, the attacker hits only on 6 in the first round. After that, normal rules apply. In a siege battle against a Stronghold, the attacker hits only on 6 every round.

**Mistake: endless siege rounds.** A siege battle lasts one round. The attacker can extend it one more round each time by turning one of their Elite units into a Regular. A besieged Stronghold holds at most five units, and any extra are removed when the siege begins.

**Mistake: picking off a Leader.** Leaders and Characters are never taken as casualties. They die only when every unit in their army is gone. A Character alone in a region is never drawn into a battle.

## Small rules with big effects

- Shadow casualties go back to the reinforcements. Free Peoples casualties, units and Leaders alike, are gone for good.
- An empty Event deck is never reshuffled. After that you draw only from the other one.
- A Companion who leaves the Fellowship can never rejoin it.
- On the Mordor Track, if the Free Peoples end the action resolution phase without trying to move or hide the Fellowship, the Ring-bearers gain 1 Corruption.
