Probes chase planets. Telescopes chase stars. Scanning is the half of SETI that never leaves Earth, and it feeds two things at once: sector wins, which pay rewards and points, and data, which you analyze into life traces. This lesson covers the rules from the English rulebook (October 2024) and the official FAQ, then how to scan with a purpose.

## The Scan action

**Cost:** 1 credit and 2 energy, as a main action. Do these steps in any order:

1. **Mark a signal in Earth's sector**, the sector Earth sits in right now.
2. **Discard one card from the card row** and mark a signal in a sector matching the color in that card's upper-right corner. Two sectors match each color. Refill the row after the whole action.
3. **Use your telescope techs**, if you have any. Most cost something extra to activate.

That is at least two signals per Scan, and up to four with techs.

## Marking a signal

To mark a signal, take the leftmost data token from that star's data slot, put it in your data pool, and put one of your markers in its place. Marking the **second position** in a slot scores 2 points at once. Marking a signal is never optional. If the slot is full, you still place the marker as an excess signal: no data, but it counts toward the win.

## Completing and winning a sector

When the last data token in a slot is replaced, the sector is complete. Resolve it after your main action:

- **The winner** is the player with the most markers in the slot. A tie goes to the player whose marker was placed **later**.
- The winner places a marker beside the star and takes the reward shown there. Some stars give a better reward to their first winner.
- **Everyone who had a marker there**, winner included, gains 1 publicity.
- **The second-place player** leaves one marker on the first space of the slot. Everyone else's markers go home, and the slot refills with data.

The win marker beside the star never counts toward winning again; it is a record. Cards and one gold tile side count your wins, including repeat wins at the same star.

## Scanning with a purpose

**Find the sector one signal from done.** Stars hold different amounts of data. A short slot fills fast, and the player who places the final marker wins any tie. Before you scan, look for a sector where your one or two signals complete it with you on top.

**Use the tie rule.** Because later markers win ties, the last signal into a tied sector is the strongest one. Excess signals count too, so a well-timed extra signal can steal a sector that looked settled.

**Second place is a head start.** The runner-up keeps a marker in the refilled slot. A close second at a star you plan to revisit is worth more than it looks.

**Read the card row.** The row card you discard sets your second signal. Sometimes the best Scan is the one that also takes away a card a rival wants, though it gets replaced at once.

**Mind the rotation.** Earth's sector shifts when the disc carrying Earth turns. If a rotation is coming, your first signal may land somewhere else next turn.

## Telescope techs

The four telescope techs each add to a Scan: mark Earth's signal in an adjacent sector instead, discard a card from hand for an extra signal in a matching sector, pay publicity for an extra signal in Mercury's sector, or pay 1 energy to launch a probe or gain a move. The FAQ confirms that launch costs only that 1 energy, not the usual 2 credits.

## Your computer and the Analyze action

Data goes from your pool into your computer as a free action, left to right along the top row. Your pool holds 6, and extra data is lost. Covering the second space gains 1 publicity; covering the fourth lets you tuck a card for income.

**Analyze data** costs 1 energy and needs all six top spaces full. Clear every data token from your computer, top and bottom, and mark a life trace for one of the two alien species. Analyzing is the main way to earn that kind of trace. Data still in your pool stays.

Each computer tech goes in a computer tech slot on your board. Its upper space scores 2 points when you place data there. Its lower space gives the reward shown, and you can fill it only once the space above it is filled. The lower spaces never have to be full to analyze.

## A scan and analyze rhythm

A steady pattern is to scan for data, pour it into the computer on the same turn, and analyze when the row is full. Each full row means a trace plus the publicity and income from the early spaces. Watch your pool: if a big Scan would push it past 6, place data first.
