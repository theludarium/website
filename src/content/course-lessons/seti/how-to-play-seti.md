[SETI: Search for Extraterrestrial Intelligence](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CZBCRC3S?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) is a heavy euro from Czech Games Edition, designed by Tomáš Holek and released in 2024. Each player runs a research institution. You launch probes across a solar system that turns under your feet, point telescopes at nearby stars, analyze the data you collect, and race to find traces of alien life. It plays 1 to 4, at about 40 minutes per player.

This lesson follows the English rulebook dated October 2024 and the official FAQ (version BGG 11.2024). Both are free on the [CGE SETI page](https://www.czechgames.com/games/seti-search-for-extraterrestrial-intelligence).

## How do you set up SETI?

![An abstract SETI setup: the main board with the rotating solar system and its eight sectors, two face-down alien boards, the card row, gold tiles, end-of-round stacks, and a player board](/academy/seti/setup.svg)

1. **Build the main board** from the planetary board, the solar system board, and the tech board.
2. **Place two alien boards.** Shuffle the five species face down and put two in the slots on the planetary board's edge. Return the rest unseen.
3. **Randomize the solar system.** The three discs and four sector boards start in a random layout. CGE's [companion app](https://seti-solarsystem.czechgames.com) gives you one, or you can shuffle by hand. The discs must line up into eight sectors, each with one nearby star.
4. **Deal the card row:** three cards face up.
5. **Fill the stars.** Each nearby star gets data tokens to fill its data slot.
6. **Gold tiles and techs.** Put the four gold scoring tiles out, random side up. Shuffle each of the 12 tech stacks, technology side down, with a 2-point tile on top.
7. **End-of-round stacks.** Make four stacks, each with one more card than there are players. Put one rotation token on the tech board and the other on the first stack.
8. **Neutral milestones.** With 1 or 2 players, put two neutral markers above the 20 and 30 spaces of the score track. With 3, one each. With 4, none.

Each player then takes a color, a player board, and a starting income card. The first player starts at 1 point, the second at 2, and so on. Everyone starts with 4 publicity, 4 credits, 3 energy, and 5 cards, then tucks one card under their income card.

## What do you do on a turn?

You take **one main action**, plus any number of **free actions** before, during, or after it. Then milestones and discoveries are resolved, and play passes left.

| Main action | Cost | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Launch a probe | 2 credits | Put a probe on Earth. One probe in space at a time by default. |
| Orbit a planet | 1 credit, 1 energy | Turn a probe at a planet into an orbiter and take the bonuses above the planet. |
| Land on a planet | 3 energy (2 if any orbiter is there) | Turn a probe into a lander for points and a life trace. |
| Scan nearby stars | 1 credit, 2 energy | Mark signals in sectors and collect data. |
| Analyze data | 1 energy | Clear a full computer row to mark a life trace. |
| Play a card | The card's cost | Resolve its effect. |
| Research a tech | 6 publicity | Rotate the solar system, then take a tech tile. |
| Pass | Free | You are done for the round. |

## What are the free actions?

- **Move.** Each energy spent moves one probe one space, orthogonally. Leaving an asteroid space costs one extra move. Moving onto a space with a publicity icon, such as a comet or many of the planets, gains 1 publicity (Earth gives none).
- **Place data** from your data pool into your computer, left to right, taking any reward you cover.
- **Complete a mission** whose condition you meet.
- **Trade** two of one kind (cards, credits, or energy) for one of any kind.
- **Buy a card** for 3 publicity, from the row or the top of the deck.
- **Discard a card** for the effect in its top-left corner.

A card is used for only one thing: its main effect, its corner, its signal, or income.

## How does the solar system move?

The solar system rotates when anyone researches a tech and when the first player passes each round. Turn the top disc first, then the middle, then the bottom, always one sector counterclockwise. Probes ride along and can be pushed. The [rotation lesson](/academy/seti/seti-solar-system-rotation/) covers it in full.

## How does a round end?

When you pass, discard down to 4 cards. If you are the first to pass this round, rotate the solar system. Then, in rounds 1 to 4, choose one card from that round's end-of-round stack. Once everyone has passed, everyone collects income: the credits, energy, and card printed on the income card plus every tucked card. The starting player marker moves one player to the left.

## How do you win?

Points come all game, from orbits and landings to sectors, techs, missions, and discoveries. When your score reaches 25, 50, and 70, you mark a gold scoring tile. After round 5, score your end-of-game cards and gold tiles. Most points wins, and a tie is shared.
