SETI's rulebook is clear, but it is dense, and a few rules hide in sidebars. This lesson collects the slips that come up most, each checked against the English rulebook (October 2024) and CGE's official FAQ (version BGG 11.2024). The rulebook's own Rules Reminders page lists several of them, which tells you how often they happen.

## Movement and probes

**Mistake: leaving asteroids for one move.** Leaving a space with asteroids costs one extra movement. A probe tech removes that cost, and so does one card for a single turn.

**Mistake: moving through the Sun.** The Sun is not a space. Probes cannot enter it or pass through it, and moves are orthogonal, never diagonal.

**Mistake: landing with an orbiter.** Once a probe becomes an orbiter or a lander, it leaves the solar system board for good. An orbiter can never land later, and neither counts as a probe for card effects.

**Mistake: paying full price to land next to a rival's orbiter.** Landing costs 2 energy instead of 3 if any orbiter is at that planet, no matter who owns it.

**Mistake: counting a planet you are already on as a visit.** A "visit" means moving onto that space. If your probe is already at Venus when you play a card that rewards visiting Venus, you have to move there again to trigger it.

## Rotation

**Mistake: picking a tech, then rotating.** Rotation comes first. Turn the solar system, then choose your tech. The order matters, since rotation can push a probe or move a planet you were aiming for.

**Mistake: forgetting the round 5 rotation.** The first player to pass each round rotates the system, and round 5 is no exception.

**Mistake: rotating twice for one card.** A card that shows both a rotation and a tech still rotates only once. The rotation icon is a reminder.

## Resources and caps

**Mistake: going past 10 publicity.** Publicity stops at 10. Spend it before a big gain would be wasted.

**Mistake: hoarding data in the pool.** Your data pool holds 6. Any data beyond that is lost, so place data in your computer as you go. Placing data is a free action, but only on your turn.

**Mistake: forgetting the instant resource when tucking.** When you tuck a card for income, you gain that resource right away, and again every round from then on.

**Mistake: mixed trades.** The trade is two of one kind for one of any kind. Two cards for a credit is fine. A card and an energy for a credit is not.

**Mistake: a hand limit during the round.** There is none. You discard down to 4 only when you pass.

## Timing

**Mistake: resolving milestones mid-turn.** Gold and neutral milestones resolve between turns, after the current player's free actions. If two players cross milestones on the same turn, the active player resolves first, then clockwise, and neutral milestones go last.

**Mistake: free actions on someone else's turn.** You cannot place data, move, or trade between turns or on another player's turn. Some triggerable missions can still trigger off your turn, as the rulebook notes.

## Sectors

**Mistake: breaking a sector tie in favor of the first marker.** The tie goes to the player whose marker was placed later.

**Mistake: clearing every marker after a sector win.** The player in second place leaves one marker on the first space of the data slot. If every marker belongs to the winner, nobody is second.

**Mistake: counting your win marker toward the next win.** The marker beside the star only records that you won. It never helps you win that sector again.

**Mistake: skipping a signal.** Marking a signal is mandatory. If the slot is already full, mark it anyway as an excess signal: no data, but it counts toward winning.

## Analyzing data

**Mistake: thinking tech spaces must be filled.** To analyze, the six top spaces of your computer must be full. The lower spaces under computer techs can be empty.

## A habit for the table

When a question comes up, check the Rules Reminders and FAQ on the back page of the rulebook before anyone votes. Then use the official FAQ PDF from the [CGE downloads](https://www.czechgames.com/games/seti-search-for-extraterrestrial-intelligence) for card questions. Thirty seconds of checking keeps the game fair.
