Pandemic is a family of games, not just one box. There are expansions, Legacy campaigns, standalone versions, and free extras on the publisher's site. That is a lot to choose from. The good news: the base game has plenty of room to grow before you spend anything.

## How does the game change by player count?

The base game plays 2 to 4.

- **Two players:** 4-card starting hands and the most turns each, about 13 at Standard. Only two roles are in play, so each player covers more of the map. Meeting up to share cards is simpler with two pawns.
- **Three players:** 3-card hands. A good balance of turns and roles.
- **Four players:** 2-card hands and four roles, but only six or seven turns each at Standard. More special powers, more conversation, and more risk that one voice takes over.

At any count, the Player deck is almost the same size, so the team gets about the same number of turns in total. More players means those turns are spread thinner.

## The difficulty dials in the box

You have three settings before you buy anything:

- **Epidemics.** 4 for Introductory, 5 for Standard, 6 for Heroic. The rulebook suggests moving to 6 once you have mastered Standard.
- **Open or closed hands.** In Standard and Heroic, the rulebook has players keep their cards private, so everyone holds information the team needs. Experienced groups may choose to play with open hands.
- **Roles.** The rules deal roles at random. Letting players choose is a house rule.

## Free extras from Z-Man Games

The [Pandemic page](https://www.zmangames.com/game/pandemic/) on Z-Man's site has free downloads beyond the rulebook:

- **Official solo rules.** You lead three roles who share one hand. [Lesson 8](/academy/pandemic/pandemic-solo-and-challenges/) walks through them.
- **Two scenarios.** "Isolation," a four-player scenario by Matt Leacock, locks down four cities and gives each player a set role and starting cards. "Government Shutdown" is the second.
- **Ten puzzles.** Each sets up a position for you to solve, with the solution on a later page. Some use roles from the expansions.

## The three expansions

All three need the base game.

**[On the Brink](https://www.zmangames.com/game/pandemic-on-the-brink/)** adds new roles and Event cards, room for a fifth player, and three optional challenges: a Virulent Strain that makes one disease more dangerous, a Mutation that brings in a fifth disease, and a Bio-Terrorist, a player who secretly works against the team. It is the natural first expansion. <!-- AFFILIATE: Pandemic: On the Brink -->

**[In the Lab](https://www.zmangames.com/game/pandemic-in-the-lab/)** adds a lab board where you collect disease samples and sequence them before you can test a cure. It needs both the base game and On the Brink. <!-- AFFILIATE: Pandemic: In the Lab -->

**[State of Emergency](https://www.zmangames.com/game/pandemic-state-of-emergency/)** adds three challenges: the Hinterlands, where diseases jump from animals to cities; a Superbug that cannot be treated normally; and Emergency Events that restrict movement or shrink hand size. It also adds quarantine tokens: spend an action to shield a city from its next two cube placements. <!-- AFFILIATE: Pandemic: State of Emergency -->

## Pandemic Legacy

**[Pandemic Legacy: Season 1](https://www.zmangames.com/game/pandemic-legacy-season-1/)** turns the game into a campaign. You play through an in-game year, starting in January, and your decisions carry over: characters gain scars, cities panic, diseases mutate, and sealed packets add new rules as the story moves on. It plays 2 to 4, about an hour a game, with Z-Man listing ages 13 and up. <!-- AFFILIATE: Pandemic Legacy: Season 1 -->

**[Season 2](https://www.zmangames.com/game/pandemic-legacy-season-2/)** is a standalone follow-up set in a changed world. **[Season 0](https://www.zmangames.com/game/pandemic-legacy-season-0/)** is a prequel set in 1962, with a Cold War spy story. Each is its own complete campaign. <!-- AFFILIATE: Pandemic Legacy: Season 2 and Season 0 -->

Legacy works best with the same group every session, once the base game feels comfortable. Our [legacy games explainer](/articles/legacy-board-games-explained/) covers how to keep a campaign group together.

## Standalone spin-offs

These play on their own, without the base game.

- **[Pandemic: Iberia](https://www.zmangames.com/game/pandemic-iberia/)** moves the game to the Iberian Peninsula in the mid-1800s, with railroads to build and water to purify. It plays 2 to 5.
- **[Pandemic: Fall of Rome](https://www.zmangames.com/game/pandemic-fall-of-rome/)** swaps diseases for barbarian invasions in 5th-century Rome, for 1 to 5 players. Z-Man's site listed it as currently unavailable in October 2026.
- **[Pandemic: The Cure](https://www.zmangames.com/game/pandemic-the-cure/)** is a dice version, about 30 minutes, for 2 to 5.
- **[Hot Zone: North America](https://www.zmangames.com/game/pandemic-hot-zone-north-america/)** and **[Hot Zone: Europe](https://www.zmangames.com/game/pandemic-hot-zone-europe/)** are smaller, 30-minute versions with three diseases. Z-Man's site listed both as currently unavailable in October 2026.

## How to choose

- **Your group loves the base game:** raise the Epidemics, close the hands, then try the free puzzles and scenarios.
- **You want more variety:** On the Brink.
- **You want a story:** Pandemic Legacy: Season 1.
- **You want shorter nights:** The Cure or a Hot Zone box.
