Pandemic plays 2 to 4 players out of the box, and the On the Brink expansion raises that to 5. Z-Man also posts free official solo rules, and all three Pandemic Legacy seasons play 2 to 4.

[Pandemic](https://link.amazon/B0eHP5GCg) is a family of games, not just one box. There are expansions, Legacy campaigns, standalone versions, and free extras on the publisher's site. That is a lot to choose from. The good news: the base game has plenty of room to grow before you spend anything.

## How many players

The base game plays 2 to 4. The starting hand is the main setup change.

| Players | What changes |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Free official solo rules: you lead three roles who share one hand. |
| 2 | 4-card starting hands and the most turns each, about 13 at Standard. Only two roles are in play, so each player covers more of the map. |
| 3 | 3-card hands. A good balance of turns and roles. |
| 4 | 2-card hands and four roles, but only six or seven turns each at Standard. More powers, more conversation, and more risk that one voice takes over. |
| 5 | Needs On the Brink, which Z-Man lists at 2 to 5 players. |

At any count, the Player deck is almost the same size, so the team gets about the same number of turns in total. More players means those turns are spread thinner.

## The difficulty dials in the box

You have three settings before you buy anything:

- **Epidemics.** 4 for Introductory, 5 for Standard, 6 for Heroic. The rulebook suggests moving to 6 once you have mastered Standard.
- **Open or closed hands.** In Standard and Heroic, the rulebook has players keep their cards private, so everyone holds information the team needs. Experienced groups may choose to play with open hands.
- **Roles.** The rules deal roles at random. Letting players choose is a house rule.

## Free extras from Z-Man Games

The [Pandemic page](https://www.zmangames.com/game/pandemic/) on Z-Man's site has free downloads beyond the rulebook:

- **Official solo rules.** You lead three roles who share one hand. The [solo and challenges lesson](/academy/pandemic/pandemic-solo-and-challenges/) walks through them.
- **Two scenarios.** "Isolation," a four-player scenario by Matt Leacock, locks down four cities and gives each player a set role and starting cards. "Government Shutdown" is the second.
- **Ten puzzles.** Each sets up a position for you to solve, with the solution on a later page. Some use roles from the expansions.

## The three expansions

All three need the base game.

**[On the Brink](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00CAG5LJ2?tag=cubecommonw0a-20)** adds new roles and Event cards, room for a fifth player, and three optional challenges: a Virulent Strain that makes one disease more dangerous, a Mutation that brings in a fifth disease, and a Bio-Terrorist, a player who secretly works against the team. It is the natural first expansion.

**[In the Lab](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00CE3IA44?tag=cubecommonw0a-20)** adds a lab board where you collect disease samples and sequence them before you can test a cure. It needs both the base game and On the Brink.

**[State of Emergency](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00SDUQ3LE?tag=cubecommonw0a-20)** adds three challenges: the Hinterlands, where diseases jump from animals to cities; a Superbug that cannot be treated normally; and Emergency Events that restrict movement or shrink hand size. It also adds quarantine tokens: spend an action to shield a city from its next two cube placements.

## Pandemic Legacy

**[Pandemic Legacy: Season 1](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00TQ0DXR2?tag=cubecommonw0a-20)** turns the game into a campaign. You play through an in-game year, starting in January, and your decisions carry over: characters gain scars, cities panic, diseases mutate, and sealed packets add new rules as the story moves on. It plays 2 to 4, about an hour a game, with Z-Man listing ages 13 and up.

**[Season 2](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B071HG6MPR?tag=cubecommonw0a-20)** is a standalone follow-up set in a changed world. **[Season 0](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08F73L5BM?tag=cubecommonw0a-20)** is a prequel set in 1962, with a Cold War spy story. Each is its own complete campaign.

Legacy works best with the same group every session, once the base game feels comfortable. Our [legacy games explainer](/articles/legacy-board-games-explained/) covers how to keep a campaign group together.

## Standalone spin-offs

These play on their own, without the base game.

- **[Pandemic: Iberia](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09XZVFQNY?tag=cubecommonw0a-20)** moves the game to the Iberian Peninsula in the mid-1800s, with railroads to build and water to purify. It plays 2 to 5.
- **[Pandemic: Fall of Rome](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07HJFLL1J?tag=cubecommonw0a-20)** swaps diseases for barbarian invasions in 5th-century Rome, for 1 to 5 players. Z-Man's site listed it as currently unavailable in November 2026.
- **[Pandemic: The Cure](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00M4KGF5S?tag=cubecommonw0a-20)** is a dice version, about 30 minutes, for 2 to 5.
- **[Hot Zone: North America](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B084ZZDSZY?tag=cubecommonw0a-20)** and **[Hot Zone: Europe](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B094DR378C?tag=cubecommonw0a-20)** are smaller, 30-minute versions with three diseases. Z-Man's site listed both as currently unavailable in November 2026.

For younger kids, Forbidden Island is a lighter game by the same designer. Our [Pandemic vs Forbidden Island](/articles/pandemic-vs-forbidden-island/) comparison helps you choose.

## How to choose

- **Your group loves the base game:** raise the Epidemics, close the hands, then try the free puzzles and scenarios.
- **You want more variety:** On the Brink.
- **You want a story:** Pandemic Legacy: Season 1.
- **You want shorter nights:** The Cure or a Hot Zone box.
