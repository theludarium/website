Pandemic has no fixed opening. The cities, roles, and cards change every game. But the first few turns follow a pattern that the rules themselves create, and once you see it, your opening stops being a scramble.

## Read the board before turn one

Take a minute before the first action. Ask four questions as a team:

1. **Where are the three cities with 3 cubes?** Are any of them connected to each other, or to the 2-cube cities? Neighbors with high counts are where chain reactions start.
2. **Which color does the team hold most of?** In the Introductory game every hand is face up. In Standard and Heroic, players keep cards private but can say what they hold. That color is your first cure candidate.
3. **What can each role do here?** A Medic near a hot cluster is worth more than a Medic on the far side of the map.
4. **Where should the next research station go?** Atlanta has the only one.

## The calm before the first Epidemic

Here is the key to the opening. At setup, the 9 infected cities have their cards in the Infection discard pile. Every card left in the Infection deck names a city with no cubes yet.

So until the first Epidemic, each Infect step puts 1 cube on a fresh city, and that card goes to the discard pile too. No ordinary infection can reach a city a second time, so **no outbreak can happen before the first Epidemic.**

Then the Epidemic hits. A new city gets 3 cubes, and the whole discard pile, setup cities included, is shuffled back on top of the Infection deck. Every infection after that comes from those cities until they are used up. Your 3-cube setup cities are suddenly near the top of the deck.

When does that happen? The first Epidemic is somewhere in the top pile of the Player deck. With 4 players at Standard, the deck is 50 cards in five piles of 10, so the first Epidemic arrives within the first five turns. It could be the very first card.

That makes the opening a race against the first Epidemic, not against the cubes on the board.

## Defuse the 3-cube cities

Your top job is to bring each 3-cube setup city down to 2 cubes or fewer before the first Epidemic, whenever you can reach it at a fair price.

- The **Medic** clears a city in one action. Send the Medic to the worst cluster.
- The **Quarantine Specialist** protects her city and every connected city from new cubes. Parked next to a 3-cube city, she guards it without treating.
- Anyone can spend an action or two treating a 3-cube city on their way somewhere useful.

The 2-cube setup cities can wait a little. After the first Epidemic, one more hit takes them to 3, but their card then sits in the discard pile until the next Epidemic. They become the danger at the second Epidemic, which the [midgame lesson](/academy/pandemic/pandemic-midgame-strategy/) covers.

## Pick your first cure early

A cure needs 5 City cards of one color at a research station (4 for the Scientist). That takes planning.

- Name the color the team holds most of, and the player who will collect it. The Scientist is ideal.
- Plan where cards will change hands. Share Knowledge needs both players in the city on the card being passed. The Researcher can give any City card, which makes her a natural hub.
- Hold the plan loosely. Two new cards a turn can change it, and that is fine.

## Place a second research station with purpose

One station in Atlanta is not enough. A second one, near where the cure collector will be or near a cluster of infected cities, gives the team Shuttle Flights: fast travel that costs no card.

The **Operations Expert** builds without discarding, so a station on their first turn is a strong start. If someone else builds, use a City card of a color the team is unlikely to cure soon.

## What each role does in the opening

- **Medic:** head for the biggest 3-cube city.
- **Quarantine Specialist:** park beside a cluster of infected cities.
- **Scientist:** start collecting the team's strongest color.
- **Researcher:** become the card hub near the Scientist.
- **Operations Expert:** build the second station.
- **Dispatcher:** move teammates together so shares happen sooner.
- **Contingency Planner:** treat and move for now. There is nothing to take back yet.

## Hold your Events

Event cards are strongest right after an Epidemic, when the danger is highest. In the opening, keep them. The one easy exception is Government Grant, which builds a research station for you, if you know exactly where you want it.

## A good opening, in plain terms

There is no perfect target, but a strong opening usually looks like this when the first Epidemic arrives:

- No 3-cube setup city left that the team could have reached.
- A named cure color and a named collector.
- A second research station on the board.

If the first Epidemic comes on turn one, none of this happens, and that is part of the game. Adjust and keep going. For deeper play at higher difficulty, our [Pandemic strategy guide](/articles/pandemic-strategy/) builds on this lesson.
