After the first Epidemic, Pandemic changes. The opening was about defusing the setup cities. The midgame is about tempo: every action either buys time or moves the team toward a cure, and you never have enough of either. The teams that win are the ones that know which cities are coming next.

## The discard pile is the future

Every Epidemic ends with **Intensify**: shuffle the Infection discard pile and put it on top of the Infection deck. Until those cards run out, every infection comes from that set. No fresh city gets a cube from an Infect step while they last.

The rules let anyone look through either discard pile at any time. So you always know two things:

- **Cities in the discard pile right now** cannot be infected by an ordinary Infect step until the next Epidemic. Then they go back on top.
- **Cities shuffled back at the last Epidemic** that have not come up again are sitting on top of the deck right now. They come next.

The size of that stack tells you how long it lasts. With 12 cities on top and an infection rate of 2, the stack covers the next six Infect steps, unless another Epidemic arrives first.

A simple habit helps: at each Epidemic, before the shuffle, one player reads the discard pile aloud. Any city on that list that has not yet shown up in the new discard pile is still on top of the deck.

## Three kinds of city

Sort every infected city on the board into one of three groups.

1. **On top of the deck.** Reshuffled at the last Epidemic and not drawn since. A city here with 3 cubes will outbreak when its card comes up. Treat it, or park the Quarantine Specialist on it or next to it.
2. **In the discard pile.** Safe from ordinary infection for now, and first in line after the next Epidemic. A city here with 3 cubes is your next emergency. Bring it down before the next Epidemic.
3. **Deeper in the deck.** Cities whose Infection card has not come up yet this game, so any cubes there came from outbreaks. They are lower risk, but the next Epidemic's Infect step draws the very bottom card. If that city already has cubes, it fills to 3 and outbreaks.

Outbreaks ignore these groups. A city at 3 cubes next to a city about to outbreak is in danger whatever its card is doing.

## Timing the next Epidemic

Each pile of the Player deck holds exactly one Epidemic. Once a pile's Epidemic is drawn, the rest of that pile is safe.

With 4 players at Standard, there are five piles of 10. If the Epidemic was the third card of its pile, the next 7 cards hold no Epidemic. Count the cards drawn, and you know when the calm ends.

When you cross into a new pile, an Epidemic can come on any draw. If one draw takes the last card of one pile and the first of the next, two Epidemics at once are possible. The rulebook covers that case: resolve all three steps, then all three again. The second Epidemic's city ends up alone on top of the Infection deck, so it outbreaks in the next Infect step unless an Event stops it.

Tip: when you build the Player deck, say the pile sizes out loud. Then anyone can track the count.

## Tempo: cures versus cubes

A turn spent treating cities that are not in danger is a turn the Player deck still counts down. A rough guide for each turn:

- **First**, deal with any 3-cube city on top of the deck.
- **Next**, bring down 3-cube cities in the discard pile before the next Epidemic.
- **Everything else** goes toward cures: moving cards, meeting up, building stations.

Cures make treating cheaper too. After a cure, one Treat action clears every cube of that color in a city, and the Medic clears a cured color just by being there.

## Build the card flow

Cures come from cards moving to the right hand at the right place.

- **One collector per color.** Everyone else feeds that player.
- **Plan meetings a turn ahead.** Share Knowledge needs both players in the city on the card.
- **Use the Researcher.** She can give any City card. On your turn, if you share with her, you can take any of her City cards.
- **Use the Dispatcher.** As an action, the Dispatcher can move any pawn to a city with another pawn, if its owner agrees. That turns a three-turn meeting into one action.
- **Watch the collector's hand.** Near 7 cards, cure soon, or the hand limit forces the team to discard cards it needs.

## Event cards in the midgame

- **Resilient Population** removes one card in the Infection discard pile from the game. The rulebook lets you play it between the Infect and Intensify steps of an Epidemic, so the new 3-cube city never goes back on top.
- **One Quiet Night** skips the next Infect step. It is strongest right after an Epidemic.
- **Forecast** lets you look at and reorder the top of the Infection deck, so dangerous cities come later.
- **Airlift** moves any pawn to any city, with permission. Put the Medic or Quarantine Specialist where the danger is.

## A four-question check

At the start of each turn, ask:

1. What is on top of the Infection deck?
2. How many cards until the next Epidemic can come?
3. Who is closest to a cure, and where can they get the card they need?
4. Which color's cube supply is getting low?
