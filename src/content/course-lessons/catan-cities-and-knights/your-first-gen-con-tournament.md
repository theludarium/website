Gen Con 2027 runs August 5 to 8 in Indianapolis. If the pattern of recent years holds, Catan will list a Cities & Knights tournament there, but no 2027 listing exists yet, and we could not confirm one for 2026. So this lesson does not promise an event. It teaches you how to find one, get a seat, and arrive ready.

For the convention as a whole, our [first-timer's guide](/articles/gen-con-first-timer-guide/) covers badges, the halls, and the rhythm of the four days. This lesson stays on the tournament.

## Step 1: a badge comes first

Gen Con runs on event tickets, and you must already hold a badge before you can buy any event ticket. The badge gets you into the convention. The ticket guarantees your seat at a specific event, at a specific time.

Tickets can be bought online, through Gen Con's app, or on site. Many events sell out before the show, so do not plan to walk up.

## Step 2: search the catalog well

The Gen Con event catalog holds thousands of events, and new ones are added after it first opens. Gen Con's own search tips give you the tools:

- **Keyword search** looks at titles and descriptions. Put "Cities & Knights" in quotes to search the exact phrase.
- **Filters** narrow by event type, game system, day, start time, and the company or group hosting. Choose Board Games as the type, and pick Catan from the game system list if it appears there.
- **Search options** let you show or hide sold out events and events that have already happened, and filter by age and experience requirements.
- **Downloads.** Gen Con offers the whole catalog as a spreadsheet, updated every six hours. Searching a spreadsheet for "Knights" is a fast way to catch listings with odd titles.

Look for the word tournament in the title or description. Recent Catan listings have used "Casual Tournament" for Cities & Knights. Catan has also run ticketed Cities & Knights learn-to-play events, which are a different thing. Read the description for format, length, rounds, and any prize or scoring details. If those are missing, note the organizer and ask.

Search again in the weeks after the catalog opens. Events keep arriving.

## Step 3: the wish list and the noon rush

Gen Con processes event registration through a ranked wish list. Our guide to [Gen Con event registration](/articles/gen-con-event-registration-tips/) explains it step by step. For a tournament, three points matter most:

1. **Rank the tournament at the top.** Popular tournaments go first. Catan's 2024 Cities & Knights tournament was listed as sold out in Catan's own guide before the show.
2. **Mind overlaps.** Gen Con will not let you hold tickets for two events at the same time. A tournament can run for hours, so leave the whole block clear, plus time to get there.
3. **Get your friend in too.** Most events let you get up to two tickets, so one of you can register a pair.

## Step 4: generic tickets, the backup plan

Generic tickets are Gen Con's standby system. They can be used in place of an event ticket but do not guarantee a seat. If an event you want is sold out, show up about 10 minutes early with enough generics to cover the event's cost. If a ticket holder does not appear, or there is extra space, you may get in. For the sold out 2024 tournament, Catan's guide said empty seats from cancellations or no-shows might be filled by other ticket holders, and to ask at the booth.

If your plans change, Gen Con lets you return event tickets to system credit before registration closes, or on site up to 30 minutes before the event.

## What to bring

- **Your badge,** and your ticket if the event uses paper tickets. Some events are e-ticketed and scan your badge instead.
- **A pen and a small notebook,** with your scoring questions from the [scoring lesson](/academy/catan-cities-and-knights/cities-and-knights-tournament-scoring/) already written on the inside cover.
- **The rulebook on your phone,** in the 6th edition. Ask which edition the event uses.
- **Water and a snack.** Cities & Knights runs long, and the [clock lesson](/academy/catan-cities-and-knights/cities-and-knights-under-the-clock/) is easier on a full stomach.

The [Gen Con packing list](/articles/gen-con-packing-list/) covers the rest of the day.

## At the table

Arrive early, find the organizer, and ask your questions before play starts: scoring, time limits, house rules, and whether there is a final. Then play the way the base course's [first qualifier lesson](/academy/catan/your-first-qualifier/) suggests: go to learn, check every score before you sign it, and never rush a slow player yourself.
