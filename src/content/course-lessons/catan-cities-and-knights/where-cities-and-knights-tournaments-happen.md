Welcome to Level 5, the championship path. Base Catan has an official ladder from local qualifiers to a World Championship, and the base course walks it in [How Catan Tournaments Work](/academy/catan/how-catan-tournaments-work/). Cities & Knights does not. Catan's championship pages describe national and international tournaments for the base game, and say nothing about a Cities & Knights circuit.

What it has instead is a set of events at large conventions and festivals, several of them listed on Catan's own event pages. This lesson covers the ones we could confirm from their own pages, and then how to find the next one yourself.

## Gen Con, Indianapolis

Gen Con is a four day gaming convention with thousands of ticketed events, and Catan has listed a Cities & Knights tournament there in several recent years.

- **2022:** Catan's Gen Con guide invited competitive players to its tournament area in Hall E for Cities & Knights and Rivals for Catan tournaments.
- **2024:** Catan's guide listed a Cities & Knights Tournament on Thursday, August 1, at 10 a.m., already sold out, with a note that empty seats from cancellations or no-shows might be filled by other ticket holders.
- **2025:** Catan's event page for Gen Con, July 31 to August 3, listed a "CATAN: Cities & Knights Casual Tournament" in Hall D/E, Organized & Casual Play, next to the National Qualifier and a Rivals for Catan tournament. It also listed a ticketed Cities & Knights learn-to-play event.

We could not confirm a 2026 Cities & Knights tournament from Catan's pages, and the 2027 catalog is not out yet. Gen Con 2027 runs August 5 to 8. When the catalog opens, search it yourself; the next lesson shows how.

Gen Con is held in Indianapolis, the city where The Ludarium is building its first lounge. If you are local, our [Gen Con tips for locals](/articles/gen-con-tips-for-locals/) will help.

## UK Games Expo

Catan's page for UK Games Expo 2024 listed a "CATAN: Cities & Knights Tournament," pitched as a more casual experience with the fan favorite expansion, alongside a UK National Championship qualifier and the championship itself. The page did not give a format or scoring. Check the UK Games Expo events listings and Catan's events pages each spring for the current year.

## Mind Sports Olympiad, UK

The Mind Sports Olympiad is a UK festival of many games, from draughts to modern board games. Its 2026 Cities & Knights event ran on August 24, 2026, from 10:00 to 18:00 UK time, with:

- Four players per table.
- Two rounds, then a final for qualifiers in the evening session.
- Victory points scored in each game, up to 13, counted toward your tournament score, with two bonus points for each table winner. The most tournament points over the event won.
- A house rule re-rolling 7s until every player had taken two turns, to keep games on schedule.

It is the clearest published Cities & Knights tournament format we found, and the [scoring lesson](/academy/catan-cities-and-knights/cities-and-knights-tournament-scoring/) uses it as its worked example.

## Online

Catan's page on Board Game Arena says Catan and Cities & Knights are both playable there, and lists access to tournaments as a Premium perk. Fan platforms and community leagues also run Cities & Knights games. Online events are a fine way to get tournament reps between conventions. Check each platform's current rules against the 6th edition, as the [practice plan](/academy/catan-cities-and-knights/cities-and-knights-practice-plan/) explains.

## Local events

Game stores and clubs run their own Cities & Knights nights and small tournaments. They rarely make the news, so ask at your local store, and look at the event calendars of nearby conventions. Our [board game night near me guide](/articles/board-game-night-near-me/) covers how to find a local scene. In Indianapolis, the [lounge list](/lounges/indianapolis/) gets first word on local learn-to-play nights as The Ludarium grows.

## How to find this year's events

1. **Catan's events pages.** Catan posts a page for most conventions it attends. Search catan.com for the convention name and year.
2. **Convention catalogs.** Search for "Cities & Knights" in quotes, and filter by board games.
3. **Festival schedules.** The Mind Sports Olympiad and UK Games Expo publish their programs ahead of each event.
4. **Write it down.** For each event: date, registration, format, scoring, and house rules. Anything missing becomes a question for the organizer.
