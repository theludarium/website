Dune: Imperium has a real competitive scene in the United States. It has an event at the World Boardgaming Championships, and Dire Wolf runs its own North American Championship at Gen Con in Indianapolis. You do not need to win either to enjoy them. But knowing how they run changes how you look at your games at home.

## How tournament play differs from home

Most tournament games share a few habits:

- **Full tables.** WBC Heats seat three or four players. Four-player games give out the third Conflict reward and start everyone at 1 Victory Point, so they reward reading the whole table.
- **A time limit.** Rounds run for hours, not all evening. Fast decisions matter.
- **A fixed set of boxes.** Each event names which game and expansions it uses, and they are not the same from event to event.

Before any event, read its rules and practice with exactly those boxes.

## The World Boardgaming Championships

The [World Boardgaming Championships](https://www.boardgamers.org/) (WBC) is a yearly tournament convention at Seven Springs Mountain Resort in Pennsylvania, where many games each crown a champion. WBC 2026 ran from July 24 to August 1, and Dune Imperium is on its [2026 winners list](https://www.boardgamers.org/wbc_results.html).

From the [2026 event preview](https://www.boardgamers.org/wbc26/previews/DNI.html):

- **Format:** three Heats, then a Semifinal and a Final.
- **Tables:** the Heats are three and four player games.
- **Time:** Heats and the Semifinal are three-hour rounds; the Final runs four hours.
- **Game:** the original Dune: Imperium with the Rise of Ix expansion, plus a Family Atomics rule (a once-per-game refresh of the Imperium Row, covered in the [Rise of Ix lesson](/academy/dune-imperium/dune-imperium-rise-of-ix-epic-mode/)). A Heat table may play the base game alone only if everyone at it agrees, or Rise of Ix is not available.
- **Advancing:** Heat finishes earn points, and the top 16 move on to the Semifinal. Semifinal winners play the Final in Rise of Ix's Epic mode, which plays to 12 points.
- **Class:** marked as welcoming beginning players.

That beginner welcome makes it one of the friendliest ways to try a real tournament. WBC 2027 runs July 24 to August 1, 2027, according to the [WBC page](https://www.boardgamers.org/wbcindex.html).

## Dire Wolf's North American Championship

Dire Wolf has run a Dune: Imperium North American Championship at Gen Con in Indianapolis since 2022, when [Karlis Povisils won the first one](https://news.direwolfdigital.com/an-interview-with-karlis-povisils-2022-dune-imperium-na-champion). The [third annual championship in 2024](https://news.direwolfdigital.com/gen-con-2024-recap) went to Brandon Manning.

In 2025 Dire Wolf billed it as the Dune: Imperium Uprising North American Championship. Its [Gen Con 2025 recap](https://news.direwolfdigital.com/dire-wolf-at-gen-con-25-recap/) reports that 256 players entered and played through four rounds, and Michael Young took the title.

The [2026 championship](https://news.direwolfdigital.com/join-the-conspiracy-in-the-dune-imperium-championship-tournament-at-gen-con/) used open qualifier games at Gen Con, played in Uprising. The winner of each qualifier game moved on to a Top 64 played with Uprising and Bloodlines. The Top 16 and the final four then added a preview of the upcoming Conspiracy expansion.

## Online play

[Dune: Imperium Digital](https://www.direwolfdigital.com/dune-imperium-digital/) is Dire Wolf's own version on Steam, mobile, and Xbox. You can play online against other people, or against AI and House Hagal. Dire Wolf announced on June 9, 2026 that [Uprising had arrived](https://news.direwolfdigital.com/uprising-digital-is-now-available/) on Steam and mobile, with Xbox to follow. <!-- AFFILIATE: Dune: Imperium Digital -->

Digital play is the fastest way to get many games in. It also does the bookkeeping, so you can watch your decisions instead of your tokens.

## Finding local play

You do not need a tournament to find good opponents.

- **Your local game store.** Many stores carry Dune: Imperium and run open game nights where heavier games get played. Ask the staff.
- **BoardGameGeek and Discord.** Dire Wolf links to the game's BoardGameGeek forums and its own Discord server from the [Dune: Imperium page](https://www.direwolfdigital.com/dune-imperium/). Both are good places to ask about nearby groups.
- **Conventions.** Dune: Imperium shows up on convention schedules, and some sessions teach the rules at the table.
- **Start your own.** Our guide on [how to start a play group](/articles/how-to-start-a-play-group/) walks through it.

If you are near Indianapolis, join the [Indianapolis list](/lounges/indianapolis/) for first word on local learn-to-play nights as The Ludarium grows.

## How to practice like a competitor

- **Play four-player games.** That is the table size most events use, and the hardest to read.
- **Play the right boxes.** In 2026 that meant the original plus Rise of Ix for WBC, and Uprising plus Bloodlines for Gen Con.
- **Watch the clock.** A three-hour round is plenty for a four-player game at a steady pace, and not enough for a slow one.
- **Review your losses.** Which Conflict did you overpay for? Which Alliance did you let go, and was it worth it?

That is the map. The last lesson turns it into a plan: six weeks of practice aimed at one of these events.
