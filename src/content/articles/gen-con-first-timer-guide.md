If this is your first time at Gen Con, the most useful thing to know is that it is two conventions at once. One is a four-day schedule of ticketed games you plan months in advance. The other is a city-sized hallway where you wander, demo, and bump into people. First-timers who understand that split have a good weekend. First-timers who treat it as one or the other burn out or feel they missed it. This guide covers the dates, the badge system, the event tickets, and the few decisions that matter most.

Dates and prices below were checked on gencon.com and in coverage of the 2026 show in October 2026. Confirm the current year before you buy anything.

## When and where is Gen Con?

Gen Con is held in downtown Indianapolis every summer. The 2026 show ran July 30 to August 2 and [sold out of badges](https://www.wfyi.org/wfyi-news/2026-07-30/a-sold-out-gen-con-returns-to-indianapolis-after-record-breaking-year). The next show is [August 5 to 8, 2027](https://www.gencon.com/), per gencon.com.

The convention is based at the Indiana Convention Center and Lucas Oil Stadium, which connect by skywalk, and spills into the surrounding downtown hotels and venues. Events also run at places like Victory Field and the Indiana Repertory Theatre. Attendance was [nearly 72,000 in 2025](https://gamingtrend.com/news/gen-con-2025-breaks-attendance-records-again/) and [topped 74,000 in 2026](https://boardgamewire.com/index.php/2026/08/05/gen-con-tops-74000-attendees-as-record-breaking-show-preps-for-much-needed-venue-hotel-expansion/), which makes it the largest tabletop convention in North America and roughly the size of a small city dropped on top of a mid-size one.

## How do Gen Con badges work?

A badge gets you into the convention. It is the first thing you buy, and in a sold-out year it is the thing you cannot buy late.

- **4-Day badge.** Thursday through Sunday. The default for anyone staying the weekend.
- **Single-day badges.** Priced by day, with Saturday highest and Sunday lowest.
- **Trade Day.** A Wednesday badge for industry and education professionals, not for first-timers.
- **Kids.** Children 10 and under attend on a free Child Wristband registered at purchase; children 11 and older need a badge. A wristband covers kid activities; joining non-kids events requires a badge. ([Badge information](https://www.gencon.com/gen-con-indy/your_badge))

For 2026, badge prices were [$164 for the 4-Day, $83 for Thursday or Friday, $112 for Saturday, and $41 for Sunday](https://gamingtrend.com/news/gen-con-announces-2026-registration-and-badge-prices/), before Marion County's 10 percent admissions tax, which gencon.com notes applies to every badge type. Prices for 2027 had not been posted when we checked.

Badges went on sale in early February for the 2026 show, with hotel housing opening about two weeks later and event registration in mid-May. The sequence matters: badge, then hotel, then events.

## How do event tickets and generic tickets work?

This is the part that confuses every first-timer, so here it is in plain terms.

**Events** are the scheduled, seated things: a four-hour D&D adventure, a Catan tournament, a seminar, a learn-to-play of a new release. Each has a ticket, usually a few dollars, sometimes free. The catalog has tens of thousands of sessions.

**You must own a badge before you can buy event tickets**, per the [Gen Con Help Center](https://gencon.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/36967172018580-How-to-get-Event-Tickets). Tickets can be bought online in advance or on site, but popular events sell out, so the serious planning happens on event registration day.

**Generic tickets** cost $2 each and are the convention's loose change. They do not guarantee a seat. You show up to an event about ten minutes early with enough generics to cover its price, and if there are no-shows or open seats, you get in. Some events accept only generics. Unused generics are refunded as system credit toward a future Gen Con if returned to Customer Service before the show closes at 4 p.m. Sunday. ([What are Generic Tickets?](https://gencon.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/36966863600404-What-are-Generic-Tickets))

Before registration opens, the catalog is published and you build a wish list of events you want, up to a cap of several dozen. When registration opens, the system processes requests and puts what it can get into your cart, and you check out quickly before the hold lapses. We have a full guide to [Gen Con event registration](/articles/gen-con-event-registration-tips/) if you want to go deep.

## What should a first-timer actually schedule?

Less than you think. Here is a frame that works.

| Day | Scheduled events | Open time |
|---|---|---|
| Thursday | One, in the afternoon | Morning in the Exhibit Hall before it is crowded |
| Friday | Two, with a gap | Evening open gaming |
| Saturday | One, early | The afternoon for the biggest crowds, parade, and wandering |
| Sunday | None | Exhibit Hall deals, then home |

Four to six ticketed events across four days is plenty. Leave half of every day open. The Exhibit Hall alone is a full day if you walk it properly, and the best games you play will be the ones a stranger teaches you at an open table at eleven at night.

A few specifics for the first visit:

1. **Book one event that is a sure thing.** A learn-to-play of a game you already want, or an RPG one-shot with a strong description. It anchors the day.
2. **Walk the Exhibit Hall on Thursday morning.** It is the least crowded it will be all weekend.
3. **Carry generics.** Ten dollars of them turns a dead hour into a game.
4. **Find the open gaming halls.** Free, badge-only, and running late into the night. Bring a game or borrow one from the library.

## What do first-timers get wrong?

The same five things every year.

- **Overbooking.** Back-to-back events in buildings fifteen minutes apart, with no lunch. See our [Gen Con survival guide](/articles/gen-con-survival-guide/) for the physical side.
- **Skipping the hotel block.** Downtown rooms in the housing block go within hours when housing opens. Staying far out means driving in and parking, which is its own event.
- **Buying on day one.** The thing you want on Thursday will still be there Sunday, often cheaper, and you will not have carried it for three days.
- **Not talking to anyone.** The room is full of people who want to teach you their favorite game. Let them.
- **Treating Sunday as a throwaway.** It is the quietest day, the best for a last demo, and the cheapest badge.

## FAQ

### When is Gen Con 2027?

August 5 to 8, 2027, in downtown Indianapolis, according to gencon.com.

### Do I need a badge to buy Gen Con event tickets?

Yes. The Help Center states you must already have a badge before purchasing event tickets.

### What are Gen Con generic tickets?

Two-dollar tickets that can be used in place of an event ticket when seats are open. They do not guarantee entry, and unused ones are refunded as credit toward a future show.

### Can kids go to Gen Con?

Yes. Children 10 and under get a free Child Wristband; 11 and older need a badge.

## Your first badge

Plan a little, wander a lot, and say yes when someone offers to teach you a game. The game is the reason. The people are the point.
