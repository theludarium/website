The Rebirth board game is a tile-laying game by Reiner Knizia for 2 to 4 players, in which you take turns placing one hex tile on a shared map of Scotland and score farms, settlements, castles, and secret missions. Turns take seconds, the rules fit on a player aid, and the board gets tighter and meaner with every tile. In July 2026 it won the Kennerspiel des Jahres, the German award for the best game for experienced players.

**The quick answer:** On your turn you place the one tile in your hand, score it, and draw a new one. Farms score for the size of the group you join. Settlements score when they fill up, with the most influence winning the biggest share. Castles go to whoever surrounds them best. Cathedrals hand out secret missions. Most points after everyone places every tile wins. A game runs about 45 to 60 minutes.

## What is the Rebirth board game?

[Rebirth](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DH5CMRF9?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) is a competitive tile-placement and area-majority game designed by Reiner Knizia and first published in 2024 by Mighty Boards, a studio in Malta. Frosted Games publishes the German edition. Its art shows a lush, hopeful future in which people rebuild the landscape with farms, villages, and old castles.

The box holds two games on a double-sided board. **Scotland** is the standard game, with private missions. **Ireland** is the advanced side, with public missions, round towers, and bigger settlements.

## Why did Rebirth win the Kennerspiel des Jahres 2026?

The Kennerspiel des Jahres is the sister prize to the Spiel des Jahres, created in 2011 for games a step more demanding than family fare. In 2026 Rebirth beat the other two nominees, Boss Fighters QR and Moon Colony Bloodbath.

The jury's verdict was that Rebirth's simple core principle opens up a multitude of tactical possibilities. In plain words: you are building something, not just blocking, and every tile you place changes what your rivals can do.

The win made history. With Rebirth, Knizia became the first designer to win all three awards from the Spiel des Jahres jury: the Spiel des Jahres (Keltis, 2008), the Kinderspiel des Jahres (Whoowasit?, 2008), and now the Kennerspiel. It was also the second Kennerspiel in a row for Frosted Games, after Endeavor: Deep Sea in 2025. If Knizia is new to you, our guide to [Reiner Knizia games](/articles/reiner-knizia-games/) explains who he is and where to start.

The same night, the main Spiel des Jahres went to a party game from Indonesia; our guide on [how to play JinxO](/articles/how-to-play-jinxo/) covers that one.

## What comes in the box?

- A double-sided board: Scotland on one side, Ireland on the other.
- 32 mission cards and 4 player aids.
- For each of four player colors: 36 tiles, 12 castles, 8 cathedral markers, scoring achievement markers, and a score counter.

Your 36 tiles are a mix of **food farms**, **energy farms**, and **settlement tiles**. Settlement tiles show 1 to 4 house icons; that number is the tile's **influence**.

## How do you set up Rebirth?

1. Lay the board Scotland side up and shuffle the mission cards into a face-down deck.
2. Each player takes their color's castles, cathedral markers, and a scoring achievement, and sets their score counter at zero.
3. Each player shuffles their tiles face down, sets two aside without looking at them, and keeps the rest as a face-down supply.
4. Each player draws one tile into their hand.
5. The rulebook says the player who most recently visited Scotland goes first. Play passes clockwise.

### Player count changes

- **4 players:** use all 36 tiles each.
- **3 players:** each player removes 3 food farms, 3 energy farms, and one settlement tile each of influence 2, 3, and 4, leaving 27 tiles.
- **2 players:** tiles from an unused color go face down on the board's marked yellow spaces to fill the map.

## How to play Rebirth: a turn, step by step

Every turn has three steps: place, score, draw.

### Step 1: Place your tile

Put the tile from your hand on an empty space it is allowed to go:

- **Food farm tiles** go on food farm spaces or blank spaces.
- **Energy farm tiles** go on energy farm spaces or blank spaces.
- **Settlement tiles** go only on settlement spaces.
- Nothing goes on castle, cathedral, or water spaces.

If no legal food or energy space is left, a food farm can go on an energy space and the reverse.

### Step 2: Score it right away

What you score depends on what you placed and where. The details are in the next section.

### Step 3: Draw a new tile

Draw one tile from your supply. That is your next turn's tile. Since you only ever hold one tile, you always know exactly what you are placing, but never what comes after.

## How does scoring work in Rebirth?

### Farms: grow your groups

When you place a farm, you score 1 point for every tile in the group of your own same-type farms that it joins, including the new tile. A third connected food farm scores 3, a fourth scores 4, and so on. Food and energy farms never combine, and your farms never combine with another player's.

This is why groups snowball. A Punchboard reviewer added up a group of eight connected energy farms at 36 points: 1, then 2, then 3, all the way to 8.

### Settlements: win the majority

Settlements are outlined groups of 1, 2, or 3 settlement spaces. A settlement scores only when its last space is filled. Each player's influence in it is the total house icons on their tiles there.

| Settlement size | When it scores | Payout |
|---|---|---|
| Small (1 space) | When you place the tile | Points equal to the tile's influence |
| Medium (2 spaces) | When it fills | Most influence 5, other player 3. One player with both spaces scores 8 |
| Large (3 spaces) | When it fills | Most influence 8, second 5. One player with all three scores 13 |

Ties matter. The player who completes a settlement loses any tie, so filling the last space with a weak tile can hand your rival the bigger payout. Some settlement spaces are **ports**: placing a settlement tile on a port scores 1 point immediately, plus the settlement's payout if it completes it.

### Castles: surround them

Castle spaces sit around the map. The first player to place a tile next to an empty castle puts one of their castles on it. After that, whoever has the most tiles adjacent to it controls it. Pass the current owner's count and you take it over, returning their castle to them.

Ties go to the player with more adjacent farm tiles. If that is still tied, the player who just placed loses. Each castle you control at the end of the game is worth 5 points, and castles can change hands many times before then.

### Cathedrals: draw secret missions

The first time you place a tile next to a cathedral space, you put one of your cathedral markers there and draw a mission card in secret. You can draw only once per cathedral, so with eight cathedrals you can collect up to eight missions.

Missions are secret goals that score at the end if you meet their conditions. Some cards are cathedral bonuses that are simply worth 3 points.

## How does a game of Rebirth end?

The game ends when every player has placed all of their tiles. Then, in Scotland:

1. **Incomplete settlements** score as though each tile in them were a small settlement, so each tile is worth its own influence.
2. **Castles** score 5 points each for their current owner.
3. **Missions** are revealed and scored.

Most points wins. If players tie, whoever controls Edinburgh Castle wins; if that does not settle it, the controller of Stirling Castle wins.

## What changes on the Ireland side?

Ireland is the advanced game. Play the Scotland side first; the rulebook presents it as the standard game. When you flip the board:

- **Public missions.** Eight missions are dealt face up. The first player to complete each one scores its higher value, and later players score the lower value.
- **Round towers.** Each tower has a bonus tile. Being next to one can trigger a one-time power, such as placing a second tile, drawing three tiles and keeping one, or drawing a private mission.
- **Sprawling settlements.** Four-space settlements that pay 12, 8, and 5 to the top three players, or 25 if one player owns all four tiles.
- **Offshore farms** replace ports, and you set aside four hidden tiles at the start instead of two.

Reviews are split on Ireland. Meeple Mountain found the public missions added little; Roll to Review found it more about blocking and less relaxed. Plenty of groups stay in Scotland happily.

## Rebirth strategy: five tips for your first games

### 1. Start farm groups early

Farm points grow with group size, so the first few tiles of a group are an investment. Start one or two groups early and keep feeding them, rather than scattering farms across the map.

### 2. Do not complete a settlement you will lose

Completing a settlement makes you lose ties. Before you fill the last space, count influence. If your tile will not win the top payout, consider placing elsewhere and leaving the settlement for someone else to finish.

### 3. Touch cathedrals early

Each cathedral gives you one mission the first time you place next to it. Missions shape your whole game, so touching several cathedrals in the first third of the game gives you more goals to steer toward.

### 4. Defend castles you care about

A castle is worth 5 points only if you still hold it at the end. Adding a second or third tile next to your best castles makes them expensive to steal. Remember that farm tiles break castle ties.

### 5. Remember everyone has the same tiles

All players start with an identical mix. As the game goes on, you can estimate what kinds of tiles your rivals still have left (minus the two each player set aside unseen), which tells you who can still contest a settlement or castle.

## Who is Rebirth for?

**A great fit if:**

- You like [Carcassonne](/academy/carcassonne/how-to-play-carcassonne/) or [Cascadia](/academy/cascadia/) and want something with more competition for the same spaces.
- Your group likes short turns. Each turn is one tile, so the game keeps moving even with four players.
- You want a game that teaches quickly but rewards repeat plays.

**Maybe not if:**

- Your group dislikes direct competition. Settlements and castles are fought over, and losing a castle late stings.
- You want a big engine or a long arc. Meeple Mountain's reviewer called it an excellent introduction to this style rather than a deep, life-changing game.

On weight, Rebirth sits in the light-to-middle range: more decisions than a family game, far fewer rules than most heavy strategy games. Our guide to [board game weight](/articles/board-game-weight-explained/) explains what that means at the table.

## Which edition of Rebirth should you look for in the US?

Mighty Boards publishes Rebirth in English. After the first print run sold out, it moved in 2026 to what it calls the International English Edition, a more affordable package with a smaller box, smaller castles, and 12 castles per color instead of 14. The double-sided Scotland and Ireland board is still included. Retailer listings for this edition have used several names, including "2nd Edition," so check the product details if you see more than one version.

Some retailer listings have shown it as sold out or on preorder, so availability in US stores may come and go. We have not seen an official US date for further reprints. A local game store can usually tell you when its distributor expects more.

## FAQ

### How many players is Rebirth?

Rebirth plays 2 to 4. The 3-player game removes a few tiles per player, and the 2-player game fills part of the map with face-down tiles from an unused color so the board stays tight.

### How long does Rebirth take to play?

About 45 to 60 minutes. Experienced groups often finish in under an hour, since each turn is a single tile placement.

### Is Rebirth hard to learn?

No. The turn is three steps: place, score, draw. Most of the teach is explaining the four scoring types. Reviewers call the rules clean and quick to pick up, while noting that every turn has plenty to think about.

### Who won the Kennerspiel des Jahres 2026?

Rebirth by Reiner Knizia, published in German by Frosted Games and originally by Mighty Boards. The other nominees were Boss Fighters QR and Moon Colony Bloodbath. See our list of [every Spiel des Jahres and Kennerspiel winner](/articles/spiel-des-jahres-winners/) for the full history.

### Should I play the Scotland or Ireland side first?

Scotland. It is the standard game, with private missions and simpler settlements. Move to Ireland once everyone knows the scoring and wants more interaction.
