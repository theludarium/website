Secrets of Strixhaven <!-- AFFILIATE --> released on April 24, 2026, and the Secrets of Strixhaven cube story is the prepare mechanic: creatures that carry a famous spell, including Lightning Bolt, Swords to Plowshares, Demonic Tutor, Ancestral Recall and Regrowth. That premise is catnip for cube owners, and the answer is more measured than the spoiler-season excitement. A few of these creatures are real. One white removal spell is the best card in the set for cube. The dragons are for Commander. Calls below follow [the slot method](/articles/how-to-evaluate-new-cards-for-cube/).

Set facts from the [Wizards collecting article](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/collecting-secrets-of-strixhaven): tabletop release April 24, 2026, main set code SOS, with a separate 65-card Mystical Archive sheet (SOA) and ten Special Guests; MTGGoldfish's set index counts 276 cards in the main set. Card text is paraphrased from Card Kingdom and mtg.wtf listings.

## What does the set do, in one paragraph?

**Prepare** returns from its introduction earlier in the year: a prepared creature lets you cast one copy of its attached spell, which unprepares it; some creatures re-prepare under a condition. **Lessons** are back as expensive sorceries with "paradigm." **Infusion** rewards gaining life in a turn. **Opus** rewards spending five or more mana on an instant or sorcery. **Increment** grows creatures when you overspend. For cube, prepare is the only mechanic that matters, because it puts a known-good spell on a body you can evaluate.

## Is Erode the best card in the set?

For cube, yes. **Erode** ({W}, instant: destroy target creature or planeswalker; its controller may search for a basic land and put it onto the battlefield tapped). **Slot:** white / 1 / removal. **Competes with:** Swords to Plowshares, Path to Exile, Condemn. **Call: keep** at every tier. It is Path to Exile that destroys instead of exiling, hits planeswalkers, and gives a tapped land instead of an untapped one. In a format where every white deck wants four one-mana answers and only has two, this is the third. [Card Kingdom's price](https://www.cardkingdom.com/mtg/secrets-of-strixhaven/erode) of $13.99 as of October 17, 2026 (MTGGoldfish's TCGplayer-based figure was lower, around $6) reflects Standard demand, not cube demand, so proxy it for testing if you must.

## Which Emeritus creatures travel to cube?

The mythic cycle of five "Emeritus" creatures each carries a reserved-list-adjacent spell. Here is each one against its slot.

| Card | Cost, body | Spell attached | Re-prepare condition | Call |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Emeritus of Conflict | {1}{R}, 2/2 first strike | Lightning Bolt ({R}) | Cast your third spell in a turn | Keep (Legacy, Modern); test Vintage |
| Emeritus of Truce | {1}{W}{W}, 3/3 | Swords to Plowshares ({W}) | Opponent controls more creatures when it enters | Test |
| Emeritus of Woe | {3}{B}, 5/4 | Demonic Tutor ({1}{B}) | Two creatures died this turn | Test |
| Emeritus of Ideation | {3}{U}{U}, 5/5 flying ward 2 | Ancestral Recall ({U}) | Exile eight cards from graveyard on attack | Test |
| Emeritus of Abundance | {2}{G}, 3/4 vigilance | Regrowth ({1}{G}) | Control eight lands on attack | Pass |

Read the table carefully, because the conditions matter more than the spells. Emeritus of Conflict does not enter prepared; you have to cast three spells in a turn to turn on the Bolt. In a red spells deck that happens, and a 2/2 first striker for two is already a playable body, so it is a keep on body plus upside. Emeritus of Truce enters unprepared too unless you are behind on board, which is exactly when white wants a Swords, so it is a test for control and midrange. Woe enters prepared, so it is a four-mana 5/4 with a Demonic Tutor attached, which is good; it is the body that is below rate for black fours, hence test rather than keep. Ideation enters prepared, so five mana gets a 5/5 flier with ward and an Ancestral Recall; that is strong, and it is the five-drop slot in blue that is the obstacle. Abundance gives the opponent a Regrowth-level effect you will almost never re-trigger, and a 3/4 vigilance for three is fine, not special.

## Which other creatures earn a test?

- **Grave Researcher** ({2}{B}, 3/3; surveil 1 each upkeep; prepares with Reanimate ({B}) when you have three creature cards in your graveyard). **Slot:** black / 3 / graveyard value. **Call: test.** A 3/3 for three that surveils every turn is on rate, and the Reanimate turns on by turn five in most black decks. Reanimator lanes should take this early.
- **Scheming Silvertongue** ({1}{B}, 1/3 flying lifelink; prepares with Sign in Blood ({B}{B}) when you gained 2 or more life this turn). **Call: test** in lifegain or control lanes. The body blocks aggro well, and each connect draws two later.
- **Moseo, Vein's New Dean** ({2}{B}, 2/1 flying; makes a 1/1 Pest that gains life on attack; infusion returns a creature with mana value up to your life gained). **Call: pass** outside a dedicated lifegain lane.
- **Pensive Professor** ({1}{U}{U}, 0/2; increment puts a counter when you overspend; draws when counters are added). **Call: pass.** Slow in a 20-life format.
- **Exhibition Tidecaller** ({U}, 0/2; opus mills three or ten whenever you cast an instant or sorcery). **Call: pass** except in mill cubes.
- **Professor Dellian Fel** ({2}{B}{G}, planeswalker, loyalty 5; +2 gain 3; 0 draw and lose 1; −3 destroy a creature; −6 drain emblem). **Slot:** gold / BG / 4. **Call: test.** A Golgari walker that removes a creature on entering and then draws every turn. Competes with Vraska, Golgari Queen and is a little better.
- **Ral Zarek, Guest Lecturer** ({1}{B}{B}, planeswalker, loyalty 3; +1 surveil 2; −1 each target player discards; −2 reanimates a creature with mana value 3 or less; −7 coin flips for skipped turns). **Slot:** black / 3 / value walker. **Call: test.** Three-mana black walkers that filter and reanimate are useful in black midrange. The discard mode hurts.

## What about the dragons and the lessons?

The five Elder Dragons are Commander cards and all pass for cube: Lorehold ({3}{R}{W}, miracle for your hand), Prismari ({5}{U}{R}, storm for your spells), Quandrix ({4}{G}{U}, cascade on spells), Silverquill ({2}{W}{B}, casualty on spells), Witherbloom ({6}{B}{G}, affinity for creatures). Silverquill is the closest to playable at four mana, and it still loses the Orzhov slot to Vindicate.

The Lessons are six- and seven-mana sorceries. **Improvisation Capstone** ({5}{R}{R}, exile until you hit four mana value and cast those spells free) is a Commander card. **Restoration Seminar** ({5}{W}{W}, return a nonland permanent to the battlefield) and **Germination Practicum** ({3}{G}{G}, two counters on each creature) pass. **Decorum Dissertation** ({3}{B}{B}, target player draws two loses 2) passes. **Withering Curse** ({1}{B}{B}, sorcery: all creatures get −2/−2; with infusion, destroy all creatures instead) is the exception: a three-mana black sweeper that scales up if you gained life. **Call: test** as a cheap black answer to aggro.

Two more spells worth a line. **Flashback** ({R}, instant: give an instant or sorcery in your graveyard flashback for its mana cost) is the spell version of Stingcaster Mage's trigger. **Call: test** in red spells decks; it is a Regrowth for burn. **Practiced Offense** ({2}{W}, sorcery: counter on each creature target player controls, double strike or lifelink to one; flashback {1}{W}). **Call: pass.** **Mathemagics** ({X}{X}{U}{U}: draw 2 to the power of X) is a Commander card, and a fun one.

The set also carries the Innistrad slow lands (Stormcarved Coast, Sundown Pass, Shattered Sanctum, Deathcap Glade, Dreamroot Cascade) as reprints. Useful budget fixing; see [cube mana fixing](/articles/cube-mana-fixing/).

## FAQ

### Is Erode better than Swords to Plowshares in cube?

No. Swords is one mana, exiles, and is an instant; Erode destroys, which matters against recursive threats, but gives a land instead of life. Run both. Erode is the third or fourth one-mana white removal spell, not the first.

### Do the Emeritus creatures work without the prepare synergy?

Yes, that is the point. Each is a creature first. Judge the body against its slot, then treat the spell as the upside. Conflict and Woe pass the body test; Abundance does not.

### Is Emeritus of Ideation an Ancestral Recall for cube?

It is a five-mana 5/5 flier that draws three when it enters. That is strong, and it is still a five-drop in the color with the deepest five-drop slot in any cube. Test it.

### Which Secrets of Strixhaven cards fit a Pauper cube?

The rares and mythics do not apply. We have not confirmed enough of the set's commons to make calls, so check your own list against a full spoiler. <!-- EDITOR: confirm -->
