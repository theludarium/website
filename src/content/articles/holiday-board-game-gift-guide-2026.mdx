A board game is a gift that keeps asking people to sit down together. The hard part is picking the right one. We wrote a guide for nearly every person on your list, so start with who you are shopping for and jump straight to theirs.

**The quick answer:** give Codenames to a family or group, Azul to someone ready for a first strategy game, and Sky Team to a couple. Shopping for someone more specific? Find them below and open their guide.

**Who this is for:** anyone buying a board game this holiday season, including people who do not know much about board games.

## One more gift idea

The best part of a board game is the night you play it. Whatever you wrap, add a note with a date on it: a promise to sit down and play together. The game is the reason. The people are the point.
