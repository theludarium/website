The best board game gifts for kids match the age on the box, then nudge one step up. A six-year-old wants to race and match. An eight-year-old wants real choices. A twelve-year-old wants to beat the adults, fairly. The picks below are sorted by age.

**The quick answer:** Outfoxed! for ages 4 to 5, Ticket to Ride: First Journey for 6 to 7, Kingdomino for 8 to 9, and Splendor or Cascadia for 10 to 12. Every one of them is a game the adults will also want to play.

Ages are the publisher's rating from the box. Prices were checked at publisher stores and major retailers at the time of writing and will move in November.

## What are the best board game gifts for 4 to 5 year olds?

At this age a good game is short, has no reading, and does not knock anyone out. We cover this age in depth in our guide to [board games for 5-year-olds](/articles/board-games-for-5-year-olds/), so here are the two we would wrap first.

**Outfoxed!** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (ages 5 and up, 2 to 4 players, about 20 minutes, per [Gamewright](https://gamewright.com/product/Outfoxed); about $24 at Amazon per [Board Game Oracle](https://www.boardgameoracle.com/boardgame/price/uwMiiKkz0I/outfoxed-2014) at the time of writing). The players work as a team to find which fox stole a pie. You roll to move or to reveal clues, then slide suspect cards into a decoder that shows whether the thief wore glasses, gloves, or a hat. Everyone wins or loses together, which takes the sting out of a bad roll. For more like it, see our list of [cooperative games for kids](/articles/cooperative-games-for-kids/).

**Dragomino** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (ages 5 and up, 2 to 4 players, about 15 minutes, per [Blue Orange Games](https://www.blueorangegames.com/games/dragomino); about $25 to $28 per [Board Game Oracle](https://www.boardgameoracle.com/boardgame/price/fwPxMOVrhJ/dragomino) at the time of writing). The kids' version of Kingdomino. Lay domino tiles that match terrain, and each match lets you flip an egg to see if a baby dragon hatched. The luck of the eggs keeps a younger child in every game.

## What are the best board game gifts for 6 to 7 year olds?

Six and seven is when kids start to read a little, add in their heads, and wait through someone else's turn without wandering off. Games can now ask for a plan.

**Ticket to Ride: First Journey** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (ages 6 and up, 2 to 4 players, 15 to 30 minutes; $29.99 at Asmodee North America per [Board Game Oracle](https://www.boardgameoracle.com/boardgame/price/H6I6x4WM_6/ticket-to-ride-first-journey-us) at the time of writing). The full Ticket to Ride, shrunk to fit. Collect colored train cards, claim short routes between cities, and finish tickets. The map is bigger and friendlier, and there is no scoring math at the end. It is also the clearest bridge to the grown-up game on the 8 to 9 list.

**Labyrinth** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (ages 7 and up, 2 to 4 players, per [Ravensburger](https://ravensburger.org/ca-en/products/games/family-games/labyrinth-26448/index.html); $29.99 at Ravensburger's US store per [Board Game Oracle](https://www.boardgameoracle.com/boardgame/price/VgNZ9zCIai/labyrinth) at the time of writing). Each turn you push a tile into the maze, shifting a whole row, then walk your piece toward a treasure. It teaches kids to see a few moves ahead, and it is one of the rare games where a seven-year-old can beat a parent on merit. Many themed editions exist; the classic is plain Labyrinth.

**Spot It!** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (ages 6 and up, 2 to 8 players; the Classic Pocket edition is $5.99 at the [Asmodee store](https://store.asmodee.com/products/spot-it-classic-pocket-eco-ml) at the time of writing). Any two cards share exactly one symbol. Find it first and shout. A stocking stuffer the whole table can play, and kids are often faster than adults.

## What are the best board game gifts for 8 to 9 year olds?

This is the sweet spot. Most good family games are rated 8 and up, so an eight-year-old can sit at the same table as everyone else. Our [best family board games](/articles/best-family-board-games/) list picks up from here if you want more.

**Kingdomino** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (ages 8 and up, 2 to 4 players, 15 to 25 minutes; about $20 at Amazon and Walmart per [Board Game Oracle](https://boardgameoracle.com/boardgame/price/f6wzmrIbqg/kingdomino) at the time of writing). Pick domino tiles, build a five-by-five kingdom, score by matching terrain and crowns. Better tiles mean you pick later next round, which is a real strategic idea in a twenty-minute game. Our free course [Every Crown Counts](/academy/kingdomino/) teaches it from the first draft.

**King of Tokyo** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (ages 8 and up, 2 to 6 players, about 30 minutes; about $32 to $40 for the second edition per [Board Game Oracle](https://www.boardgameoracle.com/boardgame/price/8PDKr0SADK/king-of-tokyo) at the time of writing). Giant monsters, chunky dice, and a city to smash. Roll up to three times like Yahtzee, then attack, heal, or buy powers. Loud, fast, and right for a kid who would rather roll than read. The free [Who Holds Tokyo](/academy/king-of-tokyo/) course has a printable sheet.

**Ticket to Ride** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (ages 8 and up, 2 to 5 players, 30 to 60 minutes; $39.99 at Walmart and $43.99 at Target per [Board Game Oracle](https://www.boardgameoracle.com/boardgame/price/_UKhZ2la6d/ticket-to-ride) at the time of writing). The family game that goes the distance. Each turn is one of three simple choices, and the board looks great on a table. Days of Wonder refreshed the base game in 2025, so newer copies have the current rules. [Coast to Coast](/academy/ticket-to-ride/) is our free course for it.

## What are the best board game gifts for 10 to 12 year olds?

Between ten and twelve, kids can hold a plan for a whole game and notice what other players are doing. They are ready for games adults play on their own, which is the best compliment a kid's gift can get. For the next step after this, see our [board games for teenagers](/articles/board-games-for-teenagers-gift/) gift guide.

**Splendor** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (ages 10 and up, 2 to 4 players, about 30 minutes; $31.99 at Amazon, Target, and Walmart per [Board Game Oracle](https://boardgameoracle.com/boardgame/price/16MeWRTs8l/splendor) at the time of writing). Collect gem chips, buy cards that give permanent discounts, and race to 15 points. The rules are short, and the strategy grows with the player. Our free course is [Gem by Gem](/academy/splendor/).

**Cascadia** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (ages 10 and up, 1 to 4 players, 30 to 45 minutes; about $32 to $40 per [Board Game Oracle](https://www.boardgameoracle.com/boardgame/price/12975B7EQh/cascadia) at the time of writing). Build a Pacific Northwest landscape from hex tiles and place wildlife tokens in patterns that score. It is calm, pretty, and has a solo mode for a kid who likes to puzzle alone. Learn it with [Wild Corridors](/academy/cascadia/).

**The Crew: Mission Deep Sea** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (ages 10 and up, 2 to 5 players, about 20 minutes; $14.95 at [Walmart](https://www.walmart.com/ip/937559244) at the time of writing). A cooperative card game where the team must win certain tricks, with almost no talking allowed. It plays as a series of missions, so kids want "one more." Our free course is [The Right Trick](/academy/the-crew/).

## How do you pick when the box age and the kid do not match?

Box ages are a guide, not a law.

- **Reading matters more than age.** If a game has text on its cards, a strong reader can play it younger. If it has no text, a younger child can often join with a grown-up partner.
- **Patience matters more than smarts.** A clever six-year-old may still not enjoy a 45-minute game. Short wins.
- **Buy for the table, not just the kid.** The gift gets played if the adults like it too. Every game on this list passes that test.
- **Siblings change the math.** For two kids several years apart, pick a cooperative game or one with lots of luck so the younger one can win.

Losing is part of every game on this list. If that is a sore spot, our piece on [teaching kids to lose at games](/articles/teaching-kids-to-lose-at-games/) has a plan that works for most ages.

One good game beats three average ones. If you want to give more, pair a big box with a small card game: Ticket to Ride with Spot It!, or Splendor with The Crew. And if the family already owns a pile of games and cannot choose, [Game Night Madness](/game-night-madness/) is a free bracket app where everyone votes from a phone until one game is left. Kids take this very seriously.

The game is the reason. The people are the point.

## FAQ

### What is a good board game for a 6-year-old?

Ticket to Ride: First Journey is our pick, with Labyrinth close behind. Both are rated for this age and give a six-year-old real choices without reading.

### What board game should I get an 8-year-old?

Kingdomino if you want a short game, Ticket to Ride if you want one the whole family plays. Both are rated 8 and up on the box.

### What is a good board game for a 10 to 12 year old?

Splendor or Cascadia. Both are rated 10 and up, play in under an hour, and are games adults buy for themselves.

### Are cooperative games better for young kids?

For ages 4 to 6, often yes. Everyone wins or loses together, so a bad roll stings less and the adult can coach without taking sides.
