If you are looking for a cube draft in Indianapolis, there is one, and it is open. Cube Commonwealth is a Magic: The Gathering cube community that met weekly for about three years, seating 12 to 16 players a night from a Discord of roughly 300 mostly local members. After a pause, we are relaunching as a branch of The Ludarium. You need no cards, no experience, and no money for packs. You need to show up.

This page covers what the night is, how to join, what to expect your first time, and where else in the metro to play Magic on the nights we are not drafting.

## What is Cube Commonwealth?

A cube is a curated set of a few hundred Magic cards that a group drafts over and over, with no packs to buy. One person owns the box; everyone drafts from it. Cube Commonwealth is the group that formed around one such cube in Indianapolis, and then around each other.

The short history: a kitchen table, a budget cube, and a weeknight that kept happening. Then a public venue, two pods most weeks, a change log people argued about, a traveling trophy of no value, and a Discord that grew to about 300. Then a stretch where the night depended on one person's calendar, and the calendar won. We paused. We are back, with more than one host this time. The long version is in [Cube Commonwealth: a community around one cube](/articles/cube-commonwealth/), and the relaunch plan is in [relaunching a game community](/articles/relaunching-a-game-community/).

## How do you join?

Three steps, none of them hard.

1. **Join the Discord.** <!-- EDITOR: add Discord invite link --> Read the pinned #start-here post. It answers the five questions everyone has.
2. **React to the weekly post in #this-week.** That is the RSVP. The reaction count is the headcount, and the headcount is what tells everyone it is worth the drive. Un-react if your plans change.
3. **Show up on time.** A host will message you before your first night so someone in the room is expecting you. You will be seated next to a regular whose job is to help.

There is no application, no tryout, and no card requirement. If you have never played Magic, say so in the Discord and we will tell you when the next new-player night is. We run those about once a month.

## What should you expect your first night?

| Stage | What happens | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Arrive | Find the table, say hello, a pack is already waiting at your seat | Before start |
| Draft | Three packs of fifteen, pass left, right, left. A veteran on your right answers questions | About 45 minutes |
| Build | Pick roughly 23 spells in two colors, add 17 lands from the land box | 15–20 minutes |
| Round one | Random pairing, or a designated teacher if you are new | 50 minutes |
| Rounds two and three | Winners play winners; standings stay private until the end | 50 minutes each |
| Debrief | What you drafted, what should get cut, who takes the trophy home | 5 minutes |

Bring nothing. A playmat if you own one. Something to track life with, though someone will lend you dice. If the venue sells food or drink, buy something; that is how we keep the table. <!-- EDITOR: confirm cost norm -->

## Where else can you play Magic in Indianapolis?

Cube Commonwealth is one night a week. For the other six, Indianapolis has a store for almost every night, and nearly all of them run Commander or Friday Night Magic. Below are the venues we confirmed in October 2026 for our fuller guide, [where to play Magic in Indianapolis](/articles/where-to-play-magic-indianapolis/); schedules shift, so check the store's own calendar before you drive.

**Downtown and near north**

- **[Good Games Indianapolis](https://www.goodgamesna.com/us/stores/indiana/indianapolis.html)**, 111 S Meridian St. The largest play space downtown, with events most days and a free Casual Commander night on Sundays listed on its [public calendar](https://meetup.com/goodgames).
- **[Paradigm Games](https://paradigmgames.co/)**, 2479 N Meridian St. Wizards Play Network store with Commander Night on Thursdays, Standard Showdown on Wednesdays, Friday Night Magic draft at 6:30, and free-play Commander on Tuesday afternoons and Saturdays at noon.

**North suburbs**

- **[Moonshot Games](https://moonshotgamestore.com/)**, 50 N 9th St, downtown Noblesville. Friday Night Magic plus a game room and demo tables.
- **[Hero's Emporium](https://www.visithamiltoncounty.com/listing/heros-emporium/5473/)**, Carmel. Magic nights and Commander sessions.
- **[Next Level Games](https://www.visithamiltoncounty.com/listing/next-level-games/5751/)**, Carmel. Magic nights and game events.
- **[Gray Gauntlet Games](https://graygauntletgames.com/)**, 7998 Centerpoint Dr, Fishers. Magic on the events calendar alongside Pokémon and painting nights.
- **[Game Time](http://www.gametimeindy.com/)**, 8244 Center Run Dr, Castleton. In-store play and tournament hosting; open until 10 on Fridays, no online schedule, so call ahead.

**South**

- **[The Game Preserve](https://www.gamepreserve.com/)**, Greenwood. Tournaments and league nights, with a second location near the Fashion Mall.

None of these stores runs a regular cube night that we know of, which is the gap Cube Commonwealth fills. Stores schedule what sells product, and a cube sells nothing. That is also why we care about venues the way we do.

## How does Cube Commonwealth fit into The Ludarium?

The Ludarium is a game lounge venture starting in Indianapolis: membership-based spaces for adults to play tabletop games together, regularly, the way coworking gave remote workers a place to work together. Cube Commonwealth is where that idea was tested before it had a name. One shared object, a fixed night, a host who cares about the people more than the matches, and rituals that make the evening belong to everyone.

When the lounge opens, the cube will have a shelf and the night will have a permanent table. Until then, Cube Commonwealth is a Ludarium branch running in a borrowed room, which is how most good things start.

## What if you are visiting, not local?

Come anyway. Gen Con brings tens of thousands of tabletop players to downtown Indianapolis each summer, and in other weeks the city gets conference traffic and travelers passing through on the interstates. ## FAQ

### Is there a cube draft in Indianapolis?

Yes. Cube Commonwealth is a weekly Magic cube community in Indianapolis, relaunching now as a branch of The Ludarium. Join its Discord to see the weekly post.

### Do I need my own Magic cards to join Cube Commonwealth?

No. The cube is the collection. You draft from the shared pool and leave with nothing but a record and an opinion about what should get cut.

### Can a beginner join an Indianapolis cube draft?

Yes. Cube Commonwealth seats new players next to a regular who helps, and runs a dedicated new-player night when enough first-timers sign up.

### Where can I play Magic in Indianapolis on other nights?

Good Games downtown, Paradigm Games on N Meridian, Moonshot Games in Noblesville, Hero's Emporium and Next Level Games in Carmel, Gray Gauntlet Games in Fishers, Game Time in Castleton, and The Game Preserve in Greenwood all run regular Magic events.
