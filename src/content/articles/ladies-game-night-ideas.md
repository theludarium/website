A great ladies game night is a standing date with your friends where the game gives everyone something to do while you catch up. The best ones mix one quick party game to warm up, one game with a little strategy or teamwork for the middle of the night, and enough breathing room to talk. You do not need a big collection or friends who already play. Three or four good boxes, a snack table, and a date on the calendar will do it.

**The quick answer:** for a first night with 6 to 10 friends, start with Wavelength or Just One, then split into smaller tables for Codenames or Azul. For four friends who want a real game, try Cascadia or Ticket to Ride. Keep the rules short, put the food on a side table, and pick next month's date before anyone leaves.

## What makes a ladies game night different from any other game night?

Honestly, not much, and that is the point. A girls game night works for the same reasons any game night works: a regular time, a welcoming host, and games that fit the people in the room.

What often stands out is what the group is there for. For many friend groups, the night is as much about catching up as about the game. That shapes the games you pick. Long, silent strategy games can crowd out conversation. Games that make people talk, laugh, guess what a friend is thinking, or team up tend to hit the sweet spot.

Every group is different, though. Some ladies nights are fiercely competitive and want a two-hour strategy game. Pick for your friends, not for a stereotype.

## How do you pick games for a ladies game night?

Start with two questions: how many people are coming, and what kind of evening do you want? Then use this table.

| Group size | Want lots of laughing and talking | Want a little strategy | Want to work together |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 to 4 | Love Letter | Azul, Cascadia | The Crew |
| 4 to 6 | So Clover! | Ticket to Ride, Wingspan | Just One |
| 6 to 10 | Wavelength, Hues and Cues | Sushi Go Party! | Codenames in teams, Just One (up to 7) |
| 10 or more | Herd Mentality | Split into two tables | Wavelength in teams |

Three rules of thumb help:

- **Teach in under five minutes.** If the rules need longer, save that game for month three.
- **Start light, get heavier, end light.** A party game while people arrive, a meatier game in the middle, and something silly at the end.
- **Pick games where you can lose and still have fun.** Laughter covers a lot of losing.

## The 15 best games for ladies game night

Each pick lists its player count and roughly how long one game takes. We chose games that new players can learn quickly and that people who already play still enjoy.

### Party and conversation games

#### 1. Wavelength (2 to 12 players, 30 to 45 minutes)

[Wavelength](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07T446163?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) is a team guessing game about how your friends think. One player sees a hidden target on a dial between two ideas, like "overrated" and "underrated," and gives a single clue. The team turns the dial to where they think it is. The arguments about whether brunch is overrated are the real game.

#### 2. Just One (3 to 7 players, 20 minutes)

[Just One](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07GJ7NDZQ?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) is a cooperative word game that won the 2019 Spiel des Jahres, Germany's top game award. Everyone writes a one-word clue to help one player guess a secret word, but any duplicate clues get erased. It is gentle, clever, and perfect for friends who don't play games yet.

#### 3. Codenames (2 to 8+ players, 15 minutes)

[Codenames](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B014Q1XX9S?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) splits the group into two teams. A spymaster on each side gives one-word clues to link several words on the table. It rewards knowing how your friends think, which is half the fun of a long friendship. Our free lesson on [how to play Codenames](/academy/codenames/how-to-play-codenames/) teaches it in a few minutes.

#### 4. Herd Mentality (4 to 20 players, 15 to 20 minutes)

[Herd Mentality](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B093HBBMPT?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) asks a question like "What's the best pizza topping?" and everyone writes an answer. You score by matching the majority. Give the odd answer and you might end up holding the pink cow, a penalty that can stop you from winning. It works with a huge group and needs zero strategy.

#### 5. Telestrations (4 to 8 players)

[Telestrations](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0F699VTB2?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) is the telephone game with drawing. You sketch a word, the next person guesses what you drew, the next draws that guess, and so on. Nobody needs to draw well; bad drawings are funnier. The 12 Player Party Pack stretches it to a dozen friends.

#### 6. Dixit (3 to 8 players, 30 minutes)

[Dixit](https://www.amazon.com/dp/2914849656?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) uses dreamlike art cards. The storyteller gives a clue for one card, everyone else adds a card from their hand that fits the clue, and the group votes on which was the original. Give a clue that is too obvious and you score nothing, so the art sparks real conversation. The current edition supports up to 8 players; older boxes stop at 6.

#### 7. Hues and Cues (3 to 10 players, 30 minutes)

[Hues and Cues](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B084D2XQ9F?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) shows a board of 480 color squares. One player picks a color and gives a one-word clue, then a two-word clue, while everyone tries to land on it. Whether "avocado" is green or brown will start a friendly debate.

#### 8. Love Letter (2 to 6 players, 20 minutes)

[Love Letter](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00AGJ4HC2?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) is a tiny card game of guessing and bluffing with just 21 cards. Each turn you draw one and play one, trying to be the last player in the round or to hold the highest card. Rounds take a few minutes, so it fits at the start or end of any night.

### Light strategy games

#### 9. Azul (2 to 4 players, 30 to 45 minutes)

[Azul](https://link.amazon/B0bGRYHex) is a beautiful tile-drafting game about decorating a palace wall. Turns are quick, the pieces feel like candy, and there is just enough strategy to make you groan when a friend takes the tiles you needed. Learn it with our free [how to play Azul](/academy/azul/how-to-play-azul/) lesson.

#### 10. Cascadia (1 to 4 players, 30 to 45 minutes)

[Cascadia](https://link.amazon/B0g1HYFt4) has you build a landscape of habitat tiles and wildlife tokens in the Pacific Northwest. It is calm, pretty, and easy to talk over, since you mostly build your own area.

#### 11. Ticket to Ride (2 to 5 players, 30 to 60 minutes)

[Ticket to Ride](https://link.amazon/B0jh8RB7f) is the classic gateway game. Collect colored train cards and claim railway routes across a map. It is easy to teach and has a satisfying tension when someone takes the route you were saving for.

#### 12. Wingspan (1 to 5 players, 40 to 70 minutes)

[Wingspan](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07YQ641NQ?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) is a card-driven game about attracting birds to your wildlife preserve. Each bird card has a real species and a fun fact. It is a step heavier than the others, so save it for a smaller table that wants a longer game.

#### 13. Sushi Go Party! (2 to 8 players, 20 minutes)

[Sushi Go Party!](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B01CETNKE2?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) is a quick card-drafting game: pick one sushi dish from your hand and pass the rest. Everyone plays at once, so there is no waiting, and it handles eight players well.

### Cooperative games

#### 14. So Clover! (3 to 6 players, 30 minutes)

[So Clover!](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0941TJHXX?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) is a cooperative word game. Each player writes clues that link pairs of words on their own clover-shaped board, then the group works together to rebuild each clover from the clues. It feels like a puzzle you solve together.

#### 15. The Crew (2 to 5 players, about 20 minutes)

[The Crew](https://link.amazon/B099S9RKf) is a cooperative trick-taking game with a campaign of missions. You can barely communicate, so you learn to read each other. It is a great pick for a smaller group that likes a challenge and wants a reason to come back next month to try the next mission.

### No-box classics

If a few friends are new to games, start the night with something nobody has to learn: Two Truths and a Lie, charades, or Would You Rather. They are a gentle way into the evening before the first box opens.

## What food works for a ladies game night?

Food is half the fun, but greasy fingers and spilled drinks ruin cards. Keep it simple:

- **Dry, bite-size snacks** like popcorn, pretzels, cheese cubes, crackers, and cookies.
- **A separate snack table** so the game table stays clean.
- **Drinks off the table** or on coasters at each seat.
- **Something for everyone to drink,** including a fun non-alcoholic option, so nobody feels left out.
- **A potluck plan** where each friend brings one thing. It spreads the work and gives guests a role.

## What does a ladies game night look like? A sample evening

Here is a sample three-hour evening for eight friends.

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 7:00 | Friends arrive, grab a drink, and catch up |
| 7:20 | Warm-up as a big group: Herd Mentality or Wavelength |
| 7:50 | Snack break and refills |
| 8:05 | Split into two tables of four: Azul at one, Codenames or Ticket to Ride at the other |
| 9:00 | Switch games or tables if people want |
| 9:40 | Everyone back together for a closer: Telestrations or Wavelength |
| 10:00 | Pick next month's date and theme before anyone leaves |

Do not overschedule. If a game is going great, let it run. If the room just wants to talk for a while, that counts as a successful night too.

## How do you turn it into a monthly ritual?

The magic of a ladies game night is in the repeat. One night is a fun evening. Twelve nights become a friendship.

- **Pick a fixed date pattern,** such as the second Friday of every month. Nobody has to check calendars every time.
- **Set a minimum.** Decide that four people is enough to play, so nobody cancels for low numbers.
- **Rotate hosts.** Each month a different friend hosts and picks the theme. It shares the work and the fun.
- **Keep a group chat.** Post the date, the theme, and photos. It keeps momentum between nights.
- **Track what the group loves.** Our [Card Catalogue](/card-catalogue/) lets you find games by how they feel, keep a Play Profile, and get picks for the whole table, all stored on your own device.

## How do you invite friends who don't play games yet?

Many people have not played a board game since childhood. They might picture a long, confusing game or worry about looking silly. A few habits make them feel welcome:

- **Invite them to a night, not a game.** "Come over Friday for snacks and a few silly games" sounds better than "Want to play a strategy game?"
- **Start with a party game.** Herd Mentality or Just One shows that nobody needs to be an expert.
- **Let new players team up.** Partner them with an experienced friend for their first game.
- **Teach in two minutes, then play.** Explain the goal and one turn, then learn the rest as you go.
- **Never keep score too seriously.** Celebrate great clues and funny moments more than the winner.

## FAQ

### What are the best games for a ladies game night?

Wavelength, Just One, Codenames, and Herd Mentality are great for big groups and lots of laughter. Azul, Cascadia, and Ticket to Ride suit smaller tables that want a bit of strategy. So Clover! and The Crew are great if your friends like working together.

### What games are good for a big group of women?

Herd Mentality handles up to 20 players, Wavelength up to 12, and Hues and Cues up to 10. For more than 10 people, split into two tables or play team games.

### How do you start a girls game night?

Invite four to eight friends, pick two or three easy games, and set out simple snacks. At the end of the night, choose next month's date so it becomes a regular habit.

### What is a good theme for a ladies game night?

Galentine's Game Night, a cozy night in, or brunch with board games are easy places to start. Pick a theme that matches your friends, and let the host choose a new one each month.

### How long should a ladies game night last?

About three hours works for most groups: some time to catch up, two or three games, and a snack break.

A ladies game night does not need to be fancy. It needs good friends, a few easy games, and a date on the calendar.
