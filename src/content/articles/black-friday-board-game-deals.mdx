## When is Black Friday 2026?

Thanksgiving is Thursday, November 26, 2026, so Black Friday is November 27, Small Business Saturday is November 28, and Cyber Monday is November 30. Big retailers now start their "Black Friday" prices days or weeks earlier. In 2025, the headline Amazon price on Catan's sixth edition was posted on November 23 (Slickdeals, Nov 2025), four days before Black Friday.

Early sales have started in 2026 too. A [Yahoo Shopping roundup from late October](https://shopping.yahoo.com/toys-games/articles/start-game-night-tradition-early-153053310.html) showed Catan at roughly a quarter off its usual list price, well short of last year's 54 percent. If the pattern holds, the deep cut comes in Thanksgiving week.

## How do Black Friday board game deals actually work?

Three forces set board game prices in late November.

**Loss leaders at big-box retailers.** Amazon, Target, and Walmart discount a handful of recognizable titles deeply to pull shoppers in. These are the headline deals. They are real, and they tend to be the same titles from year to year.

**Publisher price policies.** Many hobby publishers set minimum advertised prices. ICv2 reported back in 2018 that Asmodee's advertised-price policy limited discounts to 20 percent, and that Amazon broke through it that Black Friday with [Catan at 47 percent off, Ticket to Ride at 50 percent off, and Pandemic at 50 percent off](https://icv2.com/articles/news/view/41918/amazons-not-playing-board-game-prices). The biggest discounts still cluster on the titles Amazon chooses to fight over.

**Bundle promotions.** Target has run a "buy two, get one free" on board games in past holiday seasons, stacked on top of sale prices. A December 2025 deal post on Slickdeals listed Catan's sixth edition under that offer at a sale price well below Target's regular one. Target has not announced a 2026 version at the time of writing, but if it returns and you are buying three gifts, it can beat the individual Amazon deals.

## What is worth waiting for?

Wait if the game is on the list above, or shares its profile: a big-box staple from a large publisher with an obvious gift audience. In past years, discounts of 40 to 54 percent on these titles have shown up at Amazon and Target in the week of Thanksgiving.

Also wait on:

- **Starter sets with a holiday audience.** Wargamer tracked the [previous D&D starter set, Dragons of Stormwreck Isle, at half its list price on Black Friday 2024](https://www.wargamer.com/dnd/starter-set-black-friday-stormwreck-isle). The current set is Heroes of the Borderlands; our guide to the [best D&D starter set gift](/articles/best-dnd-starter-set-gift/) compares the two.
- **Mass-market party games.** <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B010TQY7A8?tag=cubecommonw0a-20">Exploding Kittens</AffiliateLink>, <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B084D2XQ9F?tag=cubecommonw0a-20">Hues and Cues</AffiliateLink>, and the like. Tom's Guide tracked Exploding Kittens at [more than 60 percent off its list price](https://www.tomsguide.com/gaming/black-friday-2025-board-games-card-games-deals) in 2025.
- **Previous editions.** When a publisher refreshes a game, as Catan did with its sixth edition in 2025, the old edition can clear out cheaply. The core play is the same.

## What is not worth waiting for?

Most of the hobby. Specifically:

**Small-publisher and direct-sales games.** Stonemaier Games sets its own prices for Wingspan, and its sale price at the time of writing was only about 15 percent off. Publishers like this rarely join the price war.

**New releases.** A game released in the autumn is unlikely to be discounted at its first holiday. If it is on the list, buy it from a local store and get it over with. Our running list of the [best board games of 2026](/articles/best-board-games-2026/) shows which new ones are worth it.

**Anything already under $20.** The Black Friday discount on a small card game is a few dollars. Love Letter at 16 percent off saves you less than a cup of coffee. The wait costs more than the saving. Our lists of [board games under $20](/articles/board-games-under-20-dollars/) and [board game gifts under $30](/articles/best-board-game-gifts-under-30/) are full of games like this.

**Out-of-print and crowdfunded games.** Prices go up, not down.

## When is the cheapest time to buy board games, if not Black Friday?

For the mass-market list, Black Friday is usually the floor. For everything else, there are other windows.

- **January.** Retailers clear holiday overstock, and many local stores run inventory sales.
- **Amazon's other sale events.** Deal coverage of [Prime Day in June 2026](https://www.nbcnews.com/select/shopping/amazon-prime-day-board-game-deals-2026-rcna351807) showed Catan at 45 percent off, with [Splendor](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00IZEUFIA?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), Azul, Carcassonne, and Ticket to Ride at 24 to 40 percent off. In October 2025's Big Deal Days, [Catan was about 37 percent off its list price](https://frugalfun4boys.com/amazon-big-deal-days-october-2025-deals-on-toys-and-games/). The same gateway titles come back around at near-Black-Friday prices.
- **Any time, at a local game store.** The price is often a few dollars higher. In exchange you get a person who knows the games, often a place to try one, and a store that will still be there next year. We think the few dollars are well spent; our piece on [Small Business Saturday at your local game store](/articles/small-business-saturday-game-store/) makes the case, and we have written about [why game lounges don't sell games](/articles/why-game-lounges-dont-sell-games/) and why we want those stores to thrive.

## How to shop Black Friday board game deals without wasting a weekend

1. **Make the list first.** Decide what you want before the sales start. A deal on a game you did not want is not a deal.
2. **Check prices now.** Retailers sometimes inflate "regular" prices before sales. Note the current price now, before Thanksgiving week, so you know whether the sale number is real. Our guide on [how to spot a real board game deal](/articles/how-to-spot-a-real-board-game-deal/) covers the tricks.
3. **Set one price alert per game.** Camelcamelcamel for Amazon; most deal sites for the rest. Then close the tab.
4. **Look early in Thanksgiving week.** In 2025, the headline Catan price was live by the Sunday before Thanksgiving. Stock, not price, is what tends to run out.
5. **Stack a bundle offer if you need three.** If a "buy two, get one" deal appears, three sale-price games for the price of two beats most single-item deals.

## The deal is the excuse, not the point

The best thing a Black Friday price does is get a game into a house that would not have bought one at full price. That is good. A half-price Catan under a tree in a family that has never played a modern game can change what that family does on Sunday nights for years.

So shop the deals. But remember that the game you get at 54 percent off is worth exactly as much as the number of times it reaches the table. The game is the reason. The people are the point.

## FAQ

### Do board games go on sale on Black Friday?

Yes, but unevenly. Mass-market titles like Catan, Ticket to Ride, and Carcassonne have dropped 40 to 54 percent at Amazon in past years. Many hobby games move much less.

### What is the best Black Friday board game deal?

Historically, Catan or Ticket to Ride at around half price. In 2025, Catan's sixth edition was reported at 54 percent off its list price.

### Is Cyber Monday better than Black Friday for board games?

Usually about the same. In past years many board game prices have held from Black Friday through Cyber Monday (November 30 in 2026), so the bigger risk in waiting is stock, not price.

### Should I wait for Black Friday to buy Wingspan?

Probably not. Stonemaier sets its own price for <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07YQ641NQ?tag=cubecommonw0a-20">Wingspan</AffiliateLink>, so deep Black Friday cuts are rare. If the price looks fair when you check, buy it then.
