The best board game gifts for solo players are games that were built to be played alone, or that have a solo mode as good as the full game. A solo gamer does not need a group to say yes. They need a box they can open on a quiet evening and finish before bed. The picks below run from a $14 card game to a big box for the person who already owns a few.

**The quick answer:** Friday for a small, clever first solo game, Under Falling Skies for a tense one, and Cascadia for someone who wants a game that also works when friends come over.

Prices were checked at major retailers at the time of writing and will shift during the holidays. Read each one as "about."

## What makes a good gift for someone who plays alone?

Solo players shop differently from groups. A few things make a solo gift land.

- **Fast setup.** If it takes twenty minutes to set up for a forty-minute game, it stays on the shelf.
- **A clear goal.** A score to beat or a way to lose. Without one, a solo game feels like homework.
- **Small table space.** Many solo players play on a desk or a corner of the kitchen table.
- **Replay value.** The game has to surprise its one player again and again.

We wrote a longer piece on the [best solo board games](/articles/best-solo-board-games/), with thoughts on why people play alone. This one is the gift version: what to wrap, at what price.

## Which games are built only for one player?

**Friday** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (1 player, ages 13 and up, about 25 minutes; $13.99 at Cardhaus Games and $14.99 at Miniature Market per [Board Game Oracle](https://www.boardgameoracle.com/boardgame/price/Fy_P-qhGkG/friday) at the time of writing). You help Robinson Crusoe survive his island by fighting hazards with a deck of cards. Win a fight and you add a better card; lose and you can cut weak cards from the deck. It is small, cheap, and hard to beat, which is the point. The Target listing is much higher, so shop a hobby store.

**Under Falling Skies** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (1 player, ages 12 and up, 20 to 40 minutes; $24.95 at Cardhaus Games per [Board Game Oracle](https://boardgameoracle.com/boardgame/price/zFzNnZaPTz/under-falling-skies) at the time of writing). Alien ships descend on a city, and you defend it by placing dice. Higher dice do more, but they also pull the ships down faster. It feels like a fight in a box, and it comes with a campaign for someone who wants to keep going.

## Which multiplayer games have the best solo modes?

These are safe gifts because they work twice: alone on Tuesday, with friends on Saturday.

**Cartographers** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (1 to 100 players, ages 10 and up, 30 to 45 minutes; about $22 at Cardhaus Games and Miniature Market per [Board Game Oracle](https://www.boardgameoracle.com/boardgame/price/cM4QCD3zYX/cartographers) at the time of writing). A flip-and-write game. Each card shows a shape and a terrain, and you draw it onto your map sheet to meet scoring goals. Solo play uses the same rules with a score to beat, and a full pad of map sheets lasts a long time.

**Cascadia** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (1 to 4 players, ages 10 and up, 30 to 45 minutes; $31.99 at Miniature Market and about $37 at Amazon and Walmart per [Board Game Oracle](https://www.boardgameoracle.com/boardgame/price/12975B7EQh/cascadia) at the time of writing). Build a landscape from hex tiles and place wildlife in scoring patterns. The solo mode barely changes the rules, which makes it one of the easiest solo games to learn. Our free course [Wild Corridors](/academy/cascadia/) teaches it.

**Lost Ruins of Arnak** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (1 to 4 players, ages 12 and up, 30 to 120 minutes; $49.95 at Cardhaus Games and Game Nerdz per [Board Game Oracle](https://www.boardgameoracle.com/boardgame/price/W-gni2bPnj/lost-ruins-of-arnak) at the time of writing). Deck building and worker placement on a jungle island. The solo mode uses a small deck of cards to play a rival explorer who takes spaces and races you up the research track. Our free course is [Five Rounds to the Lost Temple](/academy/lost-ruins-of-arnak/).

**Wingspan** <!-- AFFILIATE --> is the other famous solo mode, played against a deck called the Automa. At about $55 per [Board Game Oracle](https://www.boardgameoracle.com/boardgame/price/1cF2LLZC_o/wingspan) at the time of writing, it is a little more, and a lovely gift for a bird lover. Our course is [From Feeder to Flock](/academy/wingspan/).

## What is a big solo gift for someone who already has a few?

**Spirit Island** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (1 to 4 players, ages 13 and up, 90 to 120 minutes; $48.99 at Miniature Market and $64.99 at Amazon per [Board Game Oracle](https://www.boardgameoracle.com/boardgame/price/dqzBAt2yEb/spirit-island) at the time of writing). A cooperative game where the players are spirits of an island driving off colonizing invaders. Because it is cooperative, one person can run one spirit or two, with no rules changes needed. It is heavy, and it is one of the most loved solo games in the hobby. Our free course [Ravage, Build, Explore](/academy/spirit-island/) helps with the first game.

**Horizons of Spirit Island** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (1 to 3 players, ages 14 and up, 90 to 120 minutes; $20.99 at Miniature Market and about $30 at Amazon per [Board Game Oracle](https://www.boardgameoracle.com/boardgame/price/gx19oDqYB-/horizons-of-spirit-island) at the time of writing). A smaller, standalone version with spirits designed to be easier to learn. A good test before the big box, at less than half the price.

## How do you give a solo game without saying "play alone"?

Some people worry a solo game sends the wrong message. It does not have to. A few ideas:

- **Pick one that also plays with others.** Cascadia, Cartographers, and Arnak all work for a group, so the gift is an invitation, not a verdict.
- **Add a note.** "For quiet nights, and for when I come over." Then come over.
- **Know the person.** Many solo players play alone by choice, the same way people read alone. It is rest, not loneliness.

The game is the reason. The people are the point.

## FAQ

### What is the best board game gift for one player?

Friday is the best small one, and Under Falling Skies the best mid-size one. Both were designed only for solo play.

### Can you play Cascadia alone?

Yes. Cascadia includes a solo mode that uses nearly the same rules as the full game, scored against a target.

### What is a good solo game for a beginner?

Friday or Cartographers. Both teach in minutes and play in under 45, and Cartographers also works with a group later.

### Are solo tabletop RPGs a good gift?

For someone who loves stories, yes. Try a free demo in the RPG Demo Library first to see if the style fits.
