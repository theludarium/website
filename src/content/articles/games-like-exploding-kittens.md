The best games like [Exploding Kittens](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B010TQY7A8?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) are small, quick, and funny, and which one fits depends on what made your table laugh. For the same cartoon chaos, play [Unstable Unicorns](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0F19DZJJC?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), [Here to Slay](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B085JYYWJV?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), or [Throw Throw Burrito](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07TS96J7Q?tag=cubecommonw0a-20). For loud, fast fun, play [Taco Cat Goat Cheese Pizza](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B077Z1R28P?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), [Happy Salmon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09KKJMR4L?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), or [Flip 7](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DWGVM7RY?tag=cubecommonw0a-20). For the "please don't be the kitten" tension, play [Love Letter](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07PHHBWM9?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), [Coup](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00GDI4HX4?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), [Skull](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08R2BPLSN?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), or [Cockroach Poker](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07JYLC3DK?tag=cubecommonw0a-20). And for younger players or a gentler table, [Sushi Go!](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00J57VU44?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), [Sleeping Queens](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0009XBY3A?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), and [Munchkin](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H7648MST?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) cover the range from sweet to silly.

**The quick answer:** for most teens and families, start with **Flip 7** (one more card, or stop? Everyone gets it in a minute) or **Unstable Unicorns** (the closest match for Exploding Kittens' cartoon mayhem). For a group that loved the bluffing, **Coup** is a pocket-sized hit.

Below are 13 picks, sorted by what you liked about Exploding Kittens, with a comparison table, a "which one first" guide, and a plain note on how each one differs.

## What makes a game feel like Exploding Kittens?

Exploding Kittens was created by Elan Lee, Matthew Inman of The Oatmeal, and Shane Small. Its 2015 Kickstarter became the most-backed campaign in the site's history at the time. It plays 2 to 5 in about 15 minutes. You draw cards until someone draws an Exploding Kitten and is out, unless they have a Defuse card to save them.

When people want "something like Exploding Kittens," they usually mean one or more of these:

1. **The jokes.** Silly art, silly card names, and a game nobody takes too seriously.
2. **The tension.** Every draw might be the kitten. Simple, and it works on every age.
3. **The attacks.** Skip, Attack, and Favor cards let you pick on someone. That is classic "take that" design, and our guide to [take that games](/articles/take-that-games/) explains why some players love it and others quietly leave the table.
4. **The small box and short game.** Teach in two minutes, play in fifteen, and play again.

Every game on this list keeps the short game and the small box. Pick the one that doubles down on the part your table liked most.

## If you loved the cartoon chaos

These three keep the jokes, the bright art, and the cards that wreck someone's plan.

### Unstable Unicorns

Designed and illustrated by Ramy Badie, Unstable Unicorns launched on Kickstarter in 2017. It plays 2 to 8 in about 30 to 60 minutes. In [Unstable Unicorns](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0F19DZJJC?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), you build a stable of unicorns while everyone else tries to destroy, steal, or sacrifice them. The first player to collect enough unicorns wins.

**How it differs:** it is the closest match for Exploding Kittens' attitude, with more attacks and longer games. Nobody is eliminated, so everyone stays in until the end. There is also an adult edition, so check the box before buying for kids. The standard edition is listed for ages 8 and up.

### Here to Slay

Designed by Ramy Badie and published by Unstable Games in 2020, Here to Slay plays 2 to 6 in about 30 to 60 minutes. In [Here to Slay](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B085JYYWJV?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), you build a party of cute fantasy heroes and roll two dice to use their powers and slay monsters. You win by filling your party with six different classes or by defeating three monsters.

**How it differs:** it comes from the Unstable Unicorns team, but adds dice and two ways to win, so there is more to think about. It is a good step for teens who have played Exploding Kittens to death and want a little more strategy without losing the jokes.

### Throw Throw Burrito

Made by Exploding Kittens and released in 2019 after a Kickstarter campaign, Throw Throw Burrito plays 2 to 6 in about 15 minutes. In [Throw Throw Burrito](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07TS96J7Q?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), everyone races to collect sets of three matching cards. Some sets start a burrito battle, and players throw soft foam burritos at each other. Getting hit costs you points.

**How it differs:** same makers, same humor, but you are on your feet. There are no turns, so it works when kids cannot wait. Play it in a room with nothing breakable, and save it for a group that wants to be loud.

## If you loved the noise and the laughing

These three are about speed and shouting. They teach in a minute and end before anyone gets bored.

### Taco Cat Goat Cheese Pizza

Created by Dave Campbell of Dolphin Hat Games and based on the classic slap game Egyptian Ratscrew, Taco Cat Goat Cheese Pizza plays 2 to 8 in about 10 minutes. In [Taco Cat Goat Cheese Pizza](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B077Z1R28P?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), players flip cards onto a pile while saying "taco, cat, goat, cheese, pizza" in order. When the card matches the word, everyone slaps the pile, and the last hand down takes the pile.

**How it differs:** there is no strategy at all, just fast hands and a tongue twister. It is one of the best games for mixed ages, since a quick seven-year-old can beat a slow grandparent. It is also tiny, which makes it a natural [stocking stuffer](/articles/board-game-stocking-stuffers/).

### Happy Salmon

Designed by Ken Gruhl and Quentin Weir and first published by North Star Games in 2016, Happy Salmon plays 3 to 8 in a few minutes a round. In [Happy Salmon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09KKJMR4L?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), everyone flips cards at the same time and calls out actions: high five, fist bump, switcheroo, or happy salmon, a wiggly slap of forearms. Find a partner calling the same action, do it, and toss the card.

**How it differs:** there are no turns and no rules worth remembering. It is less a card game than a ninety-second icebreaker, which makes it great as a warm-up before a longer game, and a good pick for [large group game nights](/articles/large-group-game-night/).

### Flip 7

Designed by Eric Olsen and released by The Op at Gen Con 2024 in Indianapolis, Flip 7 plays 3 or more in about 20 minutes. In [Flip 7](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DWGVM7RY?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), you flip number cards into a row and add them up. Flip a number you already have and you bust, losing the round. Flip seven different numbers and you end the round with a bonus.

**How it differs:** it keeps Exploding Kittens' best feeling, "please don't be that card," without knocking anyone out of the game. It was nominated for the 2025 Spiel des Jahres. Learn it with [how to play Flip 7](/academy/flip-7/how-to-play-flip-7/), then see the real numbers in the lesson on [Flip 7 odds](/academy/flip-7/flip-7-odds/).

## If you loved the tension and the bluffing

Exploding Kittens is a guessing game at heart: who has the Defuse, and what is on top of the deck? These four make that guessing the whole game.

### Love Letter

Designed by Seiji Kanai and first published in 2012, Love Letter plays 2 to 6 in about 20 minutes in the current Z-Man edition. In [Love Letter](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07PHHBWM9?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), you hold one card and draw one card each turn, then play one. Cards let you guess someone's hand, compare hands, or peek, and a right guess knocks a player out of the round.

**How it differs:** the deck is tiny, so you can almost count what is left, and smart guessing wins more than luck. Rounds are so short that being knocked out never stings for long.

### Coup

Designed by Rikki Tahta and first published in 2012, Coup plays 2 to 6 in about 15 minutes. In [Coup](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00GDI4HX4?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), you hold two secret character cards and can claim to be any character to take its action. Anyone can call your bluff. Lose both cards and you are out.

**How it differs:** in Exploding Kittens, the deck decides who explodes. In Coup, other players do. It is sharper and more personal, and it shines with teens who love to argue. Our guide to [social deduction games](/articles/best-social-deduction-games/) covers it alongside bigger group games.

### Skull

Designed by Hervé Marly and first published in 2011, Skull plays 3 to 6 in under 45 minutes. In [Skull](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08R2BPLSN?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), everyone secretly stacks roses and skulls, then bids on how many roses they can flip without hitting a skull. Flip a skull and you lose a card.

**How it differs:** it has just a few cards per player and no text to read, so it works for anyone. All the tension of drawing from the Exploding Kittens deck, but here the danger is hidden by the person grinning across from you.

### Cockroach Poker

Designed by Jacques Zeimet and published by Drei Magier Spiele in 2004, Cockroach Poker plays 2 to 6 in about 20 minutes. In [Cockroach Poker](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07JYLC3DK?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), you pass a face-down critter card to another player and tell them what it is. They can call you a liar, or peek and pass it on. Collect four of the same critter and you lose.

**How it differs:** it is all about keeping a straight face, and the critter art keeps it light. It is a great pick for teens and adults together, since nobody needs to know any strategy to be a good liar.

## If you want something gentler

These three suit younger kids, quieter tables, or a group that wants the jokes with fewer attacks, or with more.

### Sushi Go!

Designed by Phil Walker-Harding and published by Gamewright, Sushi Go! plays 2 to 5 in about 15 minutes. In [Sushi Go!](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00J57VU44?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), hands of cards travel around the table like plates on a sushi belt. Each turn you keep one dish and pass the rest, trying to build the best-scoring meal.

**How it differs:** there are no attacks at all, just cute art and quick choices. It is a natural pick for kids who get upset when someone targets them. Learn it with [how to play Sushi Go!](/academy/sushi-go/how-to-play-sushi-go/), and the free [Sushi Go! course](/academy/sushi-go/) has strategy for older players.

### Sleeping Queens

Designed by Miranda Evarts, who invented it as a young child, and first published by Gamewright in 2005, Sleeping Queens plays 2 to 5 in about 20 minutes. In [Sleeping Queens](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0009XBY3A?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), you wake up queens with king cards, steal them with knights, and protect them with dragons. A little simple math helps too.

**How it differs:** it keeps Exploding Kittens' steal-and-protect back and forth, but it is softer and built for younger kids. It is an easy one to add to a list of [best family board games](/articles/best-family-board-games/).

### Munchkin

Designed by Steve Jackson and first published by Steve Jackson Games in 2001, Munchkin plays 3 to 6 in about 1 to 2 hours. In [Munchkin](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H7648MST?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), you kick down doors, fight monsters, and grab silly loot, while everyone else plays cards to make your fights harder. The first player to level 10 wins.

**How it differs:** it is longer and much meaner than Exploding Kittens, with more jokes on every card. When everyone gangs up on the leader, the game can stall, which our guide to [kingmaking in board games](/articles/kingmaking-board-games/) explains. Save it for teens who love the attacks.

## FAQ

### What game is most similar to Exploding Kittens?

Unstable Unicorns is closest in attitude, with cartoon art, attack cards, and plenty of chaos. Throw Throw Burrito comes from the same makers.

### What card games are like Exploding Kittens for kids?

Sleeping Queens, Sushi Go!, and Taco Cat Goat Cheese Pizza all suit younger players. Our [best family board games](/articles/best-family-board-games/) list has more by age.

### What is a good card game for teenagers?

Coup, Skull, Flip 7, and Here to Slay all work well with teens. For more ideas, see our guides to [board games for teenagers](/articles/board-games-for-teenagers-gift/) and [playing board games with teenagers](/articles/board-games-with-teenagers/).

### Are there games like Exploding Kittens without player elimination?

Yes. Flip 7, Unstable Unicorns, Here to Slay, Sushi Go!, and Taco Cat Goat Cheese Pizza keep everyone in until the end.

### What small card games make good gifts?

Most of this list fits in a small box. Our guides to [white elephant board games](/articles/white-elephant-board-games/) and [quick board games under 30 minutes](/articles/quick-board-games-under-30-minutes/) have more small picks.
