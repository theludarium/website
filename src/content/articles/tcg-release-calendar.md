## What is a prerelease, and why does the date matter?

A prerelease is a store event where you play with a new set before it hits shelves. Most games run it the same way: you buy a kit at the store, open the packs, build a deck from only what you opened, and play a few casual rounds. Nobody brings a prebuilt deck, so a brand-new player and a ten-year veteran start from the same pile of cards.

That makes the prerelease the single best on-ramp in the hobby. It is also the event that sells out first. Each game names it differently:

- **Magic:** Prerelease, the week before release. Six packs, a 40-card deck.
- **Pokémon:** Prerelease tournaments, built around the Build & Battle Box.
- **Lorcana:** Prerelease, usually one week before the set goes everywhere.
- **Riftbound:** Pre-Rift, the week before launch.
- **One Piece Card Game:** Store pre-release and release events, which vary by store.
- **Yu-Gi-Oh!:** Official Tournament Stores often get a booster set a couple of days before everyone else.

## How do you use a TCG release calendar well?

A few habits make the calendar useful instead of stressful.

**Call your store two weeks out.** Prerelease seats are limited, and many stores sell them in advance. Ask about the date, the start time, and what the entry includes.

**Pick one game per season.** Four big releases land between January 29 and February 12. Trying to chase all of them is how a hobby turns into a budget problem. Choose the game your friends play.

**Use starter products as your entry point.** If you are new, the best weeks to start are the ones with a starter deck or entry product: One Piece's December starter decks, Star Wars: Unlimited's Icons in November, or Riftbound's Proving Grounds in February.

**Remember the people.** A release week is the busiest week at most game stores. That means more players, more open seats at casual tables, and the easiest time all season to meet your local group.
