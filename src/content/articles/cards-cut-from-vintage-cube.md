The most interesting Vintage Cube cuts are not the weak cards. They are the famous ones: Tarmogoyf, Grave Titan, Wrath of God, Time Warp, Bazaar of Baghdad, Survival of the Fittest, Craterhoof Behemoth, Eternal Witness, Channel. Each left the MTGO Vintage Cube between August 2025 and October 2026, each with a one-line reason in the changelist, and each tells you something about what a powered cube looks like now.

This article takes the notable cuts from the six 2026 update posts (plus the two late-2025 ones that set them up), gives the curator's stated reason, and adds our verdict. Dates are the update's go-live date. Sources are the mtgo.com posts linked in each section. The current list is at [mtgo.com/vintage-cube-cardlist](https://www.mtgo.com/vintage-cube-cardlist), effective October 7, 2026, and none of the cards below are on it unless noted.

## Tarmogoyf and Grave Titan: when rate stops being enough

These two are the cleanest cases. Tarmogoyf was a two-mana 4/5 in a format with fetch lands and cheap spells. It still is. What changed is that a two-mana vanilla body, however large, does nothing on the turn it enters, and the 2026 Vintage Cube is built on cards that do. Kirol, Caustic Bronco, Ledger Shredder and Psychic Frog all cost two and all generate something. The March 2026 note, "has lost power," is shorthand for "does not generate advantage."

Grave Titan is the same story at six mana. It was the reanimation target of choice for a decade. In January 2026 it left for Alpha Deathclaw, a cheaper threat you can cast, and the list already had Griselbrand, Archon of Cruelty and Atraxa as the targets that end games on entry. Our general piece on [power creep and cube](/articles/power-creep-and-cube/) covers why this pattern is accelerating.

Verdict on both: deserved. If you still run either in a powered cube, test whether they win games or just fill a curve slot.

## Wrath of God and Time Warp: the end of two blue-white traditions

Wrath of God leaving on October 7, 2026 got attention because it is the original sweeper. The curator's note is that it is "resting" and white still has Damn and Sunfall. Damn does the same job with a one-mana mode. Sunfall exiles and leaves a body. Wrath is now the third-best version of itself. Deserved, and the "resting" label means it may come back when the count needs it.

Time Warp is the bigger statement. For most of the cube's life, blue extra-turn spells were automatic. The note "past its prime" means a five-mana sorcery that does nothing to the board is too slow for a format where Boros kills on turn four. Time Walk, at two mana, stays. Deserved, and we would expect Temporal Manipulation-type cards never to return.

## Bazaar of Baghdad and Survival of the Fittest: the graveyard retreat

These two left together on October 7, 2026, and the context is the whole 2026 arc. The January update built madness around discard outlets. The August post said madness and self-mill were "taking up too much space" and cut both Rootwallas, Vengevine and Anje's Ravager. October closed madness entirely. With no madness deck, Bazaar of Baghdad is a land that does not make mana and discards your hand. The curator did not need to write a reason; the row speaks.

Survival of the Fittest is the one we would argue about. It is a tutor for any creature, which means it finds Griselbrand for Reanimator, Titania for the Zuran Orb deck, and whatever the Welder deck wants to discard. Its cut row pairs it with The Echoverse Fulcrum, described as a Welder discard outlet, so the slot went to a narrower card that does a similar thing for one archetype. Debatable. Our guess is that Survival's problem was pick rate, not power: it is a card experienced drafters love and most league drafters pass.

## Channel, Leovold, Triplicate Titan: the repeat offenders

Some cards have now left twice or three times in fourteen months, and the pattern tells you more than any single cut.

- **Channel:** out August 2025 (frustrating play pattern, format too fast), back September 2025 to pair with The Endstone, out October 2026 ("cycled out"). Two exits with two different reasons means the card is marginal and the team keeps hoping. Deserved; expect it to stay out.
- **Leovold, Emissary of Trest:** out March 2026 (low on the punisher list for draw-sevens), back May 2026 (multicolor support), out October 2026 for Engineered Explosives. Three-color cards need a reason to exist beyond rate, and the five-color theme that brought it back lasted one update. Deserved.
- **Triplicate Titan:** out September 2025 (fell short in its strategies), back July 2026 to join Welder, out October 2026 for Ginger, Queen of Sweets as a castable Tinker target. Deserved; a nine-mana artifact only works when the cheat density is high, and that density keeps changing.

The lesson for paper owners is in [our piece on which changes to copy](/articles/vintage-cube-changes-for-paper/): a card that has been cut twice should not be in your cube on the strength of one return.

## Which cuts were reversed?

A cut on MTGO is cheap, so the curator reverses freely. The clearest 2026 reversals:

- **Dark Depths and Thespian's Stage:** out January 28, 2026 with the lands package, back August 19, 2026 with Crop Rotation, Sowing Mycospawn and both Reliquary cards. The team decided a two-card 20/20 was worth supporting after all.
- **Paradoxical Outcome:** out January ("rarely works out"), back July when the artifact count rose. The card did not change; the environment did.
- **Brightglass Gearhulk:** in September 2025, out August 2026, back October 2026 as a tutor for five combo pieces. Three moves in thirteen months.
- **Ephemerate:** in August 2025 for the warp mechanic, out October 2026 for Swift Reconfiguration.
- **Bloodchief's Thirst:** in August 2025, out January 2026, back March 2026, still in. Always close to the line.

A reversal is not an admission of error. It means the slot's value depends on what is around it, and the curator is willing to say so in public.

## What do the cuts have in common?

Three things, from reading all eight changelists:

1. **Cards that do not act on entry are leaving.** Tarmogoyf, Grave Titan, Time Warp, Bitterblossom, Wrath of God. The surviving list is full of cards that replace themselves, make a token, or deal damage the turn they resolve.
2. **Package cards leave with their package.** Yawgmoth, Bazaar, Survival, Crucible of Worlds, the Signets in 2023. If the archetype is rested, its tools go with it. This is what makes Vintage Cube cuts look dramatic from outside.
3. **Nothing is permanent except Power.** Black Lotus, the Moxen, Ancestral, Time Walk, Timetwister, Sol Ring, Library of Alexandria and Mishra's Workshop have not moved. Everything else has a price.

## FAQ

### Is Tarmogoyf in the Vintage Cube?

No. It was cut in the March 10, 2026 update with the note that it had lost power. It is not on the October 7, 2026 list.

### Is Bazaar of Baghdad in the Vintage Cube?

No. It left on October 7, 2026, in the same update that closed the madness archetype.

### Is Wrath of God in the Vintage Cube?

No, as of October 7, 2026. The curator describes it as resting, with Damn and Sunfall covering white's sweeper slots.

### Do cut cards come back?

Often. Dark Depths, Paradoxical Outcome, Brightglass Gearhulk, Leovold and Channel all left and returned at least once between August 2025 and October 2026.
