The best games like [Wingspan](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07YQ641NQ?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) depend on what hooked you. If it was the birds and the calm, play [Cascadia](https://link.amazon/B0g1HYFt4) or [Harmonies](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CVNPHPZY?tag=cubecommonw0a-20). If it was the engine that hums by round three, play [Earth](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0BWS9R9TZ?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), [Everdell](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09ZWQTTNR?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), or [Wyrmspan](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CJXNW71N?tag=cubecommonw0a-20). If you want Wingspan with a fresh coat of paint, play [Finspan](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DPJK3PRF?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) or [Wingspan Asia](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0BH72GXZ5?tag=cubecommonw0a-20). And if you finished your last game wishing it were harder, [Ark Nova](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09L6FCP9S?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) and [Terraforming Mars](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B01GSYA4K2?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) are waiting.

**The quick answer:** for most tables, start with **Cascadia** (lighter, faster, just as pretty) or **Everdell** (a bit heavier, with a city that grows like your aviary). For a group that already plays Wingspan every week, **Wyrmspan** is the natural next box. For the hobbyist who wants a long, crunchy evening, **Ark Nova** is the big sibling Wingspan fans keep reaching for.

Below are 12 picks, sorted by what you liked about Wingspan, with a comparison table, a "which one first" guide, and an honest note on how each one differs.

## What makes a game feel like Wingspan?

Wingspan, designed by Elizabeth Hargrave and published by Stonemaier Games in 2019, won the 2019 Kennerspiel des Jahres and brought a lot of people into modern board games. It plays 1 to 5 in 40 to 70 minutes. When people say they want "something like Wingspan," they usually mean one or more of four things:

1. **The nature theme.** Real birds, real habitats, and art you would frame. No war, no conquest.
2. **The engine.** Each bird you play adds a power that fires every time you use that row. Your board starts weak and ends up doing a lot per turn. If that idea is new, our guide to [engine building games](/articles/engine-building-games/) explains why it feels so good.
3. **The gentle vibe.** Little direct interaction. Nobody attacks your aviary. You can chat while you play.
4. **A satisfying puzzle in about an hour.** Enough decisions to feel clever, not so many that a new player drowns.

No game on this list hits all four exactly the way Wingspan does. That is the point. Pick the one that doubles down on the part you liked most.

## If you like Wingspan and want more of it

These three come straight from the Wingspan family. They are the safest bets because they share its rhythm: play a card into a row, then activate that row and watch your earlier cards fire.

### Wingspan Asia

The standalone small box, released in 2022. It plays 1 or 2 players on its own with a Duet mode built for two, adds a new set of Asian birds, and can be mixed into a base Wingspan box. Combined with the base game it also unlocks a Flock mode for 6 or 7 players.

**How it differs:** the Duet mode adds a shared map where you and your partner compete to place tokens, so two-player games get more head-to-head than base Wingspan. If you mostly play as a couple, this is the box to own. Our lesson on [Wingspan expansions](/academy/wingspan/wingspan-expansions/) compares it with the other add-ons.

### Finspan

Stonemaier's fish-themed sibling, designed by David Gordon and Michael O'Connell, with Elizabeth Hargrave helping develop it. It went on sale in early 2025 and plays 1 to 5 in 45 to 60 minutes.

**How it differs:** there is no food economy. You pay for a fish with cards from your hand, or with eggs and young from your ocean. Instead of three habitats you have three ocean zones (sunlight, twilight, and midnight), and you activate your fish by sending a diver down one of three dive sites. Eggs hatch into young, and three young become a school. It is a touch lighter than Wingspan and a little shorter, so a Wingspan group can switch to it in one teach.

### Wyrmspan

Designed by Connie Vogelmann and published by Stonemaier in 2024, it plays 1 to 5 in about 90 minutes. You run a dragon sanctuary: excavate caves, entice dragons to live in them, and send your adventurer through each cave row to collect what your dragons produce. A Dragon Guild track rewards progress along the way.

**How it differs:** it is heavier and longer. You have to dig a cave before a dragon can move in, which adds a step to every play, and every action costs a coin. Unspent coins carry into the next round, so you can save up for a bigger one. The engine starts slower and ends bigger. If Wingspan now feels a little light, this is your next box.

## Board games like Wingspan with a nature theme

If the birds, the art, and the quiet table were the draw, these nature board games deliver the mood with different puzzles underneath.

### Cascadia

Designed by Randy Flynn and published by Flatout Games and AEG in 2021, Cascadia won the 2022 Spiel des Jahres. It plays 1 to 4 in 30 to 45 minutes. Each turn you take one habitat tile and one wildlife token as a pair, add the tile to your growing landscape, and place the animal where it scores best. Bears want pairs, salmon want runs, hawks want space.

**How it differs:** there are no cards and no engine. Every turn is one small spatial puzzle, and the game teaches in a few minutes. It is calmer and faster than Wingspan, which makes it a great pick for friends who don't play yet. We compare the two in detail in [Wingspan vs Everdell vs Cascadia](/articles/wingspan-vs-everdell-vs-cascadia/), and the free [Cascadia course](/academy/cascadia/) covers the scoring cards.

### Harmonies

Designed by Johan Benvenuto and published by Libellud in 2024, Harmonies plays 1 to 4 in about 30 to 45 minutes. You take a set of colored tokens and stack them on your board to build forests, mountains, rivers, and fields. Animal cards show a small habitat pattern. When your landscape matches it, you place one of that animal's cubes there.

**How it differs:** it is a 3D pattern puzzle, not an engine. Your earlier choices shape what is possible later, but nothing "fires." It shares Wingspan's core image, animals finding homes in the right habitat, in a shorter game.

### Meadow

Designed by Klemens Kalicki and published by Rebel Studio in 2021, Meadow plays 1 to 4 in 60 to 90 minutes. You place path tokens on a grid of face-up cards to take one, then add it to your meadow. Most cards need certain symbols already in your tableau before they can be played, so you build up from ground cover to plants to animals.

**How it differs:** it trades the engine for prerequisites. Your tableau grows like a food chain rather than a machine. The art is a deliberate echo of a naturalist's sketchbook and may be the closest thing on this list to Wingspan's look.

### Parks

Designed by Henry Audubon and published by Keymaster Games in 2019, Parks plays 1 to 5 in 40 to 60 minutes. Two hikers walk a trail over four seasons, collecting resources along the way, then spend them to visit national parks. The cards use art from the Fifty-Nine Parks print series.

**How it differs:** it is a light set collection game with a shared trail, so you block each other a little more than in Wingspan, and there is no engine to speak of. It shines with families and with anyone who loves the outdoors.

## Engine building games like Wingspan

If the best part of Wingspan was the moment your board started doing three things per action, these engine building games give you that hit with new themes.

### Earth

Designed by Maxime Tardif and published by Inside Up Games in 2023, Earth plays 1 to 5 in 45 to 90 minutes. You build a 4 by 4 grid of plant, terrain, and other nature cards. On your turn you pick one of four actions (planting, composting, watering, or growing), and every player takes that action too, with the chooser getting a stronger version.

**How it differs:** nobody waits. Everyone acts on every turn, which fixes the downtime some groups feel at five-player Wingspan. Card powers chain hard by the end, so it lands a little heavier than Wingspan. It won the solo award at the 2023 International Gamers Awards, so it is a strong pick if you play alone.

### Everdell

Designed by James A. Wilson and published by Starling Games in 2018, Everdell plays 1 to 4 in 40 to 80 minutes. You send critter workers to gather twigs, resin, pebbles, and berries, then spend them to build a woodland city of up to 15 cards. Buildings let you play matching critters for free, so your city builds its own logic over four seasons.

**How it differs:** it adds worker placement, so you compete for spaces on a shared board, and the teach is longer. It is the most common "next game" for Wingspan players, and our head-to-head [Wingspan vs Everdell](/articles/wingspan-vs-everdell/) goes deeper. The [Everdell course](/academy/everdell/) has a full rules walkthrough.

### Splendor

Designed by Marc André and published by Space Cowboys in 2014, Splendor plays 2 to 4 in 30 minutes. You take gem tokens and buy development cards. Each card you own acts as a permanent gem discount, so cards get cheaper the more you have, until the big ones cost little or nothing.

**How it differs:** no theme to speak of and no tableau to admire, just the engine in its purest form. If you want to know whether you love engine building or just love birds, play Splendor. It also fits a weeknight better than anything else here. Start with [how to play Splendor](/academy/splendor/how-to-play-splendor/).

## If you like Wingspan and want a bigger brain-burn

These two are for players who finish Wingspan and want to keep going. Both are much heavier. Plan for a long first game and a patient teacher.

### Ark Nova

Designed by Mathias Wigge, published by Feuerland Spiele in 2021 and in English by Capstone Games, Ark Nova plays 1 to 4 in 90 to 150 minutes. You build a modern zoo: place enclosures on your map, then play animal cards that need the right enclosure size and habitat icons, and support conservation projects that score on a second track.

**How it differs:** it is Wingspan's theme (play animals into the right habitat, collect icons, chase goals) scaled up into a heavy strategy game. It uses five action cards that get stronger the longer you wait to use them, which is a clever system with a real learning curve. Many Wingspan fans call it their favorite game once they get there. The [Ark Nova course](/academy/ark-nova/) breaks it into manageable steps.

### Terraforming Mars

Designed by Jacob Fryxelius and published by FryxGames in 2016 (Stronghold Games in the US), Terraforming Mars plays 1 to 5 in about two hours. Each player runs a corporation buying project cards to raise Mars's temperature, oxygen, and oceans. Cards add production that pays you every generation.

**How it differs:** the theme is science, not nature, but the engine feels the closest of any heavy game to Wingspan's: buy cards, build production, watch it compound. There are more than 200 project cards, and the game runs long. Read [how to play Terraforming Mars](/academy/terraforming-mars/how-to-play-terraforming-mars/) before your first game.

### A quick bonus: Faraway

If you want the Wingspan feeling in 20 minutes, try **[Faraway](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CZG14VHH?tag=cubecommonw0a-20)**, designed by Johannes Goupy and Corentin Lebrat and published by Catch Up Games in 2023. It plays 2 to 6 in 15 to 30 minutes. You lay eight cards in a row, one per round, then score them in reverse, so each card can only see the cards played after it. It is a card game, not a nature game, but it scratches the same "my tableau finally came together" itch.

## FAQ

### What is the closest game to Wingspan?

Finspan, followed by Wyrmspan. Both come from the same publisher and keep Wingspan's row-activation engine. Finspan is a little lighter and shorter; Wyrmspan is heavier and runs longer.

### Is Everdell harder than Wingspan?

A little. Everdell adds worker placement and has more card types and free-play rules to learn, so the first game takes longer. Once it clicks, the two sit at a similar level.

### What are good nature board games for beginners?

Cascadia and Harmonies. Both teach in a few minutes, play in under an hour, and have no direct conflict. Parks is another easy choice for families.

### Are there engine building games like Wingspan that play well solo?

Yes. Earth won the solo award at the 2023 International Gamers Awards, and Wingspan itself has a solo Automa. Ark Nova, Everdell, Wyrmspan, and Finspan all include solo modes too.

### Should I get a Wingspan expansion or a new game?

If your group still enjoys Wingspan, an expansion adds variety without a new teach. If the game has started to feel samey, a new box like Everdell or Wyrmspan changes more.
