Ask a Dungeon Crawler Carl fan how to start the series and you will usually get the same answer: listen to it. The audiobooks are narrated by Jeff Hays, and for many fans his performance is the reason they fell for the books at all.

**The quick answer:** the Dungeon Crawler Carl audiobook is so good because one performer, Jeff Hays, gives every character a distinct voice, from Carl's deep, tired growl to the dungeon's gleeful announcements, and plays the comedy with real timing. Later books add guest narrators. Start with [book one on Audible](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08V8B2CGV?tag=cubecommonw0a-20); new listeners can begin with a free trial.

## Who narrates Dungeon Crawler Carl?

Jeff Hays narrates every book in the series. He is a voice actor who has narrated over 200 audiobooks, a musician and composer, and the founder of Soundbooth Theater, the company that produces the Dungeon Crawler Carl audiobooks. He started Soundbooth in 2017, a few years into his narration career, and over time brought music and sound effects into its productions.

The audiobooks are Audible exclusives, so Audible is the only place to hear his version.

## What makes his performance stand out

### Every character sounds like someone

Reviewers keep coming back to one point: Hays gives each character their own voice. In a series with a huge cast of crawlers, hosts, monsters and alien bureaucrats, that matters. You always know who is talking, often before the sentence says so.

### Carl's voice has a story of its own

In an interview with Grimdark Magazine, Hays explained that Carl's voice grew out of a Patrick Warburton impression he had used as a character voice for years. It came to him almost instantly, and it has deepened as he has lived with the character. Carl's voice is much deeper and rougher than Hays's own, and he has said it limits how long he can record in a day.

That makes one later book a treat: Patrick Warburton himself joins the cast for book six.

### The dungeon gets to be a character

So much of the series is the World Dungeon talking to the crawlers: notifications, achievements, rule changes and announcements. On the page, those are formatted text. Performed out loud, they become a voice with a personality, and the jokes in them land the way a game master's would at a table.

### The author writes him challenges

Hays has said that Matt Dinniman writes in odd accents and voice combinations to test him, several times a book by now. The result is a performance that keeps finding new voices eight books in.

### Comedy needs timing

Dungeon Crawler Carl is funny, and a lot of its humor is in rhythm: a pause before a terrible item description, a deadpan reply from Carl, Donut's outrage. Hays has said the scenes play out vividly in his mind as he performs, and it shows in the timing.

## FAQ

### Who reads the Dungeon Crawler Carl audiobooks?

Jeff Hays narrates the whole series. Book three adds The Critical Drinker, and book six has a full cast that includes Patrick Warburton.

### Is the Dungeon Crawler Carl audiobook better than the book?

The story is the same. Many fans prefer the audiobook because Jeff Hays's character voices and comic timing add to it, but the best format is the one you will finish.

### Can I get the Dungeon Crawler Carl audiobook outside Audible?

Not the classic narration: it is an Audible exclusive. Soundbooth Theater sells its own full-cast version of book one on its platform.

### Is Patrick Warburton in Dungeon Crawler Carl?

Yes, in book six, The Eye of the Bedlam Bride, as part of its full cast. Jeff Hays has said Carl's voice started as his own Patrick Warburton impression.

### Is the audiobook family friendly?

No. The books are written for adults, with strong language, violence and crude humor, and the performance does not soften any of it.
