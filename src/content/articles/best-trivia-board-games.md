## What makes a trivia game work at a family gathering?

A holiday table is the hardest crowd in games: three generations, mixed interests, and someone who would rather be watching football. The trivia games that work there share a few things:

1. **Everyone has a chance.** Pure recall games reward whoever knows the most. The best family trivia games add betting, guessing, or teams, so a ten-year-old and a retiree can both win.
2. **Questions that spark talk.** "What year was the microwave invented?" starts a story. A good trivia game is an excuse for the table to talk.
3. **Teams.** Teams hide weak spots. Pair a teenager with a grandparent and both feel useful. Our guide to [board games for all ages](/articles/multigenerational-board-games/) covers how to mix generations well.
4. **A short game.** After a big meal, attention spans run about 30 minutes. Play to a set score, not until someone collapses.

If you are hosting a bigger crowd, our guide on [how to host a trivia night](/articles/how-to-host-trivia-night/) covers question writing, rounds, and scoring for a room full of teams.

## Classic trivia

Sometimes the family wants real questions and real answers. This is the one to get.

### Trivial Pursuit Family Edition

Trivial Pursuit was created in Canada by Chris Haney and Scott Abbott and became a worldwide hit in the 1980s. It is now published by Hasbro. In [Trivial Pursuit Family Edition](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00NQQTXNK?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), you roll and move around a wheel-shaped board, answering questions in six categories to earn colored wedges. The family edition has separate question cards for adults and for kids, so a young player gets questions they can answer.

**Why it fits:** it is the trivia game most families already know, and the kids' cards fix its biggest problem. It can run long, so agree on a shorter goal before you start, such as three wedges each instead of six. If a full game is too much after a big meal, read cards aloud in teams and skip the board.

## Trivia where you do not need to know the answer

These two are the best picks for mixed ages, because a good guess or a smart bet counts as much as knowing the fact.

### Wits & Wagers Family

Wits & Wagers is a party game by Dominic Crapuchettes and Nate Heasley, first published by North Star Games in 2005. The family edition plays 3 to 10 in about 25 minutes. In [Wits & Wagers Family](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B003BLQIQK?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), every question has a number for an answer. Everyone writes a guess, the guesses go in order on the table, and then you bet on which one is closest.

**Why it fits:** nobody knows how many bones are in a giraffe's neck, and that is the point. The know-it-all can be beaten by a kid who bets on the right guess. The family edition has simpler rules and questions written for younger players. For a bigger crowd, our guide to [games for 30 people](/articles/games-for-30-people/) shows how to run a Wits & Wagers style round for a whole room.

### Timeline

Timeline is a card game published by Asmodee. The current edition plays 2 to 6 in about 15 minutes. In [Timeline](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0G7NLCKRB?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), each card shows an event or invention on one side and its year on the other. On your turn you place a card into the shared line where you think it belongs, then flip it. Wrong, and you take a new card. The first player to empty their hand wins.

**Why it fits:** you never need the exact year, only whether something came before or after. That makes it easy for kids to play and fun for grandparents who remember when things arrived. Rounds are short, so it slides in while dessert is on the way.

## Music and pop culture trivia

These two suit teens and adults, and the room tends to get loud.

### Hitster

Hitster is a music party game from Jumbo. It plays 2 to 10, and the box is listed for ages 16 and up. In [Hitster](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0D6WB39QC?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), you scan a song card with a free app on your phone and the song plays. Then you place it in your own timeline by the year it came out. Name the artist and title for bonus chips. The first player to build a line of 10 songs wins.

**Why it fits:** it turns a family's mixed music taste into the best part of the game. Grandparents nail the oldies, teens nail the new songs, and everyone sings along. Check the box for what the app needs before the holiday, so nobody is setting up accounts while the songs wait.

### Linkee

Linkee is a quiz game published by Big Potato and listed for ages 14 and up. In [Linkee](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07VKHK46W?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), the reader asks four trivia questions. The answers share a hidden link, and the first player or team to shout it out wins the card.

**Why it fits:** you can miss every question and still win by spotting the link, so it rewards quick thinking over memory. It plays well in teams around a crowded table and works as a living room quiz with almost no setup.

## When half the family hates trivia

Not everyone likes being quizzed. These five give the same "shout it out" fun with no fact questions at all. They are also strong picks for [people who don't like games](/articles/board-games-for-people-who-dont-like-games/).

### Herd Mentality

Published by Big Potato, Herd Mentality plays 4 to 20, ages 10 and up, in about 15 to 20 minutes. In [Herd Mentality](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B093HBBMPT?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), the reader asks a question like "What's the best pizza topping?" and everyone writes an answer. If you match the majority, you score. Be the only odd answer and you get stuck with the pink cow.

**Why it fits:** there are no right answers, only popular ones, so it is impossible to be bad at it. It is a natural fit for big holiday gatherings, and it is in our guide to [party games for large groups](/articles/party-games-for-large-groups/).

### Just One

Designed by Ludovic Roudy and Bruno Sautter and published by Repos Production, Just One won the 2019 Spiel des Jahres. It plays 3 to 7 in about 20 minutes. In [Just One](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07GJ7NDZQ?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), everyone writes a one-word clue to help one player guess a secret word. Any matching clues are erased before the guesser sees them.

**Why it fits:** it is cooperative, so the whole table wins or loses together. That makes it the gentlest pick here for kids who hate losing and relatives who hate competing.

### Codenames

Designed by Vlaada Chvátil and published by Czech Games Edition in 2015, Codenames won the 2016 Spiel des Jahres. It plays 4 to 8 or more. In [Codenames](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B014Q1XX9S?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), two teams race to find their secret words on a grid of 25, using one-word clues from their spymaster.

**Why it fits:** it rewards knowing how your family thinks, not knowing facts. A grandparent's clue only a grandchild would get is the best moment of the night. Learn it with [how to play Codenames](/academy/codenames/how-to-play-codenames/), and the free [Codenames course](/academy/codenames/) has tips for better clues.

### Wavelength

Designed by Alex Hague, Justin Vickers, and Wolfgang Warsch, Wavelength is listed for 2 to 12 players. In [Wavelength](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07T446163?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), one player sees a hidden target on a spectrum, like "cold to hot," and gives a clue. The team argues about where the clue lands and turns the dial.

**Why it fits:** it is half game, half friendly debate. Is soup a hot food or a warm food? The arguments are the fun, and nobody needs to know a single fact.

### Anomia

Anomia is a fast card game for 3 to 6 players, ages 10 and up. In [Anomia](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00363B5FC?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), players flip category cards. When two symbols match, those two players race to shout an example of the other's category, like "a breakfast cereal" or "a famous painter."

**Why it fits:** you know every answer, you just cannot think of it fast enough. That frozen moment is why everyone ends up laughing, and it is short enough to play while the pie cools.

## FAQ

### What is the best trivia board game for families?

Wits & Wagers Family, because you can win by betting on someone else's guess. Trivial Pursuit Family Edition is the best pick for classic question-and-answer play.

### What trivia games work for kids and adults together?

Wits & Wagers Family, Timeline, and Trivial Pursuit Family Edition all work with mixed ages. For more ideas, see our guides to [Thanksgiving board games](/articles/thanksgiving-board-games/) and [Christmas games for the whole family](/articles/christmas-games-for-family/).

### What is a good trivia game for adults?

Hitster and Linkee are both listed for teens and adults and play best with a lively crowd. Our list of [Christmas party games for adults](/articles/christmas-party-games-for-adults/) has more.

### What can we play if some people hate trivia?

Herd Mentality, Just One, Codenames, and Wavelength have no fact questions at all. Everyone can play without feeling quizzed.

### How do I run trivia for a big holiday party?

Split into teams of four to six, play to a set number of rounds, and keep each round short.
