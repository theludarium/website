The most recent Vintage Cube changes went live on Magic Online on October 7, 2026, timed to the Reality Fracture release. The update swaps 35 cards: the madness package leaves, landfall returns with real support, two infinite-mana combos come in, and red gets a prowess and spellslinger push. The curator's article is [Magic Online Vintage Cube | Reality Fracture Update](https://www.mtgo.com/news/fra-vintage-cube-update), posted October 2, 2026, and the full list is at [mtgo.com/vintage-cube-cardlist](https://www.mtgo.com/vintage-cube-cardlist).

This piece walks through the four themes and every swap. Dates and card names are taken from that post. Where we add opinion, we say so.

## What are the four themes of the October 2026 update?

**Ending the Madness.** Madness ran as a supported archetype from the January 2026 Lorwyn Eclipsed update through the summer. The October post says niche strategies deserve space but the cube needs shake-ups to keep novelty real, so madness is rested "for now," with the curator expecting it back eventually. Blazing Rootwalla and Basking Rootwalla had already left in August.

**Making Landfall.** The post calls landfall a classic, nearly forgotten cube archetype, revived by Mole Man, Moloid Master (added July 2026). Quirion Ranger keeps triggers going when Moxen replace lands. Carnivorous Cultivator plus a fetch land gives a steady stream of lands, and with Strip Mine can lock an opponent out. Avatar of Burgeoning Echoes and Escape to the Wilds keep cards flowing.

**Devotion to Combo.** The headline is Auriok Salvagers plus Lion's Eye Diamond or Black Lotus, the "Bomberman" combo, with Pyrite Spellbomb as the finisher. The post says this stayed out for years because it was slow to execute on MTGO, and that the client's recently introduced automation tools now make it feasible. Those tools were announced in [Combo Play Improvements, August 5, 2026](https://www.mtgo.com/news/combo-play-improvements); the August 4 weekly post quoted Bomberman at six clicks per loop. Devoted Druid plus Swift Reconfiguration returns as a second infinite-mana line. Enlightened Tutor returns to find Painter's Servant and Grindstone, which came in during August. Selesnya gets Brightglass Gearhulk, which can tutor Zuran Orb, Fastbond, LED, Grindstone, or Swift Reconfiguration, and Marwyn, the Preserver plus Six for infinite mana with LED or Lotus.

**Red Regaining Prowess.** The post says mono-red faded once Boros became dominant, and that "Boros remains the strongest archetype even after trimming its numbers," so aggressive slots stay limited. The new red cards are built for mono-red and Izzet spellslinger rather than traditional Boros. The Fantasticar, added in August, is the payoff that double spells like Lava Dart and adventure or prepare cards turn on.

## Which cuts matter most?

Our read, not the curator's:

- **Bazaar of Baghdad and Survival of the Fittest** leaving together is the clearest signal. Both are graveyard engines, and the August post had already said madness and self-mill were "taking up too much space." With the Rootwallas, Vengevine, and Anje's Ravager gone in August and madness closed in October, there was nothing left for them to do.
- **Time Warp** out is a power-level statement. Blue extra-turn cards used to be automatic in a powered cube. The post calls it "past its prime," which matches a cube where games are decided by turn four.
- **Channel** has now left twice in fourteen months. It went out in August 2025 for a frustrating play pattern, came back in September 2025 to pair with The Endstone, and leaves again now. Expect it to stay out.
- **Eternal Witness** out with Emeritus of Abundance (added May 2026) named as its replacement is a small example of a big trend: an old staple replaced by a strictly better new card. Our piece on [power creep and cube](/articles/power-creep-and-cube/) covers that pattern.

## Which additions matter most?

- **Auriok Salvagers plus Pyrite Spellbomb** is the first time the MTGO Vintage Cube has supported Bomberman, as far as the posts say. The curator's stated reason is the client, not the cards. That is worth noticing: a card's cube-worthiness on MTGO depends on clicks, and paper owners do not share the constraint.
- **The red package** is ten cards in one color. Monastery Swiftspear, Soul-Scar Mage, Slickshot Show-Off, Stingcaster Mage, Pompous Battlemage, Lava Dart, Mutagenic Growth, Flashback, Gandalf, and Dead // Gone are a full lane, not a hint. Note that Slickshot Show-Off and Mutagenic Growth both left in January 2026 and both return now, so the team is confident about the pieces and was only waiting on a home.
- **Devoted Druid plus Swift Reconfiguration** needs one mana and two cards and wins the game, which is as cheap as combo gets. Watch whether it survives the next update.

## FAQ

### When did the October 2026 Vintage Cube update go live?

Wednesday, October 7, 2026, at 10 a.m. PT, with the league running until the November 4 downtime per the [October 13 weekly announcements](https://www.mtgo.com/news/mtgo101326).

### How many cards changed?

35 in and 35 out, out of 540. The post's own description says "over 30."

### Is madness gone for good?

The post says it is rested "for now" and that the curator expects it to return. Madness ran from January through September 2026.

### Why did Auriok Salvagers get added now?

The post credits MTGO's new combo automation, announced August 5, 2026, for making the Salvagers loop practical within a match clock.
