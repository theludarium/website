Stocking stuffer games have a simple test: the box has to fit in a sock, and the game inside has to be good enough that someone chooses it over the phone they also got. More games pass the first test than the second. The twelve below pass both.

Each is a card, dice, or tile game in a box small enough to wrap in a napkin. Most cost under $20. Prices were checked at publisher or major retailer pages at the time of writing and will move in December.

## What makes a small game worth a stocking?

Small boxes are where a lot of the best design in the hobby lives, because cards are cheap to print and designers get to be clever instead of lavish. The ones that work as gifts share three things.

- **They teach in one minute.** A stocking game gets opened on the floor at 7 a.m. The rules have to survive that.
- **They play in fifteen minutes or less.** It is going to be played between breakfast and the next thing. Short rounds mean it gets played at all.
- **They play at two and at five.** You do not know who will be around when the stocking is opened.

## The 12 best stocking stuffer games

### Card games

**Love Letter** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (about $16; [$15.99 at the Asmodee store](https://store.asmodee.com/products/love-letter-classic-eco-box)). Sixteen cards, a red velvet bag, three-minute rounds. Two to six players. The bag alone makes it feel like a present. If you buy one game from this list, buy this.

**Sushi Go!** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (about $15; [Noble Knight lists the tin at $15.49](https://www.nobleknight.com/P/2147556632)). Pass hands of sushi cards and collect sets. The tin is the right size and shape for a stocking. Two to five players, ages eight and up.

**The Crew: Mission Deep Sea** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (about $15; [Walmart lists it at $14.95](https://www.walmart.com/ip/937559244)). A cooperative trick-taking game with fifty missions. The box is a little larger than the others here but still sock-sized. Best at three to five.

**Catan on the Road** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (about $8 to $10; [$9.99 at the Asmodee store](https://store.asmodee.com/products/catan-on-the-road) and [$7.99 at Miniature Market](https://www.miniaturemarket.com/Catan-On-the-Road/CN3143) at the time of writing). A pocket card version of Catan that plays in fifteen minutes. The cheapest recognizable name on this list.

**The Fox in the Forest** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (about $11 to $16; Wargamer tracked it at [$11.17 on Black Friday 2025](https://www.wargamer.com/board-games/love-letter-black-friday-2025-deal)). A two-player trick-taking game with a fairy-tale deck and a twist: win too many tricks and you lose. The best two-player card game at this size.

**Poetry for Neanderthals** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (about $10 to $20; the same Wargamer piece listed it at $9.99 on sale). Describe a word using only single-syllable words, or get bonked with an inflatable club. Loud, silly, and a reliable hit with teenagers and uncles alike. Two or more players.

### Dice games

**Qwixx** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (about $12; [$11.99 at Walmart](https://www.walmart.com/ip/Gamewright-Qwixx-Dice-Game-Ages-8/43459970) at the time of writing). Roll dice, cross off numbers on a score sheet, try to build long runs. Everyone plays on every roll, so there is no waiting. Fifteen minutes, two to five players. It fits in a coat pocket.

**Yahtzee** or **Farkle** in a tube <!-- AFFILIATE --> (about $8 to $13; [Walmart lists Yahtzee to Go at $12.60](https://www.walmart.com/ip/YAHTZEE-TO-GO/180836052) at the time of writing). Not clever. Reliable. Both come in tube packaging that was designed for exactly this moment, and both are already understood by every adult at the table.

### Pocket strategy

**Hive Pocket** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (about $19 to $26 depending on retailer, per [ShopSavvy](https://shopsavvy.com/products/hive-pocket)). Insect tiles, no board, chess-like play in ten minutes. The one item here that a serious strategy player will be glad to receive. It comes in a drawstring bag.

**Onitama** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (about $28 to $35; [$34.99 at Arcane Wonders](https://www.arcanewonders.com/product/onitama/) and [$27.99 at Miniature Market](https://www.miniaturemarket.com/awgdte02ona.html) at the time of writing). Over the stocking budget and borderline on size, but a beautiful two-player abstract on a small cloth board. Mentioned because people ask.

### Word and party

**Just One** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (about $25; [$24.99 at the Asmodee store](https://store.asmodee.com/products/just-one-refresh) at the time of writing). A cooperative word game for three to seven: everyone writes a one-word clue, duplicates are discarded, the guesser works from what is left. The box is a stretch for a stocking but it is the game most likely to be played all afternoon.

**Anomia** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (about $13 to $22; [$13.20 on sale at Walmart](https://www.walmart.com/ip/Anomia-Card-Game/33358572) and [$21.60 direct from Anomia Press](https://www.anomiapress.com/collections/anomia/products/anomia) at the time of writing). Flip cards; when your symbol matches someone else's, race to name an example of the category on their card. Chaotic, fast, and it makes smart people say "a type of fish" and then nothing.

## Does the box really have to be small?

A stocking stuffer is partly about the moment of discovery, so yes, size matters. But there is a second reason to favor small games: small games get played more.

A box that fits in a bag goes to the restaurant while you wait for a table, to the cabin, to the hospital waiting room. The game that is always nearby is the game that gets played. In our own bags, Love Letter and Hive Pocket have more plays than anything on the shelf at home. We have written about this effect in our piece on [quick board games under 30 minutes](/articles/quick-board-games-under-30-minutes/).

## What to skip

- **Tiny versions of big games.** Travel Catan, Monopoly Deal, mini Scrabble. The miniaturization usually removes the fun or the legibility or both. Catan on the Road is the exception because it is a different game, not a shrunken one.
- **Novelty decks.** A deck of cards with a theme printed on it is a deck of cards. Fine, but not a game.
- **Dexterity games with many small parts.** They are fun and they will be under the couch by New Year's.
- **Anything that needs a phone app.** The stocking game should work when the phone is dead, which, by the time the stockings are opened, it will be.

## The best stocking is one game and a promise

Put one small game in the stocking and write on the gift tag: "First game is this afternoon." Then clear the table and do it. The game is twelve dollars. The afternoon is the gift. The game is the reason. The people are the point.

## FAQ

### What are good stocking stuffer games for adults?

Love Letter, The Fox in the Forest, and Hive Pocket. All are under $26 at the time of writing, fit in a stocking, and hold up to repeated play.

### What is a good small game for kids' stockings?

Sushi Go! for ages eight and up, Qwixx for anyone who can add. Both play in fifteen minutes and cost about $12 to $15.

### Are there two-player games small enough for a stocking?

Yes. The Fox in the Forest, Hive Pocket, and Love Letter (which plays two) all come in pocket-sized boxes or bags.

### How much should a stocking stuffer game cost?

Most of the good ones are $8 to $20. Above $25 you are into gift territory, not stocking territory.
