The Vintage Cube archetypes that matter in October 2026 are, by the curator's own account, Boros aggro at the top, then a layer of combo (Welder artifacts, Bomberman, Painter's Servant, Devoted Druid, Dark Depths), the blue power decks, Reanimator and Sneak, and two new lanes on trial: landfall and red prowess. This is a snapshot of the list effective October 7, 2026, built from the [Reality Fracture update post](https://www.mtgo.com/news/fra-vintage-cube-update) of October 2, 2026 and the [full card list](https://www.mtgo.com/vintage-cube-cardlist).

Everything below names cards that are on that list today. When the November update lands, some of it will be wrong, and that is the point of dating it.

## What is the strongest archetype in the Vintage Cube?

Boros. The October 2026 post says it directly: "Boros remains the strongest archetype even after trimming its numbers." The deck is white and red one- and two-drops with fast mana and equipment, and it has been the top deck long enough that the curator has been cutting white aggro slots since January 2026, when Ademi, Ranger-Captain of Eos and Descendant of Storms left with notes about white aggro being "saturated."

What is still there: Mother of Runes, Giver of Runes, Ocelot Pride, Adeline, Resplendent Cathar, Luminarch Aspirant, Elite Spellbinder, Ragavan, Nimble Pilferer, Gut, True Soul Zealot, Headliner Scarlett, Screaming Nemesis, Figure of Destiny, Phlage, Titan of Fire's Fury, Forth Eorlingas!, Smuggler's Copter, Umezawa's Jitte, Kaldra Compleat, plus Chain Lightning, Lightning Bolt, Burst Lightning, Galvanic Discharge, and Unholy Heat for removal. The gold section for Boros is small (Figure of Destiny, Forth Eorlingas!, Otharri, Phlage, Zirda) but the mono-colored halves are deep.

If you want to understand why aggro works in a powered cube at all, our piece on [aggro in cube](/articles/aggro-in-cube/) covers the general case. The short version here: Moxen make everyone's turn two faster, and the deck with the cheapest threats benefits most.

## What combo decks does the Vintage Cube support?

More than it did a year ago. The 2026 updates moved steadily toward supported A+B combos, and the list now holds at least six.

| Combo | Key pieces on the list | Arrived or returned |
|---|---|---|
| Goblin Welder artifacts | Goblin Welder, Goblin Engineer, Sewer-veillance Cam, Cryogen Relic, Myr Battlesphere, Coveted Jewel, The Echoverse Fulcrum | July and August 2026 |
| Bomberman | Auriok Salvagers, Lion's Eye Diamond, Black Lotus, Pyrite Spellbomb, Aether Spellbomb | October 2026 |
| Painter mill | Painter's Servant, Grindstone, Enlightened Tutor, Brightglass Gearhulk | August 2026, tutors October |
| Devoted Druid | Devoted Druid, Swift Reconfiguration, Walking Ballista | October 2026 |
| Dark Depths | Dark Depths, Thespian's Stage, Crop Rotation, Sowing Mycospawn, Knight and Wight of the Reliquary, Elvish Reclaimer, Absorbing Man | August 2026, more October |
| Marwyn and Six | Marwyn, the Preserver, Six, LED or Lotus, Tamiyo's Safekeeping | October 2026 |
| Thopter Foundry | Thopter Foundry, Sword of the Meek, Urza, Lord High Artificer | Long-standing |
| Fastbond and Zuran Orb | Fastbond, Zuran Orb, Titania, Protector of Argoth, Baloth Prime | Long-standing, Crucible left January 2026 |

The Welder shop is the one the curator has invested in most. The July 2026 Marvel update built it around Cam plus Welder for an infinite loop, and the August Hobbit update said "Goblin Welder is considered a success" and added trinkets. Engineer tutors the pieces, Mystic Forge and The Fantasticar give it more artifacts to flip, and Tezzeret, Cruel Captain and Urza round it out.

Bomberman is the newest and the one most tied to the client. The October post credits MTGO's August combo automation for making the loop practical. Pyrite Spellbomb is the kill; Aether Spellbomb and Chromatic Star are the draw.

Blue combo in the old sense is thinner. Time Vault is not on the [October 7 list](https://www.mtgo.com/vintage-cube-cardlist) and has been gone for years. High Tide left in October. What remains: Thassa's Oracle as a Doomsday kill (neither Demonic Consultation nor Tainted Pact is on the list); Underworld Breach with Brain Freeze and LED; Tendrils of Agony with the rituals; and Paradoxical Outcome, which returned in July 2026 to go with the artifact density.

## What are the blue decks?

Three overlapping shells.

**Power blue.** Ancestral Recall, Time Walk, Timetwister, the Moxen, Sol Ring, Mana Crypt, Mana Vault, Tinker into Blightsteel Colossus or Portal to Phyrexia, Tolarian Academy, Upheaval, Time Spiral, Echo of Eons. Jace, the Mind Sculptor and Narset, Parter of Veils for control. This deck has existed as long as the cube has and the only question each update is which finisher is in. Right now Triplicate Titan is out again (October) and Ginger, Queen of Sweets is the secondary Tinker target.

**Izzet spellslinger.** Vivi Ornitier, Cori-Steel Cutter, Third Path Iconoclast, Thundertrap Trainer, Stormchaser's Talent, Flame of Anor, Expressive Iteration, Fable of the Mirror-Breaker, and the cantrips. The August 2025 post called this the cube's expanding "noncreature matters" space. In October 2026 it also gets the red prowess additions as crossover.

**Tempo.** Brazen Borrower, Ledger Shredder, The Wondrous Wasp, Fallen Shinobi, Psychic Frog, Kaito, Bane of Nightmares, Daze, Spell Pierce, Memory Lapse, Stern Scolding, Boomerang Basics. Dimir has the deepest gold section for this.

## What happened to the graveyard decks?

Reanimator is fine. Entomb, Animate Dead, Reanimate, Necromancy, Exhume, Shallow Grave, Corpse Dance, Unearth and Recurring Nightmare are all present, with Griselbrand, Archon of Cruelty, Atraxa, Grand Unifier, Troll of Khazad-dûm, Emperor of Bones and Overlord of the Balemurk as targets. Show and Tell, Sneak Attack, Through the Breach and Flash give the cheat deck a second axis with Emrakul and Worldspine Wurm.

Everything else graveyard has been trimmed. The madness package that ran January through September 2026 is closed. Bazaar of Baghdad and Survival of the Fittest left in October, Vengevine and both Rootwallas in August, Hogaak in October. Aristocrats, which the January 2026 post called the main new focus, was gone by May with the note that it "performed well" but not well enough to be permanent. The sacrifice remnants (Goblin Bombardment, Carrion Feeder gone, Super Shredder and Sedgemoor Witch still in) are now support for Chain of Smog plus Witherbloom Apprentice rather than a deck.

## Which archetypes are on trial right now?

**Landfall.** The October post's "Making Landfall" section. Mole Man, Moloid Master, Quirion Ranger, Carnivorous Cultivator, Elvish Reclaimer, Marwyn, Scythecat Cub, Bristly Bill, Badgermole Cub, Icetill Explorer, Springheart Nantuko, Titania, Escape to the Wilds, Avatar of Burgeoning Echoes, Absorbing Man, with Strip Mine, Wasteland, fetch lands, Fastbond and Crop Rotation. This is the third landfall attempt since August 2025. The first worked (September 2025 said Icetill Explorer was strong); the second was pulled back in January 2026 to make room for aristocrats. Watch whether it survives November.

**Red prowess and mono-red.** Ten red additions in October built for it: Monastery Swiftspear, Soul-Scar Mage, Slickshot Show-Off, Stingcaster Mage, Pompous Battlemage, Lava Dart, Mutagenic Growth, Flashback, Gandalf, Goblins' Bane, Dead // Gone, plus Dragon's Rage Channeler, Fireblast, Pyrokinesis, Underworld Breach and The Fantasticar already there. The curator's stated goal is a red deck that is not Boros.

**Selesnya combo.** Brightglass Gearhulk, Marwyn, Six, Figure of Fable, Knight of the Reliquary, Torsten, Okoye. The post says Selesnya has historically been "unexciting, not weak," and the fix is tutors and an infinite.

## How does this compare to the archetype map of a normal cube?

A normal 540 supports ten two-color lanes and a few overlays, as our [cube archetypes explained](/articles/cube-archetypes-explained/) piece lays out. The Vintage Cube is not organized that way anymore. It is organized around four or five mono-colored or colorless engines (Boros aggro, blue power, Welder, Reanimator, green ramp into Natural Order and Oath of Druids) with gold sections that mostly carry fixing and one or two signposts each. Rakdos has ten gold cards and seven of them are lands.

That is a design choice, and a 2026 one. If you draft it expecting a Gruul deck to exist because there is a Gruul section, you will end up with Wrenn and Six and fourteen lands.

## FAQ

### What is the best deck in the Vintage Cube in October 2026?

By the curator's statement in the October 2, 2026 post, Boros aggro. It has been trimmed repeatedly and is still described as the strongest archetype.

### Is Storm still a Vintage Cube deck?

Partly. Tendrils of Agony, Brain Freeze, Underworld Breach, Dark Ritual, Cabal Ritual, Lion's Eye Diamond, Yawgmoth's Will and Wheel of Fortune are on the list. High Tide left in October 2026 and Seething Song left in September 2025.

### Is Time Vault in the Vintage Cube?

No. It is not on the October 7, 2026 list. Manifold Key is there for Basalt Monolith and Grim Monolith.

### How many archetypes should I expect at an eight-person table?

Our experience, not the curator's data: two blue decks, one Boros, one Reanimator or Sneak, one artifact deck, one green deck, and two of whatever is on trial that month. <!-- EDITOR: confirm with recent Commonwealth Vintage Cube nights -->
