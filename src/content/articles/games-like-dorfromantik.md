The best games like [Dorfromantik](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0BQ3W9GRL?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) depend on what hooked you. If it was the hex tiles and the click of a perfect fit, play [Cascadia](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B093H8RGXX?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), [Calico](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08B53WGWX?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), or [Akropolis](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0B124J1JG?tag=cubecommonw0a-20). If it was the quiet landscape growing in front of you, play [Harmonies](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CVNPHPZY?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), [Kingdomino](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B01N3A4070?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), or [Carcassonne](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00NX627HW?tag=cubecommonw0a-20). If you loved a campaign that changes the box as you play, try [My City](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08626RMSL?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) or [My Island](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C1P9H81D?tag=cubecommonw0a-20). And if the best part was the whole table on one team, [Sky Team](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CHD8RCSJ?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), [The Crew](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B084GP7X3P?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), and [Hanabi](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B01MXTGT21?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) are waiting.

**The quick answer:** for most tables, start with **Cascadia** (hex tiles, nature, about 45 minutes) or **Kingdomino** (one of the quickest teaches on this list). For two players who want more Dorfromantik with a winner, get **Dorfromantik: The Duel**. For a family that loved opening the campaign boxes, **My City** is the natural next campaign.

Below are 12 picks, sorted by what you liked about Dorfromantik, with a comparison table, a "which one first" guide, and a clear note on how each one differs.

## What makes a game feel like Dorfromantik?

Dorfromantik: The Board Game, designed by Michael Palm and Lukas Zach and published by Pegasus Spiele, won the 2023 Spiel des Jahres. It is based on the Dorfromantik video game from Toukana Interactive. It plays 1 to 6 in 30 to 60 minutes, for ages 8 and up. When people say they want "something like Dorfromantik," they usually mean one or more of four things:

1. **The calm cooperative feel.** Everyone builds one map together. Nobody wins and nobody loses. You play to beat your best score.
2. **Hex tiles and matching edges.** Rivers meet rivers, tracks meet tracks, and a good placement feels like a small click. Our guide to [tile placement board games](/articles/tile-placement-board-games/) explains why that feeling works so well.
3. **A campaign that grows.** Your score fills a campaign sheet, and sealed boxes bring new pieces as you play. If that is new to you, start with [how to play Dorfromantik](/academy/dorfromantik/how-to-play-dorfromantik/).
4. **The relaxing landscape.** Forests, fields, villages, and streams. No monsters, no war, just a pretty countryside that you made.

No game on this list hits all four exactly the way Dorfromantik does. That is the point. Pick the one that doubles down on the part you liked most.

## If you like Dorfromantik and want more of it

### Dorfromantik: The Duel

Designed by Lukas Zach and Michael Palm and published by Pegasus Spiele in 2024, [The Duel](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CQ6WS3VP?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) plays 2 in 30 to 45 minutes, for ages 8 and up. Each player builds their own landscape from an identical set of tiles, revealed one at a time, so you both face the same puzzle. It adds new task types and optional modules, and two copies let up to four players or teams compete.

**How it differs:** it is competitive. Same hex tiles, same tasks, same calm countryside, but there is a winner. It is the best pick for couples who want Dorfromantik with a little friendly edge. Our lesson on [Dorfromantik player count and expansions](/academy/dorfromantik/dorfromantik-player-count-expansions/) also covers [Dorfromantik: Sakura](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DF4BGSKK?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), the standalone cooperative follow-up with its own campaign, for groups that want the same feeling with new puzzles.

## Board games like Dorfromantik with hex tiles

If the best moment was turning a tile until every edge lined up, these hex tile games give you that feeling with a new puzzle underneath.

### Cascadia

Designed by Randy Flynn and published by Flatout Games and AEG in 2021, [Cascadia](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B093H8RGXX?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) won the 2022 Spiel des Jahres. It plays 1 to 4 in 30 to 45 minutes. Each turn you take one habitat tile and one wildlife token as a pair, add the tile to your landscape, and place the animal where it scores best.

**How it differs:** everyone builds their own map, and the highest score wins. Matching habitats along edges still matters, but the real puzzle is the animals, each with its own scoring pattern. For a competitive table, it may be the closest feel to Dorfromantik on this list. The free [Cascadia course](/academy/cascadia/) walks through it, starting with [how to play Cascadia](/academy/cascadia/how-to-play-cascadia/).

### Calico

Designed by Kevin Russ and published by Flatout Games and AEG in 2020, [Calico](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08B53WGWX?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) plays 1 to 4 in 30 to 45 minutes. You stitch a quilt from hex tiles, drawing new ones from a market of three. Three touching tiles of the same color earn a button, matching patterns attract cats, and goal tiles score for what surrounds them.

**How it differs:** your board is small, so it fills up fast, and every tile has to please its neighbors at once. That can make it feel tighter and more brainy than Dorfromantik's open map. The cats and quilts keep it cozy. It won Game of the Year at the 2020 American Tabletop Awards.

### Akropolis

Designed by Jules Messaud and published by Gigamic in 2022, [Akropolis](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0B124J1JG?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) plays 2 to 4 in about 25 minutes. Each tile is three hexes joined together. You build a city of districts, and you can stack tiles on top of each other to build upward. Plazas multiply the score of their matching districts. It won the 2023 As d'Or, the French game of the year award.

**How it differs:** it grows up as well as out, and stone cubes add a small economy to the tile draft. It is competitive and quick, with more to think about per tile than Dorfromantik. A good pick if you want hex tiles with a sharper edge.

## Board games like Dorfromantik with a calm landscape

If the draw was the countryside itself, these games let you build a landscape at a gentle pace.

### Harmonies

Designed by Johan Benvenuto and published by Libellud in 2024, [Harmonies](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CVNPHPZY?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) plays 1 to 4 in 30 to 45 minutes. You draft colored tokens from a central board and place them on your hex grid to build forests, mountains, rivers, and fields. Animal cards show a small habitat pattern, and when your landscape matches it, an animal moves in.

**How it differs:** it is a 3D pattern puzzle on your own board, not a shared map, and the score is yours alone. But the feeling is the same: a little landscape that gets prettier every turn. It is the pick for players who loved the look of Dorfromantik most.

### Kingdomino

Designed by Bruno Cathala and published by Blue Orange Games in 2016, [Kingdomino](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B01N3A4070?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) won the 2017 Spiel des Jahres. It plays 2 to 4 in 15 to 20 minutes. You pick domino tiles with one or two terrain types and add them to a 5 by 5 kingdom. Each new tile must connect to your castle or touch a matching terrain, and fields score their size times their crowns.

**How it differs:** square tiles instead of hexes, and the picking order adds a light bit of competition. It is shorter and simpler than Dorfromantik, which makes it a great first game for kids. The [Kingdomino course](/academy/kingdomino/) covers it, and [how to play Kingdomino](/academy/kingdomino/how-to-play-kingdomino/) gets you started in minutes.

### Carcassonne

Designed by Klaus-Jürgen Wrede and published by Hans im Glück in 2000 (Z-Man Games in English), [Carcassonne](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00NX627HW?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) won the 2001 Spiel des Jahres. It plays 2 to 5, and a game runs about 30 to 45 minutes. You draw a tile, place it so roads, cities, and fields line up, and may claim a feature with one of your followers.

**How it differs:** it is one of the games that made tile laying popular, and edges must match, just like Dorfromantik's tracks and streams. But you compete for the features you build, so the map is shared and so is the tension. Start with [how to play Carcassonne](/academy/carcassonne/how-to-play-carcassonne/) and then our [Carcassonne strategy guide](/articles/carcassonne-strategy/).

## Board games like Dorfromantik with a growing campaign

If opening the next box was the best part, these games change from game to game the same way. Our explainer on [legacy board games](/articles/legacy-board-games-explained/) covers how campaigns like these work.

### My City

Designed by Reiner Knizia and published by Kosmos in 2020, [My City](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08626RMSL?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) plays 2 to 4 in about 30 minutes. You place buildings, shaped like polyomino pieces, onto your own city board. The campaign runs 24 games across 8 chapters of 3, and new rules and pieces arrive as you go. When it ends, the back of the board holds a version you can play forever.

**How it differs:** it is competitive, and the shapes are blocky pieces rather than hex tiles. But the arc is the same as Dorfromantik's: short games that add up to a long story, with changes that stick. It works well for a family that plays every week.

### My Island

Designed by Reiner Knizia and published by Kosmos in 2023, [My Island](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C1P9H81D?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) is the follow-up to My City. It plays 2 to 4 in about 30 minutes, with the same 24 games in 8 chapters of 3. The big change is the shape: its tiles are made of two to four connected hexes.

**How it differs:** of the two Knizia campaigns, this one is closer to Dorfromantik's hex look. It is still competitive, and your board changes for good as you play. Pick My City first if you want the more famous one, or My Island if hexes are the hook.

### The Crew

Designed by Thomas Sing and published by Kosmos in 2019, [The Crew: The Quest for Planet Nine](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B084GP7X3P?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) won the 2020 Kennerspiel des Jahres. It plays 3 to 5, with a 2-player variant, in about 20 minutes per session. It is a cooperative trick-taking card game with 50 missions to work through as a team.

**How it differs:** there are no tiles at all. What it shares is the shape of the campaign: short sessions, missions you work through in order, and the urge to play one more. You can fail a mission and try again, which Dorfromantik never asks of you. Learn it with [how to play The Crew](/academy/the-crew/how-to-play-the-crew/) or our [The Crew guide](/articles/the-crew-card-game/).

## Cooperative games like Dorfromantik

Dorfromantik is gentle because nobody loses. These cooperative games keep the whole table on one team, though each one adds a way to fall short together. Our list of the [best cooperative board games](/articles/best-cooperative-board-games/) has many more.

### Sky Team

Designed by Luc Rémond and published by Le Scorpion Masqué in 2023, [Sky Team](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CHD8RCSJ?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) won the 2024 Spiel des Jahres. It plays exactly 2 in about 15 minutes. You are pilot and co-pilot landing a plane. Each round you both roll dice in secret and place them on the controls: speed, axis, flaps, landing gear, and more.

**How it differs:** it is tense where Dorfromantik is calm, and you can crash. But the teamwork is just as close, and the quiet planning is part of the fun. For two players who love a shared puzzle, it is a strong next box. See [how to play Sky Team](/academy/sky-team/how-to-play-sky-team/) and our list of [two-player cooperative board games](/articles/two-player-cooperative-board-games/).

### Hanabi

Designed by Antoine Bauza and published in 2010, [Hanabi](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B01MXTGT21?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) won the 2013 Spiel des Jahres. It plays 2 to 5 in 20 to 30 minutes. You hold your cards facing out, so everyone can see your hand but you. The team gives careful hints to play cards in order and build a fireworks show.

**How it differs:** it is a card game about clues, not a map. What it shares is the scoring: like Dorfromantik, you end with a team score out of a known best, and you want to beat it next time. It fits in a pocket and teaches in a few minutes.

### A quick bonus: Cartographers

If you want to draw your own map instead of placing tiles, try **[Cartographers](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07PDNNLGR?tag=cubecommonw0a-20)**, designed by Jordy Adan and published by Thunderworks Games in 2019. It plays 1 to 100 in 30 to 45 minutes, since the box holds 100 map sheets. Each turn everyone draws the same shape of terrain onto their own map over four seasons. It is competitive and was nominated for the 2020 Kennerspiel des Jahres, but it scratches the same "look at the land I made" itch.

## FAQ

### What is the closest game to Dorfromantik?

Dorfromantik: The Duel, since it uses the same tiles and tasks. It is competitive, though. For a game from another publisher, Cascadia is a close match: hex tiles, a nature theme, and a calm pace.

### Is there a cooperative game like Dorfromantik?

[Dorfromantik: Sakura](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DF4BGSKK?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) is a standalone cooperative game in the same family, with its own campaign. Outside the family, most cooperative games can be lost, so they feel tenser. Hanabi may be the gentlest of our picks, since it also ends with a team score to beat.

### Is Dorfromantik good for solo play?

Yes. The box says 1 to 6 players, and the rules are the same at every count. Our lesson on [Dorfromantik solo play](/academy/dorfromantik/dorfromantik-solo/) covers how to play it well alone. Cascadia, Calico, and Harmonies all play solo too.

### What are good hex tile games?

Cascadia, Calico, Akropolis, and My Island all use hex tiles. Harmonies uses a hex grid on your player board. Each one plays competitively, unlike Dorfromantik.

### What should I play after finishing the Dorfromantik campaign?

If you want the same game with new puzzles, try Dorfromantik: Sakura or The Duel. If you want another campaign, try My City or My Island. Our [Dorfromantik campaign guide](/academy/dorfromantik/dorfromantik-campaign-guide/) has tips for finishing the base campaign first, spoiler free.
