The best board game gifts for dad start with what he already enjoys, not with what a dad is supposed to like. A dad who builds spreadsheets for fun wants a different box than a dad who does the crossword, or one who says he "doesn't really do games." This guide sorts the picks by kind of player, so you can find yours in one section.

**The quick answer:** Brass: Birmingham for the deep strategist, Cascadia for the puzzler, Ticket to Ride: Europe for the train and map lover, Sky Team for a dad who plays with his partner, and Heat for the one who swears he does not play.

Prices were checked at major retailers at the time of writing and will move during the holidays. Read each one as "about."

## What is a good board game for a dad who loves strategy?

**Brass: Birmingham** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (2 to 4 players, ages 14 and up, 60 to 120 minutes; $68.99 at Cape Fear Games and about $74 at Amazon per [Board Game Oracle](https://www.boardgameoracle.com/boardgame/price/K2tNPgSWOC/brass-birmingham) at the time of writing). Players build industries across the English Midlands during the Industrial Revolution, linking towns first by canal and then by rail. It is one of the most respected strategy games of the last decade. It asks for a few plays to click, so it suits a dad who enjoys learning a system. Our free course [Canals, Coal, and Beer](/academy/brass-birmingham/) walks through the first game.

**Lost Ruins of Arnak** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (1 to 4 players, ages 12 and up, 30 to 120 minutes; $49.95 at Cardhaus Games and Game Nerdz per [Board Game Oracle](https://www.boardgameoracle.com/boardgame/price/W-gni2bPnj/lost-ruins-of-arnak) at the time of writing). A lighter step into the same territory. Build a deck, send explorers to dig sites, and race up a research track. It has a solo mode, so it gets played even when nobody else is free. Teach it with [Five Rounds to the Lost Temple](/academy/lost-ruins-of-arnak/).

## What about a dad who loves puzzles?

**Cascadia** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (1 to 4 players, ages 10 and up, 30 to 45 minutes; $31.99 at Miniature Market and about $37 at Amazon and Walmart per [Board Game Oracle](https://www.boardgameoracle.com/boardgame/price/12975B7EQh/cascadia) at the time of writing). Lay hex tiles to build a landscape and arrange wildlife tokens to score patterns. It is calm, there is almost no conflict, and each turn is a small puzzle with a satisfying answer. A dad who does the crossword every morning will feel at home. Our free course is [Wild Corridors](/academy/cascadia/).

**Azul** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (2 to 4 players, ages 8 and up, 30 to 45 minutes; $31.99 at Amazon and Asmodee North America per [Board Game Oracle](https://www.boardgameoracle.com/boardgame/price/GWuqS4fbzn/azul) at the time of writing) is the other strong pick: drafting heavy, colorful tiles to fill a wall. The puzzle is in taking what you need without handing the next player what they need. See [Wall to Wall](/academy/azul/) for our course.

## What about a dad who loves history or trains?

**Ticket to Ride: Europe** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (2 to 5 players, ages 8 and up, 30 to 60 minutes; $44.99 at Legendesque and $49.99 at Target per [Board Game Oracle](https://www.boardgameoracle.com/boardgame/price/wbeZsH6VYN/ticket-to-ride-europe) at the time of writing). The same easy turns as the original Ticket to Ride, on a map of early twentieth-century Europe, with tunnels, ferries, and train stations that add a bit more to think about. If he already owns the original, this is the natural next box. Our [Coast to Coast](/academy/ticket-to-ride/) course teaches the core game.

**Memoir '44** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (2 players, ages 8 and up per Board Game Oracle's listing, 30 to 60 minutes; $55.99 at Amazon and Walmart per [Board Game Oracle](https://www.boardgameoracle.com/boardgame/price/hIaIAK-26K/memoir-44) at the time of writing). A Second World War game from Days of Wonder. Each scenario recreates a real battle, and you order units on the board by playing command cards, then roll dice for combat. It plays in under an hour, which is rare for a history game, and the scenario book gives him dozens of battles to try. Give it to the dad with a shelf of history books and a willing opponent.

## What is a good game for dad to play with his partner?

**Sky Team** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (2 players, about 20 minutes; $32.99 at [Hachette Boardgames](https://www.hachetteboardgames.com/products/sky-team), with some shops lower per [Board Game Oracle](https://www.boardgameoracle.com/boardgame/price/Md4Mff5SRi/sky-team), at the time of writing). A cooperative game for exactly two. One player is the pilot, one the co-pilot, and together you land a plane by placing dice in secret, without talking about them. It won the Spiel des Jahres in 2024. Twenty minutes fits a weeknight after the kids are in bed. Our free course is [Cleared to Land](/academy/sky-team/).

**7 Wonders Duel** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (2 players; about $28 to $30 at most US retailers per [Board Game Oracle](https://boardgameoracle.com/boardgame/price/PJgyOvkAJx/7-wonders-duel) at the time of writing) is the competitive pick. Draft cards from a shared layout to build a civilization, and win by points, science, or military. For more, see our list of [two-player board games for date night](/articles/two-player-board-games-date-night/) and the [board game gifts for couples](/articles/board-game-gifts-for-couples/) guide.

## What about the dad who says he does not play games?

He usually means he does not play the games he has seen. Give him one that does not feel like homework.

**Heat: Pedal to the Metal** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (1 to 6 players, ages 10 and up, 30 to 60 minutes; about $60 at Miniature Market and other hobby stores per [Board Game Oracle](https://boardgameoracle.com/boardgame/price/65AVExARnJ/heat-pedal-to-the-metal) at the time of writing). A car racing game. You shift gears by choosing how many cards to play. Take a corner too fast and you pay in heat cards that clog your hand, or spin out if you cannot pay. It feels like a race, not a math test, and it is a natural fit for anyone who watches racing. Our free course is [Hot Laps](/academy/heat/).

**Flip 7** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (3 or more players, ages 8 and up, about 20 minutes; about $8 at Walmart and Miniature Market per [Board Game Oracle](https://www.boardgameoracle.com/boardgame/price/dJdff4as_e/flip-7) at the time of writing). The cheapest gift here and an easy yes. Flip cards and add them up, but flip a number you already have and you bust. It plays the whole family at once. Learn it with [One More Card](/academy/flip-7/).

**Codenames** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (4 to 8 or more players, about 15 minutes, per [Czech Games Edition](https://czechgames.com/games/codenames); about $20 to $25; Target lists it at $24.99 per [Board Game Oracle](https://www.boardgameoracle.com/boardgame/price/9tB3SAHmND/codenames) at the time of writing). Two teams, a grid of words, one-word clues. A dad who loves wordplay and puns will be very good at this, and very pleased about it.

## How do you make a board game gift for dad actually get played?

A game box is an invitation. The gift works best when the invitation is clear.

- **Offer to play it with him.** Write a date on the card. The first game is the hardest one to start.
- **Learn it first.** If you know the rules, he does not have to read them. Every game above with a course link has a free lesson that teaches it.
- **Pick his player count.** If it is mostly him and his partner, buy a two-player game. If the house is full, buy one that seats five.

If you want to make it a habit, our guide to [starting a monthly game night](/articles/start-a-monthly-game-night/) has a simple plan. For a grandparent at the same table, see our [board games for grandparents](/articles/board-games-for-grandparents-gift/) gift guide.

The game is the reason. The people are the point.

## FAQ

### What is a good board game gift for a dad who likes strategy?

Brass: Birmingham for a deep game, or Lost Ruins of Arnak for a lighter one under $50. Both reward repeat plays.

### What is a good two-player game for dad and his partner?

Sky Team, a cooperative game built for exactly two that plays in about twenty minutes.

### What board game should I get a dad who does not play games?

Heat if he likes cars and racing, or Flip 7 if you want a cheap, quick game the whole family can join.

### Is a board game a good Father's Day or Christmas gift?

Yes, especially with a promise to play it together. The time you spend at the table is the part he will remember.
