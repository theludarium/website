The best New Year's Eve party games are the ones that carry a room from the first knock on the door to the countdown without anyone checking the time. Four hours is a long stretch for one game, so plan the night in acts: easy warm-up games while people arrive, team games once the room fills, and one big final game timed to end at midnight.

Below is a full evening plan from 8 p.m. to the countdown, games sorted by group size, and a homemade resolutions game that only works on this one night of the year.

**The quick answer:** Warm up with Just One or Flip 7, move to team games like Codenames and Wavelength, and finish with Monikers, played in three rounds that land the last card right before midnight. For a big crowd of 10 or more, Telestrations and Wits & Wagers seat everyone.

Prices are publisher store or list prices at the time of writing, and they move around the holidays. Read them as "about."

## Why do New Year's Eve parties need a game plan?

A New Year's Eve party has a strange shape. It starts slow, peaks around ten, and then has to stay alive for two more hours, often after the snacks are gone.

Games fix the sag. They give people something to do with their hands and a reason to talk to the person they just met. A plan with acts also makes midnight feel like a finale instead of a wait.

## The New Year's Eve game plan, 8 p.m. to midnight

| Time | Act | What to play |
|---|---|---|
| 8:00 to 9:00 | Warm-up while people arrive | Just One, Flip 7 |
| 9:00 to 10:30 | Team games | Codenames, Wavelength, Telestrations |
| 10:30 to 11:00 | The resolutions game | Homemade, below |
| 11:00 to 11:59 | The final game | Monikers, three rounds |
| Midnight | Countdown | Put the cards down |

### 8:00 to 9:00: Warm-up games people can join late

People arrive in waves, so the first hour needs games that let a new person sit down mid-game.

[Just One](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07GJ7NDZQ?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) is a cooperative word game. One player guesses a mystery word, and everyone else writes a one-word clue. The catch: any clues that match are erased before the guesser sees them. Three to seven players, about twenty minutes, ages 8 and up. About $25 at the Asmodee store. Each round is short, so a latecomer joins on the next word.

[Flip 7](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DWGVM7RY?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) is a push-your-luck card game for three or more. Flip cards and add them up, but a repeated number busts you. About twenty minutes, ages 8 and up, about $8 at Target at the time of writing. It seats a crowd, and anyone can learn it by watching one round. ### 9:00 to 10:30: Team games once the room fills

Once most guests are in, split into two teams. Team games carry more energy than anything else at a party, because every win is shared.

- **[Codenames](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B014Q1XX9S?tag=cubecommonw0a-20).** Two spymasters give one-word clues to steer their teams to the right words on a grid of 25. Two to eight or more players, about fifteen minutes a game, ages 14 and up on the box. About $25 at the Czech Games store. Play best of three and keep a running score on a whiteboard. Our free [Codenames course](/academy/codenames/) covers giving good clues.
- **[Wavelength](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07T446163?tag=cubecommonw0a-20).** One player gets a hidden target on a dial between two ideas, such as "underrated" and "overrated," and gives a clue. The team argues about where the clue falls. Two to twelve players, ages 14 and up, about $39 at the Asmodee store. The arguing is the fun.
- **[Telestrations](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0F699VTB2?tag=cubecommonw0a-20).** Telephone with drawings. Everyone draws their own secret word, then passes the sketchbook. The next person guesses the drawing, the next draws that guess, and so on until the books come home. Four to eight players, about thirty minutes, ages 12 and up. The 8-player box lists for about $32 and the 12-player box for about $45, though both showed sold out at The Op's store at the time of writing, so check other retailers. Nobody needs to be able to draw.

### 10:30 to 11:00: The resolutions game

This one costs nothing and only works on New Year's Eve. You need paper slips, pens, and a bowl.

1. **Everyone writes one resolution** on a slip, without a name. It can be real ("read twelve books") or ridiculous ("learn to juggle, badly").
2. **Fold the slips into a bowl.** The host draws one at a time and reads it aloud.
3. **Everyone guesses who wrote it.** Write guesses down, then the author stands up. One point for each correct guess.
4. **Bonus round.** After every slip is read, vote for the resolution most likely to last until February. The winner gets bragging rights.

Keep it kind. The game is about learning something about each other, not mocking a goal. Tell people up front that a joke resolution is fine, so nobody feels pressure to share more than they want.

For a quieter crowd, swap the guessing for prediction. Each person writes one prediction for the year ahead, seals it in an envelope, and the host keeps them for next year's party. It gives the group a reason to gather again in twelve months.

### 11:00 to 11:59: The final game, timed to midnight

The final game should grow louder as midnight gets closer. Monikers does exactly that, and its three rounds slot neatly into the last hour.

[Monikers](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00M07OQ1U?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) is a team guessing game played with the same deck of name cards in three rounds. In round one you can say anything except the name. In round two you can say only one word. In round three, you can only act it out. Because the cards repeat, the room builds inside jokes that pay off in the final round.

- **11:00:** deal the cards and play round one.
- **11:20:** round two, one word only.
- **11:40:** round three, charades. Stop the last turn at 11:59, count the cards, and announce the winning team in time for the countdown.

The original Monikers is rated 17 and up and was sold out at the Asmodee store at the time of writing. [Monikers: Family Edition](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0FVBB5W1W?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) is a standalone version written so younger players get the jokes, for two to twelve or more players, ages 6 and up, about $30 at the Asmodee store. It is the safer buy for a mixed crowd.

## What are the best New Year's Eve games by group size?

| Group size | Best picks | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 4 to 6 | Just One, Codenames, Wavelength | Everyone plays every round |
| 6 to 10 | Codenames, Telestrations, Monikers | Two teams of three to five |
| 10 to 16 | Telestrations 12-player, Monikers | Built for big tables |
| 16 or more | Wits & Wagers, Monikers in teams | Teams of three or four keep it moving |

For a crowd of 16 or more, [Wits & Wagers Deluxe Edition](https://northstargames.com/products/wits-wagers-deluxe-edition) works well. Everyone writes a guess to a number question, such as a year or a distance, and then bets on the guess they think is closest. You do not need to know the answers to win. The box lists three to seven players for ages 10 and up, and big groups play it as teams sharing a guess. About $50 at North Star Games.

## How do you keep a New Year's Eve game night from fizzling?

A few small habits make the whole night run better:

- **Pick a game captain.** One person teaches each game, keeps time, and calls the next act. Usually the host, but it does not have to be.
- **Teach in two minutes or less.** Party games should be learned by playing. Our guide on [how to teach a board game](/articles/how-to-teach-a-board-game/) has the order that works.
- **Keep teams fluid.** Reshuffle teams between acts so people meet someone new.
- **Have a quiet corner.** Not everyone wants to shout charades. A small table with a two-player game gives quieter guests a place to land.
- **Watch the clock, not the score.** The schedule matters more than finishing every game. Cut a game short to protect the midnight finale.

## What about kids at a New Year's Eve party?

If kids are staying up for midnight, play the warm-up and final games all together. Flip 7, Just One, and Monikers: Family Edition all work from about age 8, and the Family Edition is rated 6 and up. Send kids to a movie during the team-games act if the grown-up games run late, and bring them back for the final round. Some families also do an early "kid midnight" at 9 p.m. with the countdown from a different time zone.

## Ring it in at the table

A night of games gives the new year a good first memory. The people who laughed through charades at 11:58 are the ones you will want at the table next December too. The game is the reason. The people are the point.

## FAQ

### What games are good for a New Year's Eve party?

Word and team games work best: Just One and Flip 7 to warm up, Codenames and Wavelength for teams, and Monikers as the final game before midnight.

### How do you make New Year's Eve games last until midnight?

Plan the night in acts and switch games every 30 to 40 minutes. Save one game with rounds, like Monikers, and time its last round to end at 11:59.

### What is a good New Year's resolution game?

Have everyone write a resolution on an unsigned slip, read them aloud one by one, and guess who wrote each. Score a point for each correct guess.

### What New Year's Eve games work for a big group?

Telestrations' 12-player version, Monikers, and Wits & Wagers played in teams all handle 10 or more people.
