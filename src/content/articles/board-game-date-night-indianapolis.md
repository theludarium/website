A date night in Indianapolis built around board games solves the problem that dinner-and-a-movie cannot: it gives two people something to do with their hands and a reason to talk. Across a table with a game between you, there is no silence to fill and no screen to stare at. The city has a handful of places that are built for it, and a few more that work if you pick the hour. This guide names them, says which night suits which stage of a relationship, and gives a plan for the evening.

Venues were checked against their own sites or a current listing in October 2026. Hours shift; check before you book.

## Where is the best board game date spot in Indianapolis?

**[Elf 'N Moon](https://elfnmoon.com/)**, Fountain Square (1354 Shelby St). The obvious answer and the right one. Free first-come tables, a free game library, beer and wine, two rentable private rooms, and hours that run until 11 most nights. Fountain Square around it is the best date neighborhood in the city: dinner at any of a dozen places on Virginia Avenue, the duckpin bowling alley upstairs at the Fountain Square Theatre building, and a walk back to the car past the fountain. For a second date onward, this is the plan.

**[Hitherto Coffee & Gaming Parlour](http://hithertocoffee.com/)**, Greenfield (1558 N State St). The daytime date. Coffee, food, a large library. A Saturday afternoon here is lower pressure than any evening, and for a first date that is a feature. The drive from downtown is about 25 minutes, which is fine if one of you lives east.

**[Good Games Indianapolis](https://www.goodgamesna.com/us/stores/indiana/indianapolis.html)**, downtown (111 S Meridian St). A store with roughly 3,500 square feet of play space. Not romantic, but if you are both already hobbyists, a Sunday Commander night followed by dinner on Georgia Street is a real date. Know your audience.

**Brewery game nights.** The [Indy Board Game Nomads](https://www.meetup.com/indybgnomads/) meetups at Red Lion Grog House in Fountain Square (Mondays, 21+) and St. Joseph Brewery downtown turn a weeknight into a group date. Good for couples who have run out of two-player games and want other people at the table.

The full list of places to play is in our [Indianapolis board game cafe guide](/articles/board-game-cafes-indianapolis/), and if you are looking for a venue in the north suburbs, Moonshot Games in downtown Noblesville is a short walk from restaurants.

## What should you play on a first date?

Not Catan. Not anything with a thirty-minute rules explanation, direct conflict, or a clear loser. A first-date game has three jobs: start conversation, end in under half an hour, and make both people look good.

The pattern: early dates get cooperative or conversational games; later dates can handle competition. Wavelength in particular is a better first-date tool than most questions you could ask. ## How do you build the evening?

A plan that works in Fountain Square, adjusted to taste.

1. **6:30, dinner nearby.** Pick a place where you can finish in an hour. A long tasting menu before a game night leaves no room for the game.
2. **8:00, arrive at the venue.** Early enough to get a table on a Friday; later and you may wait.
3. **Ask the staff for a recommendation.** Tell them it is a date and how long you have. Being taught a game together is part of the fun and removes the pressure of one person being the expert.
4. **Play two games, not four.** One light, one with a little depth. Leave while it is still good.
5. **Walk.** Fountain Square and Mass Ave are both built for the twenty minutes after, which is when the conversation the game started keeps going.

For a quieter version, go on a Tuesday or Wednesday. Weekend evenings at any game venue are loud by nine.

## What do you do if one of you does not like games?

Pick the venue over the game, and the conversation over the win.

- **Choose a place with food and drink** so the night stands on its own even if the game is short.
- **Start with something that is barely a game.** Wavelength, Codenames, or a trivia deck. These feel like talking with a structure.
- **Let the skeptic pick.** Walking a game library shelf together and choosing by the box art is a fine way to spend ten minutes.
- **Quit early if it is not working.** Twenty minutes of a game and an hour of talking is a successful date. We wrote about [games for people who say they don't like games](/articles/board-games-for-people-who-dont-like-games/) for this exact person.

## Why do games work for dates at all?

Because sitting side by side or across a small table with a shared task is how most people are comfortable talking. Eye contact on demand is hard. Eye contact while deciding whether to take the blue tile is easy. A game provides pauses that are not awkward, small wins to share, and a natural way to find out whether the other person is a gracious loser, which is more useful information than their favorite movie.

It is also cheap. A table at Elf 'N Moon is free with a drink. A library night is free. Compared with a concert or a nice dinner, a game date is the best value in the city, and it is the one you will remember.

## FAQ

### Where can couples play board games in Indianapolis?

Elf 'N Moon in Fountain Square is the best evening option, with a free library and beer and wine. Hitherto in Greenfield suits a daytime date, and brewery meetup nights work for a group date.

### What is a good board game for a first date?

Wavelength, Hive, or Sushi Go. Short, conversational or cooperative, and nothing that ends with one person feeling bad.

### Is a board game cafe a good first date?

Yes, especially in the daytime or early evening. It gives you something to do together and makes conversation easier than a dinner table does.

### How much does a board game date night cost in Indianapolis?

Often just drinks and dinner. Elf 'N Moon's tables and library are free; library nights are free; store play space is usually free or a few dollars.
