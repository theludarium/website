The best games like [Codenames](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B014Q1XX9S?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) depend on which part of it your group loved. If you want more Codenames, play [Codenames: Duet](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0FJMNPGXM?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) or [Codenames: Pictures](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B01HT9DERU?tag=cubecommonw0a-20). If it was the team rivalry and the clever clues, play [Decrypto](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07BB4ND6J?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) or [Wavelength](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07T446163?tag=cubecommonw0a-20). If it was the thrill of one word clicking for everyone, play [Just One](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07GJ7NDZQ?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), [So Clover!](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0941TJHXX?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), or [Letter Jam](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07V49TSG6?tag=cubecommonw0a-20). If it was reading your friends' minds, play [Dixit](https://www.amazon.com/dp/2914849656?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) or [Hues and Cues](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B084D2XQ9F?tag=cubecommonw0a-20). And if you want word clues with a liar in the room, play [The Chameleon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07MV4NN5Z?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) or [Insider](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B01M5FYKL4?tag=cubecommonw0a-20).

**The quick answer:** for a game night group that loves Codenames, get **Decrypto**. It keeps the two teams and the clever clues, and adds a code to crack. For a party with mixed ages or people new to games, get **Just One**: the same "will they get it?" moment, everyone on one team, and it teaches in a minute.

Below are 11 picks, sorted by what you liked about Codenames, with a comparison table, a "which one first" guide, and a plain note on how each one differs.

## What makes a game feel like Codenames?

Codenames, designed by Vlaada Chvátil and published by Czech Games Edition in 2015, won the 2016 Spiel des Jahres. It plays 2 to 8 or more in about 15 minutes. When people say they want "something like Codenames," they usually mean one or more of four things:

1. **One clue, many answers.** A spymaster finds one word that links several cards. Our [Codenames clue tips](/articles/codenames-clue-tips/) and the lesson on [clue types](/academy/codenames/codenames-clue-types/) show why that is so satisfying.
2. **Two teams.** The rivalry and the groans when a team hits the other side's word.
3. **Watching people think.** Half the fun is hearing your team talk their way to the wrong answer.
4. **Quick rounds that welcome anyone.** No reading rules for 20 minutes. People join, leave, and swap teams.

No game on this list hits all four the way Codenames does. Pick the one that doubles down on the part your group liked most.

## If you want more Codenames

The Codenames family is big. Our lesson on [Codenames variants and editions](/academy/codenames/codenames-variants-and-editions/) covers all of them. These two are the best places to start.

### Codenames: Duet

Designed by Scot Eaton and Vlaada Chvátil and published by Czech Games Edition in 2017, [Codenames: Duet](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0FJMNPGXM?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) is built for two players but works with more. It takes about 15 minutes. Both players are spymasters and guessers at once, giving each other clues to find all 15 agents before time runs out.

**Why it fits:** it is Codenames for couples and small tables. You work together instead of against a rival team, and a campaign map adds a light goal across many games. Its 200 word cards also mix into the original. Our lesson on [Codenames: Duet strategy](/academy/codenames/codenames-duet-strategy/) covers how to give clues for two.

### Codenames: Pictures

Designed by Vlaada Chvátil and published by Czech Games Edition in 2016, [Codenames: Pictures](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B01HT9DERU?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) plays the same as Codenames, with odd, detailed pictures instead of words and a smaller grid of 20 cards.

**Why it fits:** pictures can mean many things at once, so the clues get stranger and funnier. It is also the better pick for younger players and anyone who finds word games tiring.

## If you loved the team rivalry

These two keep Codenames' two-team table and the feeling of reading your own teammates.

### Decrypto

Designed by Thomas Dagenais-Lespérance and published by Le Scorpion Masqué in 2018, [Decrypto](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07BB4ND6J?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) plays 3 to 8 in 15 to 45 minutes. Each team has four secret words. Each round, one player gives clues that point to a three-number code, and the team must decode it. The other team listens in and tries to crack your code too.

**How it differs:** the clues have to be clear enough for your team and murky enough for the rivals. Clues pile up across rounds, so the game gets harder as it goes. It suits groups that loved Codenames' cleverness and want more of it. We compared the two in [Codenames vs Decrypto](/articles/codenames-vs-decrypto/).

### Wavelength

Designed by Alex Hague, Justin Vickers, and Wolfgang Warsch and published by Palm Court (now CMYK) in 2019, [Wavelength](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07T446163?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) plays 2 to 12 in 30 to 45 minutes. One player sees a hidden target on a dial between two ideas, like "hot" and "cold," and gives one clue. The team debates where to turn the dial.

**How it differs:** there is no grid of words. Clues are about where something sits on a scale, so the fun is in the arguing: is a burrito more "hot" or more "cold"? It handles big groups well, which is why it sits next to Codenames on our list of [party games for large groups](/articles/party-games-for-large-groups/).

## If you loved the "they got it!" moment

These three put everyone on one team and focus on one clue landing.

### Just One

Designed by Ludovic Roudy and Bruno Sautter and published by Repos Production in 2018, [Just One](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07GJ7NDZQ?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) won the 2019 Spiel des Jahres. It plays 3 to 7 in about 20 minutes. Everyone writes a one-word clue for the guesser. Any clue that matches another one gets erased before the guesser sees it.

**How it differs:** it is fully cooperative, and the twist rewards clues that are clever but not obvious. It is the easiest game here to teach, and it works for nearly any group. It is a great follow-up when Codenames runs long.

### So Clover!

Designed by François Romain and published by Repos Production in 2021, [So Clover!](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0941TJHXX?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) plays 3 to 6 in about 30 minutes. Each player secretly writes four clues, each linking two words on a board. Then the team works together to rebuild your board from your clues.

**How it differs:** every clue links exactly two words, like a Codenames clue for two. Everyone takes a turn as clue giver, so no one sits out. It is a smart pick for word lovers who want a calmer table.

### Letter Jam

Designed by Ondra Skoupý and published by Czech Games Edition in 2019, [Letter Jam](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07V49TSG6?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) plays 2 to 6 in about 45 minutes. You hold a letter you cannot see but everyone else can. Players spell words from the visible letters to help each other figure out their own.

**How it differs:** it is a cooperative word puzzle from the same publisher as Codenames, and it trades quick laughs for a slower, satisfying click. It suits groups who love spelling and wordplay, not just links between ideas.

## If you loved reading your friends' minds

Codenames works because you know how your friends think. These two lean all the way into that.

### Dixit

Designed by Jean-Louis Roubira and published by Libellud in 2008, [Dixit](https://www.amazon.com/dp/2914849656?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) won the 2010 Spiel des Jahres. The current edition plays 3 to 8 in about 30 minutes. The storyteller gives a clue for one dreamlike picture card, everyone else adds a card that fits, and players vote on which card was the storyteller's.

**How it differs:** you score only if some players guess your card, but not all of them. So the clue has to be just right. It is gentle and artful, and it suits mixed ages and family nights.

### Hues and Cues

Designed by Scott Brady and published by The Op in 2020, [Hues and Cues](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B084D2XQ9F?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) plays 3 to 10 in about 30 minutes. The cue giver picks one color from a board of 480 shades and gives a one-word clue, then a two-word clue. Everyone else tries to land on that exact color.

**How it differs:** it swaps words for colors. "Banana" means different yellows to different people, and finding out how is the fun. It is a great choice for groups that include younger players or people new to games.

## If you want clue words with a liar in the room

These two keep the word clues and add a bluffer. If your group likes this, our list of [social deduction games](/articles/best-social-deduction-games/) has more.

### The Chameleon

Designed by Rikki Tahta and published by Big Potato in 2017, [The Chameleon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07MV4NN5Z?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) plays 3 to 8 in about 15 minutes. Everyone sees a secret word except the Chameleon. Each player says one related word, then the group votes on who the faker is.

**How it differs:** your clue has to prove you know the word without giving it away to the Chameleon. That is the same careful balance as a good Codenames clue, with suspicion added.

### Insider

Designed by Akihiro Itoh, Kwaji, Daichi Okano, and Kito Shinma and published by Oink Games in 2016, [Insider](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B01M5FYKL4?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) plays 4 to 8 in about 15 minutes. The team asks yes or no questions to guess a secret word. One player secretly knows it and steers the group toward it without getting caught.

**How it differs:** it plays like Twenty Questions with a hidden traitor. It is short and small, and it is great for a group that enjoys both the guessing and the accusing.

## FAQ

### What is the closest game to Codenames?

Decrypto. It keeps two teams and word clues, and the clever-clue feeling is even stronger. Inside the family, Codenames: Pictures plays the same with picture cards.

### What is a good game like Codenames for two players?

Codenames: Duet, which is built for two and works as a cooperative game. Letter Jam also plays at two.

### What is a good Codenames alternative for a big party?

Wavelength handles up to 12 players and keeps two teams. Codenames itself also scales well. Our guide to [party games for large groups](/articles/party-games-for-large-groups/) has more for 10 or more.

### Can you play Codenames online or on a TV?

Yes. Our guide to [Codenames Party on Netflix](/articles/codenames-party-netflix/) explains how phones work as controllers and when the box is the better choice.

### Is Codenames good for team building?

It is one of the best, because a short round shows how a team really talks. Our guide to [Codenames team building](/articles/codenames-team-building/) covers how to run a session, and our list of [team building board games](/articles/team-building-board-games/) has more options for work groups.
