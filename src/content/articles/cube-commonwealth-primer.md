## The one-page guide for first-timers

Printed and in the box; here for the car.

> **How it works.** Open a pack of 15, take one card, pass the rest. Three packs: left, right, left. You end with 45 cards. Build a 40-card deck: 17 basic lands (provided) and 23 spells. Then play the rounds.
>
> **Pick two colors.** One main, one support. Three colors is the most common first-draft mistake.
>
> **What to take.** First five picks: the card that wins a game by itself. After that: the best card in your colors. Removal beats a creature of the same cost. Cheap beats expensive. Gold cards are commitments; take them late.
>
> **Building.** Sort your two colors by cost. Cut from the top until 23 remain. Make sure at least eight cost two or less. Add 17 lands split by color.
>
> **Easy first decks here.** <!-- EDITOR: the three lanes named above, with two cards each to look for. -->
>
> **Five mistakes everyone makes once.** Three colors. Sixteen lands. Four expensive cards. Passing removal for a creature. Playing your off-color first pick.
>
> **At the table.** Slow picks are fine. Questions are fine. A regular sits with you during deckbuilding.

## Why this document exists

A primer is the cube's standing invitation: it tells a stranger what they are walking into and tells a regular the list is still being thought about. Ours is short on purpose and will be edited the same day the cube is. The game is the reason. The people are the point.

If you want to know when the list changes, when seats open, and when The Ludarium's lounge in Indianapolis gives this cube a permanent table, subscribe to the newsletter. One short email a week. Then join the Discord, react to the weekly post, and come draft with us.
