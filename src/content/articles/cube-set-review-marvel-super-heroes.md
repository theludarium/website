Magic: The Gathering | Marvel Super Heroes released on June 26, 2026, and the Marvel Super Heroes cube conversation splits into two questions: do you run Universes Beyond cards at all, and if so, which of these are worth a slot? The first question is yours. For the second, the set has one colorless artifact that any cube can consider, several cheap legends in white and blue that are properly costed, and a lot of six-mana heroes that are built for Commander. Our calls follow [the slot method](/articles/how-to-evaluate-new-cards-for-cube/).

Set facts from the [Wizards collecting article](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/collecting-marvel-super-heroes): tabletop release June 26, 2026, main set code MSH, legal in all formats including Standard; MTGGoldfish's set index counts 286 cards in the main set. Card text below is paraphrased from Card Kingdom and mtg.wtf listings.

## What does the set do, in one paragraph?

"Heroes" and "Villains" are creature types the set cares about. **Power-up** is a one-time activated ability that puts counters on a legend and does something else. **Teamwork** is an optional extra cost: tap creatures with enough total power and the spell gets stronger. The Mind Stone, the set's Infinity Stone, is a legendary artifact that taps for mana and has a late-game "harness" mode. For cube, the mechanics mostly do not travel. The individual cards do, and a few are efficient enough to ignore the typeline.

## Is The Mind Stone a cube card?

**The Mind Stone** ({1}{W}, legendary artifact, indestructible; tap for {W}; {5}{W}, tap: harness, after which it blinks one other nonland permanent you control each end step). **Slot:** white / 2 / mana rock. **Competes with:** nothing, really. White does not get two-mana rocks in most cubes; the closest comparisons are the colorless Mind Stone and Talismans. **Call: test** at every unpowered tier. A two-mana indestructible rock that only white decks can use is an odd object. It ramps white control into its four-drops a turn early, and the blink mode is a real late-game engine with any enters-the-battlefield creature. It is also the card the wider market has noticed: [MTGGoldfish](https://www.mtggoldfish.com/index/MSH-main_set) listed it at about $49 as of October 21, 2026, so proxy it for testing.

## Which cheap legends are properly costed?

The set's best cube material is in the two- and three-mana legends.

- **Captain America, Super-Soldier** ({1}{W}{W}, 3/2 first strike; enters with a shield counter; while it has one, you and your other Heroes have hexproof). **Slot:** white / 3 / aggressive creature. **Competes with:** Brimaz, Adeline, Skyknight Vanguard (adjacent). **Call: test.** A 3/2 first striker for three that shrugs off the first removal spell is a fine aggro three-drop. The Hero hexproof text is mostly blank in cube.
- **Loki, God of Mischief** ({1}{U}, 2/1; draw a card when a player or permanent becomes the target of an ability you control, once per turn). **Slot:** blue / 2 / value creature. **Call: pass** in most cubes. The trigger needs activated or triggered abilities that target, which blue cube decks have less of than you think. Test it only in artifact-heavy lists with equipment.
- **Jennifer Walters // The Sensational She-Hulk** ({1}{W}, 2/3; opponents can't cast spells during your turn; transforms for {3}{G}{W}{W}). **Slot:** white / 2 / disruptive creature. **Call: test.** Grand Abolisher on a 2/3 body is a real card against counterspells and flash. The transform is a bonus.
- **King T'Challa // Black Panther, Hope Enduring** ({1}{W}{U}, 3/2 flash; draw when a player draws their second card each turn). **Slot:** gold / WU / 3. **Call: test.** A flash 3/2 that draws off your own cantrips and the opponent's card draw. Azorius slots are competitive, so it has to beat Teferi, Time Raveler for a slot, and it does not; it competes for the second or third slot.
- **Black Widow, Super Spy** ({1}{B}, 2/1 menace; on combat damage, exile until a nonland card, then either a +1/+1 counter or cast that card). **Slot:** black / 2 / aggro. **Call: test.** Two-mana menace with a Gonti trigger. It gets in early and the card it steals is often the opponent's answer.
- **Bruce Banner // The Incredible Hulk** ({U}, 1/1; {X}{X}, tap: draw X; transforms for {2}{R}{R}{G}{G} into an 8/8). **Call: pass.** Slow, and the transform is three colors deep.
- **Thanos, the Mad Titan** ({R}{W}{B}, 4/4 deathtouch lifelink). **Call: pass.** Three-color gold is a slot most cubes do not have.

## Which midrange cards get a test?

- **The Vision** ({4}, 2/5 flying vigilance artifact creature; whenever you cast a noncreature spell, choose one not yet chosen this turn: double strike, indestructible, or draw a card). **Slot:** colorless / 4 / value creature. **Call: test.** A colorless four-drop any spells deck can run, that draws a card off the first spell each turn and attacks for four in the air with double strike off the second. Compare Solemn Simulacrum, which it beats in a blue or red spells deck and loses to elsewhere.
- **The Sentry, Golden Guardian** ({3}{W}, 5/5 flying vigilance indestructible; gives target opponent a 5/5 flying indestructible Void token that must attack). **Call: test** as a curiosity, probably **pass**. Four mana for a 5/5 indestructible flier is well above rate, and the drawback is a 5/5 that has to attack into your 5/5 vigilance. It is a stalemate machine. Fun once.
- **The Mighty Thor, Jane Foster** ({1}{W}{U}, 3/3 flying; on attack, exile a nontoken artifact or creature and return it tapped; draw when an Equipment enters). **Slot:** gold / WU / 3. **Call: test.** The attack trigger resets a blocker every turn. Competes with T'Challa above for the same slot; we prefer T'Challa.
- **Taskmaster, Mercenary Mimic** ({2}{U}{B}, 3/5; each turn becomes a copy of a creature on the battlefield or in a graveyard). **Call: pass.** Four-mana Dimir has better options.
- **The Scarlet Witch** ({2}{R}, 2/3; instants and sorceries with mana value 4 or more cost X less, X equal to her power). **Call: pass.** Big-spell red is a Commander archetype.
- **M.O.D.O.K.** ({3}{B}{B}, 2/2 flying lifelink artifact creature; opponents' creatures get −1/−1; pay 3 life to connive). **Call: test** in Legacy-power cubes as a Night of Souls' Betrayal on a body. The static shrink wipes aggro boards of X/1s.
- **Doctor Doom** ({4}{B}{B}, 3/3; makes two 3/3 Doombots; indestructible with an artifact creature; draws at end step). **Call: pass.** Nine power for six and a card a turn is strong, and Grave Titan still holds the slot.
- **Kang the Conqueror** ({2}{U}{U}, 4/5 flying; power-up for an extra turn). **Call: test** as a blue four-drop finisher. A 4/5 flier for four with a late extra-turn mode is a real card in blue control.
- **Namor the Sub-Mariner** ({1}{U}{U}, */4 flying; makes Merfolk off blue spells). **Call: pass** outside tribal cubes.

## What about the equipment and the big spells?

- **Mjölnir, Hammer of Thor** ({3}{R} legendary Equipment; deals 4 to a creature on entering; doubles the equipped creature's damage; equip worthy {1}; {2}{R}, discard: 2 to each creature). **Slot:** red / 4 / removal with upside. **Call: test.** A four-mana Flametongue Kavu effect that stays on the battlefield as a damage doubler. Only a worthy creature, a legendary non-Villain that is red or white, can carry it, so in most cube decks it is a four-mana Flametongue Kavu with a sweeper mode attached.
- **Iron Man Armor** ({3} Equipment; attaches on entering; +2/+1 and flying; {2} animates it as a Construct scaled to your artifacts; equip {2}). **Call: test** in artifact cubes, pass elsewhere.
- **Cosmic Cube** ({5} artifact, ward {2}; on attack, look at six and cast one free with mana value up to your biggest attacker's power). **Call: pass.** Does nothing until you attack with something large.
- **Avengers Assemble!**, **Multiversal Incursion**, **Earth's Mightiest Heroes**: all pass. Tribal or six-plus mana.

No keeps, ten tests (plus Iron Man Armor in artifact cubes). That is unusual: the set has a lot of cards that are close to the line and none that clear it on paper. Expect two or three to survive three drafts.

## FAQ

### Is Marvel Super Heroes good for cube?

Moderate. Nothing is an automatic include, but The Mind Stone, the cheap white legends and The Vision are all worth testing. The six-mana heroes are Commander cards.

### Is The Mind Stone too strong for cube?

No. It is a two-mana rock restricted to white decks with a slow late-game mode. It is strong in white control and irrelevant to everyone else, which is the profile of a healthy cube card.

### Does The Vision belong in a cube without an artifact theme?

Yes, if your cube has spells decks. The Vision cares about noncreature spells, not artifacts. It fits Izzet and Azorius control as a colorless threat.

### Should I treat Hero and Villain as blank text?

In cube, yes. A 540-card list will not have enough Heroes for the tribal payoffs. Judge each card on the rest of its text.
