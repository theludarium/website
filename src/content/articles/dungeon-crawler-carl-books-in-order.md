Dungeon Crawler Carl is one continuous story told across eight books, so order matters. Each book picks up where the last one stopped, and each covers part of Carl and Donut's descent through the World Dungeon.

**The quick answer:** read them in publication order, starting with *Dungeon Crawler Carl*. There are eight books as of 2026, ending (for now) with *A Parade of Horribles*. Most fans recommend the audiobooks, narrated by Jeff Hays.

## The Dungeon Crawler Carl books in order

No spoilers here, just titles and years.

| 8 | A Parade of Horribles | 2026 |

The series is planned to continue past book eight. The dungeon has eighteen floors, and the story is still working its way down.

## Why so many fans listen instead of read

The audiobooks are Audible exclusives, produced by Jeff Hays's company, Soundbooth Theater, and performed by Hays himself. The books are full of game announcements, alien hosts, and system messages, and hearing them performed is a big part of the fun. Some later books add guest narrators.

A practical reason too: the books are long. Listening on a commute, while painting miniatures, or while setting up a game is how many fans get through all eight.

## What else is in the series?

- **Graphic novels.** A graphic novel series adapts the story, for readers who want the pictures.
- **A tabletop RPG.** Renegade Game Studios publishes the official Dungeon Crawler Carl roleplaying game. See our guide to the [Dungeon Crawler Carl RPG](/articles/dungeon-crawler-carl-rpg/).
- **A card game.** Renegade also publishes Dungeon Crawler Carl: Unstoppable, a deck-building game.

## FAQ

### Do you have to read Dungeon Crawler Carl in order?

Yes. It is one story split into books, and each book continues directly from the one before.

### How many Dungeon Crawler Carl books are there?

Eight as of 2026. Book 8, A Parade of Horribles, came out in May 2026, and more are planned.

### How long are the audiobooks?

Long. Book four alone runs about eighteen hours, which is why so many fans listen on commutes and during game prep.

### Are the audiobooks only on Audible?

Yes. The Dungeon Crawler Carl audiobooks are Audible exclusives.
