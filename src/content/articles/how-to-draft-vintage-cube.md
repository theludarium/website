To draft Vintage Cube well, take the fast mana early, decide by pick five of pack one whether you are the aggro drafter or the engine drafter, and then stop taking cards that are powerful but not in your deck. Most losing Vintage Cube decks are not short on power. They are 23 strong cards that do not do one thing.

This guide is written for the list effective October 7, 2026, per the [Reality Fracture update](https://www.mtgo.com/news/fra-vintage-cube-update) and the [current card list](https://www.mtgo.com/vintage-cube-cardlist). General principles will outlast it; the card names will not. For the basics of signals and reading a table, start with our [cube draft strategy](/articles/cube-draft-strategy/) guide. This one assumes you know that and asks what is different when there is a Black Lotus in the pack.

## What do you take first in Vintage Cube?

Colorless fast mana, then the best blue power, then everything else. The reason is structural: a card that goes in any deck keeps every lane open, and in a cube where turn-two plays decide games, the cards that make turn two faster are worth more than any individual threat.

The tier most experienced drafters agree on, in rough order:

1. **Black Lotus, Sol Ring, Mana Crypt, Ancestral Recall, Time Walk.** Any of the five over any other card.
2. **The five Moxen, Mana Vault, Mox Diamond, Chrome Mox, Mox Opal, Lotus Petal.** Colorless or near enough.
3. **Tinker, Oko, Thief of Crowns, Ragavan, Jace, the Mind Sculptor, Sneak Attack, Show and Tell, Natural Order, Mind Twist, Balance, Demonic Tutor, Vampiric Tutor, Library of Alexandria, Tolarian Academy, Mishra's Workshop.** Powerful but committing.
4. **Premium removal and the best two-drops.** Swords to Plowshares, Lightning Bolt, Fatal Push, Counterspell, Mana Drain, Force of Will, Dark Confidant, Ledger Shredder, Luminarch Aspirant.

Two things about this list are opinion, and we hold them loosely. Some strong players take Sol Ring over Lotus for consistency across the draft. Some take Oko over anything in tier three. Both are defensible. What is not defensible is taking a tier-four card over a tier-two one because it is on-color: in pack one, pick one through four, you do not have colors yet.

## When should you commit to a lane?

Earlier than in a normal cube, and by lane rather than by color. The Vintage Cube in October 2026 is not organized into ten balanced guild decks. It has a few engines and a lot of fixing. Our [archetype map](/articles/vintage-cube-archetypes/) walks through them; the short version for drafting:

| Lane | Commit signal | What you need to see by end of pack one |
|---|---|---|
| Boros aggro | Two good one-drops and a Jitte or Copter | Six creatures at one or two mana |
| Blue power | Ancestral, Time Walk, or two Moxen plus a Tinker target | A card-draw engine and a way to win |
| Welder artifacts | Goblin Welder or Engineer pick one to three | Six artifacts worth welding and a draw engine |
| Reanimator or Sneak | Entomb, Animate Dead, Sneak Attack, Show and Tell | Two enablers and two targets |
| Green ramp | Natural Order, Gaea's Cradle, Oath of Druids | Four mana creatures and the payoff |
| Red prowess (on trial) | Monastery Swiftspear plus two cheap spells late | Twelve one- and two-mana instants and sorceries |
| Landfall (on trial) | Quirion Ranger or Carnivorous Cultivator wheeling | Three payoffs and fetch lands |

The reason commitment matters more here than in an unpowered cube is depth. The Vintage Cube has about 60 cards per mono color and gold sections of ten to fourteen. If two people fight over blue power, both lose; if two people fight over Boros, both end up with 2/2s and no Jitte. A clean lane with less raw power beats a contested lane with more.

Aggro has a special rule. The curator said in October 2026 that Boros is still the strongest archetype after repeated trims, and the January 2026 post called white aggro "saturated." Both statements mean the same thing for a drafter: the deck is good and the slots are limited. If you see Ocelot Pride, Adeline, or Ragavan after pick three, somebody upstream is not in it, and you should be.

## What are the mistakes that lose Vintage Cube drafts?

**Taking power for a deck you are not building.** Griselbrand is a first-pick in Reanimator and a seven-mana 7/7 in blue tempo. Natural Order in a deck with eight creatures is a Lava Axe. The discipline in the title is this: after pick six or so, a card's value is its value in your deck, not its value on the list.

**Drafting a four-color pile because the fixing is good.** The Vintage Cube has the ten duals, ten fetches, ten fast lands, Triomes and Verges. It is possible to play everything. The decks that do so lose to the Boros deck that curved out on turn two. Two colors and a splash is the usual ceiling, and mono-color is a real option in red and white.

**Overvaluing counterspells in a non-blue deck's shell.** Force of Will is a great card in a deck with 18 other blue cards to pitch. In a two-color deck with nine blue cards it is a three-mana Cancel.

**Ignoring the trial lanes.** When the curator adds a ten-card package, as with red prowess in October 2026, nobody at the table has drafted it before. For the first week of a league those cards wheel. If you see Soul-Scar Mage at pick nine, the lane is open.

**Forgetting what left.** If your memory says Time Warp, Channel, Eternal Witness, Bazaar of Baghdad, or Survival of the Fittest are in the cube, it is out of date as of October 7, 2026. Read the changelist before each league.

## How do you build the deck once the draft is done?

Forty cards, sixteen or seventeen lands unless you have three or more Moxen, in which case fifteen is fine. The aggro deck plays sixteen with a low curve and treats Moxen as spells.

Run every piece of fast mana you drafted that produces your colors. Run Sol Ring in aggro. Do not run Mana Vault in a deck without a four- or five-drop to accelerate into; it is a worse card than it looks in a deck of two-drops.

For the engine decks, count your win conditions. Blue power with Tinker and no target is a common sight in league decklists. In October 2026 the targets on the list are Blightsteel Colossus, Portal to Phyrexia, Myr Battlesphere, Ginger, Queen of Sweets and Kaldra Compleat; Triplicate Titan is out. Reanimator wants three or four targets, not one, because Griselbrand alone gets Thoughtseized.

## How should you sideboard in a league?

Vintage Cube leagues on MTGO are best-of-three with sideboarding from your entire pool, which matters more than in most cubes because the pool has so much narrow interaction. Cards to keep in the sideboard that come in constantly: Containment Priest and Soul-Guide Lantern against Reanimator and Sneak, Force of Vigor and Haywire Mite against the artifact decks, Pyrokinesis against aggro, Dismember as colorless removal. All six are on the October 7 list. The one-line rule for Vintage Cube is: draft the hate cards late and board them in early.

## FAQ

### What is the best first pick in Vintage Cube?

Black Lotus, with Sol Ring and Ancestral Recall as the usual alternatives. All three go in any deck, which is the quality that matters most at pick one.

### Should you draft aggro in the Vintage Cube?

Yes. The curator's October 2, 2026 post says Boros remains the strongest archetype. The constraint is slot count, so you need to see the one-drops flowing before you commit.

### How many lands in a Vintage Cube deck?

Sixteen for aggro, seventeen for midrange and control, fifteen if you have three or more pieces of zero-mana artifact acceleration. These are house guidelines, not a rule.

### How many colors should a Vintage Cube deck play?

Two, with a light splash at most. The fixing supports more, but the format's speed punishes clunky mana.
