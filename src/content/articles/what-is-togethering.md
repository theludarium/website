There is a word we use a lot at The Ludarium: togethering.

It means people coming together, usually in person, to share life. Not just being in the same room, but actually being with each other. Talking, laughing, losing gracefully, and asking for a rematch.

## The game is the reason

Getting friends together is harder than it should be. Everyone is busy. Dinner plans fizzle. "We should hang out sometime" turns into months.

A game changes that. It gives the evening a shape. It gives people something to do with their hands and something to talk about besides work. It makes it easy to say yes, because you know what the night will be.

## The people are the point

Here is the secret. Nobody remembers the final score a year later. They remember the friend who bluffed with nothing in hand, the impossible comeback, the inside joke that started over a card game and never stopped.

The game starts the time together. The people make it worth it.

## Why we built The Ludarium

The Ludarium exists to help more people gather. That is why we write guides for hosting and teaching, and why we build free tools for game nights. It is also why we are working toward a membership game lounge in Indianapolis: a place where you can walk in on your own and find a table and someone to play with.

Togethering for a lifetime. Never stop playing.
