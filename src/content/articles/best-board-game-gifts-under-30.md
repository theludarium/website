The best board game gifts under $30 are not the cheapest games on the shelf. They are the ones that get opened, taught in five minutes, and asked for again. That is a narrower list than you might think, and it is the list below.

**The quick answer:** Codenames (about $25) is the safest board game gift under $30 for a group. For a couple, give Patchwork or Jaipur; for a family, Qwirkle or Sushi Go!; and if you know nothing at all, Love Letter is about $16 and plays like a bigger game.

Every game here is in print and widely stocked at the time of writing. Prices were checked on publisher or major retailer pages and will move around, especially in November. Treat them as a guide, not a promise.

## What makes a board game a good gift?

A game is a strange gift because it asks the recipient to do work. They have to read rules, gather people, and teach. So the games that succeed as gifts share a few traits.

- **Teachable in under five minutes.** If the rulebook needs a second read, the box stays shut.
- **Plays well at the recipient's real player count.** A couple needs a two-player game. A family of five needs a game that seats five.
- **Short.** Thirty minutes or less means it gets played on a weeknight, not saved for a holiday that never comes.
- **Small enough to wrap.** Petty, but true.

### For a couple or a two-player household

**Patchwork** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (about $24 to $30; list price $29.99 at the [Asmodee store](https://store.asmodee.com/products/patchwork-revised-edition), which was sold out at the time of writing, and [$23.99 at GameNerdz](https://www.gamenerdz.com/patchwork-revised-edition)). **Jaipur** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (about $27; [$26.99 at the Asmodee store](https://store.asmodee.com/products/jaipur)). Two merchants trade goods and camels in a market. Fast, tense, and easy to teach. A better two-player card game than many at twice the price.

**The Fox in the Forest** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (about $16; [$15.99 at Noble Knight](https://www.nobleknight.com/P/2147670823/Fox-in-the-Forest-The) at the time of writing). A two-player trick-taking game with a fairy-tale deck. The twist: win too many tricks and you lose. A good pick for a couple who grew up on card games, at about half the budget.

### For a family

**The Crew: Mission Deep Sea** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (about $15; [$14.95 at the Thames and Kosmos store](https://store.thamesandkosmos.com/products/the-crew-mission-deep-sea)). A cooperative trick-taking game. The table works together to win specific tricks with very limited communication. It teaches card sense to kids and humility to adults. Three to five players, ages ten and up.

**Qwirkle** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (about $22 to $25; [$21.97 at GameNerdz](https://www.gamenerdz.com/qwirkle) at the time of writing). Wooden tiles with shapes and colors; make lines that share one attribute. It is Scrabble without the vocabulary gap, which makes it one of the few games where a grandparent and a seven-year-old can be evenly matched. Two to four players, ages six and up.

**Blokus** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (about $22; [list price $21.99 at Mattel](https://shop.mattel.com/products/blokus-game-bjv44), though Mattel's own store was out of stock at the time of writing, so check a big-box store). Up to four players place colored pieces on a grid, each new piece touching your own only at the corners. Visual, quick, and a quiet lesson in spatial thinking. Best at exactly four.

### For a game-night host

**Hues and Cues** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (about $25 to $26; list price $25.95 at [The Op](https://theop.games/products/hues-and-cues), sold out there at the time of writing, and [$24.95 at GameNerdz](https://www.gamenerdz.com/hues-and-cues)). One player gives a one-word clue, then a two-word clue, for a specific color on a 480-shade board; everyone else guesses. It seats three to ten and gets people arguing about whether "salmon" is pink or orange, which is the point.

**Hive Pocket** <!-- AFFILIATE --> (about $25; [$24.47 at GameNerdz](https://www.gamenerdz.com/hive-pocket) at the time of writing). Chess-like, with chunky insect tiles and no board. Two players, about twenty minutes, fits in a bag. The one game on this list that plays well on a bar table.

## What should you skip under $30?

A few categories tend to disappoint as gifts, even when the price is right.

1. **Licensed versions of classic games.** Monopoly with a movie on it is still Monopoly. The license adds nothing to the play.
2. **"Party games" that are really drinking games.** Many are a single joke stretched over a deck of cards. They get played once.
3. **Expansions for a game you are not sure they own.** Check first, or buy the base game.
4. **Anything that says "10 to 15 players" on the box.** Many of those are trivia or charades in a new box, and the recipient already owns charades. It is free.

## Does the price matter to the recipient?

Less than you think. The games that become a household staple are often not the expensive ones. Wingspan and Gloomhaven are wonderful, and big boxes like them can sit on a shelf while a quick party game gets played every week.

A $15 card game that gets played forty times is a better gift than a $70 box that gets played twice. The game is the reason. The people are the point.

## FAQ

### What is the best board game gift under $30 for adults?

Codenames, for a group, or Jaipur for a couple. Both are deep enough to reward repeat plays and simple enough to teach in minutes.

### Can you get a good two-player board game under $30?

Yes. Patchwork, Jaipur, The Fox in the Forest, and Hive Pocket are all designed for two players and all sold for $30 or less at the time of writing.

### Are cheap board games worth buying?

Price tracks component count more than quality. Many of the best-designed games are card games that cost under $20 because cards are cheap to print.

### Where are board games cheapest?

Amazon and Target often have the lowest everyday prices on mass-market titles, and both have discounted heavily in late November; see our guide to [Black Friday board game deals](/articles/black-friday-board-game-deals/). Your local game store may be a few dollars more and will let you ask a person which game fits.
