For a bigger step up, **Spirit Island** is the co-op many Pandemic fans end up calling their favorite.

## What makes a game feel like Pandemic?

Pandemic, designed by Matt Leacock and published by Z-Man Games in 2008, plays 2 to 4 in about 45 minutes. It made cooperative games mainstream. Our explainer on [the Pandemic board game](/articles/pandemic-board-game-explained/) covers why it still works. When people say they want "something like Pandemic," they usually mean one or more of four things:

1. **Everyone wins or loses together.** No one at the table is your opponent. The game is.
2. **A threat that spreads.** Diseases pile up, outbreaks chain, and every turn ends with things getting worse.
3. **Roles that matter.** The Medic, the Scientist, and the Dispatcher each break a rule. Using them well together is the puzzle. Our [roles guide](/academy/pandemic/pandemic-roles-guide/) shows how.
4. **Planning out loud.** The best part of Pandemic is the table talking through the next three turns together.

That last point has a downside. In an open-information co-op, one confident player can end up making every decision. That is called the [quarterback problem](/articles/quarterback-problem-cooperative-games/), and the last group on this list is built to fix it.

## If you loved Matt Leacock's design

Pandemic's designer has made more co-ops, and they share its DNA: roles, a clear threat, and a board that gets worse every turn.

### Forbidden Island

Designed by Matt Leacock and published by Gamewright in 2010, [Forbidden Island](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B003D7F4YY?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) plays 2 to 4 in about 30 minutes. Your team collects four treasures from an island that is sinking tile by tile, then escapes by helicopter.

**Why it fits:** it is Pandemic's core loop with less to track. Collect sets of cards, use your role, and stop the flooding in time. It is the pick for families and newer players, or for a quick game before a longer one. We compared the two in [Pandemic vs Forbidden Island](/articles/pandemic-vs-forbidden-island/).

### Forbidden Desert

Designed by Matt Leacock and published by Gamewright in 2013, [Forbidden Desert](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00B4ECHJI?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) plays 2 to 5 in about 45 minutes. Your team digs through a shifting desert to find the parts of a flying machine while a sandstorm buries the board and your water runs low.

**Why it fits:** it is the best middle step between Forbidden Island and Pandemic. The sandstorm moves the tiles themselves, so the map changes every turn. Water adds a second clock, and sharing it matters. Groups that found Forbidden Island too easy usually find this one just right.

### Daybreak

Designed by Matt Leacock and Matteo Menapace and published by CMYK in 2023, [Daybreak](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C79QBRMJ?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) plays 1 to 4 in about 60 to 90 minutes. Each player leads a world power, and the team works to cut emissions and protect communities from climate crises. Under its German title, e-Mission, it won the 2024 Kennerspiel des Jahres.

**Why it fits:** it feels like Pandemic grown up and pointed at a real problem. There is still a threat that builds, and still a team racing to stay ahead of it. The difference is that each player has their own engine to build, so turns feel more personal. It is a good pick for groups that like Pandemic's tension but want more to think about.

## If you want more Pandemic

### Pandemic Legacy: Season 1

Designed by Matt Leacock and Rob Daviau and published by Z-Man Games in 2015, [Pandemic Legacy: Season 1](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00TQ0DXR2?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) plays 2 to 4 in about 60 minutes a game. It is Pandemic played as a campaign of 12 to 24 games across one year of game time. The board, rules, and characters change permanently as you play.

**Why it fits:** it is the same game you already know, with a story and real stakes. Cities fall, characters get scars, and new rules open up as the months go by. It needs a steady group that can meet regularly, since you play it once through. Our guide to [legacy board games](/articles/legacy-board-games-explained/) explains how the format works. For a lighter way to stay in the family, our lesson on [Pandemic expansions](/academy/pandemic/pandemic-expansions/) covers the add-ons and spin-offs.

## If you loved racing a spreading threat

These four keep Pandemic's best feeling: things are getting worse, and the team has to decide what to fix first.

### Flash Point: Fire Rescue

Designed by Kevin Lanzing and published by Indie Boards and Cards in 2011, [Flash Point: Fire Rescue](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0084M8UEE?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) plays 1 to 6 in about 45 minutes. Your team of firefighters rushes into a burning building to rescue people before it collapses.

**How it differs:** smoke turns to fire, and fire spreads and causes explosions, much like Pandemic's outbreaks. But the theme is close to home and easy to grasp, and it plays up to six. Firefighter roles add Pandemic-style special powers. It is a good pick for families with older kids.

### Horrified

Designed by Prospero Hall and published by Ravensburger in 2019, [Horrified](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07QQHTD4Q?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) plays 1 to 5 in about 60 minutes. Classic movie monsters roam a village, and your team must protect the villagers while solving a different puzzle to defeat each monster.

**How it differs:** each monster is a small puzzle with its own rules, so you choose which ones to face and how hard the game is. It moves around a map and uses items the way Pandemic uses cards. It is warmer and sillier, and it is easy to teach.

### Spirit Island

Designed by R. Eric Reuss and published by Greater Than Games in 2017, [Spirit Island](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B01MUHP51S?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) plays 1 to 4 in 90 to 120 minutes. You play island spirits defending your home from colonizing invaders, who explore, build, and ravage the land in a predictable order.

**How it differs:** this is the big step up. Like Pandemic, you can see the threat coming and must plan around it. Unlike Pandemic, each spirit plays very differently, and you grow in power over the game. It rewards planning and is a lot to learn. Learn it with [how to play Spirit Island](/academy/spirit-island/how-to-play-spirit-island/).

### Aeon's End

Designed by Kevin Riley and published by Action Phase Games in 2016, [Aeon's End](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B072BG9Z9V?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) plays 1 to 4 in about 60 minutes. You are mages defending the last city from a monster, building your deck of spells as the fight goes on.

**How it differs:** it swaps Pandemic's map for a boss fight. You never shuffle your deck, so you can plan your next hand, and turn order is random, so you plan for surprises. It suits groups who like Pandemic's tension and want a card game at the center.

## If one player kept running the game

Pandemic's open information lets the most confident player decide everyone's turn. These co-ops limit what you can see or say, so every player has to think for themselves. Our list of [co-op games without a quarterback problem](/articles/co-op-games-without-quarterback-problem/) has more.

### The Crew: Mission Deep Sea

Designed by Thomas Sing and published by Kosmos in 2021, [The Crew: Mission Deep Sea](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08TX1GGSH?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) plays 2 to 5 in about 20 minutes a mission. It is a cooperative trick-taking game: the team must win certain tricks with certain cards, but you cannot show or discuss your hand.

**How it differs:** there is no board or spreading threat. The tension comes from reading each other with very little talking, across a campaign of missions that get harder. Learn it with [how to play The Crew](/academy/the-crew/how-to-play-the-crew/) or read our [guide to The Crew](/articles/the-crew-card-game/).

### Hanabi

Designed by Antoine Bauza and first published in 2010, [Hanabi](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B01MXTGT21?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) won the 2013 Spiel des Jahres. It plays 2 to 5 in about 25 minutes. You hold your cards facing out, so you see everyone's hand but your own, and the team works to play a fireworks show in order.

**How it differs:** clues are limited, so no one can tell anyone what to do. It is short and quiet, and it fits in a pocket. It is a good pick for a table that liked talking through Pandemic but wants everyone to carry their share.

### Sky Team

Designed by Luc Rémond and published by Le Scorpion Masqué in 2023, [Sky Team](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CHD8RCSJ?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) won the 2024 Spiel des Jahres. It is for 2 players and takes about 15 minutes. One player is the pilot and one the co-pilot, landing a plane with dice you cannot discuss.

**How it differs:** it is built for two, and it bans the table talk Pandemic thrives on. You plan together before you roll, then trust each other. It is the pick for couples who love Pandemic. Learn it with [how to play Sky Team](/academy/sky-team/how-to-play-sky-team/), and see our list of [two player cooperative board games](/articles/two-player-cooperative-board-games/) for more.

## FAQ

### What is the closest game to Pandemic?

Forbidden Desert, from the same designer. It shares roles, a spreading threat, and the same "draw cards, then things get worse" turn. Pandemic Legacy: Season 1 is closer still, but it is Pandemic itself.

### What is a good game like Pandemic for kids?

Forbidden Island. It teaches the same ideas in about 30 minutes, with simpler rules and a friendlier theme. Flash Point: Fire Rescue and Horrified suit older kids.

### What is a harder game like Pandemic?

Spirit Island. It keeps the visible, spreading threat but asks much more of every player. If you want to stay with Pandemic, our [Pandemic strategy guide](/articles/pandemic-strategy/) covers beating it on Heroic difficulty.

### Is Pandemic Legacy worth it if you already own Pandemic?

Yes, if you have a group that can play it through. It starts as plain Pandemic and changes as you go, so the first games feel familiar. Just know you play the campaign once.

### What is a good co-op for two players like Pandemic?

Pandemic plays well at two. For something new, Sky Team is built for two, and Forbidden Desert and Spirit Island also play well with a pair.
