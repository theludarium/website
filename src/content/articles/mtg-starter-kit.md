The best MTG starter kit for most new players in 2026 is the Foundations Beginner Box. It is the one product Wizards of the Coast built to teach two people the game at a table, with guided decks and no rulebook reading aloud. After that, the right next step depends on how you want to play: a Commander precon if your friends play Commander, a 60-card deck if you want store events, or the Starter Collection if you want a pile of cards to build with.

**The quick answer:** two people learning together should get the Beginner Box. Someone joining friends who already play Commander should get a Commander precon. Anyone who wants to try before spending should download MTG Arena, which is free, or ask a local store about free Magic Academy events and Welcome Decks.

This is the product comparison. If you want the learning path, our Academy lesson on [how to start playing Magic](/academy/magic/how-to-start-playing-magic/) lays out what to do first, second, and third. Here we look at every beginner product Wizards currently offers, what is in each box, and who each one is for. Prices change by store and season, so we leave them out.

## Foundations Beginner Box: the best first purchase

[The Foundations Beginner Box](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0D9KXBNBB?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) is our first pick for anyone new. Wizards designed it to teach, and it does that better than any other product.

**What's inside:** 10 themed Jumpstart decks of 20 cards each, built from the Foundations set, two "How to Play" guides, a reference guide booklet with two reference cards, two gameboard playmats, and two life counters.

**How it works:** two of the decks, Cats and Vampires, come stacked in a set order. Do not shuffle them. The guides walk two players through a first game turn by turn. After that, the other eight decks come out. Pick two, shuffle them into a 40-card deck, and play. Ten decks make dozens of pairings.

**Who it's for:** two people learning together, a couple, a parent and teen, or a friend teaching a friend. It is not built for one person alone, since the tutorial needs two players.

**Why it lasts:** Foundations cards stay legal in Standard for years, so nothing in the box goes stale. You can mix these decks with cards you get later.

**For Marvel fans:** Wizards also made a Marvel Super Heroes Beginner Box for the June 2026 set. It works the same way: ten 20-card half-decks, with Captain America and Iron Man as the two stacked tutorial decks. Either box teaches the same rules.

## Foundations Starter Collection: a collection in a box

[The Foundations Starter Collection](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0D9KYHN4D?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) is a different idea. Instead of ready-made decks, it gives you a pile of cards and lets you build.

**What's inside, per Wizards:** 387 cards, including 26 traditional foils, 3 Foundations Play Boosters, 90 basic lands (10 of them full-art), 13 tokens, 2 reference cards, a "How to Build Your Deck" booklet, and a click wheel.

**Who it's for:** a player who knows the basics and wants to build decks of their own. It pairs well with the Beginner Box: learn with the box, build with the collection.

**What it isn't:** a tutorial. There is no step-by-step first game here. A brand-new player alone with 387 cards can feel lost, so start with the Beginner Box or Arena first.

## Two-deck Starter Kits

For years Wizards has sold Starter Kits with two full 60-card decks, ready to play against each other. The most recent one we could confirm is the Final Fantasy Starter Kit from 2025, with a Cloud deck and a Sephiroth deck, four tokens, two deck boxes, and two MTG Arena code cards in select regions. We found no new two-deck Starter Kit for 2026; Wizards' 2026 ready-to-play decks are the single Theme Decks below.

**Who it's for:** two players who want full-size, 60-card decks, especially fans of the theme on the box. Older Starter Kits stay on shelves for a long time, so you may see several themes.

**The catch:** Starter Kits teach less than the Beginner Box. They come with decks, not a guided first game. If neither of you has played, learn the turn on Arena first.

## Theme Decks: a 60-card deck for store play

Theme Decks are new in 2026. Each is a ready-to-play 60-card Standard deck that mixes common Standard staples with cards for one strategy. The first two, Angels and Pirates, arrived with Lorwyn Eclipsed in January. Eerie and Lifegain followed with Secrets of Strixhaven in April, and Wizards announced two more for Reality Fracture. Each comes with a deck box, five double-sided tokens, a strategy insert, and a reference card.

**Who it's for:** a solo player who has learned the basics and wants to try Standard events at a local store. A Theme Deck gives you a legal deck for Friday Night Magic without building one from scratch. Our Academy lesson on [your first Friday Night Magic](/academy/magic/first-friday-night-magic/) covers what to expect.

## Welcome Decks: the free-at-the-store option

Welcome Decks are free single-color decks that Wizards sends to participating stores, one for each color, while supplies last. The April 2026 refresh, which arrived with Secrets of Strixhaven, uses 40-card decks of Standard-legal Foundations cards. Marvel Super Heroes got its own version in June 2026: two 30-card half-decks in each box, one themed around a Marvel hero.

Each store decides how to hand them out, so ask yours. Many pair them with a learn-to-play session. A Welcome Deck is a lovely first taste, but it is one deck, so you need a friend with a second deck to play.

## Commander precons: the start for most friend groups

If your friends already play Magic, odds are they play Commander, the 100-card multiplayer format. A Commander precon is a preconstructed deck that is ready to play at their table the day you buy it.

**Our pick for new players:** the [Foundations Commander decks](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GXCBW828?tag=cubecommonw0a-20), released October 2, 2026. Wizards says they are designed to introduce the format to new players. Each is one color with one clear plan, which keeps color-identity puzzles out of the way:

| Deck | Color | Plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Calling All Angels | White | Angels, led by Giada, Font of Hope |
| Keen Engineering | Blue | Artifacts and Thopters, led by Sai, Master Thopterist |
| Wretched Ranks | Black | Zombies, led by Ghoulcaller Gisa |
| Reign of Dragons | Red | Dragons, led by Lathliss, Dragon Queen |
| Tramplesaurus Rex | Green | Big creatures, led by Ghalta, Primal Hunger |

Each box holds a 100-card deck with a foil borderless commander, ten double-sided tokens, a reference card, and a deck box. Wizards publishes the [decklists](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/foundations-commander-decklists) if you want to compare.

**For fans:** the [Star Trek Commander decks](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H6H4PL4G?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) come out November 13, 2026, led by Picard, Spock, Worf, and The Borg Queen. They are three-color decks, a little more to manage, but a great draw for Trek fans. Our [MTG Star Trek guide](/articles/mtg-star-trek-set-guide/) covers them.

**Before you buy:** read [how to play Commander](/articles/how-to-play-commander-mtg/). The rules are a short add-on to regular Magic, and the pregame bracket talk is worth knowing.

**A note on choosing:** pick by theme, not by which deck the internet calls strongest. A deck you love the look of will get played more.

## Jumpstart: two packs make a deck

[Jumpstart boosters](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0D9KYCWZY?tag=cubecommonw0a-20) are 20-card half-decks, each built around one theme. Shuffle any two together and you have a 40-card deck with its lands already in it. Wizards has made Jumpstart packs for several sets, including Foundations.

**Who it's for:** casual players who like surprise. A few Jumpstart packs make a quick game for two people who already know the rules. They also make good stocking stuffers for a friend who plays.

**The catch:** they do not teach, and the decks vary. The Beginner Box is, in effect, ten Jumpstart decks plus a tutorial, which is why we would start there instead.

For a group that wants to reuse the same packs, our [Jumpstart cube](/articles/jumpstart-cube/) guide shows how to build a reusable set of half-decks.

## Draft Night: a starter for a group of four

Draft Night is another 2026 product: everything four people need for a Pick-Two Draft at home. It has 12 Play Boosters, 90 basic lands, tokens, and a Collector Booster to give the winner, and Wizards says a Pick-Two Draft takes about 90 minutes.

It is not for brand-new players. Drafting asks you to build a deck from cards you have never seen. But for four friends who have each played a few games, it is one of the most social ways to play. Our guide on [how to draft MTG](/articles/how-to-draft-mtg/) explains how picking and passing works.

## What are the free ways to start Magic?

You can start without buying anything.

### MTG Arena

MTG Arena is Wizards' digital Magic, free on desktop and mobile. It has a tutorial that teaches the turn, then gives you starter decks to play against other people. It is the best way to learn the rules alone, at your own pace. The catch is that it is a screen, not a table. Use it to learn, then bring what you learned to real people.

### Magic Academy events

Local game stores run free Magic Academy events. Wizards describes two kinds: learn-to-play sessions with simple decks, and deck-building sessions for your first deck. The store provides the cards. Find one with the [Wizards store locator](https://locator.wizards.com/).

### A friend's cube

If a friend owns a cube, a shared collection built for drafting, you can play without owning a card. Our piece on [teaching Magic with a cube](/articles/how-to-teach-magic-the-gathering/) explains why that may be the best start of all.

## What should new players skip?

- **Collector Boosters.** They are built for collecting foils and alternate art, not for learning to play.
- **A full box of Play Boosters.** Opening a box is fun once. It is a slow way to build a deck you can actually play.
- **Single cards before you have a plan.** Wait until you know which deck is yours.
- **Every new set.** A new set arrives every few months. You do not need to follow them to have fun.

## FAQ

### What is the best MTG starter kit for beginners?

The Foundations Beginner Box. It includes two guided decks that walk two players through a first game turn by turn, plus eight more themed decks to mix and match afterward.

### What is the difference between a Starter Kit and the Beginner Box?

A Starter Kit has two full 60-card decks, ready to play against each other. The Beginner Box has ten 20-card decks and a guided tutorial that teaches the rules as you play. The Beginner Box teaches more; a Starter Kit gives you bigger decks.

### Is a Commander precon good for a beginner?

Yes, if your friends play Commander. Learn the basic turn first, then pick a precon by theme. The Foundations Commander decks are single-color and were designed to introduce the format.

### Can you learn Magic for free?

Yes. MTG Arena is free on desktop and mobile with a built-in tutorial, and many local stores run free Magic Academy events where they provide the cards.

### Which MTG starter deck is best for one player?

For one person, start on MTG Arena to learn the rules. Then pick up a Theme Deck for store play or a Commander precon to join a group. The Beginner Box is great, but its tutorial needs two players.
