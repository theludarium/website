## What the history teaches

Reading back through four versions, the lessons are the ones every long-running cube seems to arrive at:

1. **The first version is always a pile.** It becomes a format the first time someone drafts a lane on purpose.
2. **Power is the easy mistake.** It feels like improvement from the owner's seat and like exclusion from the new player's.
3. **Write it down.** The cube got better the month the changelog started, and not before.
4. **Habits are design.** The trophy and the read-aloud changelog shaped the cube as much as any card.
5. **The box outlasts any one night.** A pause is not an ending if the object and the people are still there.
