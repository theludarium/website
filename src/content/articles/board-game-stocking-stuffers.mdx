Stocking stuffer games have a simple test: the box has to fit in a sock, and the game inside has to be good enough that someone chooses it over the phone they also got. More games pass the first test than the second. The twelve below pass both.

**The quick answer:** Love Letter (about $16) is the best all-round stocking stuffer game. Add Sushi Go! or Qwixx for kids and families, The Fox in the Forest or Hive Pocket for a couple, and Poetry for Neanderthals for a stocking that belongs to a teenager.

Each is a card, dice, or tile game in a box small enough to wrap in a napkin. Most cost under $20. Prices are approximate at the time of writing and will move in December. Every game name links to Amazon.

## What makes a small game worth a stocking?

Small boxes are where a lot of the best design in the hobby lives, because cards are cheap to print and designers get to be clever instead of lavish. The ones that work as gifts share three things.

- **They teach in one minute.** A stocking game gets opened on the floor at 7 a.m. The rules have to survive that.
- **They play in fifteen to twenty minutes.** It is going to be played between breakfast and the next thing. Short rounds mean it gets played at all.
- **They play at a range of counts.** You do not know who will be around when the stocking is opened.

## Does the box really have to be small?

A stocking stuffer is partly about the moment of discovery, so yes, size matters. But there is a second reason to favor small games: small games get played more.

A box that fits in a bag goes to the restaurant while you wait for a table, to the cabin, to the waiting room. The game that is always nearby is the game that gets played. We have written about this effect in our piece on [quick board games under 30 minutes](/articles/quick-board-games-under-30-minutes/).

## What small games should you skip?

- **Tiny versions of big games.** Travel Catan, Monopoly Deal, mini Scrabble. The miniaturization often removes the fun or the legibility or both. Catan: On the Road is the exception because it is a different game, not a shrunken one.
- **Novelty decks.** A deck of cards with a theme printed on it is a deck of cards. Fine, but not a game.
- **Dexterity games with many small parts.** They are fun and they will be under the couch by New Year's.
- **Anything that needs a phone app.** The stocking game should work when the phone is dead, which, by the time the stockings are opened, it might be.

## The best stocking is one game and a promise

Put one small game in the stocking and write on the gift tag: "First game is this afternoon." Then clear the table and do it. The game is about twelve dollars. The afternoon is the gift. The game is the reason. The people are the point.

## FAQ

### What are good stocking stuffer games for adults?

Love Letter, The Fox in the Forest, and Hive Pocket. All are about $25 or less at the time of writing, fit in a stocking, and hold up to repeated play.

### What is a good small game for kids' stockings?

Sushi Go! for ages eight and up, Qwixx for anyone who can add. Both play in about fifteen minutes and cost about $10 to $16.

### Are there two-player games small enough for a stocking?

Yes. The Fox in the Forest, Hive Pocket, and Love Letter (which plays two) all come in pocket-sized boxes or bags.

### How much should a stocking stuffer game cost?

Most of the good ones are $7 to $20. Above $25 you are into gift territory, not stocking territory.
