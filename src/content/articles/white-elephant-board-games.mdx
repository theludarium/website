A good white elephant board game gets stolen. It is easy to recognize, fun to unwrap in front of a crowd, and useful to almost anyone who takes it home. Party games and quick card games do this best, because the whole room can picture playing them that night.

**The quick answer:** at a $20 limit, bring Exploding Kittens or Sushi Go!. At $25, bring Codenames or Herd Mentality. At $30, bring Hues and Cues. For a laugh that is still a good game, bring Poetry for Neanderthals, which comes with an inflatable club.

## How does a white elephant gift exchange work?

Everyone brings one wrapped gift under a set price limit and draws a number. The first person opens a gift from the pile. Each person after that can either open a new gift or steal one that has already been opened. Anyone who gets robbed picks again, either a new gift or another steal. Most groups cap steals, often at three per gift, so the last steal locks it in. The fun is in the stealing, which is why the gift you bring should be one people want to take.

## What makes a board game a good white elephant gift?

- **Instant recognition.** If people know the name, they want it before the paper is off. Codenames and Exploding Kittens have that pull.
- **A funny unwrap.** The room is watching. A box with a pink cow or a foam burrito gets a reaction.
- **Plays with a crowd.** The person who takes it home should be able to play it at the next party, not need exactly two friends.
- **Inside the limit.** Going over the limit makes everyone else look cheap. Going way under makes you look cheap. Aim for within a few dollars.

## Best white elephant board games under $20

### Exploding Kittens

<AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B010TQY7A8?tag=cubecommonw0a-20">Exploding Kittens</AffiliateLink> is the most stolen-looking box on this list. Players draw cards until someone draws an Exploding Kitten and is out, unless they have a Defuse card. Action cards let you skip, attack, and shuffle your way out of trouble. It plays 2 to 5, ages 7 and up, in about fifteen minutes, for about $20 at the time of writing. Nearly everyone has heard of it, which is half the battle.

### Sushi Go!

<AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00J57VU44?tag=cubecommonw0a-20">Sushi Go!</AffiliateLink> is a card drafting game in a tin. Pick one card, pass the rest, and collect sets of sushi for points. It plays 2 to 5, ages 8 and up, in about fifteen minutes, for about $17. It works for a family with kids, a couple, or a group of coworkers, which makes it one of the safest gifts in the pile.

### Flip 7 and Taco Cat Goat Cheese Pizza, as a pair

Two small games in one box beats one small game every time. <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DWGVM7RY?tag=cubecommonw0a-20">Flip 7</AffiliateLink> is a push-your-luck card game for 3 or more, ages 8 and up, with a list price of about $8. Add <AffiliateLink href="https://www.amazon.com/dp/B077Z1R28P?tag=cubecommonw0a-20">Taco Cat Goat Cheese Pizza</AffiliateLink>, a slap game where players flip cards while chanting the five words, and slap the pile when the card matches the word. It plays 2 to 8, ages 8 and up, in about ten minutes, and sells for about $10 at the time of writing. Wrap them together and the pair comes in just under $20.

## What should you not bring to a white elephant?

- **Heavy strategy games.** A two-hour game with a thick rulebook will not get stolen. People cannot picture playing it.
- **Two-player-only games.** Great for couples, risky for a stranger.
- **Shock-humor card decks.** They are fine among close friends and awkward at an office party. If the swap is at work, see our guide to a [board game gift for a coworker](/articles/board-game-gift-for-coworker/).
- **Games that need an expansion.** Bring the base game, never just the add-on.

Whatever you bring, a game is a gift that asks people to spend time together. That is a better present than a candle. The game is the reason. The people are the point.

## FAQ

### What is the best board game for a white elephant exchange?

Codenames at a $25 limit, Exploding Kittens at $20. Both are widely known, play with a crowd, and get stolen.

### What is a good white elephant gift under $20?

Exploding Kittens or Sushi Go!, or pair Flip 7 with Taco Cat Goat Cheese Pizza for two games under $20.

### What white elephant gift gets stolen the most?

Gifts people recognize and can picture using that night. In games, that means party games like Codenames and Herd Mentality.

### Are board games good white elephant gifts?

Yes, if they teach fast and play with a group. Avoid long strategy games and two-player-only boxes.
